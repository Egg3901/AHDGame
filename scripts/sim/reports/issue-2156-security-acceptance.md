## Real authority, costs and sanctions

- Each national choice uses `submitCrisisDecision` and checks its actual treasury debit against the authored GDP share. Repeating the same national choice is rejected with no second charge.
- Current military readiness, logistics and domestic support govern expensive military options. The retained US logistics score is below its aid requirement. The command correctly refuses that option; the coalition adapts with US sanctions, German/British guarantees and Polish transit support. No capacity gate or input balance is lowered to force acceptance.
- Ukrainian neutrality and reciprocal Russian consent are both required for negotiated neutrality. Bystanders cannot impose it over either principal's objection.
- Participating sanctioning countries create real oil embargo records against the resolved opposing backer. Their consent is retained even if a different outcome wins the aggregate headline. The war cases create 4 to 116 embargo records; renewal is explicit across response windows.
- National military, covert and humanitarian commitments persist separately. Relief, military aid and sanctions affect their tracks together with the selected security outcome. Aid and reconstruction continue after firing stops.
- Origin damage/displacement and host-country burden feed the real macro consumer. Combined conflict exposures are bounded to 10% displaced labour, 0.5% host burden and a unit damage index, with movement limited per elapsed turn. These are declared game bounds, not calibrated refugee estimates.
