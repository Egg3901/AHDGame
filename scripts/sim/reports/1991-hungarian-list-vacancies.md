# Hungarian certified list replacement qualification

Issue: #2488. Runtime source: `11a0e35fdc626320c3e8476c7d231ae14599bda5`. Executed 2026-10-03. This is bounded election qualification, not a whole-world simulation.

The original [1989 election act, section 46(5)](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/1989-34-00-00.0) gives the party the choice of an originally filed nominee when a territorial or national-list deputy leaves. This differs from ordinary nomination withdrawal under section 5(8). The replacement path therefore preserves the certified party and list, requires a chair's choice, and excludes former mandate holders, seated people, unavailable owners and a player's second mandate. No new person or financial profile is created.

## Handover and bounded conventions

A shared chamber generation write serializes list replacements, constituency replacements and a new whole-Assembly handover. A retry rereads the latest certified parent. Original holder rows are archived before replacement; each physical list slot retains its stable office ID. Monotonic journal generations distinguish multiple departures in the same turn. The original result stays immutable apart from administrative counters.

Without a human chair, the bounded party default selects the first available originally filed nominee and records `autonomous_original_list_order`. At most one autonomous designation across Hungarian parties runs per turn; human chairs can act through their authenticated controls. Regional capacity remains with the certified physical slot, including national-list slots. NPC nominees share existing financial owners; their original list positions distinguish individual nominees in the selector.

All replacement journals are runtime collections and clear with a world reset. No live database is used. Joint or linked lists, later electoral reform political decisions, legacy duplicate candidature migration, territorial ballots with no filed list, and fresh source-pinned whole-world acceptance remain unfinished parent criteria.
