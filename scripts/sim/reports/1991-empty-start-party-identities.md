# Historical party identities in empty 1991 starts

Issue #3034 item 9 records that Northern Ireland's unionist decisions target a
DUP or UUP party leader, while absent historical identities are reserved by the
charter system. Players cannot create the required identity in a world that
intentionally removed its starting parties.

The portable reservation rule now recognizes an explicit `1991-default` world
with `startingPartiesMode: none` in a currently enabled player country. Existing
live parties and active charters retain their uniqueness checks. Background
countries, missing starting metadata and other presets keep reservations.
The ordinary founder, signature, ratification and party-leadership process is
unchanged. No leader or political decision outcome is created automatically.

Forty-five passing cases across four suites include four actual isolated Mongo
journeys. They verify real UUP charter creation and its historical abbreviation,
a second-draft rejection, existing-party protection, default-mode protection,
and a country becoming background. Portable rule and charter regression cases
also cover missing metadata and other eras. Existing Northern Ireland actor
requirements continue to pass their tests. Qualification does not claim that a
charter has been ratified or that an unattended world acquires a party leader.

The additional authority reads occur only when a requested identity conflicts
with a reserved default. Ordinary custom-name drafts gain no extra reads.
No production world is modified by qualification. Remaining seed-audit items
and full-world/release qualification remain open.
