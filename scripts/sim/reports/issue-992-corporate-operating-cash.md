# Corporate operating cash qualification for #992 and #968

## Result

The real sector cash writer could debit a loss while the transaction emitter wrote no operating entry. A stateful regression reproduced an 800-unit cash loss against zero ledger movement. Positive revenue and tax witnesses also rounded whole native units while cash retained fractions.

The repair emits a named `corp_operating_loss` sink and preserves native precision for operating revenue and tax. It changes accounting records only. Existing earnings, cash operations, taxes, transfers and reconciliation tolerances are unchanged.

A source-pinned retained-world continuation reconciled all 41 selected corporate accounts on every one of 12 isolated turns. The unchanged baseline had 33 to 39 divergent accounts per turn, including 210,444.1804 anchor units of unexplained movement on the first turn. Treatment divergence is zero throughout. Every corporation's closing cash is exactly identical to baseline on every turn, verified by complete corporate-cash hashes.

Both runs used isolated copies and preserved all copied source selections. No live-world mutation or historical compensation was performed.
