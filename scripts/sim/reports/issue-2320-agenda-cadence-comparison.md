# Issue #2320: controlled agenda cadence comparison

This deterministic timing harness compares baseline source `aa1812b56dfaa4df7996c87b16b0b16bc4a9d0b8` with the candidate rules in this change. It samples the six-turn strategic slot through five in-game years (turn 240). It exercises trigger timing only: it does not execute policy, model elections or player behavior, or claim economic outcomes. Run with `tsx --tsconfig tsconfig.json scripts/sim/2320-agenda-cadence.ts`.

The synthetic scenario starts without a crisis. A healthcare crisis begins at turn 24 with a metric effect of -1. At turn 78 its effect changes to -4 and its chain rung changes from 1 to 2. Healthcare urgency changes from 0.20 to 0.55 at turn 102. The crisis domain clears at turn 126. Turns are game-calendar turns; 48 turns equal one game year.

| Policy                                                 | Agenda recompute turns through turn 240    | Meaning                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Current: 168-turn cadence plus new-crisis exception    | 0, 24, 192                                 | Crisis onset is immediate, but the changed active-crisis effect and later condition change wait for the stale interval.                       |
| Candidate: annual cadence plus material-input triggers | 0, 24, 48, 78, 96, 102, 126, 144, 192, 240 | Crisis onset and later effect/condition/removal changes trigger early; annual reviews remain anchored to the last annual accountability turn. |

The candidate agenda interval is one game year. Provisional v5 goal holds are 48/96/144 turns (one/two/three game years for easy/normal/hard). Goals are reviewed during agenda recomputes, so expiration is observed at the next review slot at or after the hold duration. These authored durations remain uncalibrated until a multi-term source-pinned worldsim.

Accountability report cards have a persisted turn separate from agenda `computedTurn`. A due annual report forces a recompute even when an early event recently updated the agenda; early replans at turns 24 and 78 do not advance the report-card clock, while reports run at turns 48 and 96. The input snapshot stores normalized domain urgency, inflation, active crisis domain pressure, and sorted metric-effect/rung fingerprints. `Crisis` has no generic severity scalar, so the crisis trigger compares active metric-effect values and chain rung; it does not measure continuous severity beyond those persisted inputs. A legacy agenda without a snapshot recomputes once to establish its baseline.

The read-cost fixture used an isolated local Mongo database with a generated namespace. Cleanup in `finally` dropped only that database; a follow-up check verified it was absent. Ten matching synthetic crisis documents returned one `find` command in each case. Full documents returned 215,089 BSON bytes; the proposed crisis projection returned 3,189 bytes, a 98.52% reduction. The fixture deliberately included large unrelated narrative and decision-tree payloads, so this reduction is illustrative, not a production-world estimate. The agenda phase's existing crisis read remains one query per six-turn strategic slot. Material condition detection reuses `loadConditionsSignal`, adding two reads for countries without political boards (budget + national macro metrics) or three for board countries (budget + political metrics + state population weighting) on each slot instead of only on agenda recomputes. A v5 event-driven review also loads its existing domain-health inputs (two batched political metrics/states reads plus one macro-metrics read); the v4 early-replan path avoids that domain-health load unless its annual report is due. There is no per-domain N+1. This added condition-read cost needs qualification against real phase telemetry before merge.
