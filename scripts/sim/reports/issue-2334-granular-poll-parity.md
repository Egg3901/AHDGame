# Issue #2334 granular poll and vote-kernel parity

Run `npx tsx scripts/sim/issue-2334-granular-poll-parity.ts` for the deterministic fixture. It invokes the production granular-poll builder, `buildGranularElectorateSubstrate`, the shared campaign bonus function, and production `distributeVotesBySwingFlow`. The fixture pins the 2019-default US/Connecticut substrate, adds mapped archetype approvals to both candidates, and compares a poll at the survey turn against the next-turn vote tally.

The 48 cases cross three office configurations (governor, house, president), two actor mixes (human-human and human-NPP), four favorability pairs (50/50, 80/20, 20/80, 70/40), and two campaign phases (before campaign and after a real targeted senior ad). Before-campaign uses turns 19/20; after-campaign polls at turn 20 and tallies at turn 21 so the election kernel applies one-turn ad decay. Each case passes. Maximum absolute poll/tally difference is 0.050 percentage points (the harness allows 0.5 pp for rounding and fixture tolerance).

The slight nonzero tie-case offset comes from the fixture's asymmetric mapped archetype approvals (`retirees` and `young_renters`); the poll uses the same pure projection and effective-favorability/approval scalar as the vote kernel. The NPP actor cases also use the shared general-election NPP multiplier, with `hasPlayerInRace: true`, as the poll request always contains the requesting player. Undecided remains the existing illustrative heuristic based on raw positional appeals; it is not presented as sampled uncertainty. The panel now labels its band illustrative and explicitly says no respondents are sampled.

These are controlled parity scenarios, not a production-election forecast: the synthetic fixture holds the Connecticut electorate and candidate positions fixed across office configurations, so it verifies each production office option path but does not establish that real office-specific electorate inputs are complete. Campaign timing verifies a targeted age-bucket ad and next-turn decay, not every campaign instrument. Results are deterministic and use no live data or database.

Regression evidence: before the fix, the candidate-favorability regression failed at 50/50 against the shared kernel's 75.195% expected decided share. The focused poll, archetype mapping, and route tests pass after the fix. The route now forwards player and opponent archetype approvals and the opponent NPP flag. No full checks, builds, worldsim runs, or production actions were performed.
