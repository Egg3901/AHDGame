## Explicit political choices

Constitutional drafts now bind either the historical immediate-dissolution transition or an explicit alternate continuation clause. The amended1971 Constitution's transitional section50(4) allowed the Grand Assembly to continue as an ordinary chamber until its original four-year term ended or to dissolve itself. The historical1991 Constitution instead required dissolution after adoption, retained legislative functions until the elected successor took office and required an election within three months. The continuation option therefore changes the proposed transitional text explicitly. [Amended Constitution, transitional section50](https://www.ciela.net/svobodna-zona-darjaven-vestnik/document/516706304/issue/349/konstitutsiya-na-narodna-republika-balgariya-ot-1971-g), [1991 Constitution, transitional sections1 and7](https://www.constcourt.bg/en/legal-info-92).

Both draft variants still require267 votes from the whole400-seat constituent chamber. Each keeps its own collective100-seat initiative; signatures for one clause never introduce the other. An active draft cannot silently change its clause. Existing proposals, bills and signature journals without a disposition retain their historical immediate-dissolution meaning.

An enacted continuation retains the original Grand term. Untouched future ordinary campaigns keep that deadline, with240 seats for the elected successor. A separately introduced dissolution motion can advance that election using the bounded four-turn filing and two-turn general window. It requires more than200 deputies present and a majority of those present supporting it. The ordinary threshold is separate from constituent adoption. Sitting deputies retain caretaker functions until the elected successors take office. The motion is available only after a recorded continuation and cannot reopen an existing ordinary settlement. [Amended Constitution, Articles69 and81](https://www.ciela.net/svobodna-zona-darjaven-vestnik/document/516706304/issue/349/konstitutsiya-na-narodna-republika-balgariya-ot-1971-g).

Recorded votes, renewed polls and certified ballots retain their frozen count and clock. A political motion cannot erase them. Rejected motions preserve their bill history and may be revised with a new bound revision. Authorization, admitted campaign changes and the journal mark use one required transaction. Its session reads are sequential; independent read-only display queries may run together.

The authenticated route validates the draft clause, distinguishes collective endorsement from a separate dissolution motion and returns private, non-cached decision state. The rendered controls offer both clauses and link each actual bill to its normal parliamentary vote. Legislature presentation distinguishes Grand, continued, caretaker and ordinary chambers. Public composition counts an NPC bloc's mandate weight and excludes executive offices.
