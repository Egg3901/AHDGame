# Manufacturing product lines v2 deterministic balance report

Generated by `scripts/sim/manufacturingProductLinesV2Balance.ts` from the production lifecycle, allocation, and NPP scoring rules. No game database or world simulation is used.

## Development funding

- Allocated physical capital stock: 100,000 anchor units.
- Development threshold: 5,000 anchor units (5% of allocated capital). Elapsed threshold: 12 receipts.
- Funded at 500 anchor units of paid R&D per turn: 5,000 goes to development, 1,000 continues to generic research, and launch occurs on receipt 12.
- Unfunded for 12 turns: 0 development spend and stage remains development.
- The 5,000 cost is charged once as paid R&D is redirected. It does not add a second capex debit.

Assumption: the established legacy recipe produces 100,000 anchor units of nominal output value per day. Half of plant capacity is allocated to vehicles. Product output redirects recipe value; it does not add output value. Figures are gross nominal output at base prices, not net income or a sales guarantee.

## NPP candidate scoring

The rule scores capacity-weighted margin multiplied by scarcity premium and the kind's actual legacy strategy mix weight. A high scarcity ratio cannot win solely for a commodity the selected plants barely produce.

- Selected candidate in the deterministic comparison: recipe-exposed-steel.
- Price discovery, sales fill, input costs, wages, taxes, and financing affect realized P&L outside this nominal output conservation report.
