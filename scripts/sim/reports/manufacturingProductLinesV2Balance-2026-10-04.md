# Manufacturing product lines v2 deterministic balance report

Generated by `scripts/sim/manufacturingProductLinesV2Balance.ts` from the production lifecycle, allocation, and NPP scoring rules. No game database or world simulation is used.

## Development funding

- Allocated monetary plant capital: 100,000 anchor units.
- Physical capacity stock: 100,000 capacity units, kept separate from currency.
- Development threshold: 5,000 anchor units (5% of allocated capital). Elapsed threshold: 12 receipts.
- Funded at 500 anchor units of paid R&D per turn: 5,000 goes to development, 1,000 continues to generic research, and launch occurs on receipt 12.
- Unfunded for 12 turns: 0 development spend and stage remains development.
- Development is a separate capitalized cash investment, limited to available cash and paid once per turn. The matching paid-R&D allocation funds development first; any remainder continues to generic research.

## One representative operating day by stage

Assumptions: the established legacy recipe produces 100,000 anchor units of nominal output value per day. Half of plant capacity is allocated to vehicles. Product output redirects recipe value; it does not add output value. The market has demand for 80% of each offered commodity at base prices and neutral posture, so cash receipts use the production clearing rule. These are gross sales receipts before input, wage, upkeep, tax, financing, and development costs, not net income or a sales guarantee.

- Simulated lifecycle transitions from production rules: launch at turn 12, growth at turn 35, mature at turn 82, decline at turn 201, retired at turn 260.
- Zero-allocation identity check: 5,000 steel and 5,000 building materials, exactly matching the legacy recipe.
- The allocated 50% plant portion receives 50,000 of that value and retains 50% input throughput, so redirected units do not multiply recipe value or input charges.
- Lifecycle cash timing: the funded path spends 5,000 in capitalized development cash over 12 receipts before launch; its first launch day attributes 6,000 of gross receipts to the redirected vehicle offer at 80% fill. This sales attribution displaces baseline recipe sales rather than adding to them.
- Applying a 60% physical production factor scales both commodity quantity and nominal value: 10,000 becomes 6,000 with unchanged unit values.

## NPP candidate scoring

The rule scores capacity-weighted margin multiplied by scarcity premium and the kind's actual legacy strategy mix weight. A high scarcity ratio cannot win solely for a commodity the selected plants barely produce.

- Selected candidate in the deterministic comparison: recipe-exposed-steel.
- Price discovery, sales fill, input costs, wages, taxes, and financing affect realized P&L outside this nominal output conservation report.
