# Bulgaria's 1991 Assembly transition: persisted qualification

Issue: #2488. Runtime source: `3c08a92a4df2f307e1f8be45aa0563e324275a01`, stacked on Track 1 base `9c1e847188c5c077b0b55c92502bee71b955d9a4`. Qualification harness: `src/lib/turn/bgAssemblyTransition.mongo.test.ts` in this PR. Executed 2026-09-30 against a disposable `ahd_sim_issue2488_bg_*` database on the dedicated loopback sandbox Mongo service. The fixture database was dropped after the test. No live game database was accessed.

Command: `AHD_BG_TRANSITION_REAL_MONGO=1 npx vitest run src/lib/turn/bgAssemblyTransition.mongo.test.ts --reporter=dot`. Result: one passed test, zero failures, 50.52 seconds at exact runtime source. The ordinary CI run skips this opt-in test because CI has no sandbox Mongo service.

The replay starts with five authored 1991 Bulgarian macroregions summing to 400 `houseDistricts`, five persisted incumbent `assemblyDeputy` blocs totaling 400 mandates, and a 400-seat government formation with a 201-seat majority. It stores five completed ordinary Assembly races with 240 apportioned seats and real NPP candidate/tally documents. The production nationwide threshold reader admitted both fixture parties. Five calls to the production `resolveOneGeneralElection` seated 240 new mandates, removed the old 400-mandate blocs, cleared all five old NPP `currentOffice` values, and marked every race resolved. Every new winner NPP's `assemblyDeputy` `currentOffice` matches its persisted mandate. The live lower-chamber reader still reported the founding 400-seat chamber until the transition marker was written.

The production `processBgAssemblyTransition` refused the October turn, then on the November turn persisted the five 240-seat regional allocations, a 240-seat formation with majority 121, and the transition marker. After restoring the founding region/formation state for an interruption probe, calls under the 1953, 1979, and 2027 presets with **no marker** left all 400 founding region seats, formation values, and marker unchanged. A failpoint then wrote only the first two region updates and threw. The marker stayed absent and the formation stayed at 400. A normal 1991 retry converged to all five expected regional allocations, formation majority 121, marker turn 41, and live lower-chamber size 240. A following turn did not repeat the transition. The elected officials still represented exactly 240 mandates after retry.

The strengthened replay exposed an actual identity defect in the prior Track 1 source: the election resolver used the raw `nationalAssembly` chamber key for new NPP `currentOffice` and used a generic `assemblyDelegate` key when clearing old holders. Bulgaria's configured elected office key is `assemblyDeputy`. This PR resolves and sweeps with the country-specific office key in normal and empty-election paths. The exact-source replay above passed after that repair.

This is a focused persisted transition proof. It uses modeled NPP votes and incumbents to test the implemented lifecycle, not a historical claim about individual 1991 Bulgarian deputies or vote totals. It does not replace the fresh whole-world 1991 bootstrap, full horizon, or final release-source evidence required elsewhere in #2488 and #2159.
