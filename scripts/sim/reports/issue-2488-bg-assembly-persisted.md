The elected officials still represented exactly 240 mandates after retry. Calls under the 1979 and 2027 presets performed no transition and left the marker unchanged.

This is a focused persisted transition proof. It uses modeled NPP votes and incumbents to test the implemented lifecycle, not a historical claim about individual 1991 Bulgarian deputies or vote totals. It does not replace the fresh whole-world 1991 bootstrap, full horizon, or final release-source evidence required elsewhere in #2488 and #2159.
