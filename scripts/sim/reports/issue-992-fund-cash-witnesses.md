## Historical defect explanation

All 46 original fund accounts are evaluated against full turn-12/13 snapshots and actual turn-13 ledger rows. Of those, 35 originally diverge. Saved retained dividends explain **24,832.664628596303 anchor** of missing flow. Reconstructing 1,015 actual fund coupon/maturity rows with pre-forex checkpoint rates explains the remaining **0.20 anchor net**: the cash writer rounded each fund's aggregate payment while witnesses rounded each bond independently. The largest unexplained residual after both explanations is **1.8011e-8 anchor**, floating arithmetic noise. No tolerance was increased.

The repair emits the existing named `fund_dividend_retained` and `fund_distribution` reasons. Fund/NPP balances are already anchor-valued; account currency suffixes identify their wallets and do not authorize a second FX conversion. Bond witnesses allocate the difference between successive rounded cumulative totals. Their sum equals the existing rounded fund cash write. Each row retains its unrounded amount and `cumulative_fund_credit` provenance. No artificial balancing entry or additional cash is introduced.

## Regression coverage and limits

No full world simulation was started. This evidence does not prove the separate #992 combined 12-turn material-decline requirement or the global tracker zero-divergence goal. Existing best-effort financial-log delivery and phase retry semantics are not changed or newly qualified. Existing tolerance thresholds and all normal economic formulas are retained.
