# The next world: 1991

**Draft announcement. The reset date and reopening time have not been announced.**

Our next world is planned to begin in **1991**. You will build a new political and economic career as the Cold War order changes. The starting conditions reflect the period; the future follows what governments, parties and players do.

## Where you can play

The planned opening roster supports player characters in the **United States and United Kingdom**. Other countries take part in the economy and international events; appearing on a map or in a crisis does not make a country available for character creation.

You can join a party, organize campaigns and stand for the offices available to your character. Winning an election, forming a government and holding a cabinet role bring different responsibilities. You can also build an economic career through the businesses and financial services available in the new world. Eligibility, resources and the rules of your country still apply.

## A fresh start

The new world begins with new officeholders, elections, budgets and economic positions. Offices, companies, investments and balances from the outgoing world do not become your new character's starting position. Wait for the reopening announcement before creating your new character.

## What changes

### Elections and governments

Election results preserve the electoral map used when the race was decided. US presidential races can go to the House and Senate when no ticket secures an electoral majority. A change of president updates the executive team and vacates incompatible offices; re-electing the same incumbent preserves their cabinet.

Campaign choices use your available resources. A government taking office must still work through the legislature, cabinet or public vote where the decision requires it.

### Banking and the economy

Credit, savings, funds, securities and government finance are connected to the wider economy. Borrowing creates obligations; intervention and rescue policies consume resources. A bank or government response can change who bears a loss, rather than making that loss disappear.

Financial products and opening conditions are still being checked before launch. The final announcement will identify any product that is unavailable at reopening.

### Living international crises

Crises now have decisions and consequences that continue after the initial news event. Diplomacy, sanctions, military support, public health and humanitarian choices depend on authority and national capacity. Damage, displacement and reconstruction can persist after an agreement.

Recognizable historical pressures do not guarantee a historical war, winner or settlement. Decisions by different governments can lead to de-escalation, prolonged conflict or a later relapse.

## The opening situation

The Northern Ireland peace process and the breakup of Yugoslavia form part of the early 1991 world.

Northern Ireland requires negotiation across governments and communities. A settlement can require parliamentary approval and a public vote, followed by the practical work of making devolved institutions function. An agreement is not an automatic end to violence or political disagreement.

The Balkan crisis follows the states and authorities that actually exist in the world. Recognition, ceasefires, intervention, displacement and reconstruction respond to those conditions and the choices made during play.

As the calendar advances, later pressure windows introduce terrorism, financial crises, the Arab uprisings, Russia-Ukraine security tensions and pandemic risk. Their outcomes remain open.

## What is still being checked

- Long games are being checked for persistent shortages, accounting errors, election failures and crisis recovery. The reset will not be scheduled solely because the new features are implemented.
- International participation is broader than the playable character roster. Some foreign governments are simulated actors.
- The final opening roster, available financial products and first election schedule will be confirmed in the reopening announcement. No launch time or feature exception is approved by this draft.

Until a reset date is announced, continue using the current world normally. The final notice will include the shutdown and reopening times and the steps for starting your next character.
