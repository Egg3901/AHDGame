Player entry into a repeat ballot now checks its immutable opening journal and certified predecessor against the original constitutional mandate. Eligible players can file in reopened home constituencies, while existing constituency winners cannot contest another constituency. List campaigning still uses the one-seat allocation rules.

Unregistered replacement ballots and mismatched predecessors fail before creating a candidacy. Original first-generation filing keeps its current behavior. Repeat NPC admission, automatic opening and chamber handover remain in progress.
