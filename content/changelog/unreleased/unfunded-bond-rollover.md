## What changed

- A matured government bond that the Treasury cannot afford now rolls the bond market's holding into a new bond at the same face value, with a coupon set from current rates and the country's credit rating.
- Debt is unchanged by the rollover, and individual and fund holders are still paid in cash when the Treasury can cover them.
- Bonds that were stuck past their due date resume automatically on the next turn.
