## What changed

- In 1979 and 1991 worlds, Czechia and Slovakia are drawn as Czechoslovakia, and the six Yugoslav republics as Yugoslavia, in the tier those countries hold.
- After the break-ups the map follows history: Yugoslavia covers only Serbia and Montenegro until 2006, and Czechoslovakia is gone from 1993.
- The 1991 landing names Bulgaria and Yugoslavia alongside the other countries the 1991 world runs.
