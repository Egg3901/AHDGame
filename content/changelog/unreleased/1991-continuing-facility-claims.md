title: Retained private headquarters keep successor compensation rights
tags: [1991, succession, corporations]
---

Private firms that retain their headquarters now receive payable compensation claims for facilities assigned to a successor. They keep operating without a relocation hold. Retrying activation preserves claims already paid. Reseeding also clears the previous provisional Congress capacity with its transition markers.
