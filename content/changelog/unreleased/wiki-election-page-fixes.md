## Fixed

- Election wiki pages show each candidate's party name instead of a number.
- The page, summary and info box show the game year of the election instead of an internal cycle number.
- Commons, Bundestag, Shugiin and other parliamentary races, including snap elections, are described as a region's share of the national chamber instead of a state senate race.
- The region link at the bottom of an election page now opens the region's page instead of a missing page.
