## What changed

- Removed acting-only restrictions on policy, personnel, doctrine, nuclear programmes, procurement, debt operations and departmental assets.
- Preserved the 24-turn appointment limit and one acting appointment per office per presidential term.
- Updated cabinet appointment and office copy and the cabinet wiki to explain the full authority and remaining limits.
- Tightened country ownership validation when dismissing generals.
