## What changed

- House members vote: while the House vote is open, any sitting member of the House can back one of the three candidates from the election page or the results page, and change their vote until the window closes.
- Live standings: the House vote panel shows how many state delegations currently back each candidate. Members who have not voted back the candidate their party and ideology favour, so the board starts at the simulated ballot and moves as players vote.
- Closing: the vote always runs its full window. If a candidate then holds a majority of state delegations, that candidate becomes president and the acting president becomes vice president. If nobody does, the acting president keeps serving.
