## What changed

- On iPhones and other notched phones, the top bar now fills the area behind the status bar. Page text no longer scrolls through a gap above the bar.
- Menus, drawers and pop-up windows keep clear of the notch and the home indicator.
- Profile stats (Charisma, Debate, Energy and the rest) have their own icon and colored bar again.
