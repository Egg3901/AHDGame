## What changed

- The header keeps the state's photo and puts its name, motto and flag on it. Below it, population, GDP, districts, lean, approval and the governor sit in one row, and each figure links to where it is explained. The governor shows their portrait and party, and links to their profile.
- Lean shows once, in its color, instead of twice.
- On Overview, Organization and Registration each show a ring chart beside its parties. Pointing at a party in the list highlights its slice and puts its share in the middle, and each party links to its page in that state. Build Org sits right under the Organization chart, with the cost of the next click.
- Economy, close races and contested primaries sit in a side column, with the margin bonus on top sectors and the toss-up, lean or likely rating on each race.
- On Politics, senators, representatives, the governor and state senators are listed as rows with portrait, office, seats and party. Long chambers show the first eight with a button to show the rest.
- The US Senate block shows the state's two seats instead of the chamber's 100.
- The governor's party shows its abbreviation instead of a number.
- Non-player politicians show the office they hold instead of always reading "Private Citizen".
- Quick actions no longer list actions that are not in the game yet.
- Small labels on the state page are sentence case at 12px or larger.
