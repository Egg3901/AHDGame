## What changed

- Part of a business's running costs is a fixed amount per unit, set when it first ran under the current cost model. For businesses set up before mid-August that amount already included the bonuses they had at the time, and the separate "Policy, tax and support" line added the same bonuses again. They now count once.
- The extra was largest for plants with big input and wage bills. A few businesses showed profit equal to revenue, a 100% margin and a cost of zero per unit. They now show their real costs.
- Businesses that started under penalties, mostly state firms in the Eastern bloc, were paying those penalties twice. Their costs fall.
- About a third of businesses are affected. Businesses set up after mid-August are unchanged.
