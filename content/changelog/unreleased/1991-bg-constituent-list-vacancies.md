## What changed

- A departed list deputy is replaced by the next eligible nominee on the original party and district list. Constituency seats follow their separate election rules.
- A replacement keeps the seat's original term. Exhausted lists leave seats vacant.
- Players receive a notice and one mandate. Pending relocation and incompatible offices prevent automatic seating.
- Office records, financial-owner mirrors and replacement history update together. Existing balances and prime minister offices are preserved.
