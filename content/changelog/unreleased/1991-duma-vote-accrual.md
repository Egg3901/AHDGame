Bound Russian Duma ballots use the electorate frozen at cohort opening. National lists aggregate regional demographics, while each constituency counts against its own district register. Votes already cast for withdrawn nominees remain part of participation and certification, and total participation cannot exceed the frozen register.

The first-Duma runtime still needs cohort resolution, repeat elections and chamber handover before opening is connected to the turn loop.
