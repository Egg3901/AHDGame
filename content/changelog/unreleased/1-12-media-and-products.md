## What changed

- Media corporations fund named titles through paid research and advertising to improve quality and audience.
- Manufacturers develop products at 5% of their plant capital, put marketing into product advertising to build brand, and sell product output through market clearing.
- Products keep progressing after development is paid, then grow, mature, decline and retire.
- CEOs can agree to spend part of their marketing budget with a media supplier, and the supplier's reach in the buyer's states builds more brand.
- Heavy US ad concentration unlocks ownership laws, and an enacted ownership limit caps each owner's delivered share in a state.
- Audience limits and state press controls bound how far outlets can reach.
- Political ads in a state with no outlet fund a weaker local fallback, and the rest is refunded.
- Entertainment keeps its own market and full output, separate from the news media penalty.
- Vehicle plants keep their automobile recipes under manufacturing.
