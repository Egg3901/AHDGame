title: Defence tenders check minimum lot prices
summary: Defence tenders now reject maximum prices below every compatible production line's minimum price before reserving funds.
badges: [patch]
areas: [backend]
tags: [defence, government-contracts]
---

Publishing a defence tender now checks its maximum price against the lowest current price floor for its component and grade, and shows the minimum in national budget units if the price is too low. This uses the existing price bands and does not require a domestic supplier to exist. Existing tenders and signed contracts keep their prices.
