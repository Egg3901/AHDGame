title: Russian deputies can use their active legislature
tags: [1991, Russia, legislation]
---

Russian bill proposals, lists and details now follow the active Union Congress, successor Congress or Federal Assembly. Current deputies can file bills under the correct chamber, activated bills remain visible, and vote counts exclude obsolete offices. A dissolved Congress refuses proposals, including administrative proposals. An established president receives the bill action controls.
