## What changed

- CEOs can offer, counter, accept and cancel advertising agreements with media
  suppliers from the Sectors tab. Agreements have a fixed term, a four turn
  cancellation notice and expire on schedule. The tab shows the share, the
  coverage overlap and the effective advertising each agreement earned.
- An allocation splits the buyer's existing marketing budget. It is never an
  extra expense, and sellers are still paid only for advertising they actually
  delivered through ordinary clearing.
- Supplier coverage comes from the media operating models the supplier runs plus
  the states where it has an active sector. Overlap is weighted by the buyer's
  revenue by state. Efficacy is capped at 1.5 times the paid amount.
- Product brand formation for media titles and manufactured products now uses
  the effective advertising from the matching turn. A buyer with no agreement,
  no delivery or no matching settlement is unchanged.
- The feature follows the media operating model gate. With that gate off, no
  advertising agreement data is read or written and no panel is shown.
