EU member corridors now pair modeled origin and destination demand, limited by the existing annual migration cap. The demographic turn moves the same age and sex cells from the source to the host. Border and migration-rate decisions still determine whether a region offers or accepts migration. A later repair connects refugee choices, host service budgets and explicit migration-route history.
