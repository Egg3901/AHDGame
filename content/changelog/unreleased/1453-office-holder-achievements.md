## What changed

- Holding a cabinet seat in any country, acting or confirmed, now unlocks At the President's Table without visiting your profile.
- Chairing a central bank now unlocks Master of the Mint the same way.
- Both achievements are checked each turn, so leaving the role before opening your profile no longer costs you the award.
