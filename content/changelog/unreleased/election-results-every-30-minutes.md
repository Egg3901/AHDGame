## What changed

- Votes in a general election used to arrive once an hour, with the turn. Half of each hour's votes now arrive at half past the hour, and the rest arrive with the turn, so results update every 30 minutes.
- Every race still ends at the same time, on the hour, and finishes with the same number of votes as before. Campaign funds and campaign actions still arrive once an hour with the turn.
- Primaries, Irish ranked ballots, the presidential race and a few national ballot systems with their own counting rules still update once an hour for now.
- Vote counters that showed how many turns had been counted now count each turn once.
