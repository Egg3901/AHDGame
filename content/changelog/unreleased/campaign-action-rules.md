- Campaign quotes and effects use the same portable cost, eligibility and influence-gain rules. Existing balance, AP tiers, GDP scaling and stat multipliers are preserved.
- Campaign validation rejects unallocated stats and missing home-state economics with the quoted reason instead of substituting neutral values.
