## What changed

- Settings, Profile: add or change your email. We send a confirmation link to the new address, and the change takes effect when you open it. Accounts with a password must enter the current password first.
- When the email changes, the previous address gets a notice.
- Fixed Settings showing "Not set" for every email, and fixed wrong password and linked-account details on the same page.
