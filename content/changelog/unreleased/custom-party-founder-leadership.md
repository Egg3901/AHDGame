## What changed

- The three founders of a chartered party can now run for and vote in that
  party's leadership elections straight away, instead of waiting 24 turns. This
  covers national, state and caucus leadership.
- The exemption applies only to the party you founded. Joining somebody else's
  party still means serving the full membership requirement there before you can
  stand or vote.
- Leaving, being purged from, or merging away the party you founded clears the
  exemption, so it cannot be banked and used later.

## Why

Charter ratification stamps every founder as having joined the new party on that
turn, which is what the leadership membership requirement measures. The result
was that a brand new party's own founders were the only members it had, and none
of them could contest the seats the party opened on day one.
