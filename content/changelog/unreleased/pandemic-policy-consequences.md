- Every government can fund research, manufacturing, containment or shared medical supply.
- Vaccines require clinical development, production and distribution; unanswered response windows provide no free investment.
- Disease harms population and health capacity even during a cooperative response. Restrictions trade reduced transmission for fiscal, economic and political costs.
- Waning immunity and new variants can reopen an emergency. Settled outbreaks retain routine management choices.
