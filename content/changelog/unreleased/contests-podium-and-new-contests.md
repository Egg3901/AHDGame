## What changed

- The top three in each weekly contest split the cash prize 50 / 30 / 20. Government approval stays winner takes all.
- Every entry shows its corporation or party logo and the player's portrait, linked to their pages. The top three are highlighted.
- When a week ends, the National Wire Service posts every contest's results, and every player who finished ranked gets a notification with their places.
- New weekly contests:
  - **Legislator of the Week**: most bills you sponsored that became law, national or state.
  - **Personal Wealth Growth**: percent growth in your net worth. Wires you receive and loan principal do not count.
  - **Party Growth**: most members gained by a party under the same player chair for the whole week.
