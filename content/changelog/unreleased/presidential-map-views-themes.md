## What changed

- A "Colour by" switch under the presidential map shows the race four ways: margin (the default), the winner of each state, one ticket's vote share, or which way each state has moved over the last few turns. Counties follow the margin, winner and vote share views when you zoom in.
- Drag the edge of either side panel to resize it. Your widths are remembered on this device; double-click an edge to reset it.
- The left panel is narrower by default, giving the map more room.
- The presidential page and the campaign screens now follow your chosen theme, including the light theme. Map shading fades toward your theme's background.
- On a phone the page uses the same layout, stacked: the headline and map first, then the race, then the national context. Tap the lock to pan and zoom the map.
- When you are signed out during primary season, the map shades each state by when it votes instead of showing a blank map.
