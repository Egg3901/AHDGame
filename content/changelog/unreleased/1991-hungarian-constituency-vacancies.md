Hungarian constituency vacancies in the 1991 world open replacement elections within the sitting Assembly's term. Replacement deputies keep its original expiry; territorial and national compensation results stay certified. A regular campaign already running for the next term no longer blocks these elections.

Players can enter only a vacant constituency in their home region and can hold one mandate. Winners receive their result and career record once. Failed ballots preserve the vacancy and can open a fresh campaign. Existing funds and earlier votes remain intact.
