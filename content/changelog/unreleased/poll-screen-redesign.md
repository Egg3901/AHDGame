## What changed

- The page header shows your state and the positions, favorability, influence and party organization every poll number is computed from.
- Commissioning is one choice between the quick and full poll, with the cost and what you have on hand in green or red.
- Results lead with overall appeal, its strength band and a meter, then estimated and reachable voters.
- The election comparison colors each candidate by party, shows the combined pool as one bar, and says plainly whether you lead or trail and by how many votes.
- Granular electorate bars split into your party, each rival's party and undecided voters, and the explorer reads as a plain section instead of a box inside a box.
- Recent polls add an appeal trend chart with labeled axes.
