---
date: 2026-10-08
title: Freight bills no longer outrun the goods they ship
summary: >-
  A sector's shipping bill for a commodity is now capped at 15 percent of the
  value of the goods it takes in. Sectors in states with scarce freight were
  being billed several times their own revenue for hauling cheap bulk cargo.
tags: [economy, logistics, corporations]
badges: [patch]
areas: [backend]
---

Shipping costs are billed to corporate buyers in proportion to the goods they
take in. When freight was scarce and the cargo cheap, that bill could exceed
what the cargo was worth. Each sector's bill per commodity now stops at 15
percent of the value of the units it demands there.
