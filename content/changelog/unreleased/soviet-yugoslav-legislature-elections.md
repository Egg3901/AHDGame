## What changed

- The 1991 Soviet Congress of People's Deputies (2,250 seats) now runs regional elections. The founding round seats it, and regular elections follow on a five-year cycle starting in 1994.
- The 1991 Yugoslav Federal Chamber (220 seats) now runs elections too, with the first regular vote in 1992.
- With deputies seated, both countries can form a government, choose a head of state and sponsor bills.
- The Union Congress stops holding elections once the Soviet succession replaces it with a Russian chamber.
