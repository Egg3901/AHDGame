## What changed

- The shipping bill for goods hauled between states is now spread evenly over the day's 24 turns, so each turn pays one twenty-fourth of it. Before, every turn charged the whole day's bill.
- Haulers' freight income is corrected the same way, so the money still moves from buyers to haulers in full, just at the right size.
- The freight figures on sector pages are now a true daily amount.
