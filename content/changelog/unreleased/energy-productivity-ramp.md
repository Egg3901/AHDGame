## What changed

- In the 1991 world, each energy plant's output per unit of capacity rises gradually, about 30% over one game year, then holds.
- The gain arrives in small hourly steps with no sudden jump.
- Plants buy more inputs as they make more, so input cost per unit of electricity is unchanged.
