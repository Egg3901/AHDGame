## What changed

- Half of each plant's overhead (site services and plant management) is now billed on the capacity you built, not on what you produced this turn. A plant running flat out pays exactly what it did before. A plant running at 40% of capacity now pays about 30% less overhead instead of 60% less, so under-used or poorly sold capacity costs more than it did.
