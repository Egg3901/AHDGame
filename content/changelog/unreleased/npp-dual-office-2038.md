# Presidential seating vacates incompatible legislative offices

Seating a president or vice president now vacates any incompatible office the
holder already keeps (Senate, House, governor) through the real vacancy path:
unheld vacancy record, governor notification for Senate seats, chamber
leadership re-trigger, and party presence recount. Previously an NPP seated as
vice president kept a Senate seat won in the same founding-election wave. The
reverse order is covered too: a sitting executive can no longer take a
legislative seat in a presidential system, the seat falls to the next eligible
candidate. Parliamentary heads of government still sit in the legislature.
