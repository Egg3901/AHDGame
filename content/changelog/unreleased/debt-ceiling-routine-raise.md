## What changed

- At fiscal year close, any country whose national debt is within 5% of its debt ceiling, or already past it, raises the ceiling to 110% of the current debt.
- The US debt ceiling crisis flag is cleared when this happens. Before, nothing ever cleared it.
- Debt still costs what it costs: interest rates and credit ratings follow debt against GDP, not the ceiling.
