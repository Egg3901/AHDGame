- Scotland and Wales preserve live population, output, metrics, regional assets and public finances when becoming independent.
- The First Minister stays as caretaker national leader. Other old mandates end, and elections open immediately in every new region.
- Northern Ireland keeps its region and carries its Commons representatives into population-adjusted Dail mandates. Public money converts at the same exchange rate as residents' balances.
- New countries automatically join the UN and negotiate their own other international agreements.
- Pending construction is cancelled and refunded at its full original cost. Capacity already delivered by the cancelled order is removed, and secured refunds repay the lender before returning the remainder to the owner.
- Unpublished underwriting cash receipts are refunded once. Placements that already issued instruments finish their existing settlement.
- Completed pledged plants retain their existing loan and corporate currency when joining Ireland.
- Party funds, leadership permissions, law programs and government seat totals follow the new borders. Source-owned international facilities and payment receipts stay with their original country.
