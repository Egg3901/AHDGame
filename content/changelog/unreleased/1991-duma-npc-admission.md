The Duma slate planner uses existing NPC profiles for individual constituency nominees and bounded national list slates. Player candidates remain limited to one active candidacy and one seat. Index migration verifies both replacement guards before removing the legacy guard and retains that guard if replacement creation fails.

NPC admission and office handover are still being connected. This change alone does not activate the Federal Assembly.
