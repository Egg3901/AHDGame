- Rookie of the week: most National Influence gained by a character created in the two weeks before the round opened.
- Backbencher of the week: most National Influence gained by a character who opened the round in the lower half of the field.
- Public image: most favorability gained during the round.
- Statehouse approval: most approval points gained in a state led by a player governor, who must hold office for the whole round.
- Kingmaker: most races won by candidates you endorsed. The endorsement must stand when voting closes, and endorsing yourself does not count.
- Comeback of the week: market cap growth for player corporations that lost at least 10% of their value in the week before the round. Capital you inject does not count.
- Each new contest pays ₳5,000,000 (scaled to the era) split 50 / 30 / 20.
- The Contests page now groups contests under Business, Politics and Government. Each contest is one row showing its leader and prize; open it for the full standings. Signed-in players see a summary of where they stand in every contest they are in.
