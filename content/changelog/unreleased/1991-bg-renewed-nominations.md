## What changed

- Eligible players choose among reopened constituencies in their home campaign region.
- Existing NPC owners supply bounded direct-only party slates without new financial profiles.
- Repeated polls retain admitted nominees, first ballots and party-list allocations. Ordinary top-two runoffs admit no new entrants.
