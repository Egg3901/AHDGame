## What changed

- The corporation name is larger, with the ticker, sector, legal form, headquarters and CEO on one line under it.
- The share price is the one large figure, with its change, a short price trend and the bid and ask under it.
- Market cap, shares, float, dividend, retained income, cash, credit, marketing, brand and quality sit in a grid, each value under its label.
- Main tabs are larger with an underline. Sub-tabs are smaller with a filled marker on the open one, so the two rows no longer look alike. This also applies to the region page tabs.
- The "new" marker on a tab is small text instead of a filled badge.
