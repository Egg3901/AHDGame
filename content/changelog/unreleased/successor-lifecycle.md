## What changed

- Scottish and Welsh chamber elections allocate the full regional delegation, including snap elections.
- Successor governments process national bills, government formation and subsequent elections normally.
- Independent countries participate in commodity pricing, trade clearing, national law drift and party history.
- Northern Irish peace ballots preserve outstanding constitutional referendum cooldowns.
- Conversion cancels unfinished UK election resolution as well as open races.
- Scottish and Welsh maps use a shared grouping of all 32 and 22 whole council areas, respectively. These are modern reference boundaries across game eras.
- Regional parliamentary seats follow the game's regional population estimates while preserving the 129-seat Scottish and 60-seat Welsh chambers.
