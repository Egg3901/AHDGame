## What changed

- Automobiles is no longer its own corporation type. Car and truck makers are manufacturing corporations running the vehicles line, with the same recipes, prices, plant sizes and tech tree as before.
- Entertainment is no longer its own corporation type. Studios, venues and labels are media corporations running the entertainment line, with the same economics as before.
- Founding, expanding, type switching, unions, strategic sectors, tariffs, subsidies and nationalization all offer the vehicles and entertainment lines by name.
- Existing corporations, sectors, markets and unions move to the new form automatically. Balances, plants, workers and shareholdings do not change.
