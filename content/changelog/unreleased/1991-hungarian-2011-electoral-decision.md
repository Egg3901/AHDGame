From December 2011, Hungarian deputies can propose the later electoral system through a normal parliamentary bill. A quorate two-thirds vote must approve the 199-seat Assembly. Rejection keeps the earlier system; 2014 alone no longer forces the change.

Approval applies from January 2012 to new or untouched primary campaigns. General ballots already cast retain their rules. The complete modern count installs 106 constituency and 93 national-list deputies together, preserving each player's single seat and existing accounts. If a nominee withdraws before handover, their direct seat stays vacant and their national-list mandate uses another available nominee from the original slate.

Eligible NPC governments can introduce the decision with a recorded supermajority mandate and normal parliamentary votes. Completed modern settlements in existing saves remain valid.
