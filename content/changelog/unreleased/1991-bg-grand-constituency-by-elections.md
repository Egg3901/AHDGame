## What changed

- Vacant Grand Assembly constituency seats open individual elections with a month's notice. New elections cannot be scheduled in the term's last six months.
- First-round victory requires a majority of valid votes and turnout above half the register. Failed ballots proceed to a runoff; a sole unsuccessful candidate permits new nominations.
- Players can file one vacant constituency in their home region. Winners fill one physical mandate and keep its original expiry.
- Ordinary Assembly handover, dissolution and term expiry cancel pending replacement ballots. List seats retain their separate succession rules.
- Offices, seat counts, campaign funds and notices commit together. Replacement journals are cleared when a world resets.
