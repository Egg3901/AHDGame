## What changed

- Peace agreements use public campaigns and the normal referendum result process, with separate authorization from Westminster and the Dáil.
- Rejected agreements return to talks and require new legislation. National leaders cannot supply community or public consent on their own.
- Ratified power sharing enables regional institutions and election cycles. Suspension withdraws authority; restoration opens a new electoral cycle.
- Political violence adds an explicit regional security cost to the UK budget. Peace and conflict affect Northern Ireland's safety, local autonomy and participation.
- Unanswered crisis decisions apply their authored fallback consequences without pretending a player made the decision.
