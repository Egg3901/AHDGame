title: Russia's 1991 spending no longer counts the regional grants twice
summary: >-
  New 1991 worlds fill Russia's non-law spending up to its fiscal envelope. The
  grants Russia pays its republics were left out of that sum and then added
  again once the republics existed, so Russia spent about 4.6% of GDP more than
  its envelope. The grants now count inside the envelope from the first turn.
