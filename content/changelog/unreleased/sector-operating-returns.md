## What changed

- Raised the primary industry bonus from 5 to 10 percentage points and the secondary bonus from 2.5 to 5. Existing margin caps still apply.
- Preserved the previous payroll basis so the stronger operating bonus does not cut workers' pay.
- Withheld cash-return estimates while either revenue transition is active. Revenue-linked policy credits and charges now follow forecast sales, including when demand limits an expansion.
- Updated founding guidance, sector tooltips and the corporation guide. Bond trading, coupons and maturity rules are unchanged.
