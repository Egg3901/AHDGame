## What changed

- Spin-off subsidiaries get the free baseline tech from past decades, the same grant a newly founded corporation receives. Existing spin-offs have been given it too.
- A parent corporation with more than three subsidiaries takes a discount on its share price. It grows with each subsidiary past three and stops at 50% at nine.
