## What changed

- In a new 1991 world, Brazil's spending programs were priced from its 1953 budget and came to only about 3% of GDP, so the government opened with a large surplus.
- Brazil's programs now follow the 1991 budget's split between social security, health, education, defence, infrastructure, administration and grants to the states. The total is set by what Brazil collects after paying interest on its debt, so the opening deficit is small, in line with the other playable countries.
- Health spending is now booked under the healthcare budget line the health law uses.
- The split holds through every budget refresh. Existing worlds are not changed by this update.
