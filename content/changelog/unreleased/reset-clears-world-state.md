## What changed

- Commodity prices restart from the new world's base level, not the level the previous world reached.
- Voting age and registration access follow the new era until the new world passes its own electoral laws.
- The European Community, euro membership and cabinet seats opened by legislation start from the new era's history.
- Statehood admissions and new military branches are scheduled against the new world's calendar.
- Retail capacity is open from the first turn. The Retail demand unwind begins only when it is started for the new world.
- Freight tier change history is cleared when a reset returns the world to the default freight tier.
