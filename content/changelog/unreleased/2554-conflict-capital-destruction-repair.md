## What changed

- A resolved military escalation destroys half a percent of the capital stock in Croatia and Bosnia and Herzegovina, once per outcome, bounded by an outstanding-damage ceiling.
- The region's current sovereign carries the repair cost as a national budget line for 96 turns, and the rebuilt capital returns to the stock on the same schedule.
- Regional output scales with the capital stock, so destruction and repair move GDP symmetrically.
- The campaign infrastructure score stays a pressure signal: points already destroyed as real capital are no longer also charged through the growth penalty.
