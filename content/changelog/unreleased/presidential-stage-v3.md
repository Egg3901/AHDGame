## What changed

- A "road to 270" strip above the general election map lines every state up by how safely it leans from one ticket to the other, sized by its electoral votes, with the 270 line and the tipping-point state marked. Click a state in the strip to open it on the map.
- States open inside the presidential page now. The old per-state page takes you to the map with that state open, and the address updates as you open states, so a state view can be shared.
- If you are running in the race, "Your campaign" under the map shades every state by your Campaign Presence. A state's overview shows your presence there, lets you build it up a level, and links straight to opening a field office in that state.
- County maps load much faster and read more cleanly: close counties are pale tints of the leader's colour instead of dark, muddy shades.
- Primary candidates get softer, more distinct colours, and two campaigns that picked nearly the same colour no longer look identical.
- The controls under the map are no longer hidden behind the status bar at the bottom of the screen.
