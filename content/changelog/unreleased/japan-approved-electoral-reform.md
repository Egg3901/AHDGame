  Japan starts the 1991 world with its 512-seat rules and can approve a future

From 1994, an approved electoral-law bill can move future Japanese lower-house
elections to 300 constituency seats and 200 regional list seats. Ongoing races
keep their frozen rules. Sitting chamber capacity changes as regional elections
resolve under the new system.
