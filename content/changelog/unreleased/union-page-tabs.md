title: Union pages laid out like corporation pages
summary: The union page is now a compact header with tabs, and the sectors table can be sorted, filtered and organized several shops at a time.
tags: [unions, ui]
badges: [minor]
areas: [fullstack]
---

- The union page opens on an Overview with the organize drive, the head's wage claim and a count of shops by strike status. Dues, services and politics, Sectors, Bargaining, Leadership and Legislative stances each have their own tab, so nothing needs a long scroll.
- Click any sector column heading to sort by it. Every heading has a tooltip that explains the column.
- Filter the sectors table by corporation or state, by strike status, and by whether this union, a rival union or no union holds the shop. Each status count on the Overview opens the table already filtered.
- Union heads can tick up to 10 shops and run every drive at once. The bar shows the total action points and treasury before you commit, and if a drive is refused partway the drives that already ran are kept and the message names the shop that stopped the batch.
- A raid that loses now says so, and that its cost is spent, instead of reporting "Done.". Results now show under the sectors table, not in the Organize panel.
