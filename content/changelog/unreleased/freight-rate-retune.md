title: Shipping costs are a small share of the goods again
summary: >-
  Hauling goods between states used to eat a fifth to half of what the cargo
  was worth, which pushed delivered prices up and starved short states. A
  typical bulk haul now costs roughly 5 to 12 percent of its cargo at normal
  freight prices, and no haul can cost more than a fraction of the goods it
  carries, even when freight is scarce.
