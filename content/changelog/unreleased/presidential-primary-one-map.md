## What changed

- The Campaign Presence map under the primary is gone. Pick "Your campaign"
  in the map's colour options to see your presence, and open a state to build
  it.
- Funds, actions, levels, the campaign manager and a link to each campaign are
  now columns in the field table. The separate Campaign operations list and
  Your campaign card are gone.
- Your own campaign's funds show its net income per turn.
- A finished presidential race waits for its results before drawing, so the
  old page no longer flashes first.
