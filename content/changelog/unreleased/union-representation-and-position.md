Organizing and raiding shops now refresh the union dashboard in place, keeping your filters, sorting and expanded shop list. If a refresh fails, the previous dashboard stays available with a warning.

The shop table names each representing union and attributes members to it. Unrepresented shops count as having no union members, and the table explains how exclusive representation and raids work.
