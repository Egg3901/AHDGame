## What changed

- A party's color now appears once, as a small square beside its name, instead of as blocks across cards and headers. Treasury figures read in plain numbers and a party's economic and social lean are plain words.
- The party headquarters header shows the party, its figures and its sections in one plain band, and the section tabs fade at the edge when there are more to scroll to. On phones, the country links on the parties list wrap instead of running off the screen.
- Action cards keep their photos but lose the label pill on top. Cost and effect are two plain lines, and only money you gain is shown in green. The Actions page header shows the year after the title instead of in a pill.
- The home page lists what you can do as a simple two-column list, shows each nation as Playable or Economy only, and drops the boxes around the guides and community sections.
- Small labels across these pages are now sentence case at a readable 12px.
