title: Clearer sign-up and character creation when the game is closed
summary: Sign-up and character creation now say up front when they are closed, keep your answers, and recover from a dropped connection.
tags: [onboarding]
badges: [minor]
areas: [frontend, backend]
---

When sign-ups or character creation are paused for maintenance, or registration is closed, the sign-up page and the character creator now say so straight away, with the expected reopen time, instead of failing after you fill in the form. The login page also says when new accounts are paused.

The character creator saves your answers on your device as you go. A reload, a dropped connection or a refused submit no longer loses them, and if the game does not answer you get a Try again button instead of being sent back to the login page.

New accounts that arrive while the game is down see a page that confirms the account is saved and explains when character creation opens. The Discord link on the home page, which pointed at an expired invite, works again.
