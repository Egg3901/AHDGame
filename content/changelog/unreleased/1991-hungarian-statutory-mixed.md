Hungarian ordinary Assembly elections in the 1991 world count 176 constituencies, 20 territorial lists and national compensation separately. The original territorial capacity is 152 seats and compensation capacity is 58; unused territorial seats transfer to compensation. National eligibility uses the original strict 4% threshold.

Constituency and territorial ballots use their own turnout and second-round rules. Qualified candidates campaign again with their existing funds. Insufficient turnout in a territorial second round opens fresh repeat ballots and preserves the previous votes. No turnout is invented to finish an election.

Players can choose a constituency in their home region and hold one mandate. Withdrawal and re-entry preserve the same candidate, constituency, campaign limits and cast votes. Bounded NPC slates represent individual deputies without duplicating financial accounts.

The complete count and chamber handover happen together. Incumbents remain until certification; failed transactions roll back and retries reuse the receipt. Regional screens await the national count instead of displaying an approximate seat award.
