## What changed

- Bank management menus, charter forms and capital warnings appear only for viewers who can take the action. Customers can still use available account services.
- Sector pricing, purchases, attacks, splits and workforce menus omit unavailable choices.
- Product creation, advertising proposals, share controls and bond default remedies show only available actions.
- Published editorial positions and relevant nationalization status use plain sentences for viewers without management actions.
- Deals appears only when acquisitions are available. Sponsored funds and index eligibility remain accessible from the CEO Office independently of acquisitions.
