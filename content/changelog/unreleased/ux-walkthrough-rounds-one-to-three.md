## What changed

- Listing prices on the stock market, status bar, corporation header and fund
  holdings use one quote and the player's display currency.
- Remaining date surfaces show game-calendar months.
- NPP is spelled out on hover, and Close race badges follow the 15-point rule.
- Crisis counts agree between the board and its historical toggle, the World
  menu map is labeled as a country map, and trade lists use canonical country
  names.
- The White House page links to presidential powers and shows time in office.
