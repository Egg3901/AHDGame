## What changed

- The natural gas deposits in New York and Cork and the timber deposit in London are larger, so the mines working them have room to run at full output with some to spare.
- Mines owned through companies that keep their books in yen, lira, pesetas and other non-dollar currencies were treated as far larger than their deposits, which held their output down. They now produce what their deposits actually allow. Output rises gradually over about 20 turns rather than all at once.
- Regions with spare deposits in those countries now show their real free capacity, so new mines can be opened there.
