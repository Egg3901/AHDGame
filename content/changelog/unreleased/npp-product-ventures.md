## What changed

- Computer-run media and manufacturing companies now fund new products over three days, the same way a player's company does.
- A company only starts one when it can pay the full development bill and still keep its cash reserve. Companies behind on payments or in distress never start one.
- Roughly one in three eligible companies has a product in development at any time. Each company runs at most one per area.
- Manufacturers lean toward goods that are in short supply in their market.
- Decisions that come up during development are settled by the default response if nobody answers, and a hit lifts the company's matching sectors for three days.
- Player companies are unchanged.
