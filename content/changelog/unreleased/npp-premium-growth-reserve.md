## What changed

- A computer-run company that owes a price premium on a supply contract now pays it only out of half of the cash it holds above its safety reserve.
- Any premium it cannot cover is recorded as unpaid, not borrowed or forgiven, and the supplier simply receives less that turn.
- Previously these premiums could swallow most of a buyer's profit every turn, leaving it permanently under its reserve and unable to build.
- Player-run corporations are unchanged, and shortfall damages owed by suppliers are unchanged.
