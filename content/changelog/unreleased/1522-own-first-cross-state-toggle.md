Company supply settings now offer an optional own-first cross-state toggle. It stays off for existing companies. Own local plants keep first claim, and shipping still needs a reachable domestic route and freight capacity. The supply panel shows recorded own hauls and freight per unit in anchor currency so you can judge whether hauling outweighs your internal discount. Reservations use the previous turn's deliveries.
