Bulgaria's July 1991 date opens a constitution decision. The government or President can introduce the draft, and at least 267 of the 400 constituent deputies must vote for adoption. Rejection preserves the Grand National Assembly and allows an explicit revised draft.

An approved draft authorizes later 240-seat ordinary Assembly campaigns. Existing cast votes, closed primaries and incomplete regional cohorts retain their rules. An autonomous government can introduce one draft when it has sufficient constituent support and no player deputies participate. Existing ordinary Assembly settlements are preserved.
