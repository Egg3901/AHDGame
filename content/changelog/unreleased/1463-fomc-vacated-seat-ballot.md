title: Committee votes no longer count a ballot from a vacated seat
summary: >-
  When a committee seat went vacant during an open meeting, its earlier ballot
  kept counting, so a motion could show more votes than there were members.
tags: [central-banks, bugfix]
badges: [minor]
areas: [economy]
---

## What changed

- A ballot cast by a seat that has since gone vacant (term expiry or removal) is dropped from the open meeting and ignored by the tally, so the vote count never exceeds the number of seated members.
- The central bank committee tab shows the corrected tally for an open motion.
