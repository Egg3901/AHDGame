summary: The game can now run a recession that gradually closes part of a country's productive capacity, announced on the news wire when it begins.

- A recession can now shrink one country's productive capacity by a set share, spread evenly over several turns, so no company loses its plants all at once.
- Every company operating in that country is affected alike. Closed capacity comes off the books together with what was paid for it, the same way ordinary wear and tear does.
- The start of a recession is announced on the national news wire.
