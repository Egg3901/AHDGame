## What changed

- Bank owners can create a fund under Bank > Funds. Pick up to 10 public companies in the bank's home country. At least a quarter stays in cash, and each company can hold at most 15% of the fund value.
- Fund money stays separate from the bank's capital, deposits, reserves and investments. Retail banks cannot manage funds.
- Investors reserve money on the fund page and receive units at the next turn's value. Pending investments can be cancelled for a refund. Withdrawals use the existing fund settlement process.
- Unavailable shares and amounts above an investment limit stay in cash. The fund keeps its published company choices and fees.
