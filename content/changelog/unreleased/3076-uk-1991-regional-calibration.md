## What changed

- The twelve UK regions previously sat within about one point of each other on the economic axis. They now span about two and a half points, ordered by the published Labour minus Conservative margin.
- Only the economic axis moves, by a fixed small rate per point of margin, centred so the national position is unchanged. The social axis is not derived from vote shares.
- Northern Ireland, which votes on a separate party system, is left as it was.
