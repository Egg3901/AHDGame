An enacted Russian presidency decision opens a national popular ballot. The registered electorate is fixed when the ballot opens. A valid election requires at least 50 percent turnout, and the first round requires a majority of participating voters. A failed multi-candidate first round opens a fresh ballot for the two finalists; an invalid or unsuccessful election opens a new filing cycle.

Candidates register a president and vice-president ticket. NPC candidates can contest the first round in playable Russia. Both certified finalists remain in a runoff even if they share a party. Only a certified winning ticket replaces the Chairman. Congress and its government remain in place, with any incompatible seats held by the winning ticket becoming vacancies.

Russian races display popular votes and their certified outcome rather than a US electoral college. Opening, resolution and office handover require database transactions and reject an altered constitutional mandate.

Campaign strength changes popular-vote shares within the fixed registered electorate. NPC vice-presidential nominees are visible, and the paired ticket locks when filing closes. The winning ticket vacates its cabinet posts while other ministers remain in office.

Runoff finalists retain their campaign accounts and upgrades while the new ballot starts at zero votes.
