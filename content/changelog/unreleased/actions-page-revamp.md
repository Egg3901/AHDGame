## What changed

- Campaign, Run Advertisements, Fundraise and Build Donor Network show an After line, such as Influence 12.0% to 13.0%, worked out the same way the game works out the real result.
- The result of your last action stays on its card until you take another one, instead of covering the photo for four seconds.
- An action you cannot take keeps its card readable: the photo turns gray, the button is disabled and the reason sits beside it.
- All operations is grouped into Influence, Fundraising and Intelligence, each with a short heading. The Fundraising heading shows your donor level, what each fundraise brings in and what the next level costs.
- In compact view, each group is one list, and the outcome preview sits beside the cost.
- The influence and favorability bars are in color again.
- Action result messages no longer use dashes.
