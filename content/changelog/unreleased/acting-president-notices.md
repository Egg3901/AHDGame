## What changed

- A House deadlock now sends notifications to the acting president, the candidates still on the House ballot and every player in the House, and posts the outcome to the game events feed.
- The White House shows the office holder as Acting President until the House chooses.
- An acting presidency is recorded as an appointment and does not count toward presidential term limits.
