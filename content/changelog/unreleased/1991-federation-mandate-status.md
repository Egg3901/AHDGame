title: Federation consent waits for an enacted mandate
tags: [1991, federation, legislation]
---

Federation ratification waits until the source legislature's mandate is enacted. A veto that is still awaiting an override vote cannot start successor consent or interrupt the turn's ratification sweep. A successful override becomes eligible through the ordinary enacted bill status.
