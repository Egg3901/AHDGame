title: Supply reservations use each plant's actual industry
summary: Vehicle and entertainment plants now reserve contract and own-group inputs against their actual production recipe.

---

Supply agreements and own-group sourcing now keep the industry identifiers used by vehicle and entertainment plants. Input reservations follow the plant's actual recipe instead of its parent manufacturing or media category. Existing agreement limits, delivery rules and hardware input relief continue to apply. This correction changes future allocation and does not replay past cash settlements.
