## What changed

- Label extraction shares as percentages of total state resource capacity, with current nominal daily unit equivalents.
- Explain that concessions grant extraction rights, with a signing fee and capacity-based royalties paid by the corporation.
- Show local currency fee estimates, including bid history, and explain that FX changes can affect the final debit at award.
- Filter the whole country board by any managed corporation or one selected corporation before pagination. Defence matches account for current output, free production lines, required grade, pricing and supplier quota.
- Open a corporation's matching opportunities from its overview link.
- Keep existing settlement rules and stored contract values.
