## What changed

- The Terms of Service state the zero-tolerance policy for objectionable
  content and abusive players, and explain how to report or block a player
  from their profile.
- Player mail is checked by the same language filter as news posts and
  discussions.
- Signing in or registering with Apple, Google or Discord shows that doing so
  accepts the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
- In the phone app, the Patreon and supporter perk settings are hidden.
