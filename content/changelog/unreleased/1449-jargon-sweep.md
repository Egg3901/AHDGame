## What changed

- The Political Strength budget on national and state party treasuries now says what PS is spent on (building Organization in states and party campaigning), how much the party gets free each turn, and what the budget buys.
- The state page explains Organization and Political Strength together, and its buttons read "Build Organization (state party PS)" and "Build Organization (national party PS)". The cost line says how much Organization the next build adds.
- Build Organization panels, the party HQ state table and map, treasury breakdowns and the transaction log use full names instead of "Nat'l PS", "Org bucket", "pressure ladder" and "PS investment".
- Presidential results and primary tables explain EV, Fav, NPI and CS on hover or tap, and the primary table spells out its delegate columns.
- Other short labels spelled out: office action points, national average, government approval, expected default, and sector names in the corporations guide.
- Wiki pages no longer show internal setting names; the glossary gains Political Strength and Organization.
