- Bond choices now identify each series and show the units available to buy or sell, face value and current market unit price.
- Whole-unit orders show their face value and estimated consideration in the native currency. Invalid quantities and unavailable selections cannot be submitted.
- The page explains monetary transmission, the last operation and the standard cooldown. Quotes and availability remain subject to server checks.
