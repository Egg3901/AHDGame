## What changed

- The margin bonus for a sector that matches your corporation's main type drops from +10 to +5 points, and the secondary type bonus from +5 to +2.5.
- Sectors in your HQ state earn +5 instead of +10, and sectors elsewhere in your home country +2.5 instead of +5.
- A state's primary sector specialty is now worth +5 points and its secondary specialty +2.5.
- Technology, financial, healthcare and real estate sectors now pay roughly 35 to 45% of their revenue as wages, up from about 10 to 28%.
- Off-type sectors still take the same -15 point penalty, subsidies, tech bonuses, state metrics and export premiums are unchanged.
- Expect typical corporations to show thinner profits, and weaker operators in service sectors to run at a loss more often. The player guide and wiki figures are updated to match.
