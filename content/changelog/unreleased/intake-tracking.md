title: Better measurement of how new players arrive and settle in
summary: Staff can now see every new account's path from sign-up to character to returning, and which sites send new players.
tags: [onboarding]
badges: [minor]
areas: [backend, admin]
---

The admin traffic page has a new-player intake panel. It shows every new account in the chosen window, whatever the sign-in method: how many created a character within an hour and within a day, how many came back after their first day and after a week, and which sign-in methods and referring sites they came from.

To support it, the game remembers where a visitor first arrived from (only the referring site's domain and any campaign tags in the link) and saves that with a new account, and records the last day each account loaded the game. Both are described in the privacy policy. Players who accept optional analytics also contribute tutorial progress, so we can see which tour steps people reach and where they stop.
