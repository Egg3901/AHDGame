In a 1991 world, Russia's June 1995 date opens an electoral law decision. Both chambers must approve by a majority of their full membership, followed by presidential enactment.

The law keeps 225 constituency seats and 225 national-list seats. Invalid ballots count toward the five-percent list threshold and list turnout. District turnout uses recorded ballot receipt counts. Existing campaigns and repeat elections retain their frozen law.

New campaigns require the actual enacted law receipt. Rejection preserves the original decree, and a failed authority write leaves no partial adoption. NPC governments introduce the draft through normal votes when the government and legislature are entirely NPC controlled.
