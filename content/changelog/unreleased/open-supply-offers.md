summary: CEOs can advertise buy and sell interest and start a private negotiation from an offer.

badges: [minor]

- Browse open offers by commodity, with quantity per turn, market price premium or discount, contract duration and expiry.
- Publish up to ten offers per corporation and withdraw them at any time. Advertisements expire after 168 turns.
- Responding fills the existing agreement form for review. Offers do not reserve capacity or become contracts until both CEOs agree.
