## Fixed

- The Article 5 posture no longer describes itself as collective defence. It
  raises readiness at a civil-liberties and economic cost and never enrolls
  members in a war.
- The alert posture panel and the alliance tab state how members enter a war:
  a Security organization has no mutual-defence clause, so each member must
  declare war itself, while a Bloc enters through a unanimous conflict-entry
  resolution.
- The Security category description on the founding form states that members
  are not pulled into each other's wars.
