date: 2026-10-14
title: Cookie banner no longer covers sign-up on phones
summary: On small screens the cookie banner is more compact, so the sign-up and login forms stay reachable.
tags: [onboarding, mobile]
badges: [minor]
areas: [frontend]
---

On phones the cookie banner used to cover about a third of the screen, right over the sign-up buttons. It now uses smaller type, side-by-side buttons and a height limit, and the page leaves room beneath it so every part of the sign-up and login forms can be scrolled into view. The wording and the choices are unchanged.
