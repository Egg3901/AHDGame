title: Plainer headings and fewer colors
summary: >-
  Headings and section labels use one plain style, and color is kept for actions,
  gains and losses, and real warnings.
tags: [ui]
badges: [patch]
areas: [frontend]
---

- Section labels lose the red tick and capitals, and guide callouts are now either notes or amber warnings for things that are hard to undo.
- Company cards, bill cards, treasury rows and nomination cards no longer have colored side stripes, and sector type tags are neutral.
- Search results, the tutorial, the wiki and the World page drop emoji and tinted icon tiles, and leaderboards show plain rank numbers instead of medals.
