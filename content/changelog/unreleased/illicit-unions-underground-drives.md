## Added

- Suspended unions now offer underground organizing to anyone in the country:
  quiet cell work that is slow and deniable, or a mass drive that builds
  faster and runs hotter. Both cost action points only; the frozen treasury
  is never touched.
- Every cell reads dark, suspected, or exposed, with a vague cold / warm /
  hot gauge instead of an exact number. Loud stretches risk exposure, which
  halves the cell's pace for several turns, while quiet turns cool the trail.
- Repealing a ban now converts everything built underground into legal
  strength at half value, so a ban followed by repeal leaves labor weaker
  than if it had never been banned, but not erased.
