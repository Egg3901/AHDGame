## What changed

- AI-run companies with a plant that is selling well below its capacity now begin shrinking it after 2 turns instead of 6, and cut 15% of the unused part each turn instead of 4%.
- AI-run plants that sell everything they make but still run far below capacity now begin shrinking after 6 turns instead of 24.
- AI-run companies no longer add new capacity in a sector where plants are running below 90% of capacity because of weak demand. Replacing worn-out capacity is unaffected.
- Player-owned and autopilot plants are unchanged.
