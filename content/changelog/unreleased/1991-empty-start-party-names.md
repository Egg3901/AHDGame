## What changed

- In a 1991 world explicitly started without parties, founders in player countries can use an absent historical party's name and abbreviation, including UUP.
- Existing party names and active charter names remain protected. Background countries and other starting modes retain the default reservations.
- Northern Ireland's unionist decisions can therefore acquire a player party leader through the normal charter process.
