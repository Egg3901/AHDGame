---
date: 2026-09-19
title: Parties grow outward from where they already are
summary: Recruiting a politician, moving one, and joining a party are now limited to regions a party already holds or borders.
tags: [parties, npp, recruitment, regions]
badges: [minor]
areas: [fullstack]
---

- A party can only recruit a politician into a region it already holds or one next to it, so parties expand outward instead of appearing anywhere on the map.
- Moving a politician follows the same rule, so relocation can no longer plant a party in a region it has no connection to.
- Players can only join a party that is established in their home region or in one next to it.
- A party counts as present in a region when it has a member living there, a politician based there, or someone holding office there. Recruiting into a new region therefore opens up that region's neighbours for the next step.
- A party with no members, politicians or officeholders anywhere is unaffected, so a brand new party, or one that has emptied out, can still be built up from any region.
- Region pickers now mark unreachable regions and say why, instead of leaving a button that does nothing.
