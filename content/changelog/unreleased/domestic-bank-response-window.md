  Head of government, finance ministers, and central bank chairs can act when a

When a bank first enters red solvency risk and remains solvent, the affected
country receives a three-turn response choice for funded recapitalization,
deposit guarantees, or resolution. The choice is available to the country's
head of government, finance minister, or central bank chair. Banks already
determined to have failed continue directly to the existing depositor and estate
waterfall.
