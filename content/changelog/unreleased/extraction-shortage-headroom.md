title: Extraction responds to wider shortages
summary: Mines can increase production during a wider commodity shortage even when they sold less than 99% of their previous output.
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
tags: [economy, corporations, extraction]
---

Extraction sectors that sold at least half their output can now claim their share of unmet demand in their reachable country or global market. Existing production limits still apply, and markets without a shortage keep the previous demand throttle.
