## What changed

- Action cards and the actions page header use period photos for 1991, 1999, 2007, 2019, 2023 and 2027, joining 1953 and 1979.
- Worlds without matching art fall back to a neutral set instead of another era's pictures.
- Weak action card photos in several eras were replaced with images that fit the action: rallies, billboards, donor dinners, street stands and press scrums.
