title: Manufacturing development costs use monetary plant capital
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine, backend, frontend]
tags: [manufacturing, products]
---

New manufacturing product projects cost 5% of their allocated monetary plant
capital, with the existing one-anchor minimum and 12-turn development period.
Player previews and autonomous corporations use the same paid capital basis;
plants without a recorded basis use their era-priced capacity value.
Existing projects retain their original costs and paid development receipts.
