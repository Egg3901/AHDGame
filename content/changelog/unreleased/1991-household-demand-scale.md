## What changed

- 1991 households were sized for a world with about eighty times more goods per
  person. Every consumer market showed a huge shortage that the price cap then
  hid, so build signals pointed everywhere at once.
- Household demand in 1991 worlds is now scaled to what those worlds start with,
  so the average market opens close to balanced.
- Worlds that start in any other year are unchanged.
