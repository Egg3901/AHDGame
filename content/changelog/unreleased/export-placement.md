date: 2026-10-14
title: Exported goods count as sold in the exporting state
summary: Mines and plants in surplus countries now offer their exported output to the market instead of only what their own country buys.
tags: [economy, trade, logistics]
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
---

- When a foreign buyer imports a good, the units now leave the exporting country's states as well as its national export pool. Before this, exports were still counted as unsold where they were made, so producers in surplus countries could only offer what their own country bought. Brazil's main oil-producing state placed 23 of about 24,000 units it produced in one turn.
- Sector pages and delivery figures for exporting producers now show their real sales abroad.
