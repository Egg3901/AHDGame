## What changed

- Public recapitalization and guarantees use funded, balanced settlements.
- Guarantees pay through the ordinary bank-resolution process and return unused funds.
- Exhausted bank assets trigger resolution even before a confidence-driven cash run.
- Stimulus and credit losses reach household orders; temporary austerity preserves debt service.
- Sovereign restructuring follows ordinary legislative decisions, including rejection.
- Euro feedback uses conserved sovereign holdings and enacted membership.
