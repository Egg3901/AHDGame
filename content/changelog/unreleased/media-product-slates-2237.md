## What changed

- Added a gated Media Product Studio for paid title projects across available
  media models. Project development spends paid research once, records only the
  owner's delivered commercial advertising, and retires projects that remain
  unfunded for four model cadences. Launched titles modify quality and audience
  loyalty within existing output and clearing, without adding production.
- Title coverage and decline tails now limit reachable offers before commercial
  and political ad clearing. Title sales record exact current-turn delivery,
  subtract unpaid political plans, and include only applied seller receipts.
- Settled paid development advertising now contributes to bounded title brand.
  That brand joins paid research evidence once with live four-pillar quality for
  the existing premium-pricing rule. A deterministic per-model ROI sensitivity
  report records the cash hurdle and finite price-defense effect without adding
  market demand or claiming a world forecast.
