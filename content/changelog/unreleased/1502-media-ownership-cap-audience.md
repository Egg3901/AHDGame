## What changed

- The media ownership limit caps each owner at the legal share of the state's advertising audience. Before, it was measured against what every outlet delivered, so a station with only small competitors could sell far less than the law allows, and a sole station could sell nothing.
- Ads that buyers wanted but the law blocked no longer count as unsold, so a capped station keeps its production instead of cutting it turn after turn.
- Political ad space uses the same limit.
