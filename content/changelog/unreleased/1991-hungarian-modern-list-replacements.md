After an authorized modern Hungarian Assembly takes office, parties can replace departed national-list deputies from their original frozen slate. Human party chairs retain the choice; NPC parties follow the original list order, with one autonomous designation per turn.

Replacement preserves the Assembly term, each player's single mandate and existing private accounts. Seat counters for both affected owners update with the office and replacement receipt. Concurrent or repeated choices cannot fill the same vacancy twice. Older modern receipts retain their stored nomination order, and older Assembly lists cannot supply the new chamber.
