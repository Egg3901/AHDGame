Russia's presidency and Federal Assembly are separate parliamentary choices. Each constitutional bill needs two-thirds of the current chamber's full seat capacity. Passing a mandate leaves the existing offices in place until the required election is certified.

Current legislators can introduce and revise a rejected decision from the legislature page. An NPC government can introduce each eligible decision once, leaving the result to the ordinary bill process. Establishing the Assembly alone no longer invents a presidency.
