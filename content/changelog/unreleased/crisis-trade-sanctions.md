## What changed

- Participating governments block arms trade with the crisis belligerents for one game year. Food and medical relief remain outside this restriction.
- Nonparticipating governments are not bound by another government's response.
- Trade panels identify crisis and organization sanctions, and the responsible trade minister can lift a crisis embargo.
- Replayed resolution cannot duplicate or restore a restriction that was deliberately lifted. Enforcement failures leave resolution retryable.
