Four research upgrades did little or nothing once the plants economy replaced the growth slider. Each now has a real effect, and the in-game effect labels say what it is.

- **Capacity build cost** (was growth cost): still discounts what you pay to build capacity, and now also shortens construction by 60% of the same percentage. A 20% discount means builds finish 12% sooner.
- **Output sale price** (price realization): now applies to cost-plus sectors too, adding to your markup on top of the posted price. Previously it did nothing for any sector set to cost-plus.
- **Market-dominance toll** (dominance shield): cuts the extra build cost a dominant sector pays on new capacity. This is the part of the dominance penalty that does not fade out, so the shield no longer drops to zero when the launch ramp ends.
- **Logistics strength**: besides easing the sprawl penalty, it now cuts the freight bill and the landed-price surcharge on inputs bought from other states, by up to 40% at a strength of 200. It pays off below the sprawl threshold too.

Existing caps are unchanged. The corporate R&D and logistics wiki pages describe the new effects.
