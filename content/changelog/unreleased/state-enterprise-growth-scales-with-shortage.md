## What changed

- A state-owned enterprise in a mild shortage still gets an order of about 5% of its capacity.
- The more its products are priced above normal, the larger the order, up to half of its current capacity for a severe shortage.
- It is still one order at a time, paid from a healthy treasury, and arrives after the usual build time. Governments near their debt ceiling, in a sovereign crisis, or short of cash still do not fund expansion.
