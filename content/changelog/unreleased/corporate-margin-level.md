## What changed

- Each sector's margin now includes a flat 8-point charge alongside its other modifiers (location, specialization, subsidies, tariffs, technology and the rest).
- The typical corporation had been earning a net margin above 20%, and almost no corporation lost money. The charge brings margins closer to real-world levels, so running a sector well matters and running one badly costs you.
- The charge is the same for every sector and every owner, so the ranking between corporations does not change. Your bonuses keep their full value.
