Hungarian party chairs can choose a replacement for a departed Assembly list deputy from their original filed list. The Chair Office shows available nominees and their original list positions. The seat keeps its party, district and remaining term.

Parties without a chair can designate a replacement automatically, with each decision recorded. Exhausted lists leave the seat vacant. Players hold one mandate and receive their appointment and career record once; replacing a deputy does not rerun the election or change certified compensation.
