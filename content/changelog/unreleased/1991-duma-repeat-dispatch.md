The turn resolver certifies a Duma repeat round only after every ballot named in its opening journal has finished. Incomplete or inconsistent rounds wait, and failed certification remains available for retry. Individual districts and lists cannot fall back to generic seating.

Opening journals load in one projected batch. Original first-generation certification remains separate. Repeat admission, filing, automatic opening, chamber handover and recurring elections remain in progress.
