## What changed

- Opening fields use eligible parties with regional presence and distinct unseated actors.
- Nationwide candidates have a real home region in their country.
- Historical officials and No Parties player countries retain their opening state.
- Existing worlds, reference refreshes and older presets retain their candidate population.
