## What changed

Euro-adoption provisions now pass the bill schema and shared legislative validator, remain attached to the proposed bill and use the normal legislative cost. The economy bill composer offers an adoption checkbox while the eurozone is inactive. Invalid jurisdictions, duplicate provisions and already-recorded adoption votes are rejected by the server.
