title: Product advertising pays a bounded set of media sellers
summary: >-
  Each product advertising order now pays its largest media sellers directly
  and one smaller seller for the rest, which keeps the corporation turn from
  running out of time without changing what any seller earns on average.
tags: [economy, products, media, performance]
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
---

- A product advertising order used to split its budget across every company
  that sells advertising, often hundreds of payments of a fraction of a cent
  each. Settling those payments grew with buyers times sellers and had become
  most of the corporation turn's running time.
- An order now pays the 24 largest advertising sellers their exact share. The
  remaining smaller sellers' combined share goes to one of them, chosen in
  proportion to the advertising each delivered, so every seller still earns its
  proportional share on average across orders.
- Order totals, buyer costs and the cash guards on each payment are unchanged.
