## What changed

- New `/market` page with headline figures: market index, sectors for sale,
  open supply offers and commodities above or below base price.
- Tabs for Overview, Stocks, Bonds, Funds, Sectors for sale, Commodities,
  Supply deals and Currencies. Each tab loads only when you open it.
- Supply deals lists open offers across every commodity, with Take and
  Negotiate. Offers from AI corporations are labeled.
- The nav item is now "The Market". Old `/stockmarket/global` links go to the
  matching tab. Country exchange pages are unchanged.
