## What changed

- Character profiles have Report and Block buttons for signed-in players viewing someone else.
- Reports pick a reason (harassment, hate speech, sexual content, violence, spam, impersonation, other) with optional details. One open report per player and target. Staff are notified, and the reports appear under Player Reports in the moderator Content tab and the admin Support tab, with a pending badge.
- Blocking refuses new mail from that player, hides their existing mail (restored on unblock), and hides their bio and campaign song on their profile for you.
- Settings, Security lists blocked players with Unblock.
- Unread mail counts and the inbox skip mail from blocked players.
