Fresh historical worlds now scale campaign action costs, campaign-manager upgrades and income together. Starting campaign funds, NPP income, campaign maintenance and price previews use the same era basis. Early donor-base upgrades retain a nonzero price. Existing worlds retain their configured pricing behavior.
