## What changed

- Constitutional drafts offer the historical dissolution clause or an explicit alternate continuation clause. Existing drafts retain their original meaning.
- Each transitional draft keeps its own collective signatures and still needs 267 constituent votes for adoption.
- A continued chamber retains its original term. A separate dissolution motion requires more than 200 deputies present and a majority in support, then advances untouched ordinary campaigns.
- Recorded ballots, failed-vote history, financial owners and the pending elected handover are preserved.
- The legislature displays Grand, continued, caretaker and ordinary chamber states. Public seat composition counts the mandates held by an NPC bloc and excludes executive offices.
