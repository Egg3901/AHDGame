## Changed

- The per-turn limit on what one member can receive from party funds is now
  five times higher when the paying body has two or more different officers
  seated. It applies in every country, multiplying whatever that country's
  limit already was rather than replacing it with one figure, so an economy
  tuned lower keeps its proportions.
- Each treasury is judged on its own oversight. A national party counts its
  Chair, Vice-Chair and Treasurer; a state party counts its own three; a caucus
  counts its Chair and Vice-Chair. Paying from a body with one officer stays at
  the base limit even if the national party is fully seated.
- Officers are counted as people, not seats. One person holding two posts is
  still one officer and does not raise anything.
- Send to Member, Request Funds, the state party fundraising panel and the
  caucus chair panel all show the limit actually in force, and say either that
  it has been raised or what seating another officer would raise it to.
