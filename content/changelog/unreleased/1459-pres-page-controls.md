## What changed

- During a presidential primary, each candidate in "The field" on the left of the map has an Endorse button (and an Endorsed state you can undo). One endorsement per race, never your own candidate, and in a primary only candidates in your own party. Candidates from other parties no longer show a button that the game would refuse.
- A "Race detail" section under the map brings back the tabs the map rebuild had dropped: Campaign presence, Trends, State drivers, Turnout (when the race has it) and Factor ledger. In a primary it shows only the tabs that have something to read yet.
- A party strip above the map lets anyone switch between each party's primary, so you can see their map and field, not just your own party's.
- Campaign presence on the map itself is unchanged: if you are running, switch the map to the Campaign presence view. Everyone else can read each candidate's presence in the Campaign presence tab.
