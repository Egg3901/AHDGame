## What changed

- During a presidential primary, each candidate in "The field" on the left of the map has an Endorse button (and an Endorsed state you can undo). The same rules apply as before: one endorsement per race, never your own candidate, and in a primary only candidates in your own party. If the game refuses, it says why under the button.
- For a general election or a finished race, a "Race detail" section under the map brings back the tabs the map rebuild had dropped: Campaign presence, Trends, State drivers, Turnout (when the race has it) and Factor ledger.
- Campaign presence on the map itself is unchanged: if you are running, switch the map to the Campaign presence view. Everyone else can read each candidate's presence in the Campaign presence tab.
