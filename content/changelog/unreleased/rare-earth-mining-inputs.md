## What changed

- Rare earth mining buys less energy, chemicals and ordnance per unit of output. Its input bill is now 60% of capacity value at base prices, in line with iron, coal, oil and timber mining, down from 72%.
- At current prices this cuts a rare earth mine's input bill by about 17%, which turns most loss-making rare earth mines profitable and lets them reinvest.
