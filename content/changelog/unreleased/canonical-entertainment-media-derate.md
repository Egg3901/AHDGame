## What changed

- Fresh 1991 worlds store entertainment sectors as media with an entertainment
  lane. Supply sizing, the world commodity ledger, the clearing offer, supplier
  capacity and the US media delivery data now read that lane, so entertainment
  output is sized exactly as it was under the legacy entertainment type.
- The 0.1 market-economy media derate and the planned-economy advertising remap
  now apply only to news and broadcast media.
- The commodity price turn now loads the operating lane fields it needs, so the
  world supply ledger and the clearing offer agree for entertainment and
  vehicle plants.
