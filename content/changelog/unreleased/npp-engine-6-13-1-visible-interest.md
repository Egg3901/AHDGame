## What changed

- When the 6.8 business-interest force applies, the vote prediction's donors label gains a line: the bill helps (pushes for) or hurts (pushes against) a company the NPP runs or holds. It shows on the NPP profile's forecast card.
- No decision changes.
- NPP engine version 6.13.1, recorded in the [NPP changelog](/changelog/npp).
