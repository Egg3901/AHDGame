## What changed

- On a 1991 world, a "What's new in the 1991 world" link sits under the front page headline and opens [lakesidegames.net/ahd-1991](https://lakesidegames.net/ahd-1991/) in a new tab.
- The explainer covers the 1991 starting setup, the systems added since Beta 2, the differences from the 1953 world, starting metrics for every country, and a map of each region's economic and social lean.
- Front pages for other eras are unchanged.
