New suggestions in the Discord suggestions channel now open with the suggester's name and picture. Players with a linked Discord account show their in-game character name with their Discord picture. Players without a linked Discord account show their in-game name and character portrait. Suggestions sent from Discord by someone without a game account show their Discord name and picture.
