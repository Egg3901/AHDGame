## What changed

- If your company controls a private subsidiary, its Tech tab now shows the research tree, unlocked technologies, R&D budget and research points instead of the "visible only to the CEO" notice. This matches the subsidiary's finance tabs, which parent CEOs could already see.
- Unlocking or abandoning technology is still done by the subsidiary's own CEO.
