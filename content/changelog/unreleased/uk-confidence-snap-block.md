## What changed

- UK Prime Ministers must wait for the post-Commons election confidence vote to resolve before calling a snap election.
- Blocked attempts explain why and leave Parliament, legislation, election schedules, and snap-election allowances unchanged.
- Administrative and automatic dissolution paths are unchanged.
