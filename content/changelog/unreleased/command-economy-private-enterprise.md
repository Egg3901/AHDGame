## What changed

- Whether private enterprise is permitted is now read from a country's
  marketization level rather than a per country setting. A country that becomes
  a planned economy closes to private enterprise from that point, and one that
  marketizes opens again, with no further work.
- This also closes the gap for the Soviet union republics and for China, which
  were planned economies that the old setting never covered.

## Fixed

- State enterprises can no longer be privatized inside a command economy. The
  block now covers every route: the treasury action, a legislative provision, a
  court ordered reversal, and the auction path.
- A privatization provision is now refused while the bill is being written,
  rather than passing its vote and then quietly doing nothing.
- Non player parties no longer found corporations inside planned economies, and
  their corporations no longer expand into planned markets.
- Production inside a planned economy can no longer change hands through the
  sector market, and state enterprises can no longer list production for private
  sale.
- A subsidiary can no longer be spun out inside a planned economy.
- Privatization controls are hidden where the mechanic does not apply.
