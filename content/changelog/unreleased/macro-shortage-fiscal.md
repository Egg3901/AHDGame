summary: Existing AI suppliers can expand in affordable increments, while large fiscal deficits gradually encourage government restraint.

- Existing AI plants can order smaller capacity additions when a whole new facility is unaffordable. Cash reserves, utilization checks, construction times and resource limits still apply.
- Producer price pressure uses the damped input price factors applied to physical input bills, reducing excessive cost feedback during shortages.
- Deficits above 3% of GDP increasingly favor fiscal restraint in autonomous governments. Changes still pass through the normal political process.
