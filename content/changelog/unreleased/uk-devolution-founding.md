## What changed

- Historical UK worlds require a devolution settlement before regional executive elections open.
- Founding and restoration establish an election schedule without reusing completed cycles.
- Later settlement amendments retain the existing election anchor.
- Abolition cancels unfinished executive races and removes access to the abolished offices while retaining election history.
