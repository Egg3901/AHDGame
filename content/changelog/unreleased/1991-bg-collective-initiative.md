One hundred of Bulgaria's 400 constituent deputies can now endorse a collective constitution draft. Players sign for their own single mandate. NPC co-partisans decide whether to endorse using their existing party loyalty and stubbornness, retaining their original financial accounts.

Signatures introduce a normal bill. Adoption still requires 267 votes. Departed deputies no longer contribute, and a rejected revision needs new endorsements. The introduction and its threshold signature settle together; a failed bill write rolls both back.
