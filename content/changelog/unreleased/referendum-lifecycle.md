- NPP-led Westminster lets its party chair or vice-chair decide referendum requests. After 24 turns, unanswered requests follow the ministry's regional stance.
- Parliamentary consent considers the public mandate and declared party positions, alongside normal whips.
- Campaign opening support matches Desire even at its limits; reported turnout follows the electorate and mobilization.
- Consent voting normally lasts 24 turns. Government formation and Royal Assent may extend the process; conversion requires enacted consent.
- Referendums remain visible during counting, and rejected conversion withdraws outstanding consent votes.
- Campaign payments, vote resolution and territorial conversion commit together on transaction-capable storage. Unsupported storage rejects these operations before spending resources.
- The bundled singleplayer database now starts with transaction support, preserving existing world data.
