## What changed

- The founding chamber has 200 constituency seats and 200 independent party-list seats.
- Players choose one constituency and can hold one seat, including when they also appear on a party list.
- The full chamber takes office together. Pending counts preserve ballot records, campaign funds and existing financial owners.
