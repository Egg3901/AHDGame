title: Central banks now hike faster when inflation runs far above target
summary: >-
  Automatic central bank chairs, the committee, and player chairs or
  governments setting the rate by hand can now raise rates by up to 3 points in
  one move when inflation is at least 5 points over target, instead of being
  held to 0.75. Rates in high-inflation economies catch up in a few moves rather
  than years. The central bank page shows the range you can use this turn.
tags: [economy, central-bank]

Rate moves still land on the quarter-point grid and show in the published rate history. Cuts are unchanged, and no automatic move takes a rate above 25%.
