title: Central banks now hike faster when inflation runs far above target
summary: >-
  Automatic central bank chairs and the committee can now raise rates by up to
  3 points in one move when inflation is at least 5 points over target, instead
  of being held to 0.75. Rates in high-inflation economies catch up in a few
  meetings rather than years. Rates you set yourself are unchanged.

Rate moves still land on the quarter-point grid and show in the published rate history. Cuts are unchanged, and no automatic move takes a rate above 25%.
