Repeat certification combines completed replacement ballots with preserved successful results in one transaction. Failed replacements can open another generation with fresh registration. Each generation retains its own immutable receipt, and list assignments keep constituency winners out of player list seats.

Certification rechecks the constitutional mandate, predecessor, ballot identities and current nominee eligibility. A late write failure rolls back tallies, election completion and candidacy retirement together. Congress remains in place while repeat admission, runtime dispatch and chamber handover are completed.
