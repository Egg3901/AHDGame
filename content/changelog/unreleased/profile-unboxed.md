## What changed

- Political standing is now a table showing each measure, its change per turn and what it means.
- Colour is kept for gains, losses and penalties. Your party colour appears once, beside the party name.
- Stats, career history and achievements read as plain lists, and achievements appear once instead of a box inside a box.
