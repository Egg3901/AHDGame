## What changed

- Political standing leads the page as a table showing each measure, its change per turn and what it means.
- Colour is kept for gains, losses and penalties. Your party colour appears once, beside the party name.
- Positions, social and career history sit in a narrower side column. Stats, finances and achievements read as plain sections, and achievements appear once instead of a box inside a box.
