Personal actions now refresh at a baseline of 5 per turn. Elected-office, cabinet, central-bank chair and party grants rise by 25%, and fractional grants carry forward. Player campaign allowances rise by about 25%, rounded to whole actions.

Diplomats and ministers receive 5 actions per refill. Vice presidents, justices and current presidential ticket surrogates receive 3 daily actions. Governors can bank 4 office actions and regenerate one every 14 turns. Settlement-seat action grants and banks rise by 25%.

At Energy 1, the personal action cap is 250 and the hoard threshold is 125. At Energy 10 they reach 313 and 156. The hoard tax is 5 actions per turn, keeping ordinary idle banks near their soft cap. The dashboard and profile breakdowns show the actual increased grants, including a minister's underlying legislative seat.
