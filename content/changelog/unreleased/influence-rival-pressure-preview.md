- The nation dossier shows the rival's recent push in points and subtracts it from the estimated gain, the same way the turn nets opposing pulls before the movement ceiling.
- A single maximum play against a rival landing nearly the same pull now previews as the small margin it really is, with a note that a second play in the same turn adds its own pressure.
- Spending, refunds and the movement ceiling are unchanged. Rival spend is still never shown.
