## What changed

- The Iterations category gains an Iteration 1 page. It opens with a table of the world in numbers (start date, snapshot turn, countries, parties, elections, bills, corporations, share trades, wars) and then has a section for the United States, the United Kingdom, East Germany and the German settlement, the Soviet Union, the wider world, and corporations, markets and currencies. A standings section and a closing section finish the page.
- The Beta 1 and Beta 2 pages carry an index of iterations that links forward to it.
- The country histories were extended in a second pass.
