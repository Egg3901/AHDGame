title: NPP-run companies keep up R&D, change course when losing, and empty caretaker seats are refilled
summary: Companies run by NPPs no longer let product quality decay when margins are thin, and a player company whose caretaker seat went empty gets a caretaker again.
tags: [npp, corporations, autonomy]
badges: [patch]
areas: [backend]
---

- NPP-run companies, including player companies run by a caretaker, now keep a small R&D budget even when margins are thin or cash is short. Their technology score and product quality no longer decay turn after turn.
- An NPP-run company that is losing money now changes strategy within 8 turns instead of holding its course for up to 48 turns because it is losing a little less than before.
- A player company handed to a caretaker that later showed no CEO now gets a caretaker again on the next turn. The owner keeps the right to reclaim it.
- This applies in worlds on NPP autonomy V6 (R&D) and at every autonomy level that installs caretakers (empty seats).
