## What changed

- Government purchases of a commodity in a turn are limited to 1.5 times what
  the whole world produced the turn before. Every government keeps its share
  of the total; only the total is cut.
- Demand above the cap is dropped, as it is for households, and recorded as
  unmet demand so companies still see the shortage as a reason to build.

## What players will notice

- In the live 1991 world, health care was meeting about 10% of demand and
  ordnance about 15%. Both prices ease from close to 3x their base toward the
  range other shortage goods sit in, which also eases inflation.
