title: General-purpose mines make far less timber
summary: Diversified mines now put their timber share into oil and natural gas, ending the timber glut that kept loggers losing money.
tags: [economy, extraction, commodities]
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
---

- Diversified mines produced most of the world's timber as a side product, about three times what the world buys, which held timber near its price floor and kept dedicated logging companies at a loss.
- Diversified mines now produce a quarter of the timber they did, and that share of their output goes to oil and natural gas instead, the two shortest raw resources. Their total output is unchanged.
