title: Media ownership limits bound delivered shares
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]

---

When media regulation is enabled, an enacted ownership limit now caps each private owner's final delivered advertising share in a state. Commercial and political sales, supplier receipts and unfilled political refunds use the same bounded delivery. Multiple outlets cannot bypass the owner limit. A market with too few delivering owners to satisfy the law leaves the affected demand unserved.
