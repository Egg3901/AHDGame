## What changed

- Tariffs cut how much can be imported even from a single supplier, and tariff inflation follows the goods households really import.
- An exchange-rate move causes a one-off inflation push instead of permanent extra inflation.
- Central bank credibility is judged against each country's own neutral rate and inflation target.
- Monetary committee votes resolve as soon as the outcome is decided, so no-shows no longer delay a rate change.
- Hospitals, construction, leases and entertainment events sell only inside their own state.
- Computer-run firms open or expand healthcare and entertainment producers when those services stay far above base price.
- Computer-run mining firms forecast revenue the way the market actually pays out.
- Sector sentiment events move stock prices again.
