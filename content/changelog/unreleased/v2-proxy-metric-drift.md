On the V2 metrics board (United States, United Kingdom, Japan, Ireland), most lines had no live measuring system yet and were carried forward unchanged from the opening, even though the board showed enacted-law and Cabinet pressure beside them. Energy security in Japan sat at its opening value for 170 turns of funded energy law (ticket 1539).

Those lines now drift toward a settlement set by the opening value plus the combined funded-law and Cabinet pressure, closing part of the gap on each refresh, and they drift back toward the opening when a program is defunded or an initiative expires. Lines with a live source (unemployment, growth, poverty, prices, budget balance, debt, coverage, waiting times, life expectancy, population, readiness) are unchanged.
