Background successors now contribute their agreed shares of inherited federation bond payments when a sovereign issuer continues after the split. New borrowing remains the continuing issuer's responsibility. Missed successor contributions remain with the owing successor, reduce stability, and can be repaid later. The World finance panel shows contributions, arrears and the issuer's own share.
