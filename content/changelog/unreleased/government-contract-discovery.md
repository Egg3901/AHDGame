Eligible player CEOs receive a notification when a government tender opens, with one alert per CEO even when multiple corporations qualify. Corporation overviews include a compact link to open government bids.

Governments can choose bidding periods from 6 to 48 turns, defaulting to 24. After bidding closes, officials have another 24 turns to select a qualifying bid. Defence tenders retain their original funding and quarterly quota reservation while bidding and award decisions cross quarter boundaries. Existing tenders keep their stored deadlines.

The board explains sealed bidding, payment direction and manual award decisions. Notification links open the specific tender even when it is outside the current list page.
