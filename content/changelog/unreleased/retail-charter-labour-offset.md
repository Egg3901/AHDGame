title: Retail bank charters stop charging full staffing for branch capacity
summary: >-
  Financial sectors now pay labour and upkeep only on the capacity that produces
  services, so an active deposit-taking charter no longer guarantees a structural
  loss.
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
tags: [banking, corporations, economy]
---

Financial sectors with an active deposit-taking charter now charge labour and maintenance on the capacity that actually produces financial services, instead of the whole sector. The capacity reserved for the branch network is still fully staffed, and the bank's deposit business is what covers it, so running branches no longer forces the sector into a structural loss on its own. Sectors without an active branch charter, and worlds with private banking switched off, are unchanged.
