## Changed

- An organization at the Article 5 alert posture now defends its members. When
  a member is declared on, every other member with a government joins the war on
  the defender's side, and its leaders are notified. This applies to
  organizations players found as well as built-in ones.
- NATO and the Warsaw Pact in Cold War worlds keep their standing charters and
  still defend their members at any posture.
- The pact is checked every turn. Raising the posture to Article 5 during a
  member's war, or joining an Article 5 alliance while a member is at war, brings
  the allies in on the next turn. Lowering the posture stops new entries but does
  not take anyone out of a war already joined.
- Allies pulled in by a pact send part of their ready reserve to the front and
  carry war weariness and approval effects like any other entrant.
- An ally stays out when it holds a truce with the attacker, is already fighting
  the attacker elsewhere, shares a bloc or a binding alliance with the attacker,
  or has already left that war. An alliance takes no side when the attacker is
  one of its own members.
- The alert posture panel, the alliance tab and the founding form now describe
  how each organization's members enter a war.
- War pages name player-founded alliances correctly when they pull a country in.
