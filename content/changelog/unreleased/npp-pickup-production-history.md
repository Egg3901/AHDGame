title: Keep plant sales history when an automated operator takes over
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
tags: [economy, corporations, production]
---

Automated plant takeovers now carry the transferred plants' measured production and sales into their new operator. A merge no longer cuts a sold-out plant's output simply because its recipient was smaller. Plants serving a glut keep their demand signal too, so a new owner does not restart them at full output. Existing takeover shares and operating costs are unchanged.
