Authorized modern Hungarian Assembly campaigns retain all regional slate actors through their primary transition. Each region freezes its registered electorate, and vote growth accounts for votes already cast for withdrawn candidates.

The general count preserves filed nominees and their recorded votes. Unavailable winners cannot take office: direct seats remain vacant and national seats use the original replacement slate. Older native tallies retain their cast history, and existing overruns cannot gain additional votes. Pending races display their actual slate instead of an unrelated regional proportional seat estimate.
