After an authorized modern Hungarian Assembly takes office, a departed constituency deputy opens a fresh single-seat election. Candidates compete by plurality in the original frozen district. National-list awards and the original Assembly term stay intact.

Players can file for one vacant constituency in their home region, withdraw and re-enter before filing closes. Cast votes remain recorded after withdrawal. Existing NPC financial owners supply the other candidates without creating extra accounts. Concurrent elections seat each winner once, while expired or superseded chambers cancel their outstanding vacancy ballots.
