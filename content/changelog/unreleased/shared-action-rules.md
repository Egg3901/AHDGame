- Fundraise quotes and effects use the same portable cost, eligibility and yield rules. Existing balance, stat multipliers and campaign currency conversion are preserved.
- Singleplayer bundles record the Game revision used to build them, with explicit dirty or unknown provenance when a clean source revision cannot be established.
