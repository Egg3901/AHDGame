title: Successors service private facility compensation
tags: [1991, succession, corporations]
---

After bond servicing, successors use available treasury cash to compensate firms for facilities lost in a federation split. Scarce cash is shared proportionally across unpaid claims. Remaining compensation carries forward, protected firms await their headquarters choice, and payment receipts prevent duplicate transfers.
