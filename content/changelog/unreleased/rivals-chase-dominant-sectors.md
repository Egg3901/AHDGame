## What changed

- Some sectors were ruled by one company with every rival far behind. Computer-run investors now found new companies more often in a sector like that, and the rival companies already in it put more of their spare cash into new capacity and grow in bigger steps.
- The sector leader is never slowed or taxed by this. Sectors with a healthy field of competitors behave exactly as before.
