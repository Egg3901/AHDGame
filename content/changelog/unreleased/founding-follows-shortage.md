## What changed

- Founding a company was priced against one flat amount of investment cash for every country, so executives in most countries could never qualify. The cash check now uses each country's own exchange rate.
- When many commodity markets are short, more executives try to found a company each cycle, and a cycle can complete more foundings. A calm market keeps a small ceiling so startups never flood in at once.
