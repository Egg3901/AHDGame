## What changed

- The fee to found a company was a flat 100,000 in each country's own money, which made it nearly free in weak currencies and out of reach in strong ones. It is now a fixed amount of world-anchor money (converted to local at the home exchange rate), so it costs the same real value everywhere and only the wealthiest executives can afford it.
- When many commodity markets are short, more executives try to found a company each cycle, and a cycle can complete more foundings. A calm market keeps a small ceiling so startups never flood in at once.
