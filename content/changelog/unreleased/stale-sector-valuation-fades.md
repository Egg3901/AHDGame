title: Old profit peaks fade out of company valuations
summary: >-
  When a sector earns much less than it once did, the value it adds to its
  company now fades toward what it earns today over about a year, instead of
  holding the old peak. Share prices follow current profit more closely.
tags: [economy, stock-market, corporations]
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
---

A short profit dip is still smoothed over, but a sector that has genuinely
earned less for a while no longer carries its old high-water mark in the
company's book value. Rising profits are credited straight away.
