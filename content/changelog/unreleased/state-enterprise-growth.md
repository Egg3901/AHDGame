## What changed

- A state-owned enterprise whose products are scarce now gets one build order at a time, worth about 5% of its current capacity, paid from its owning treasury.
- The order goes through the normal construction queue, so the capacity arrives after the usual build time.
- Governments close to their debt ceiling, in a sovereign crisis, or short of cash do not fund expansion. Replacement of worn-out plant is unchanged.
- Extraction enterprises are not expanded this way, because their output is limited by deposits.
