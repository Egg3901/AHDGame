## What changed

- A UK cabinet minister who still holds a Commons seat sees the constituency picker on their profile. Choosing a constituency updates their seat and their Commons career entry; their cabinet post is unchanged.
- When a government falls or a minister leaves the cabinet, the restored MP keeps the constituency they had chosen instead of losing the label.
