## What changed

- A party merger now moves the absorbed party's live candidacies and campaigns to the surviving party along with its members.
- A race that resolves with a winner still filed under a merged party seats the winner in the party that absorbed it. If the absorbing party later merged too, the winner goes to the party that survives the chain.
- The same applies to presidential races.
- Fourteen US legislators returned to the Federalist Party of the United States this way after it merged into the Constitutional Union Party. They have been restored to the Constitutional Union Party.
