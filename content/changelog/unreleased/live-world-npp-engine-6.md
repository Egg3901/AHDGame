- The live multiplayer world moves from autonomy V4 to V6. NPPs there now pick races they can win, rally in their own race, count votes before filing bills, whip their programme, defend their currency, invest on value and choose CEOs by track record. See the [NPP engine changelog](/changelog/npp) for each change.
- V6 also turns on two V5 behaviours: an NPP government keeps up to five standing goals across agenda changes and grades each one, and NPPs age, so an elderly NPP can die in office and a same-party successor takes the seat.
- NPPs get no extra action points, money or election weight. Players keep every office, company and decision they hold.
- Singleplayer worlds keep the level their player chose.
- Fix: when an NPP dies, its successor also takes its cabinet posts, national leadership and any player company it was caretaking.
- Fix: NPPs no longer withdraw from a primary their party chair slated them to.
