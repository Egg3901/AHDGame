## What changed

- A national state company with no appointed chief executive now shuts any of its mines whose region holds none of the resources the mine is set up to extract. Such mines produce nothing but still pay workers and buy inputs.
- Mines are shut a few per turn, largest first. A shut mine can be restarted.
- State companies with an appointed chief executive, and every player-run company, are left to their owners.
