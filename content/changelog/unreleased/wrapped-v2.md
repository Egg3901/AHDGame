## What changed

- Wrapped now opens with the season itself: the years it covered, how many
  politicians played, elections held, bills passed, and the closest race any
  player stood in.
- Your season strip shows your actions across every turn of your career, with
  the races you won marked on it, your busiest stretch and your longest streak.
- A quick question before the reveal: guess what you did most.
- Your climb lists each office you reached, in order, with the date.
- Election night shows your biggest win and your closest race with the full
  result, plus the total votes cast for you.
- If you met the same player in two or more races, they appear as your rival,
  with your record against them.
- Your signature bill is shown with its roll call. Bills that passed or failed
  by less than your own vote are called out, along with how often you voted
  with your party.
- Your fortune is charted across the season with its peak, and your final
  standings show the players just above and below you.
- Season awards go to the top three in the world and the leader in each
  country across races won, votes, bills passed, influence, streaks and more.
- Each Wrapped ends with one line on how you played and a card you can share
  or save as an image sized for phone stories.
- Highest office now recognises every country's offices. A General Secretary,
  Taoiseach or Chancellor is no longer listed below a backbench seat, and the
  Hall of Fame scores them the same way.
- Money in Wrapped uses your country's currency as your era knew it.
- Recaps from earlier seasons still play, using the data they recorded.
