  The change that lets player-built sectors earn the market price from their
  first turn now only ever raises their revenue. Sectors whose local market
  pays less than their usual rate, such as media stations in smaller states,
  keep their usual revenue.

## What changed

- A player-built sector earns whichever is higher: its full market revenue or the revenue it would have earned under the normal market support. Before, some sectors in weak local markets saw their revenue fall sharply.
