## Manufacturing product studio detail

The manufacturing Product Studio shows the development cost estimate, eligible strategy technology gates, live quality inputs, and settled output sold for an active product. Each plant records redirected project units separately from its full recipe output, and product sales use only the matching project's units after market clearing.
