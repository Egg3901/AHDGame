## What changed

- How well a candidate's ideology fits a state's primary voters now counts for more, so a progressive can carry the coasts while a moderate carries the South in the same primary.
- Each state has its own fixed swing for each candidate in a race, so results are no longer a copy of the national picture in every state. The swing is the same every time a race is replayed.
- The home-state bonus in presidential primaries rises from 10% to 20%, and candidates now get a smaller 6% boost across their home state's region.
- Primary projections on the map use the same rules, so the projected winner of a state still matches the likely result.
