date: 2026-10-17
title: Plants restarting from a stop no longer run flat out for a turn with nothing to sell
summary: A plant that made nothing last turn (back from mothball, newly finished, or after a full outage) now restarts at a quarter of its run, instead of paying full inputs and wages for a turn in which it cannot sell anything yet.
tags: [economy, corporations]
badges: [minor]
areas: [engine]
---

- Plants sell what they made the turn before, so the first turn after a stop has nothing on the market. Until now such a plant ran at full output anyway and sold nothing.
- A restarting plant now runs at a quarter of its capacity (or more if a supply contract needs it) and grows from its real sales from the next turn.
- A plant with no crew on site hires only the crew that run needs. A plant whose crew stayed through an outage keeps the usual gradual staffing changes.
