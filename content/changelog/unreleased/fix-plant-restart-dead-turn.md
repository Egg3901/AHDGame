---
date: 2026-10-17
title: Plants restarting from idle no longer run flat out for a turn with nothing to sell
summary: A plant back from mothball, idle, or with newly finished capacity now restarts at a quarter of its run and hires only that crew, instead of paying full inputs and wages for a turn in which it cannot sell anything yet.
tags: [economy, corporations]
badges: [minor]
areas: [engine]
---

- Plants sell what they made the turn before, so the first turn after a stop has nothing on the market. Until now such a plant ran at full output and full crew anyway and sold nothing.
- A restarting plant now runs at a quarter of its capacity (or more if a supply contract needs it) and grows from its real sales from the next turn.
- A plant that made nothing last turn hires straight to the crew it needs instead of carrying its old full crew.
