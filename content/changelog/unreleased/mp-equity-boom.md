## Changed

- Non-player investors now consider a wider range of listed companies instead of concentrating almost all new demand in the largest few firms.
- Autonomous investors buy shares somewhat more often and trim smaller portions less often, giving a healthy, profitable economy a mild long-run expansion bias.
- Every investment remains funded by existing investment cash. The finite equity-market pools do not create money.
