title: Over-building a good costs sharply more, and dominant producers cannot undercut
summary: Building far more capacity of a good than the world wants now carries a steep cost, and a producer that supplies over half the world's supply of a good can no longer cut its price below the market.
tags: [economy, corporations, balance, pricing]
badges: [minor]
areas: [fullstack, engine]
---

- Capacity builds now compare what your companies could make of each good (existing capacity, builds already under way and the new order) with what the world wants of it. Up to half of world demand costs the normal price, about 3 times at full world demand and about 10 times at double. Past that the cost keeps climbing faster. Rival count and tech do not discount this charge.
- The build dialog shows a "Share of world demand" line with the multiplier and a plain explanation.
- A producer that sells more than half of a good's world supply cannot post a price below the market for that good. Negative pricing is held at the market price, for players and for AI-run companies alike.
- Capacity you already own and orders already queued are not changed.
