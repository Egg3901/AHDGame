## What changed

- New **Field offices** tab in the campaign room. In the US, pick a county on a map shaded by its partisan lean and open an office there. The office lifts your turnout in that county and, with diminishing returns, across the state.
- Offices pay off most in large counties where your side is already strong. An **Opportunity** view shades every county by what a new office there would add.
- Offices take three turns to reach full strength and cost upkeep every turn. A campaign that runs out of money closes its newest offices first.
- Office locations are public, so rivals can see where you are organising.
- County leans now match your world's era. A 1991 world starts from the 1984 and 1988 county results, a 1999 world from 1992 and 1996, a 2007 world from 2000 and 2004, and a 2027 world from 2020 and 2024 plus the 2025 Virginia and New Jersey governor races. Election result maps use the same leans. Once your world holds its own presidential election, each state's counties shift with that result.
- Ground Game upgrades now affect Senate, House, governor and state legislative races, not just the presidency.
- UK Commons candidates and Japanese House of Representatives, House of Councillors and governor candidates can now run campaigns. Their field offices work region by region, up to three per region, with a smaller bonus.
- The Ground Game branch previously called Field Offices is now called Swing Canvassing. It works the same way.
