- Central banks run by the game used raw GDP growth as their only read on the real economy. Growth figures swing widely from turn to turn, so these banks held rates far above inflation while unemployment sat near 12% in most economies.
- They now weigh the output gap and unemployment above 5% alongside inflation, and never target a rate below 0.25%. Expect AI-run banks in high-unemployment economies to cut over the next several meetings.
- Player-run central banks and the votes on rate committees with a player chair are unchanged.
