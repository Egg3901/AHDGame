## What changed

- The 1995 and 2000 dates open distinct formation laws. Each chamber must approve
  by a majority of its full capacity. Enactment and actual office handover remain
  separate, so rejection or unavailable representatives preserve existing custody.
- Regional executive and legislative authorities supply two distinct people for
  each of the 89 subjects. Delegates follow their appointing authority's remaining
  term. Appointed Council members continue to vote on ordinary laws.
- Bounded NPC regional decisions use certified Duma constituency support as an
  explicit simulation proxy and a four-year regional term default. Existing NPC
  groups back distinct individuals without creating financial profiles.
- Regional renewal, vacancies and delegate changes retain held mandates and party
  defections. Ended mandates are not recreated under the same appointment identity.
- The Duma, government, private accounts and original election roots survive
  Council handovers. Players are never assigned regional authorities automatically.
- Player ballots, co-sponsorship eligibility, discussions, vote displays and whip
  panels follow the installed Council's voting authority. Concurrent votes and
  presidential veto overrides still require both chambers.
