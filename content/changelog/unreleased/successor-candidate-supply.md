## What changed

- The existing candidate-supply phase covers National Assembly, Sejm, Senat and Chamber of Deputies primaries.
- Candidate entry retains regional party eligibility, primary windows and the one-active-candidacy rule.
- Player countries with No Parties retain their empty party rosters.
