## What changed

- Moving a private corporation's headquarters into a country with a fully state-run economy is refused, whether the CEO relocates and takes the corporation along or shareholders vote for the move. The same rule already stopped private corporations being founded there.
- A shareholder vote to relocate into such a country can't be opened, and a vote opened earlier no longer carries the corporation in when it passes.
- The rule follows the country's economy. Once a reform makes private enterprise legal, corporations can move in on that same turn.
- Moves inside a corporation's own country and moves by state-owned corporations are unaffected. A CEO can still relocate alone.
