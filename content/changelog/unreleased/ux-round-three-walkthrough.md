## What changed

- The crises board labels its header figure as resolved crises across all scopes, and the per-tab toggle counts only the resolved crises that tab can list. Resolved crises carry a Resolved badge, and an empty tab says how many resolved crises sit below it.
- The Supreme Court explainer scrolls instead of running off screen, and the Docket History tab explains Affirmed history and Diverged and how cases reach the Court.
- Trade uses the same nation names as Sectors and the country pages, including era names such as Soviet Union and per-world names.
- The White House and presidential race pages link to the presidential powers reference and the running for office guide. Bills on the Desk show their sign-by deadline and what happens when it passes.
- The World menu item for the Nations page now says it holds the world map.
