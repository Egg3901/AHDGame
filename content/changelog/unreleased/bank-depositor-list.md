## What changed

- Bank > Deposits & Rates now lists named depositors and their current savings balances, largest first.
- The list is private to the bank CEO, administrators and the currency's central-bank chair.
- Household deposits remain a separate combined balance. Legacy assigned savings are clearly labelled.
