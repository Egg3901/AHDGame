title: Fresh worlds start with every gameplay feature on
badges: [minor]
areas: [backend, engine]
tags: [flags, reset]
---

A fresh world, whether a first start or a reset, now begins with every gameplay
feature switched on and every rollout ladder at its top tier. The one exception
is autonomous politicians, which start at v4 instead of v5. This includes funded
Treasury cash, sovereign primary offers, bank underwriting and contagion, freight
settlement, media editorial and regulation, and the Cold War systems.

Maintenance, access, security and pause switches keep their safe defaults, and
worlds already running are never changed by a seed top-up or a startup
migration. A test now fails whenever a new feature flag is neither on for fresh
worlds nor recorded as a deliberate exclusion.
