## What changed

- In a world that opened with no parties, the 48-hour waits on NPP controls (party age and your own membership time) are lifted for the first 240 hours of the world. You can slate NPPs, recruit and whip as soon as your party exists.
- You still need to be a current member of the party.
- After 240 hours the normal 48-hour waits apply to parties founded or joined from then on. Worlds that start with parties are unchanged.
