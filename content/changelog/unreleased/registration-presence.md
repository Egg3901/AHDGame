---
title: Registration drives require local party presence
summary: Stop registration drives from growing party registration in regions without a local player, active NPP or officeholder.
date: 2026-10-08
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
tags: [parties, registration]
---

National and regional voter registration drives now require a local player, active NPP or regional officeholder. National spending is shared only across eligible regions, and drives with no eligible regions do not spend funds. Eligibility uses live rosters rather than cached presence flags.

The treasury panel explains this restriction and no longer presents an all-region estimate as the expected gain.
