## What changed

- Expansion advice and automated corporations can respond to demand hidden by
  the market ledger cap without changing prices or trade clearing.
- Government healthcare and defence spending now reaches regional markets,
  and building materials regain an economy-wide buyer.
- Automated corporations can mothball persistently loss-making plants, and
  advertising demand grows more slowly as the economy expands.
