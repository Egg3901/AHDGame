## What changed

- Each turn of the move, every business closes the same share of the gap between what it earns now and what it would earn at real market prices.
- Businesses that started with the world no longer wait until the final turns of the move. They change a little every turn, like everyone else.
- Businesses that had already moved partway keep that progress.
