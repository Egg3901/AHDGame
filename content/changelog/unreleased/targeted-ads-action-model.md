- Canvassing and targeted ads open in a window on the Actions page.
- Targeted ads reach your home state. Active presidential candidates can choose another state in their country.
- Choose how many actions to spend. Each costs 1 action point and 100 campaign funds before the campaign currency conversion.
- Each action immediately adds one point of target strength, capped at 25%. Alignment and cohesion shape the actual voter bonus.
- Strength slowly returns toward zero, halving over 24 turns. There is no flight duration or automatic future purchase.
- Purchases beyond the target's remaining capacity are rejected before charging.
