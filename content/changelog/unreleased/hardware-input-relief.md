Hardware factories gradually need 15% fewer materials for the same output over twelve turns after the change is activated in their world. Energy, rare earths, steel and chemical requirements all fall together. Factories switching production methods receive the improvement in proportion to their hardware activity. Market prices and production levels still affect the final bill.
