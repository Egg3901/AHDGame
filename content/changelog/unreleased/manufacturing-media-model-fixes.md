## What changed

- Changing strategy on a vehicle manufacturing plant opens the confirmation again instead of doing nothing.
- The strategy confirmation on a sector page now includes media operating models.
- Corporations without a media sector see a collapsed "Buy coverage advertising" entry instead of the full media panel. Any live agreement still shows in full.
- Sector focus, union, subsidy, tariff, nationalization, strategic sector, sponsored fund, and market sector pickers list manufacturing and media instead of automobiles and entertainment. A corporation that still carries an old focus keeps it.
