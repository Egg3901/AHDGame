- Low Income, Medium Income, and High Income each have a separate income-tax provision and rate.
- Income-group names are consistent across countries. Existing demographic targeting remains compatible.
- Existing tier rates start at the previous income-tax rate. Budget revenue and bill estimates use income-weighted demographic shares.
- Personal salary, dividends, bond coupons, and savings interest are taxable. Starting capital, transfers, borrowing, campaign fundraising, and returned principal are exempt.
- Characters retain their starting income group; their applicable group follows recent personal earnings. Personal tax statements show gross income, tax, net income, and unpaid assessments.
- Tax collection and treasury delivery recover safely after interruptions. Unpaid tax remains a liability rather than disappearing when cash is insufficient.
