## What changed

- Five weekly contests: small business growth, big business growth, National Influence gained, government approval gained, and weekly referrals. Rounds last a week, standings update every turn, and each card shows its prize.
- Each week's leader wins cash in personal funds, sized to the world's era: 7,500,000 for the growth contests and 10,000,000 for weekly referrals. A week where nobody grows pays nothing.
- Corporate growth is measured in percent, with capital the owner injects during the round taken out. Corporations are split into small and large at the median opening value so the biggest firms race each other.
- The approval contest counts only while the same player leads the government for the whole round.
- Weekly referrals count invited players who create a character that week. Accounts strongly linked to the referrer as alts do not count.
- The iteration referral contest runs all iteration. When a new iteration starts, the top three referrers get Supporter for that whole iteration, and the previous winners' contest Supporter ends. Paying supporters keep their own plan.
