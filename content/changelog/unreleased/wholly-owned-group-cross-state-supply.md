date: 2026-10-14
title: Wholly owned groups can reserve cross-state plant supply
summary: Group supply priority can use recorded freight hauls to serve eligible plants across wholly owned subsidiaries.
tags: [economy, corporations, logistics]
badges: [minor]
areas: [engine]
---

- With Group priority and cross-state shipping enabled, output hauled for the group can be reserved for plants in wholly owned subsidiaries, including nested subsidiaries. Own priority remains within one corporation, and minority ownership does not qualify.
- Local own plants keep priority before group plants. Only freight delivered on the previous turn can be reserved, and the haul's freight cost still applies.
