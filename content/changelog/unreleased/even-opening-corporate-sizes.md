## What changed

- Starting AI-run corporations used to take a fixed slice of the capital region's market, so countries with a huge capital (Japan, France) opened with far more corporate capacity than countries with a small one (the United States, Germany).
- In a new 1991 world, each country's starting corporations now hold a share of the country's own market, so corporate revenue and market value line up with each economy's size.
- Worlds already running are not changed.
