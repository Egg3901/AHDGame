Investment and universal banks can subscribe to unplaced same-currency sovereign
units when primary subscriptions are enabled. The console shows the available
units, coupon, maturity and reviewed maximum price. Actual vault cash above the
cash floor pays the issuing Treasury; only funded units become public debt.

Interrupted subscriptions keep their original charter epoch and quoted price.
Existing short-bill automatic purchases keep their maturity limit. Primary
subscriptions are off by default and create no pool cash or money.
