title: Russian legislature resets follow the active constitution
tags: [1991, Russia, elections]
---

Russian legislature elections reopen the appointments belonging to their active constitution. A directly elected president keeps their mandate, and obsolete Soviet results cannot reset a successor government. Legislature-appointed heads of state lose both their office records and their character or NPC office links when their mandate ends.
