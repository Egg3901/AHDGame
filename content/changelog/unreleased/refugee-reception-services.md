Yugoslav humanitarian reception now moves civilian age and sex cohorts from actual origin regions to the accepting country. Admission respects migration limits, available civilian cohorts and the asylum restrictions enacted when the leader responds. Decisions without displacement, closed routes and expired corridors receive nobody.

Initial support is based on the people actually received and appears as recurring national spending for six game months. It replaces the old upfront reception charge and fixed host penalty. Crisis responses show received totals and route details after the population turn completes, and interrupted population or history writes resume from the same frozen plan.
