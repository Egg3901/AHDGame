## What changed

- Each chamber's bill list opens with its seal, seat count and number of open votes. Chambers without an official emblem get a standard chamber icon.
- Senate confirmation cards show the nominee's portrait, party and office, plus who nominated them. Names and portraits link to their profiles, and the card opens the nomination.
- Confirmation cards use the same vote count bar as bills, show how long is left to vote, and mark how you voted.
- Bill cards show the sponsor's portrait and a small chamber seal next to the chamber name.
- Filter tabs show how many items are in each status.
- Nominee party now shows the party name instead of a number.
