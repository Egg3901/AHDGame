title: Political advertising has a funded fallback when a state has no outlet
---

When a funded political advertising order has no media outlet in its target state, up to 25% of its budget now funds a weaker local fallback through the target country's treasury. The rest is refunded. The fallback keeps the order's original country and state, uses the frozen treasury currency and exchange rate, and settles through a replay-safe money move. It does not create a media seller or redirect an order to another state. The effect remains proportional to the amount actually funded.
