## What changed

- The 1991 starting values are 35 for Scotland, 23 for Northern Ireland and 10 for Wales.
- Regional and Westminster approval have twice their former sensitivity near 50%, including fractional changes, with the same maximum contribution.
- Small changes now accumulate, including the gradual return toward the long-run baseline.
- Trend readings reflect the actual movement when desire reaches 0 or 100.
