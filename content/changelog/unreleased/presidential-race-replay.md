## What changed

- A finished presidential race has a "Replay the campaign" button above its map. It plays the general election back one week at a time: who led each state that week, the running electoral vote count and the national popular vote. Pause it, or drag the slider to any week.
- Switch between Electoral votes (each state in the colour of whoever led it) and Popular vote (states shaded by margin). The road-to-270 strip follows the week too.
- Zoom in during a replay to see every county's popular vote as it stood that week.
- "Final result" returns to the finished race.
