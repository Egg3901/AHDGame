## What changed

- When an FOMC meeting opens and you hold the chair as a player, the committee tab shows a "Table your motion" panel. Pick Raise rate, Cut rate or Hold, and for a raise or cut the size in quarter-point steps.
- Your motion replaces the automatic one until the first player governor casts a ballot. After that the motion is locked so nobody votes on something that changes under them.
- The committee still has to pass it, and the usual limits apply: the largest move allowed per meeting, the cooldown between moves, the per-term budget and any FX commitment.
