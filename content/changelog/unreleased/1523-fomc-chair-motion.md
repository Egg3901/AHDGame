## What changed

- While a meeting is taking votes, a player chair sees a "Table motion" control on the committee tab. Pick Raise rate, Cut rate or Hold, and for a move its size in quarter points.
- The committee still decides: the motion needs a majority of the seated governors to pass, and NPP governors keep voting their own view of the economy.
- The usual limits still apply. A move bigger than the per-meeting cap, or one tabled after the term's rate changes are used up or during the cooldown, is refused; a hold can always be tabled.
- When the chair is an NPP, the motion is still set automatically, as before.
