title: Economy-wide demand matches each country's size
summary: Demand for services and building materials in each region now follows the size of its economy, so no country's companies have an outsized market.
tags: [economy, commodities, corporations]
badges: [patch]
areas: [backend]
---

- Demand for real estate, network, entertainment and construction services, and for building materials, now follows each region's economy measured in the same money for every country. Before, regions whose figures were kept in their own currency could count as far larger than they really were, which gave their companies far more customers than the size of their economy justified.
- New worlds start with the corrected demand. The current world moves onto it gradually over 24 turns, so companies have time to adjust. Japanese demand for these services falls the most; British and German demand rises somewhat.
