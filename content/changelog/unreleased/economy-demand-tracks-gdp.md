date: 2026-10-15
title: Economy-wide demand matches each country's size in new worlds
summary: In newly started worlds, demand for services and building materials in each region now follows the size of its economy, so no country's companies start with an outsized market.
tags: [economy, commodities, corporations]
badges: [patch]
areas: [backend]
---

- Demand for real estate, network, entertainment and construction services, and for building materials, now follows each region's economy measured in the same money for every country. Before, regions whose figures were kept in their own currency could count as far larger than they really were, which gave their companies far more customers than the size of their economy justified.
- This applies to worlds started from now on. Worlds already in play keep their current demand so nothing shifts under existing companies.
