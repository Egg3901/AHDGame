## What changed

- The first time a country outside the player law book refreshes its budget,
  the gap between its seeded spending envelope and the cost of its laws is
  fixed as a share of GDP and kept as ongoing spending. Russia already worked
  this way. Law changes still move spending on top of it.
- In the live 1991 world this restores German spending from about 12% to 33%
  of GDP, Brazil's from about 8% to 33%, China's from about 7% to 18% and
  Nigeria's from about 8% to 12%. Germany's 27% of GDP surplus shrinks to a
  believable level.

## What players will notice

- Those countries' budgets show realistic spending again, which feeds their
  deficits, debt and borrowing.
