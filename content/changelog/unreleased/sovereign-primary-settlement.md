Sovereign bond offers now credit the treasury only for funded units. Scheduled auctions, administrative offers and later placements record the buyer payment, issued debt and proceeds together so interrupted settlements can recover without paying twice. Central-bank funding reaches the issuer directly, while unplaced units await buyers.
