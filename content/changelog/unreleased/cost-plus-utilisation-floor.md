title: Keep cost-plus quotes stable when production slows
summary: Cost-plus pricing spreads fixed costs over at least half of plant capacity.
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
tags: [economy, pricing, manufacturing]
---

Cost-plus quotes now spread fixed operating costs over at least 50% of plant capacity. Low demand no longer concentrates the whole fixed bill into a small production run and drives the next quote sharply higher. Input costs still follow actual production, and full-utilisation quotes are unchanged. The new allocation applies from the next producing turn; it does not change past accounts or guarantee a profit at low sales.
