## What changed

- Pending national counts prevent a region from opening another campaign, and a settled founding poll cannot restart while other countries are still founding.
- A constitutional transition opens an untouched ordinary campaign with four filing turns and two ballot turns. Later ordinary elections retain a four-year term from the actual first ballot.
- Native founding deputies' term displays align once the founding calendar resumes. An authorized early election can seat its results before the old historical date.
- Admin timer repair preserves native ballot deadlines, status, cycle numbers, candidacies and vote records.
