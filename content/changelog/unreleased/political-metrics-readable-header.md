## What changed

- The header names the country in large type, with the overall condition below it as a large figure, its status tag and a line on what the figure measures. The registry line, the live series marker, the turn and the statistics office line sit around it, and the Compare countries button is beside the name.
- Every label on the page is now at least 12px, including the small capitals on the cards, the governance card and the detail pages.
- Under Underlying statistics, the trend arrows are coloured by whether the change is good for the country, so rising unemployment shows red.
- The category and metric pages now show how much the score moved since the last snapshot.
- The governance card's headline sits at the top of the card instead of leaving an empty corner above it, and a penalty of zero reads 0.0.
- On a region's metrics tab, the back and compare buttons on a category page now name the region instead of the nation.
- The metric list on a category page says which order it is sorted in.
- On a phone, the country comparison says to scroll sideways for the other countries.
