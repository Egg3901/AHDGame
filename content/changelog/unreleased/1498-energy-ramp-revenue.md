## What changed

- When an energy sector produced more per plant thanks to the productivity ramp or output technology, the revenue safety cap treated that extra output as a windfall and trimmed it, so every unit sold for less than the market leg promised. The cap now expects the extra output, so each unit earns its full price.
- Sectors without those gains are unaffected.
