## What changed

- About 45 blocks of running text, including the CEO operations panel, the nation dossier and the German question ladder, move from 10px to 12px.
- Around 190 section headings and the masthead labels are now sentence case at a readable size, instead of tiny spaced capitals.
- The red line at the top of the page appears only while a page loads, and it is a solid bar instead of a glowing gradient.
- Frosted panels and pills are now solid, the home page tiles no longer fade in a blurred photo on hover, and the last colored side stripes on cards are gone.
- Long party names wrap onto a second line instead of being cut off.
- Guide text no longer uses a spaced hyphen as a dash, and the line of credit box on your profile is neutral instead of red.
