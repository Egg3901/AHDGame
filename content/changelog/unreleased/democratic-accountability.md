## What changed

- The ruling-party penalty from low Democratic Health is now twice as strong,
  and a sitting President can face up to a 30% total penalty at complete
  institutional failure.
- Democratic Health below 60 now subtracts up to four percentage points from
  annual potential GDP growth. The drag flows through realized growth,
  unemployment, tax bases, and the wider economy.
- Election screens now show the annual GDP-growth drag beside the electoral
  penalties so the consequence is visible before votes are counted.
