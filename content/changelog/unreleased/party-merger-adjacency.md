date: 2026-10-07
title: Party mergers require neighboring presence
summary: Party mergers now require overlapping or directly neighboring regional presence.
tags: [parties, mergers, adjacency]
badges: [patch]
areas: [frontend, backend]
---

- Both parties must have members, elected officials, or active NPPs in the same or neighboring regions. Empty footprints do not qualify.
- Geography is checked before proposing and again before transfers. An approved proposal is rejected if the parties no longer touch.
- The proposal form and both voting panels explain the requirement. Existing completed mergers are unchanged.
