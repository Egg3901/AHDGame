Russian bills continue through the Union Congress, provisional Congress and Federal Assembly after a negotiated Soviet settlement. Votes use the active deputies and chambers, direct presidents receive their signature window, and a dissolved Congress cannot enact bills. Older worlds retain their one-party legislature rules.
