## What changed

- Democratic Health below 60 now creates a nonlinear electoral drag for every ruling-party candidate in a presidential race.
- The sitting President carries an additional personal drag when running, while temporary constitutional relief can soften only that extra effect.
- Presidential election screens show the health score, ruling-party drag, sitting-President drag, and any active temporary relief.
- A scheduled US Constitutional Crisis event offers judicial review, a temporary continuity protocol, or a cross-party constitutional conference. Outcomes can lower Democratic Health while giving the President a short-lived cushion.
