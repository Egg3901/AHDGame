## What changed

- Standard layout: "N Players Online" now sits in the status bar row after the turn timer and price refresh note, instead of being pinned to the centre of the bar where it covered them.
- Corp layout: the turn timer and price refresh note keep their width, so they no longer spill over the player count beside them.
