date: 2026-10-16
title: Commodity demand counts every input plants actually use
summary: Global demand for industrial inputs again matches the units plants consume and pay for.
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
tags: [economy, commodities]
---

Global demand for steel, iron, coal, oil, natural gas, fertilizers and other industrial inputs had been drifting below what plants actually consume. Plants were still buying their full inputs, but the market only counted part of that purchase as demand, so several inputs showed a surplus and falling prices that did not reflect real use. Global demand now counts the same input units each plant is charged for, so Top consumers and the global demand figure agree and market balance reflects real consumption. Expect demand for affected inputs to rise and some markets that showed a surplus to show a shortage again.
