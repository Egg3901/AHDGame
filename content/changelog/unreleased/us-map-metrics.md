The US map now separates Politics and Metrics. Browse or search state GDP, GDP per capita, employment, income, demographic indicators and policy scores by category. Compare states, inspect distributions, sort the selected metric and show values directly on the map. A dedicated electoral-vote layer displays the current apportionment independently of presidential results.
