date: 2026-10-16
title: NPP plants choose production methods for their actual industry
summary: Vehicle and entertainment plants no longer select recipes from their parent manufacturing or media categories.
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
tags: [economy, corporations, npp]
---

Computer-run vehicle and entertainment plants now choose production methods for the industry they actually operate in. They no longer pick metal, electronics or news-broadcast recipes that belong to a different industry. Existing tech requirements, retool timing and paid-capacity accounting still apply. Correcting previously stored mismatched recipes is separate work.
