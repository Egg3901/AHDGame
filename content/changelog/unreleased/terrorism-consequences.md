- Preparedness now distinguishes prevented, limited and catastrophic attacks without scheduling a historical replay.
- Military responses require attribution. Coalition members independently choose deployments, civilian support or neutrality.
- Emergency authority and military commitments affect national spending and political conditions until governments repeal powers or withdraw forces.
- Civilian responses and reconstruction can end intervention blowback; unresolved networks can return after normalization.
