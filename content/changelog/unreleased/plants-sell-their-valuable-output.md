## What changed

- A plant that makes more than one product now sets its output by what each product is worth to buyers at market prices. A hardware plant that sells all of its electronics into a shortage climbs back toward full output, even while the software it also makes is oversupplied.
- A plant whose main product is the oversupplied one still runs below capacity, so plants do not run flat out into a glut.
- Energy, oil, iron and natural gas demand was understated by a correction applied twice. These markets read as oversupplied at every level of supply, so their producers could never sell everything they made. They now show the demand buyers have, and producers can grow into it.
- Room to build reads 0 while your own plant in that market is running below capacity because of sales. Your idle capacity reaches buyers before new capacity would. The build dialog says so.
- Unclaimed share shows the part of the market nobody has built into. It read 0% everywhere.
- Room to build for energy, oil, iron, natural gas, advertising and healthcare no longer counts demand the market does not clear.
