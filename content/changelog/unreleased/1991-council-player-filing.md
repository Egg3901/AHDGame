Players can file into a bound first-Council subject ballot from their home macroregion. Filing checks the frozen ballot, ratified mandate, registered association, deadline and incompatible Duma or Council mandates.

An association may register two individuals. A player can replace a bounded automatic nominee without removing another player or changing the NPC profile's account. Independent nominating groups remain separate. Nominee replacement, player entry and the filing receipt commit together, including party changes and concurrent filings. Automatic opening and chamber handover remain in progress.
