## What changed

- Live and online dots no longer pulse or ping, and stay still next to their label.
- Page headers no longer show large faded letters, rotated stamps or soft glows behind the title, and labels over photos use solid backgrounds instead of frosted glass.
- Cards and buttons no longer lift or glow on hover, and the cabinet office now uses the same fonts as the rest of the game.
