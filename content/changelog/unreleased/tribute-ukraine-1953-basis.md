## What changed

- Ukraine's 1953 GDP is authored in Soviet-ruble millions. The tribute calculation converted it with Ukraine's era-neutral exchange rate. It now uses the 1953 Soviet-ruble rate of 9 rubles to the dollar, the same as Russia, Belarus and the Baltic republics, so the Pact's yearly tribute from Ukraine is 0.75% of its GDP.
- Only worlds that begin at the 1953 preset are affected, because only they levy tribute.
