title: Commodity prices explain what moved them
category: corporations
date: 2026-09-12
---

Commodity market pages and corporation input/output details now carry an exact breakdown of the
global price. It separates general inflation, lasting scarcity, current supply and demand,
producer input costs, gradual adjustment, and explicit price controls. The components always add
back to the displayed global price change.

The sector balance audit now checks every operating strategy at balanced prices and under bounded
input and output shocks. It distinguishes a genuinely impossible recipe from a healthy strategy
that is merely exposed to a severe market shock.
