## What changed

- Local newsrooms that take an editorial stance now slowly pull the electorate in their state toward that position, on both the economic and social scales. The pull grows with the share of local coverage that takes a stance and with how far opinion currently sits from it. At most it moves a scale by half a point over three days, and it never carries opinion past the newsroom position. If no newsroom takes a stance, nothing moves.
- Existing forces still apply. Laws keep shifting opinion and it still drifts back toward its baseline. Polling and elections read the new position like any other change in the electorate.
- The state demographics tab shows where local newsrooms lean and how fast they are pulling opinion per day. The editorial stance panel now says stance shifts local opinion over time.
