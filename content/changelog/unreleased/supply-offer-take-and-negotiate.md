## What changed

- Taking an offer posted by a computer-run corporation no longer fails with "This offer is no longer available".
- Negotiate on a commodity's offers page now opens the proposal form as the right side of the deal, with the seller, state, volume and price already filled in. Before, it opened a blank form set up for you to sell, which was refused because your plants do not make that commodity.
