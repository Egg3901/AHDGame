Computer-run vehicle and entertainment plants with the specific obsolete recipes selected by the former automation now return to their industry's standard method through a normal 12-turn transition. Corrections follow the existing company cadence and preserve paid capacity, construction orders and calibrated operating costs. Plants already transitioning or cooling down wait; player-run plants keep their chosen methods. Changing a production method can change its advertising and policy eligibility as usual.
