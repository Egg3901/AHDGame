The first Duma ballot model contains 225 constituency elections and a separate 225-seat national party list. Constituencies use individual district identifiers within the game's ten Russian regions. List mandates follow the five-percent threshold and exact proportional remainders; every player remains limited to one seat, with bounded NPC slates representing additional list candidates.

Opening and certification preserve Congress and its officeholders. Failed constituencies remain vacancies, invalid owners cannot receive mandates, and a failed transaction cannot partially certify the cohort. The election runtime and certified office handover are still being connected on this feature branch.
