title: Economies no longer read as overheated while companies are still being founded
summary: >-
  The growth signal behind the output gap now strips out inflation and ignores
  the jump from an almost empty economy to its first companies. Before this,
  every region looked permanently overheated, which pushed unemployment too low
  and added to inflation. Output gaps and unemployment will drift back toward
  normal over the following turns.
