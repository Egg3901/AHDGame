- The UK wiki overview now says Commons seats are allocated by largest remainder in every era, with a 10% qualifying gate. It previously described a pre-1999 winner's bonus that no longer exists and a 20% gate.
- Granting a central bank independence by law now says rate-setting passes to the bank's governor. Only the US Federal Reserve has a rate committee, so the news post and bill editor no longer promise one elsewhere.
