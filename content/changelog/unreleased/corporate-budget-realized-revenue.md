Corporation operating budgets now use the same daily revenue figure in the CEO statement and the settings check. The 150% limit follows realized sales, includes freight credits, converts revenue into the corporation's currency, and uses the history-based fallback for sectors without a realized-sales record. Existing budgets can still be reduced after revenue falls.
