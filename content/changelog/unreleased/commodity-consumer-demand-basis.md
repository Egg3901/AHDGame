The Top consumers list on commodity pages and the consumption figures on corporation commodity tabs now apply the same input-demand correction the market ledger uses. Before this, per-corporation demand ignored the correction, so the listed consumers could add up to more than the global demand shown in the header. Rankings and totals now reconcile. Market prices, production and settlement are unchanged.
