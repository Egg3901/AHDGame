Romania's later parliamentary handover updates chamber capacities, held mandates, financial owners' seat counters and the government majority threshold together. A failed or competing attempt cannot leave a partly resized Parliament.

Players retain at most one parliamentary mandate. Existing NPC owners retain their shared accounts and balances, and the prime minister keeps their executive office. Missing financial owners, duplicate player seats or insufficient party slate capacity prevent an inconsistent handover.
