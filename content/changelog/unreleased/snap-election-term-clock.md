## What changed

- A snap election now resets the parliamentary term clock permanently. Each Parliament after the snap runs a full term from the previous election, so the Parliament after the next one no longer stretches to eight years.
- Japan and Germany now show the correct year on the first general election after a snap, matching the United Kingdom.
- If a scheduled date has already passed after a long pause, the next election falls back to the regular calendar instead of never opening.
