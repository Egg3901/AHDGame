## What changed

- When healthcare services or entertainment services stay far above base price, computer-controlled firms now treat the producing sector as a priority place to found or expand, so supply can catch up instead of the shortage persisting for years.
- Entertainment firms answer whichever of advertising or entertainment services is shorter.
- The response switches itself off once the price falls back under the existing critical-shortage bar, and the existing per-turn and logistics limits still apply.
