badges: [minor]

Hungarian deputies can propose the 1994 electoral amendment from the legislature page when its date arrives. A quorate two-thirds parliamentary vote must approve the stricter five-percent list threshold and expanded candidate lists. Rejected bills retain their history and can be revised.

The amendment can apply to an existing primary before general ballots are cast. Ongoing general elections, second rounds and certified results retain their frozen rules. Eligible NPC governments may introduce the bill through the same votes, with a recorded reason.
