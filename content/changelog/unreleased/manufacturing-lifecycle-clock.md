title: Manufacturing products keep progressing after development is paid
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine, backend]
tags: [manufacturing, products]
---

Manufacturing product projects advance from completed turns even when no new
R&D cash is paid. Fully funded projects can finish their development period,
and launched products progress through growth, maturity, decline and retirement.
Paid development receipts retain a separate acknowledgment so retries cannot
count cash twice or clear an interrupted project update prematurely.
