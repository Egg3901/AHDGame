## What changed

- The header shows the country, the year and turn, and the overall condition as a large figure with its status word and what it measures. The change over the last snapshot and the past year, the number of critical metrics, and the strongest and weakest category sit just below it.
- Governance style shows political direction and democratic health on plain scales with one marker each. Democratic health turns amber once it is low enough to cost growth, borrowing and votes, and red at failed-state level. The balance of power is a list of figures with what each one measures.
- The nine category cards are now one table with each category's score, status, and strongest and weakest metric. Click a metric name to open it directly, or Open to see the whole category. Only strained, weak and critical statuses are coloured.
- The category and metric pages use the same plain sections and tables, and now show how much the score changed since the last snapshot.
- On a phone the tables fold so nothing scrolls sideways, except the country comparison, which says when to scroll.
