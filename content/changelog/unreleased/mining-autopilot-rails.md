## What changed

- A resource priced at 1.6 times its base price or more now counts as short for mining companies, the same bar new companies use when deciding what to found. Rare earth, iron, coal, oil and natural gas all qualify today.
- A new mine can open in a region that already has a mine of another kind, as long as the region holds the resource and no mine there already focuses on it.
- A mine sitting on a deposit it cannot extract now switches to the least costly resource its region does hold, even if every option loses money, because producing something beats producing nothing. If the region holds nothing to mine, the mine is shut down instead and stays shut.
- Changes stay gradual: a few switches, openings and shutdowns per turn.
