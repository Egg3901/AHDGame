title: Deliver own supply without blocking surplus sales
summary: Mixed-output mines keep selling usable coal when a byproduct cannot be sold, and own-first supply can use another mine when the nearest haul is limited.
tags: [corporations, economy, logistics]
badges: [patch]
areas: [engine]
---

Own-first cross-state supply now keeps alternative origins available until goods are actually shipped. Partial or blocked hauls leave the remaining demand for another reachable own supplier. Company output and plant demand are shared across those routes so goods cannot be reserved twice, and remaining surplus stays available to ordinary market buyers.

Each output now follows its own measured delivery placement. Unsold timber during a mine's retooling no longer wipes out its coal offer. Freight capacity, transport charges, internal discounts, and existing supply agreements still apply.
