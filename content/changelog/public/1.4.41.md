## Contracted haulage

- **Freight is back in the agreement form.** Pick Freight & Transportation, then the state the contract is fulfilled from. Only states where you have plants producing freight are offered.
- **Fulfilled where it is based.** Freight is haulage capacity in one state, so the contract reserves your capacity there for the buyer's plants in that state. It cannot reserve Texas trucks for a New York factory.
- **Same rules as every other contract.** Price band, volume cap against your plants in that state, shortfall damages and cancellation notice all apply. The agreement card shows the state.
