## Specializations

Finish either branch of a decade's lane and a new tier opens: three
specializations, each with its own capstone technology.

- **Scale** cuts costs: cheaper inputs, lower wages, cheaper growth.
- **Premium** raises what your output sells for and strengthens your brand.
- **Resilience** shields you from dominance penalties and tariffs and makes
  expansion cheaper.

You can take exactly one per decade. The other two lock, and switching means
abandoning the decade, so a corporation now has a build, not a checklist.
Across the two lanes and three specializations there are six distinct ways
to play every decade in every industry: 1,020 new technologies in total.

## Effects that do something

The new tier leans on effects wired straight into the plants economy: a new
realised-price bonus, input cost cuts, output increases, labor savings,
expansion discounts, and penalty shields. This builds on the margin fix in
1.1.8.

## Pacing

Specializations cost double and capstones triple a decade's base research
cost, so a well-funded corporation now finishes its chosen path deep into the
decade rather than in the first year or two. Idle research points late in a
decade should be rare.

## Your progress is safe

- Everything you have researched stays researched, at the price you paid.
- Past decades still grant their baseline technologies free to new
  corporations, but not the specializations. Those were choices, and history
  does not make choices for you.
- Computer-run corporations pick specializations under the same rules you do.
