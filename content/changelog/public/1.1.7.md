  If regulators revoke your bank's charter for staying undercapitalized, your
  cash reserves and your trading book now return to your corporation instead of

- **Losing a charter no longer loses your money.** When the supervisor revokes a
  bank charter for missing the recapitalization deadline, the bank's cash and its
  trading book now come back to your corporation, the same as any other charter
  revoke. Investment banks, whose value is in their trading book rather than in
  deposits, were the ones hit hardest by the old behavior. Banks that still owe
  depositors pay those depositors first, as before.
