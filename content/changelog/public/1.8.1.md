Historical setup fills missing records across eras and avoids repeating work.
Local startup handles the desktop client startup path more reliably.

## Underground union balance

Banned unions can organize through quiet cell work or louder mass drives.
Both spend action points without spending the frozen treasury, and organizers
see a broad dark, suspected or exposed risk state. Repealing a ban converts
underground strength into legal strength at **half value**. A fresh ban clears
the old underground trail.

## Supporter accounts

Patreon membership matching uses linked provider account IDs. Unlinked
supporters are reported for review while retaining their existing grants.
