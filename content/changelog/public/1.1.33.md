## The fix that did nothing, now does something

The recent change that lets computer-run firms retool toward scarce goods could
only see shortages in mined resources. For everything else — fertilizer above
all — the scarcity signal was blank, so no firm ever retooled, and fertilizer
stayed expensive while farms ran throttled and unprofitable.

That signal is now wired up for every commodity. Chemical plants will retool to
fertilizer where it is short, fertilizer supply grows over the following turns,
and the fertilizer squeeze on farming eases — the relief the last update was
supposed to bring.
