*Dev diary by the Lakeside Games team. Published 9 October 2026, covering the original release and the subsequent 1.13 work.*

## TL;DR

- New: Direct supply contracts, rebuilt presidential campaigns, weekly contests and an expanded Discord casino.
- Fixed: 1991 incomes and budgets, campaign estimates, founding controls and the mobile sign-out label.
- Still broken: Sovereign financing needs more qualification; earlier missed bond payments are not proven recovered.

## The story

A company short of steel used to look for a seller in Discord. A presidential candidate could see a national total without a clear picture of the counties behind it. Between turns, prices and election maps waited for the hour to finish. We spent the 1.13 period working on those gaps together.

That became a broader overhaul than the initial Supply Exchange release. We rebuilt trading, campaign views and primaries, added competitions, repaired the 1991 economic foundations and changed the world's clock. The common thread was making the activity already happening in the world easier to see and participate in.

We also worked underneath those screens. Settlement now batches more of its payments and records enough progress to recover safely from an interruption. The complete implementation record remains in the developer changelog. This post covers the changes that matter when returning to play; availability depends on deployment and world settings.

## If you run a company

**The Market and Supply Exchange.** Stocks, bonds, funds, sectors, commodities, supply deals and currencies came together on one page. Commodity offers show volume, term, country, credit rating and price relative to market. Companies can take part or all of an offer, and computer-run companies offer spare capacity and seek unmet inputs. They can accept purchases with discounts no deeper than 10 percent. Contracts give supply priority and a price adjustment, rather than a guaranteed fixed price. We restored the exchange picker and exchange-size comparison panel, and old stock-market links now open the matching Market view. We restored the founding control and the link to an existing company in the market header after the new page omitted them.

**Products and media.** Companies gained named manufactured lines and media titles. Funding takes about three days; a hit lifts applicable revenue by 10 to 20 percent for another three days. Events ask for decisions along the way. Newsroom alignment can make political advertising up to 15 percent cheaper and more persuasive in aligned states. Editorial influence on state opinion depends on the world enabling that feature.

**Competition and company identities.** Shortages and concentrated markets attract more computer-run entrants and expansion. Vehicle makers became manufacturing companies on a vehicles line, while entertainment businesses became media companies on an entertainment line. The identity conversion preserves plants, workers, balances and holdings. Ordinary manufacturing plants can retool into vehicle assembly.

**Fund investing.** We moved buying and redemption beneath the fund header and separated long holdings lists into pages. CEOs can invest company cash in funds; those holdings contribute their current value to the balance sheet. Corporate redemptions need available, unreserved fund cash. Otherwise the units remain invested.

**Research.** We restored branching technology paths and their decade illustrations. The screen now explains free baseline grants, prerequisites, funding gaps and the consequences of committing to a track or specialization. Research costs and effects stayed the same.

## If you run for office

**The presidential page.** We rebuilt it around a full-screen electoral map, county zoom, in-page state views, phone layouts and a road-to-270 strip. Campaign presence appears alongside results. Completed general-election campaigns can replay week by week when their history was recorded.

**Campaigns and primaries.** Field offices and county targeting made campaign presence more local. Presidential primaries gained stronger regional variation, momentum, county results and a primary-night closing window. Shared campaign work also improved other race types. Income previews now use the race's size, currency and era prices, with current effects separated from the next upgrade. Payments and upgrade prices stayed the same.

**Party and chamber work.** Party capacity grows with membership and tapers as parties become larger. The country ceiling is now six NPPs per region, with a minimum of 25: 306 in the seeded US and 72 in the UK. The sixth local recruitment slot opens at 50 percent organization; active-member requirements and costs still apply. Registration drives require local presence. Independent candidates remain in their races when joining a party, and withdrawal remains final for that race. Contingent presidential elections can continue after a House deadlock, with coalition whips and an acting president when needed.

## If you run a country or follow the economy

**The 1991 foundations.** Nigeria, China, Turkey and seven Western European countries received household incomes in 1991 currency. Opening budgets were repaired for Brazil, Germany, China and Nigeria; Russia's regional grants sit inside its budget envelope. Company sizes use country GDP. We also corrected demand, freight and valuation rules that distorted the opening economy. Bills replacing public programs now show their current and proposed annual costs captured with the proposal, retained obligations and separate one-time transition costs.

**The clock.** Share prices update every 15 minutes. At half past the hour, elections, exchange rates, inflation and growth take half of the coming turn's step; the hourly turn takes the rest. The total hourly movement stays the same. Earnings, campaign spending and race closure still settle on the hour. A separate price countdown shows the next refresh, and views can update without resetting their selected state. Scheduled updates also record their completed, skipped or interrupted outcomes for diagnosis.

**Settlement.** Treasury, bonds, funds and corporate taxes settle with fewer individual writes. The corrected settlement steps record payments once, including on retry. State-enterprise work, coupons and capital grants no longer stop the corporation turn.

## If you follow contests or play in Discord

Weekly contests cover corporate growth, National Influence, approval, referrals, sponsored bills, personal wealth growth and party membership growth. Most prize pools split 50, 30 and 20 percent across the podium; approval remains winner takes all. Entries show linked portraits and logos, and results appear in the news. New rounds converge on the running weekly window.

The Discord casino added slots, roulette, crash, craps, high-low, races, lottery and poker alongside blackjack. Games share a bank across home currencies. Stakes can reach 12.5 percent of the bank and payouts 25 percent; stakes return when the bank cannot cover a win.

Migration review also found that an interrupted retry could add the same spare market capacity twice. The table records verified staging dates separately from historical dates we could not establish. ## Still broken

Sovereign financing remains deferred pending testing in a copy of the live world and recovery review. Earlier missed bond payments are not proven recovered. Completed races need stored history for replay, and editorial influence depends on world settings. Some historical reporter credits and individual live dates are still missing from the release record.

## What is next

Persistent party organization, qualified sovereign financing and closer links between campaigns, media and corporate power are possible next steps. They are not confirmed 1.14 scope, and there is no release date to announce for them.
