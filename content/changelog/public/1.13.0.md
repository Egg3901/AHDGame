The 1.13 arc overhauls the economy, politics, pacing and competitive activities. It brings together direct supply contracting, weekly contests, an expanded Discord casino, rebuilt presidential maps and campaigns, stronger primaries and a faster turn system. This post covers the original release and subsequent 1.13 work; availability still depends on deployment to your world and feature settings.

## Markets and the Supply Exchange

1.13 adds The Market, one page for everything you can buy or trade. The market cap chart leads the page and can be collapsed. Tabs cover stocks, bonds, funds, sectors for sale, commodities, supply deals and currencies. Supply deals lists open offers across every commodity, with company logos and CEO portraits. You can filter it by players or NPPs, by offering or seeking, and by commodity, and each offer shows its volume and estimated value per turn.

It also opens the Supply Exchange. Until now, finding someone to sell you energy or buy your steel meant asking around in Discord. Now every commodity has an offers page listing sell offers and buy requests, with volume, price against market, term, the company's country and its credit rating. Take an offer in one step, for all of its volume or part of it, and the contract starts that turn. Your sector inputs panel tells you when sellers are offering your costliest input.

NPP companies take part. They post standing offers from spare capacity and unmet demand, marked as NPP, and they accept your buy proposals when they have capacity to spare and the discount is no deeper than 10%. Contracts still buy you priority when supply is short and a price up to 35% above or below market. They do not lock in a fixed price.

Contracts are safer, too. You can amend a pending offer instead of cancelling it, and you are told when a contract is accepted, taken, started, cancelled or expires. Companies with the same owner, the same CEO, or a 5% stake in each other cannot contract with each other, and no contract can cross an embargo or the iron curtain.

## Economic foundations

The 1991 world gets steadier foundations. Nigeria, China, Turkey and seven Western European countries open with household incomes in 1991 currency, every populated region has a real population that changes through births, deaths and migration, and Brazil, Germany, China and Nigeria keep budgets sized to their economies. Share prices no longer count the era's free technology as company value. Bond issues no longer inflate financial services demand, and plants that sell out in short markets ramp up faster. Freight bills now follow each sector's share of state demand. Logistics sectors no longer pay the sprawl penalty.

## Turn processing and pacing

Markets and elections now move between turns. Share prices are recalculated every 15 minutes from what changed during the hour: company cash, bonds, interest rates, exchange rates, trading and sentiment. Earnings still update on the turn. The market cap chart draws a candle for each 15-minute update on ranges up to a year, and the stock market page refreshes on its own. At half past each hour, election results, exchange rates, inflation and growth take half of the coming turn's step and the turn takes the rest, so each hour moves exactly as far as before, in two steps. Races still close on the hour and campaign money is still spent hourly. Next to the turn countdown, a second countdown shows when prices next refresh, and clicking either opens the status page. Economy, budget and central-bank figures, currency conversions, company values, portfolios and election maps refresh between turns without resetting your selected views.

Turns run faster and more reliably. Treasury, bond, index fund and corporate tax steps settle in fewer writes. A turn interrupted mid-step resumes without repeating payments, and state enterprises, coupons and capex grants can no longer stop the corporation turn.

## Politics and accounts

Politics and accounts: Japan's proportional seats are allocated correctly, party mergers require neighboring presence, withdrawing from a race keeps you out of it, and new parties see their regional registration pools. You can change your account email, sign in with the Discord app on Android, and choose between a modern and a classic interface. The tutorial covers the 1991 world.

## Products and media

Products and media: fund a named media title or manufactured line for about three days. A hit lifts revenue 10 to 20 percent for three days across the sectors it applies to, and a flop costs only what you spent. Each product meets one or two random events that ask for a decision. Your plants decide which lines you can make. Newsroom slant now makes political ads up to 15 percent cheaper, and more persuasive, in states whose local press leans close to you. Buyers of coverage advertising now pick a media corporation from a list. Vehicle plants can switch strategy again, and ordinary manufacturing plants can retool into vehicle assembly. Vehicle makers are manufacturing corporations on the vehicles line, and entertainment companies are media corporations on the entertainment line in every era. Founding, expansion and sector policies use those lines directly; the identity conversion preserves balances, plants, workers and shareholdings.

## Presidential maps, campaigns and primaries

Presidential races gain a full-screen map, county zoom, a road-to-270 strip, in-page state views, phone layouts and campaign presence. Completed races with recorded history can replay the general campaign week by week. Primary results have stronger regional variation and a primary-night closing window. Field offices can be placed from a county lean map, with campaign improvements for other races too.

Contingent presidential elections keep voting after a House deadlock, allow party and coalition whips, and identify an acting president when necessary. Chamber lists show seals, portraits and linked nomination cards. Majority banners count filled seats, Supreme Court nominees support Senate whips, and a first national bill earns its achievement outside the US. Independent candidates remain in their race when joining a party. Reviewed tax bills preserve selected rates. Campaign operation income previews use the race size, campaign currency and era prices, and distinguish current effects from the next upgrade; payments and upgrade prices are unchanged.

Party NPP capacity grows with membership, tapering as parties get larger, with a ceiling of three NPPs per country region and a minimum ceiling of 25. Registration drives require local party presence. The 1991 Soviet Congress can hold elections.

## Corporate competition and finance

Supply offers show unit prices, open proposal forms beside their controls, and support taking or negotiating computer-run offers. Contract premiums use one turn of actual buyer volume. Parent CEOs can inspect subsidiaries' sector books; company pages explain ownership, arrears, product funding and closure reasons. Retooling plants use their blended production basis, and older profit peaks fade from valuations. Spin-offs retain baseline technology, while larger subsidiary groups receive a valuation discount.

Shortages attract more computer-run entrants and expansion, including bounded state-enterprise growth. Competitors target concentrated sectors. Eligible product ventures can be funded from operating income with a cash buffer, and media ventures advance each turn. Editorial stance can gradually affect state opinion where the feature is enabled. Half of plant overhead follows built capacity, and service payroll and home-sector profit bonuses are adjusted. New 1991 company sizes use country GDP; Russia's opening budget includes regional grants within its envelope. Energy productivity ramps over a game year, power plants require less machinery, and deeper shortages can command higher producer prices.

Equity trading spreads narrow and stocks have a direct Trade shortcut. Bond fixes address bank purchases, large repayments, rollover accounting and unfunded Treasury maturities. New primary placements skip due and overdue bonds to preserve repayment inventory. These fixes do not imply that previously missed payments were recovered.

## Weekly contests

Weekly contests cover corporate growth, National Influence, approval and referrals. Rounds open on first visit, and iteration referrals use the iteration's start and the same eligibility checks as weekly referrals. The contests also include enacted sponsored bills, personal wealth growth excluding received wires and loan principal, and party membership growth under the same chair. Most weekly prizes split 50/30/20 across the top three; government approval remains winner takes all. Entries show linked logos and portraits, and weekly results appear in the news with notifications for ranked entrants. New rounds share the running weekly window, so they settle together. The leading iteration referrers can earn Supporter.

## Discord casino

Discord casino games share a bank across home currencies. Stakes are returned when the bank cannot cover a win; the latest limits allow a stake up to 12.5% of the bank and a payout up to 25%. Slots, roulette, crash, craps, high-low, races, lottery and poker join blackjack.

## Reliability

Turn work reads less payment history and batches eligible corporate receipts, bank work, fund quotes, government repayments and Treasury updates. Fund rebalancing is spread across turns. Additional fixes address regional statistics, targeted advertisements, duplicate media entry and whip-message delivery. The mobile account menu shows a translated sign-out label instead of a raw translation key. Dependency and build maintenance accompanies these changes.
