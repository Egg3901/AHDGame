1.10 brings together the changes released since 1.9. It changes how parties
expand, how votes become seats, how failing institutions affect the economy,
and what profitable companies are worth. The 1991 world also gets a substantial
historical correction pass, from its population and currencies to the political
decisions that shape Europe.

## Election and government balance

- **UK Commons eligibility falls from 20% to 10%.** The party-pooled seat
  threshold is lower in ordinary and snap elections. Structural support now
  follows a party's current regional registration, removing the advantage
  inherited from its starting-era share.
- **US House eligibility follows delegation size.** The threshold remains 20%
  in states with one to four seats, gradually falls for larger delegations,
  and reaches 10% at nine seats. Projections and final results use the same rule.
- **Presidential campaigning has a smaller ceiling.** Under the active v3
  rules, campaign strength approaches a maximum 25% vote boost instead of
  100%. State lean is no longer applied a second time after votes are allocated.
  Incumbent approval blends national and state approval equally. During the
  closing period, 5% of new ballots for candidates outside the local top two
  move toward the nearest viable candidate. Ballots already cast keep their
  recorded totals; older v1 and v2 races retain their rules.
- **Institutional failure costs votes and growth.** The ruling-party penalty
  from low Democratic Health doubles, and a sitting President can face a
  combined penalty of up to 30% at complete failure. Below 60 health, the
  economy loses up to four percentage points of annual potential GDP growth.
  That loss reaches employment and tax bases, and election screens explain it.
- **Snap elections reset the term clock permanently.** Later parliaments
  follow the new schedule rather than reverting to the original calendar.
  Dissolution vacates outgoing MPs, and election retries preserve the correct
  winners, offices and presidential terms.
- **Past presidential results stay historical.** Previous and next links work
  again, and completed races show the result recorded at resolution rather
  than being recalculated against today's political map. Wiki history also
  labels snap elections and shows the correct game year.

Non-player politicians now fill recurring direct-election contests across the
supported election families, reducing races that reopened without candidates.
Divergent Supreme Court justices also receive a much lower departure risk:
roughly **0.1% per active turn instead of 1.5%**, calibrated to a fifteen-year
median after the protected tenure period. Historical departure dates retain
their separate rules.

## Parties grow from their existing base

Recruiting or relocating a politician now follows a party's growth frontier:
regions where it already has a member, politician or officeholder, and their
neighbours. Player membership follows the same regional presence rule, with an
exception for players who cannot be placed anywhere on the frontier. A party
with no presence anywhere can still start from any region. Recruitment and
relocation pickers explain which regions are reachable.

UK parties can reach Major status after qualifying in three regions. That is
three of the UK's twelve regions; other countries retain their one-third rule.

Party, state-party and caucus treasuries with two different seated officers can
pay a member **five times the base per-turn limit**. One person holding two
seats still counts as one officer, and each treasury earns its own increase.
Any two different officers can provide the two approvals a transaction needs.
Approved transfers pay once even when officers approve together or a request
is retried.

Anyone can contribute to a presidential candidate's campaign strength again,
and the control is usable on a phone.

## Corporate earnings and market balance

- **The earnings ramp arrives sooner and grows further.** Operating revenue
  ramps to a **20% boost**, replacing the originally planned 15%. It begins
  at turn 1213 and reaches its target over 192 turns. Financial sectors keep
  their additional 10% revenue multiplier.
- **Valuations follow earnings sooner.** The valuation ramp starts at turn
  1253, forty turns after the revenue ramp, and also lasts 192 turns. The
  existing targets remain **30% higher sector NPV** and **50% higher bank
  income NPV**, with the financial risk premium easing from 6% to 4%.
  These are gradual multipliers, so the full increase does not arrive at once.
- **More autonomous investors buy equities.** Computer-controlled investors
  participate more broadly, while cash, share inventory and fund reserves
  still constrain what they can buy.
- **An IPO lists the whole selected float immediately.** Every available
  share can be bought without waiting for a market-pool placement. The
  corporation receives proceeds as buyers pay. Old pending issuances no
  longer block new proposals.
- **Building respects national dominance.** Plant expansion now applies the
  same national market-dominance checks as the other investment paths.
- **Critical shortages can attract new suppliers.** Automated corporations
  can close essential supply gaps, with clearer demand, capacity and
  investment accounting.

The stock market gains candlestick charts using recorded intraday prices,
comparison lines with aligned timestamps, and a layout that stays readable on
mobile. Corporation Financials and market views give clearer labels and
consistent historical currency values.

## Banking and investments

The Banking page now presents private and central banks in sortable tables,
with your balances, bank leaders, rates and direct deposit or loan actions.
Bank CEOs get a console built around the next turn's outlook and the controls
that can change it.

Savings withdrawals work again, including balances still linked to a private
bank that has been removed. Subscribing to or redeeming a fund preserves the
original order when a response is lost, so retrying does not create a second
order. Funds preserve their cash reserves when quoting liquidity bids and
complete backed subscriptions and redemptions consistently.

Interrupted bank transfers, line-of-credit payments, central-bank facilities,
reserve transfers and interest payments recover the original settlement.
Sovereign bond placements deliver proceeds and record the corresponding debt
together. Corporate operating losses, fund dividends, treasury accrual and
rounded bond payments now leave matching cash records.

## A more faithful 1991 world

- **Era-correct institutions.** Historical worlds use the selected era's
  country roster, policies, executives and chamber sizes. The 1991 Commons
  starts from the 1987 result on its historical seat map; Japan's Diet has
  the era's seat allocation and a populated upper house. The US executive
  is seated in 1991 and 2019 worlds, and countries absent from an era are
  consistently excluded.
- **Dated population inputs.** China, Nigeria, France, Spain, Sweden and
  Turkey receive reconciled 1991 populations and regional allocations.
  France and Spain also start with age and sex population stocks. Background
  country estimates regain the variation lost in their previous sampling.
- **Money stays in its historical units.** German marks, Irish pounds and
  euro displays convert the amount together with the denomination. 1991 GDP
  uses the era's exchange rates, and East German and Soviet income benchmarks
  restore profile loading and campaign income.
- **Campaign costs and income scale together.** New historical worlds apply
  the same era basis to action prices, manager upgrades, maintenance and
  income. Existing worlds keep their configured pricing.
- **European integration is decided by governments.** The European Community
  continues until members ratify Maastricht. Domestic negotiating mandates
  are distinct from treaty ratification and membership. Eligible legislatures
  can propose euro adoption; membership fixes the conversion relationship
  and connects common monetary policy to national financial accounts.
- **Devolution needs an enacted settlement.** UK governments can debate it
  from the 1979 decision window, including in 1991. Regional executives and
  their elections follow the institutions established or abolished by law.
- **A clean political start is available.** The 1991 reset can leave
  player-enabled countries without seeded parties or political officeholders,
  ready for player-created parties, while keeping the era's economy and
  institutions.

The 2019 budgets for Spain, Italy, France, Sweden, Turkey, Greece, Austria and
Finland now use explicit 2019 inflation, wages, growth and debt assumptions
rather than carrying older figures under a rewritten year label.

Contemporary worlds also get corrected 2027 party rosters and fresh opening
states for seven living crises.

## Crises have lasting consequences

Northern Ireland peace now needs public ratification after parliamentary
approval. The settlement and any suspension affect devolved authority,
security spending and regional politics.

Yugoslav displacement and war damage reach labour, GDP and background output;
reconstruction restores capacity. Military intervention needs readiness,
logistics and domestic support, and coordinated arms sanctions restrict actual
trade. Crisis participants remain distinct, and decisions open on the correct
phase and recurring schedule.

Financial rescue, guarantees, stimulus and austerity affect banks, households
and treasuries, with parliamentary approval for sovereign responses. Euro-area
contagion follows membership and holdings. Pandemic policy, counterterrorism,
European security choices and Arab uprisings also retain their economic and
political consequences. Arab regional paths respond to local choices between
reform, repression, transition and war, including displacement at origin and
destination.

## Alliances and underground unions

Countries can found their own geopolitical Bloc, choose its map colour and
build a new alignment pole. Influence, accession, defection, strain and Bloc
resolutions work around that new pole.

**Article 5 is a mutual-defence commitment for any Security or Bloc
organisation.** An attack on a member brings the other eligible members into
the war on its side. Wars no longer end merely because a front briefly returns
to the defender's starting line, and armed blocs can vote on protecting a new
member in an existing war.

Banned unions gain a fuller underground contest: limited slowdowns, wildcat
resistance and organizing drives face investigation, raids and prosecution.
Stronger enforcement costs labour approval. Detection scales with activity,
organizers face action limits, and exposed cells and organizers cannot evade
restrictions through another union action. Cell information stays private,
and raids and turn retries preserve their original fines and outcomes.

## Maps, sign-in and everyday play

- The US map adds state inspection, seated officeholders, political and
  economic layers, electoral votes and a sortable data view. Background
  countries have distinct borders and labelled macro summaries.
- Navigation search restores autocomplete and adds a results page. Profile
  switching, the mobile menu and Actions alignment are clearer.
- Sign in with Apple can create, sign into or link an account. Apple, Google
  and Discord sign-in return to the game more reliably.
- Profile campaign songs open in YouTube's visible player when you press
  Play. They no longer autoplay, and the phone app hides them.
- Race entry asks for confirmation. Election, banking, market and news
  screens use clearer prices, labels and game dates. UK ministers can hold
  Deputy Prime Minister or First Secretary alongside a departmental office.
- The phone app hides browser-only purchase links and the unsupported Lakeside
  sign-in option.
- Opted-in analytics and authenticated player surveys help identify confusing
  flows and game errors. The older OpenReplay integration is removed.
- Turns and pages do less repeated work: election tallies, politician
  decisions, corporate histories, fund processing and status polling read
  smaller records and reuse shared results. Long-running worlds receive the
  indexes those reads need, and market updates use bounded recent history.
