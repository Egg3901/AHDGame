## What changed

- **The margin you see is the margin you keep.** The financials and CEO tabs
  now show net margin over every cost, upkeep included. A plant that was
  reading 30 percent while losing money now reads negative.
- **Fund units count.** Buying into an index fund no longer drops you down the
  wealth list. Units are valued at the fund's quoted NAV.
- **No more one-company funds.** An index fund caps any single name at a fifth
  of the fund and spreads the rest.
- **Profit cannot exceed revenue.** Policy credits are capped at the costs they
  offset. The public API now reports the same profit as the corporation page.
