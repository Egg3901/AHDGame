## NPC industry follows the money now

When a commodity runs short, its price rises — and until today only mining
NPCs could actually respond by retooling. Everyone else kept making whatever
they were founded to make, forever, no matter what the market begged for.

Now computer-run corporations in every industry re-evaluate their production
strategy against real prices. A chemical plant making pharmaceuticals while
fertilizer trades at multiples of normal will retool to fertilizer. Retooling
takes time — a transition period with reduced margins, and a cooldown so
NPCs do not flip-flop — and the pace is capped so the economy shifts rather
than lurches.

What this means for the current squeeze: Western fertilizer and freight
capacity starts growing this week, input costs for farms ease as it comes
online, and shortages become opportunities that get answered — by NPCs
slowly, or by you first, if you move faster than they do.

Your own sectors are never touched: strategy remains entirely your call.
