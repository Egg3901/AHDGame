## Essentials get built now

Some goods are needed by nearly every industry but produced by only one.
Freight is the clearest case: manufacturing, farming, mining, chemicals,
carmakers and retail all consume it, but only logistics companies make it. When
freight ran critically short, computer-run firms still would not build it unless
they were already logistics firms — so the shortage just sat there, taxing
everyone's costs and choking deliveries.

Now, when an essential input is critically short, any healthy computer-run firm
will build it — no matter what industry it started in. Freight and fertilizer,
both severely short right now, are first in line. Capacity grows over the coming
turns, input costs ease, and the deliveries that freight scarcity was throttling
start flowing.

If you have spare capital, this is still a chance to move first: build freight
or fertilizer capacity yourself before the NPCs catch up, and sell into the
shortage while it lasts.
