The world keeps a Cold War Tension reading from zero to one hundred. Tests,
crises, coups and wars raise it; calm lowers it toward a floor that reflects
how armed the world is. High tension tightens defence posture, increases
procurement demand and can bring panic buying, bank runs, civil-defence drills
and peace marches onto the executive's desk.

## Labour, growth and corporate balance

- **Employment is limited by the available workforce.** Sectors cannot employ more people than a
  state can supply. Productive capacity sets labour demand, higher wages
  compete for scarce workers, and capacity without staff cannot produce.
  Persistent vacancies help grow and attract the local workforce.
- **Non-US catch-up growth increases.** Productive public investment and
  capable planned development support the UK, Soviet and East German
  economies without reducing US growth.
- **Freight clears where the trucks are.** Each state's freight capacity serves
  its own market instead of being claimed by unrelated logistics companies
  elsewhere. Shortage warnings identify the actual origin state that could
  not haul the goods.
- **Supply agreements divide scarce supply proportionally.** Private supply is divided
  proportionally, rather than by database order. Shortfall damages use output
  the plant could actually deliver. Cards explain settlement cash, and
  Commodities reports the production booked by the world market.
- **Contracting economies show contraction.** Countries without a national
  growth record derive it from their regions instead of reporting a fixed
  2.5%. Budgets, GDP, inflation, national metrics, revenue and market values
  use shared definitions across pages and public data.

## Nuclear programmes and defence

Defence chiefs in nuclear powers can fund a weapons programme, advance from
fission devices toward hydrogen weapons and MIRVs, and conduct tests that
raise world tension. Bombers, missiles and submarines require their own
investment. Production and upkeep draw on the defence budget; a stockpile
without delivery capacity supplies no deterrent. New worlds start with the
historical programmes and arsenals for their era.

East Germany can pursue a covert programme. More funding advances it faster
but makes detection more likely, and Soviet intervention can set it back.
A successful breakout test becomes a world event.

NATO and Warsaw Pact charters bring player-led treaty allies into a defensive
war when a member is attacked. Those allies cannot make a separate peace while
the defended country still fights. Members of the same bloc cannot declare war
on each other.

Cancelling a live defence contract compensates the supplier for the promised
margin. Cancelled lots remain charged against the quarter's tranche and the
responsible minister is named publicly.

## The German Question and international crises

Delegations can fund plays with political capital as well as money, and capital
arrives faster. A treasury carrying debt no longer blocks every paid decision.
The board names the eligible offices and their holders.

Personal participation no longer lets one actor consume the whole public
floor: each play is limited to once per character per turn, and the shared
ceiling grows with participation. Cards show the category's used capacity and
when another play becomes available.

International crises retain commitments across a campaign. Governments receive
choices appropriate to their role, commit real funds or forces, and influence
escalation, sanctions, relief or settlement. Covert action remains concealed
according to its rules, while human and strategic consequences persist.

## Government and elections

Impeachment requires the relevant majority of the whole chamber, including
empty seats, rather than only the people who vote. Automated blocs vote on the
merits and ideology as well as party affiliation.

A chair's slate assignments persist, explain filing failures and take precedence
over automatically selected candidates. Renaming a party preserves membership
tenure, and changing parties withdraws incompatible endorsements.

Cabinet office records are accessible to their holder, national heads of state
or government and admins. Regional bills preserve a snapshot of the law they
replace, so the detail page continues to explain the actual change after passage.
