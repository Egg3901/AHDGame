Singleplayer adds manual turns and custom turn intervals. End a turn when you
are ready, or choose an interval for automatic turns. The player screen removes
the broad admin panel.

The corporation Sectors tab groups operations by business type, with a division
briefing, live figures and the strategies available to each type of site.

Under the hood, regional budget work alternates between turns without changing
its long-term result, and an interrupted server update resumes the turn instead
of dropping the remaining phases. The mobile client also keeps browser-sized
ads and consent prompts out of its in-app view.
