Singleplayer now feels like your game rather than a staff console. End a turn
when you are ready, or choose a custom interval and let your local world move
at your pace. The player screen stays focused on play, with no broad admin
panel attached.

Corporations are easier to read too. Their Sectors tab groups operations into
the businesses they actually run, with a division briefing, live figures and
the strategies available to each type of site.

Under the hood, regional budget work alternates between turns without changing
its long-term result, and an interrupted server update resumes the turn instead
of dropping the remaining phases. The mobile client also keeps browser-sized
ads and consent prompts out of its in-app view.
