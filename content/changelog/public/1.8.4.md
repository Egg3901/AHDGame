  Supply-contract premiums and unpaid amounts are visible on both sides of a deal.
tags: [corporations, economy, financials]

---

Supply-agreement premiums are cash transfers separate from operating income.
Corporation Financials now names the last turn's net contract cash and any
unpaid remainder, so profitable operations no longer conceal a draining deal.
Buyer cards show settlement cash just as supplier cards do. Settlement records
retain each side's own currency delta.
