## A Budget with real authority

- **Set tax and programme law together.** The Chancellor can choose legal tax
  targets and statutory programme levels, then table the package as one annual
  Commons confidence vote.
- **Ordinary Acts still matter.** A Budget uses the same law and fiscal ledger
  as every other Act. Whichever measure passes later controls the setting.
- **Preview before tabling.** The authoring panel shows projected receipts,
  spending, surplus or deficit, category changes and how many turns a tax
  change will take to phase in.
- **Vacancies no longer stop the Budget.** The Prime Minister can act while the
  Chancellorship is vacant, and authority transfers when a Chancellor is
  appointed.
- **Clearer Treasury figures.** The Budget page explains raw and smoothed
  debt-to-GDP, primary balance, debt interest and the one-percentage-point tax
  phase-in rule.
