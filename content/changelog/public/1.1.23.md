## One margin, every screen

Yesterday's update made the corporation page report the margins the engine
really charges. The sector detail page still computed its own version, so a
sector genuinely losing money each turn could show a healthy margin and a
positive daily profit on its own page while the corporation page showed the
opposite. Both now read the same real figure.

If a sector of yours flipped from profitable to loss-making overnight, the
sector was losing money before too — its page just said otherwise. Common
cause right now: input costs. Fertilizers, plastics and freight are all in
world shortage and cost well above normal, while food sells into a world
surplus below its normal price. Farms are squeezed from both ends. That is
real, it is the same for every farmer, and it will ease as the shortages
clear — or you can act on it: change strategy, renegotiate supply, or ride
it out with the market.

One line item is still being improved: "Things you bought" can understate
sharply-priced inputs, with the difference appearing under "Other running
costs". The totals and profit are correct; the split between those two lines
is not always, and a follow-up will finish it.
