## What changed

- **Fighting alongside allies no longer costs you extra.** Casualties carry a
  term meant to make an army's largest formations absorb the worst of a battle.
  It was measured against your own country's force, which meant a small
  contingent in a large coalition was treated as an entire army in its own
  right and took the full penalty. The same divisions bled harder for being
  filed under a different flag.

  A small ally could lose more than twice as many men per division as a large
  partner, of the same type, in the same role, on the same ground. That gap is
  now within a percent or two. Battles with one nation on each side are
  unchanged.
