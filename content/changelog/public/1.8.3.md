Start in January 2027 as the United States, United Kingdom, Germany, Japan or
China, with the preset's own regions, parties, seats, demographics, budgets and
policy baselines. Era-valid countries also participate through expanded or
background economies where full domestic play is unavailable.

## Corporate investment and balance

- **Ordinary sector expansion receives a 20% build discount.** Heavy
  construction finishes sooner, and surplus capacity can be parked while a
  smaller operation stays active. Build estimates compare returns after costs.
- **Specialization raises operating margins.** Sectors in a corporation's primary industry
  gain ten percentage points of operating margin; secondary industries gain
  five. Investment estimates exclude temporary market support and apply
  policy credits only to sales.
- **History keeps its exchange rate.** Recorded revenue, costs, cash and share
  prices remain on their historical rates. Current market capitalization uses
  the current quote.
- **Banking and monetary decisions use consistent balances.** Loans and
  repayments respect current cash, monetary decisions use the correct
  authority and reserves, and union services remain active for the turn paid.

## Campaigns and parties

Coalition canvassing adds a larger turnout effect when political alignment is
closer. Targeted ads buy an immediate, gradually decaying bonus and can be
purchased from Actions between elections; regional exposure carries into
future races. Canvassing and advertising open directly from the Actions screen.

Party slates allow up to three candidates per race, shared between players and
non-player politicians. Every race shows its remaining slots. Founders can
stand for leadership in their new party immediately instead of waiting through
the membership clock. Committee-only elections restrict who votes, while any
eligible member can stand. Leadership pages show the actual populated race
when an empty duplicate exists.

Party treasury payouts to one member now have a per-turn ceiling across the
national party, state parties and caucuses. Transfers pause during the final
two turns of a leadership election, reducing the opportunity to empty a
treasury immediately before leaving office.

## Government and world setup

Permanent singleplayer rulers can propose and enact validated national laws
directly. Other legislatures retain their voting process. Local setup proceeds
through character creation, and turns require a character.

Historical seed completion checks country, party and budget records against
the selected world and rejects incomplete founding elections. Background
countries use lightweight economic simulation. Cabinet offices show national
outcome metrics alongside facilities, and finance-minister projections explain
GDP, inflation, population and debt-ratio assumptions.

A country can belong to only one competing bloc at a time, and organisations
cannot embargo their own members. Military commands remain editable when an
old unit reference is no longer in the roster.

Supreme Court vacancies opened by historical departures go to players for
nomination and confirmation. Later historical successors are not seated
automatically, and vacancy announcements occur once.

## Client and interface

Mobile players can opt into private inbox push notifications, with their mute
and snooze preferences respected. Temporary singleplayer access can be granted
to linked accounts. Local setup continues into character creation, and turns
require a character. The runtime package excludes development material.

UK election pages load manifestos in one request rather than one per race.
Unused analytics requests are removed, and index funds batch repeated bond
reads. Account integrations can use a lightweight current-session check.
