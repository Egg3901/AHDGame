## What changed

- **Government bond funds.** Each of the eight fund countries has a fund that
  holds its own government's bonds.
- **Four global bond funds.** Investment grade sovereign, emerging markets
  sovereign, investment grade corporate, and high yield corporate. The
  emerging markets and high yield funds hold the riskier paper and pay for
  it in yield.
- **They trade with the bond market.** Bond funds buy from the market at its
  ask and sell to it at its bid when holders redeem. Coupons build the fund's
  value turn by turn.
- **Every fund can raise cash from bonds.** A fund that owes redemptions and
  has too little cash sells shares first, then bonds.
