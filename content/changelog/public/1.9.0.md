  Campaign Presence now marks states already invested in during the current turn.

  Coalition chairs can update their coalition name, abbreviation, and colour.

  Severe shortages hidden by market caps now guide expansion, while automated corporations stop carrying plants that lose money for many turns.

  Presidential races now account for institutional strain, with deeper democratic-health failures weighing more heavily on the ruling party and the sitting President. Constitutional crises add temporary, consequential choices around that pressure.
