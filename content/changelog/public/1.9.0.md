1.9 increases the weight of earnings and growth in company valuations, reduces
input requirements in several industries, and connects government institutions
to election support. Parliamentary government adds cabinet reshuffles, recalls
and by-elections.

## Economy and balance

- **Earnings matter more to share prices.** The earnings-power weight rises
  from 0.4 to 0.5, and the growth weight from 0.1 to 0.15. Plants-economy
  growth premiums use actual trailing sector value growth. The top risk
  premium falls from 7% to 6%, and sector asking prices rise from 75% to
  85% of NPV. This changes valuations, without changing operating cash flows.
- **Bank deposits stop charging the base interest twice.** On legacy deposits,
  a private bank pays the premium above the central-bank savings rate; the
  central bank supplies the base. Computer-run banks price deposits at the
  lower quarter of their corridor, leaving more room for a player bank's
  lending margin. Deposits with full cash backing retain their full-rate rule.
- **Commodity prices follow inflation.** Their nominal level now follows the
  world's median inflation instead of remaining fixed at the world's opening
  level. Input-heavy retail, automotive, construction, manufacturing,
  healthcare and defence strategies use reduced input requirements, lowering
  their operating costs. Output rates and other strategy recipes are retained.
- **Shortages guide automated expansion.** Demand hidden by market caps remains
  visible to autonomous investors. Automated companies can retire persistently
  losing plants, and private supply contracts influence actual production.
- **Labour scarcity affects employment and bargaining.** Measured workforce
  tightness reaches unemployment and, when the labour macro system is active,
  union bargaining. Plants measure production growth at constant commodity
  values so price inflation does not masquerade as output growth.
- **Bank takeovers respect the bank's assets.** Squeeze-outs, fund-only buyouts
  and take-private transactions have a realizable bank net-asset-value floor,
  including the marked proprietary book.
- **State-enterprise losses and outstanding debt reach national accounts.** Realized state-enterprise
  losses draw on the owning treasury. Outstanding bond principal drives debt
  ratios and issuance, while bond-pool cash is excluded from measured money
  supply. Commodity clearing and leaderboards use the same realized basis.
- **Interrupted financial transfers resume their original settlement.** Agreed-acquisition payouts and
  bank-charter transfers resume after interruption, with refunds when a
  coordinated acquisition cannot complete. Retained cash from primary share
  sales is recognized without inventing equity liquidity.

CEOs can set wages across a sector type or a country's holdings in one preview
and confirmation. Open supply offers help find trading partners, and bilateral
negotiations support counter-offers, fixed terms and inbox reminders. Supply
agreement cash is shown separately from operating income on Financials and on
both sides of the settlement card, including an unpaid remainder.

The bank console groups health, limits and the controls affecting them. Its interest figures distinguish what the bank earns, what it pays and
the net result.

## Political balance

Low Democratic Health now weighs on presidential support, with deeper failures
hurting the ruling party and sitting President more. Constitutional crises
create temporary choices around that pressure.

Packing the Supreme Court with one party's justices also reduces Democratic
Health: a 5-4 split carries no packing penalty, while a 9-0 bench carries the
largest. Vacancies go to presidential nomination and Senate confirmation;
later historical justices are no longer seated automatically.

Cabinet effects approach their per-source cap of eight without reaching it.
Increasing a cabinet effect continues to increase its contribution near the
cap, with diminishing returns.

Vacant presidential and governor seats no longer borrow the previous winner's
margin as an incumbency advantage. Non-player politician endorsements once
again supply the campaign actions shown on the campaign desk. Withdrawn
candidates stop receiving campaign income and leave Campaign Operations.

## Parliamentary government and party management

UK cabinets support reshuffles, minister resignation, Chief Whip actions and
whip withdrawal. Departing ministers return to their previous office.
Prime ministers who lose their Commons seat leave office and reopen government
formation.

Commons vacancies and recall petitions persist through turns and lead to
by-elections that do not disturb sitting MPs. Sitting MPs cannot stand for a
second Commons seat through the by-election paths.

UK parties gain annual conferences and leadership challenge committees.
Conference rolls and resolution are fixed to the relevant event so membership
changes and concurrent votes cannot change a decision after the fact.

Vacant congressional leadership seats open elections promptly, and losing a
chamber seat is detected during the turn. Presidential and vice-presidential
seating vacates incompatible legislative offices. Party mergers preserve
membership tenure, and coalition chairs can edit their name, abbreviation and
colour.

## Campaigns and historical worlds

The campaign ledger keeps its full activity history and lists endorsements,
with player and politician filters. Campaign Presence marks states already
invested in during the current turn. Targeted advertising audiences are grouped
by their own demographic category, and election summaries link to the full
candidate field when a condensed result omits someone.

Country- and era-specific GDP benchmarks now support campaign income, action
costs and fund projections. China gets its own benchmarks rather than falling
through to another country. Economic projections explain their assumptions.

The 1991 and 2019 presets seat historical non-player officeholders with birth
years. Under v5 autonomy, they age, die and are replaced within their party.
New 1953 worlds also receive bounded regional political variation while
preserving national averages. Private enterprise eligibility follows a
country's marketization level across founding, expansion and privatization.

## Singleplayer, accounts and interface

Local-world owners can configure world rules and feature controls, manage their
own world and host LAN guests. Permanent heads of state recover their canonical
government on local load and can appoint non-player ministers. Sole corporate
owners can reclaim their CEO seat.

Character creation offers an optional guided conversation alongside the classic
form. Profiles gain political, military and business views. Notification
settings expose per-type mutes and direct-message badges, and inbox read state
recovers after failed requests.

Sign-out and password changes consistently revoke sessions. Provider linking,
account migration and client sign-in use current account authority and recover
interrupted handoffs. Staff role changes take effect consistently, and account
cleanup returns all held shares to the public float. Singleplayer skips
unnecessary identity collection; optional anonymous local statistics contain
aggregate world measures.

Search finds proposable legislation, public economy guides describe current
commodities and currency regimes, and event cards show the correct chamber and
working action links. Commodity pages explain price movements rather than
showing an unexplained price alone.
