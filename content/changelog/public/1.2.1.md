## What changed

- Conflict open years follow the game calendar, including the founding-phase
  offset. A war opened on turn 214 in 1956 no longer reads as 1957.
- The Vietnam front map shows North and South Vietnam together, advances
  north-south, and uses a denser coastline in place of the blocky 110m outline.
