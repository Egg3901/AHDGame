Twenty-eight pull requests that were waiting on full world simulation have been reviewed and merged. Economy fixes, UK political mechanics, corporate autonomy, and balance improvements across the board.

## What landed in this release

69 changes shipped.

### Elections and campaigns

- **Democratic health shapes presidential elections**
  Presidential races now account for institutional strain, with deeper democratic-health failures weighing more heavily on the ruling party and the sitting President. Constitutional crises add temporary, consequential choices around that pressure.
- **Politician endorsements generate campaign actions again**
  Endorsements from non-player politicians once again add to a campaign's action income each turn, matching the figure the campaign desk has been promising. Player endorsements are unchanged.
- **See who is backing your campaign, and every action you have taken**
  The campaign ledger now has two tabs. Activity keeps the full history of what you bought rather than only the last ten entries, and a new Endorsements tab lists everyone currently backing you, filterable by players or politicians.
- **Open executive seats no longer carry an incumbency bonus**
  A governor or president race for a vacant office is an open race. It no longer borrows the last winner's margin as an incumbency shield, so challengers stop being dragged by a result they had nothing to do with.
- **Withdrawn candidates leave Campaign Operations**
  A candidate who withdraws no longer appears in a race's Campaign Operations list, and their campaign stops drawing income while they are out.
- **Leadership chairs no longer sit empty with no election**
  Every congressional leadership seat that falls vacant now opens an election straight away, and losing your chamber seat is noticed on the turn it happens rather than whenever someone next opens the page.
- **Keep membership tenure through party mergers**
  Party mergers no longer restart the leadership eligibility wait for absorbed members.
- **Open NPP management from analytics**
  Analytics links open the selected NPP in the correct management scope and region.
- **Presidential seating vacates incompatible legislative offices**
- **See when election summaries omit candidates**
  Election summaries link to the full field when candidates have no reported votes or polling share.
- **See where you built campaign presence this turn**
  Campaign Presence now marks states already invested in during the current turn.
- **Targeted ad audiences are grouped by demographic**
  The targeted ads audience list now groups age with age, income with income, and the rest of each country's voter categories, using the same names as other campaign targeting.

### Government and legislation

- **Cabinet residuals saturate smoothly instead of pinning at the cap**
  Cabinet-driven political residuals used a hard per-source clamp, so any channel at or above 0.8 points per turn sat at exactly 8.0 and extra investment bought nothing. Pinned regions then rendered identical values. The fold now approaches the same cap of 8 asymptotically, so a bigger push always buys a strictly bigger residual.
- **Understand changes in regime health**
  Leaders can inspect the causes of legitimacy and party confidence changes.

### War and diplomacy

- **Explore political, military and business profiles**
  Your profile and other characters now share three role-based views.

### Economy and corporations

- **Close private enterprise in command economies**
  Command economies no longer admit private corporations by any route. Whether a country permits private enterprise now follows its marketization level, so a country that converts to or away from a planned economy is honoured automatically instead of relying on a fixed list.
- **Find trading partners through open supply offers**
  CEOs can advertise buy and sell interest and start a private negotiation from an offer.
- **Set wages across corporate holdings**
  CEOs can preview and apply wage settings by sector type or across a country.
- **Stronger stock valuations for profitable corporations**
  Share prices now track operating performance more closely: earnings carry more weight in the price, expanding corporations earn a growth premium from actual enterprise-value growth, and sector asking prices sit nearer fair value.
- **Command shortage reads country physical gaps**
  Command economies include observed country commodity shortages in shortage pressure.
- **Separate production growth from sales prices**
  Plants economies measure their cyclical output trend using production at constant commodity values, keeping price changes out of the volume signal.
- **Conserve duplicate shares in account cleanup**
  Account cleanup now returns every share an exiting holder owns to the public float, including split positions, and retries safely when the ledger changes mid-cleanup.
- **Improve bank console hierarchy**
  Makes bank health, limits, and the controls that affect them easier to find.
- **Demand signals and automated plant cleanup**
  Severe shortages hidden by market caps now guide expansion, while automated corporations stop carrying plants that lose money for many turns.
- **Stabilize equity market valuation and liquidity**
  Equity prices now recognize retained cash after primary share sales, while market liquidity and share settlement preserve finite cash and inventory.

### Other changes

- **1991 and 2019 presets seat real historical NPPs with ages and V5 mortality**
  Every seated NPP in the 1991 and 2019 presets is now a real historical office-holder with a verified birth year and portrait where findable. Under V5 rules they age, die (Gompertz mortality), and are replaced by new random NPPs from the same party.
- **Bilateral supply agreement negotiations**
  Corporation CEOs can now negotiate private supply agreements with opening offers, counter-offers, fixed terms, and inbox reminders.
- **Guided chat mode for character creation**
  Character creation has an opt-in guided chat mode that walks through the same steps one at a time. The classic all-steps form stays the default.
- **A one-party Supreme Court hurts democratic health**
  Packing the Court with one party's justices subtracts from democratic health on a sliding scale. A 5-4 split is free. A 9-0 bench is the most expensive.
- **Account permissions stay consistent after role changes**
  Staff access now always reflects the current account record.
- **Add account deletion command primitives**
  Add tested internal command state for future resumable account cleanup.
- **Check current account authority before deletion**
- **Current account state gates optional API personalization**
  Public election, auction, corporation, bond, suggestion and government views now resolve optional player details from the current account record.
- **More consistent session checks before pages and reads load**
  Page and internal read gating now uses the same current-account session check.
- **More reliable admin password resets**
  Admin password resets confirm the credential write and detect concurrent account changes.
- **More reliable sign-in after signing out**
  Password, Discord and Google sign-in now handle recent sign-outs consistently.
- **More reliable sign-out and password updates**
  Sign-out confirms session revocation, and password updates detect concurrent account changes.
- **Protect account credentials during login migration**
- **Require replica-set transactions for coordinated account writes**
  Add a required-transaction helper for coordinated account writes.
- **Safer Google and Discord linking**
  Provider link and unlink now verify the current session and detect concurrent account changes.
- **Signed-out sessions stay signed out**
  Signing out now stays in effect even if another page was still finishing its work.
- **Source password ownership helper for future migration**
- **Source password ownership proof kernel**
- **Source security snapshot helper for future ownership proof binding**
- **Stricter session token checks**
  Sign-in sessions now validate the token algorithm and timestamps more strictly.
- **Supreme Court vacancies go to the President**
  When a justice leaves, the President nominates and the Senate confirms. Later historical justices are never seated automatically, and the Court page no longer labels those successors as Original Roster.
- **Validate stored session revocation state**
  Session checks consistently require valid account revocation data.
- **Commodity prices explain what moved them**
- **Commodity prices now follow inflation**
- **Edit your coalition identity**
  Coalition chairs can update their coalition name, abbreviation, and colour.
- **Require UK prime ministers to retain a Commons seat**
  A prime minister who loses their Commons seat leaves office and government formation reopens.

### Follow-up changes merged after the initial 1.9.0 cut

- **Country- and era-aware economic baselines**
  GDP baselines now follow the selected country and historical preset, so campaign costs and fund projections stay in the world’s own economic context.
- **Commons vacancies, recalls, and by-elections**
  Vacancies and recall petitions now persist through the turn engine, with additive special elections that do not disturb sitting MPs.
- **Cabinet reshuffles and minister departures**
  Parliamentary cabinets now support reshuffles, resignations, Chief Whip actions, and safe restoration of a departing minister’s prior office.
- **Regional political texture**
  The 1953 playable regions now receive deterministic, bounded political deviations while preserving authored national means.
- **Canonical commodity clearing and warnings**
  Commodity books now share one clearing basis, and material clearing breaches flow into durable health warnings without duplicate alerts.
- **Bond-pool cash is excluded from observed M2**
  Sovereign bond-pool settlement cash no longer appears as spurious money creation, while genuine QE/QT effects remain visible.
- **Crash-safe agreed-acquisition payouts**
  Acquisition settlement claims and holder payouts resume idempotently after a crash instead of risking duplicate debits or credits.
- **Measurable synthetic actor coverage**
  Sandbox world runs can seed and drive deterministic synthetic actors through real game seams, with coverage manifests and honest partial-mode reporting.
- **Sovereign issuance diagnostics**
  Sovereign demand gaps and tranche-consolidation diagnostics are now available behind their existing opt-in gate.
- **Shared NPP price signals**
  NPP corporation behavior now uses a shared price/state signal helper, keeping market decisions consistent and the architecture audit clean.
