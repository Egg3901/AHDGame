Cut from development.

Distress-driven retooling (1.1.33) proved itself on its first live turn —
seven bleeding construction/financial/retail sectors switched to
cheaper-input recipes and CA farm margins crossed back above zero. What it
could not do was relieve the fertilizer shortage, because the sectors best
placed to relieve it were already profitable and the trigger required
sustained losses.

Sectors now retool on either of two bars: the low advantage bar when
bleeding, or a bar three times higher when profitable — profit-seeking, not
distress management. Measured live, the industrial-chemicals-to-fertilizer
switch scores well above the new bar, so fertilizer capacity grows where the
price says it should. Cooldown, cohort stagger and the one-shift-per-corp
limit bound churn exactly as before.
