Cut from development. PR #1494.

## Fixed

- `idleUpkeepUnitPrice` clamps the cost basis to `IDLE_UPKEEP_BASIS_MAX`
  (one minus the default sector margin, 0.65) instead of 1. On the live
  world 937 of 5,733 plants sectors carried a held basis at or above 0.9
  because their stamping turn ran at a zero or negative margin; a healthcare
  plant with a 1.009 basis was paying 8.1M upkeep on 3.3M revenue. The
  anchor still stops the bill growing as a margin falls; it just cannot
  start at a level a healthy sector never pays.
