## What changed

The Deals tab acquire search (and the same picker used for supply-agreement
buyers) only listed corporations whose `ceoType` field was exactly
"character". Older player-founded companies never had that field written, so
Mongo skipped them even though a human CEO sits in the seat and can accept an
offer. Ticket 1095 added an empty-state message for that blank result; ticket
1105 is the same reporter trying to buy Creek Energy, a fully private
player-run firm that still did not appear.

The search now treats a missing `ceoType` the same way onboarding and
stat-allocation already do: as a player-run seat. AI-run (NPP) and imperial
CEOs stay excluded, because they still have no player who can accept.
