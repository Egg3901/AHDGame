## What changed

- Infrastructure, transport, housing, and energy budgets now form productive
  capital alongside paid corporate investment under one shared safety cap.
- State-heavy economies can unlock catch-up growth through industrial-plan
  execution, workforce skill, transport efficiency, productive public investment,
  and trade access.
- GDP-per-capita convergence comparisons now use the world's canonical era
  denomination instead of fluctuating market exchange rates, and only active
  player countries can set the frontier.
- The UK transport budget now feeds both infrastructure outcomes and capital
  formation.
