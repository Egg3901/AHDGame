## Changed

- The five historical era electorates (1953, 1979, 1991, 1999, 2007) are
  recalibrated. Before this, only the modern eras had been tuned state by
  state; the older eras were rough drafts whose states bunched together near
  the centre, and two of them leaned so uniformly one way that the other party
  had no ground to stand on. Each era now shows its real geography: state
  spreads comparable to the modern eras, and state rankings that line up with
  the era's actual election maps.
- Positions are authored by region and demographic group, not by state. A
  group's values are set once per region (Deep South, Border South, Yankee
  New England, Mid-Atlantic, Great Lakes, Plains, Mountain, Pacific), with
  single-state exceptions only where history demands them, and state
  differences otherwise emerge from each state's own demographic mix and
  turnout.
- Party is no longer read as ideology. The 1953 Solid South voted Democratic
  through registration and one-party organisation while being the most
  socially traditional region in the country and only mildly populist on
  economics; the tables now say exactly that. Mississippi is the social
  ceiling of 1953, West Virginia its clearest economically-left and socially
  traditional state, and Vermont still votes like the most Republican state
  in the union.
- The economic and social axes now measure different things in every era. The
  1953 Southern Black electorate is authored as the constituency for social
  change that it was, which also makes the Voting Rights Act mechanics move
  election results the right way when that electorate gains the vote.
- New road-to-1960 arc for fifties worlds: starting around 1957, northern
  Black voters consolidate and register, working-class economics swing left
  through the 1958 recession, the new suburban middle class drifts right, and
  Yankee New England begins leaving the Republican column, ending at the 1960
  dead heat.

## Not changed

- The modern era electorates (2019, 2023) are byte-identical.
- Live worlds keep their current state leans until their demographics are
  reseeded or healed; the road-to-1960 arc, however, begins applying on live
  fifties worlds as soon as it deploys.
