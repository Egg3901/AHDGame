summary: >-
  Issuing a bond used to fund the issuer in full the instant the button was
  pressed, with every unit parked in a float nobody had paid for. The bond
  market pool now underwrites a new issue with its own cash at par, the
  issuer is funded for exactly the units it took, and the remainder places
  turn by turn as the pool finds cash. A sovereign auction the market will
  not absorb feeds the sovereign-default crisis classifier with its real
  fill; an autonomous central bank monetizes a bounded slice of the
  shortfall, a player-run one is told.

Cut from development. PR #1334. Phase 3 of the bond market pool; phases 1 and 2 were #1330 and #1333.

## Added

- Primary market (`src/lib/bonds/primaryMarket.ts`). Corporate capacity is a
  fifth of the pool's secondary-liquidity cash (the M2 share, not the
  sovereign working balance) scaled by credit tier, AAA and AA at full weight
  down to CCC at a fifth. Sovereign capacity is ninety percent of the pool's
  cash scaled by the demand model's appetite for the issuer, floored at ten
  percent so even a shunned sovereign finds some buyers.
- `Bond.requestedUnits`, `unsoldUnits`, `primaryFillRatio`. Unsold units are
  not debt: outside `totalIssued`, no coupon, not in the unit invariant.
- Per-turn placement in the bond turn. Each pool spends at most a tenth of
  its cash per turn placing unsold units at its ask, at most two percent of
  each issue's requested size per turn. Corporate proceeds land in
  liquidCapital in the corp's currency; sovereign face lands in the budget
  through the same adjustment issuance uses.
- `FederalBudget.lastPrimaryFillRatio` and `lastPrimaryAuctionTurn`. The
  crisis classifier takes the lower of the synthetic demand ratio and the
  real fill when the fill is below one.
- Autonomous central banks (`chairMode: "npp"`) take unsold sovereign units
  onto their book at par, up to two percent of GDP per auction, creating
  deposits as under QE. Player-run banks receive a `cb_auction_shortfall`
  notification instead.
- The pool's target now includes one quarter of maturing sovereign face as a
  working balance, since it re-buys the rollover before the maturing series
  pays it back. Migration `2026-09-03-bond-market-pool-working-balance` tops
  existing pools up to that level and stamps `m2Local`.
- Bond page shows units still placing; the corporate issuance confirmation
  says what share the market took up front.

## Changed

- A world with no pool for the currency (seeds, pre-migration) keeps the old
  full funding, so seeds and tests are unaffected.
- Relocation bonds and refinance bonds are unchanged: neither raises cash
  from the market.
