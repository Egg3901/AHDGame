## What changed

- The department-wide readiness setting held by the Secretary of Defense, the Defence Secretary, and the Ministers of Defense in China and Japan scaled nothing a player could see. It multiplied an internal force average that existed only for one metric, and it multiplied upkeep by 1.25. No unit's stored readiness moved by a single point, so Elevated was a 25% larger defence bill in exchange for a number that never changed.
- The tier now scales the readiness baseline every formation drifts toward, alongside the unit's own posture. Elevated raises that target by 10%, Reduced lowers it by 10%, and the target is capped at 100 so Elevated on High Alert settles at full readiness rather than an impossible 101.
- Because the tier now reaches the stored readiness, the force average no longer re-applies it. Counting it in both places would have doubled the setting rather than fixed it.
- The conflict record and the order of battle quote recovery against the same tier-scaled target, for the same reason they already quote it against unfunded upkeep: the record must not promise a climb the turn processor will not deliver.
- All three options on those four readiness tiers shipped with no metric effects at all. They now carry the trade-off their own descriptions promised. Elevated buys public safety confidence and disaster preparedness and costs budget balance, social cohesion or public trust; Reduced does the reverse.
- Two more dead levers turned up in the same audit and are fixed with it. The UK First Secretary of State declared social cohesion under the wrong category, so every cohesion effect that seat had wrote to a path that does not exist. The UK Health Secretary's regional public health campaign was keyed on a display label rather than a metric id, so it wrote to nothing.

## Why

A player set his department to Elevated, put units on High Alert, and reported that readiness did not improve. He was right, and the setting had never worked. A lever the interface offers, charges for, and describes in terms of readiness has to move readiness, or it is a tax on reading the tooltip.
