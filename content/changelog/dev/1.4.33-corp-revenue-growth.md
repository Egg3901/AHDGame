## Fixed

- Company growth rate showed 0.00% for every company, no matter what you did.
  When the economy moved to plants, revenue stopped being driven by a sector's
  growth-rate setting and started coming from what your plants produce and
  sell. The setting was left behind, and the Overview and Financials pages were
  still averaging it, so they were reporting a number the economy no longer
  keeps up to date.
- Both pages now show the growth in the revenue your company actually booked,
  measured over the past game year and stated as an annual rate, and they say
  so in the label. Companies too young to have a year of history keep the old
  reading rather than being given an invented one.
- The measurement window is deliberately wide. Annualizing a single turn would
  turn ordinary week-to-week revenue movement into a swing of several hundred
  percent, so a company needs a meaningful stretch of history before a rate is
  quoted at all.
