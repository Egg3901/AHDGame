## Fixed

- The cyclical sector signal under plants mode measured growth as this turn's
  realized-revenue delta times 48. Live per-turn revenue churn of 3 to 17
  percent therefore printed as annualized growth of minus 480 to plus 846
  percent, pinned the signal against its bounds for most US states, and fed
  the output-gap integrator a biased, saturated series. The result was a
  standing drag of roughly 8 points on every print and a sawtooth in which
  any dip in one turn's revenue produced a deep negative GDP number.

- The signal now keeps an exponentially smoothed revenue level per region,
  logs a snapshot of it every 8 turns, and measures annualized growth against
  the snapshot closest to one year old. Both endpoints are smoothed and the
  span divides the noise instead of multiplying it, so a one-turn revenue
  wobble moves the print by under 2 points instead of pinning it. The one-turn
  path remains only as a cold-start fallback for the first turns after a world
  flips to plants, and the legacy weighted average below plants is untouched.

- No migration needed: the smoothing state populates organically and the
  trailing signal takes over per region once its first snapshot is 8 turns
  old, reaching full one-year measurement depth over the following day of
  real time.
