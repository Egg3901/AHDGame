Cut from development.

## Fixed

- Bond trades, fund redemptions, share offers, dividends, endorsements and bill
  proposals now preserve a single consistent result when requests arrive close
  to turn processing.
- Pension contributions and liabilities now use the correct local and Anchor
  currency units for corporations outside the Anchor currency.
- Internal migration now uses the standard cost-of-living scale and appears in
  realized migration totals.
- Inactive candidates are withdrawn before an election is resolved.
- Inflation recalculation no longer replaces the wage-growth value produced by
  the labour system.
- Caucus dues on NPP members are based on current income generation instead of
  taxing the caucus member's accumulated balance.
- One extraction contract is now shared across all same-owner sectors instead
  of being repeated for each sector.
- Investment banks can disburse named corporation loans while remaining barred
  from household lending.
- Euro-denominated lines of credit use the shared ECB prime rate consistently.
- Expired private share offers record refunds against the turn that processed
  them.
- Follow-up Bundestag elections finish seeding their Landeslisten.
