Cut from development.

## Fixed

- A corporation's bond portfolio is now counted as an asset when the turn
  processor decides whether it is insolvent. It never was, even though the
  liquidation that a default leads to has always redeemed those same holdings at
  face value before settling. Corporations were being declared unable to pay
  using assets the resulting liquidation would have spent paying. Six
  corporations in the live world were carrying a shortfall smaller than the
  portfolio sitting next to it.

- The debt ceiling offered at issuance is now capped by what a corporation could
  actually realize by selling up, so it can no longer exceed the assets the
  insolvency test recognises. Previously the ceiling was priced on going-concern
  value while insolvency was judged on realizable value, and under the plants
  economy those differ enormously: one corporation was offered headroom of
  roughly 469 billion, drew under one percent of it, and was defaulted twice in
  three turns for the trouble.

- The Bonds tab and the credit rating what-if slider were each re-deriving the
  debt ceiling in the browser from one of the two rules, so a server-side fix
  alone would still have shown the old number. Both now render the ceiling the
  server enforces, and say which limit is binding when it is the realizable-asset
  one.

- Relocation bonds answer to the same ceiling as an ordinary issuance. They are
  new borrowing and were the remaining way around it.

## Changed

- Refinancing defaulted debt is deliberately exempt from the new ceiling. A
  refinance rolls principal that already exists, adds no debt and hands over no
  cash, so capping it by realizable assets would lock a corporation out of the
  only cure it has.

- Added an index for creditor-side bond lookups, which the corporation page and
  the dissolve preview already ran and the issuance route now needs.
