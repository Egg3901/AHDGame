## Added

- The documented country collection now works and reports runtime availability,
  currency, population, GDP, and region counts.
- Country regions expose public population, GDP, representation, political lean,
  voting-system, and leading-sector summaries in one request.
- National budget responses expose revenue, spending, balance, debt, credit
  rating, tax rates, and core economic indicators without private defence or
  player data.
- Forex collection and detail responses expose current rates, targets, volumes,
  policy regimes, intervention bands, and bounded rate history.
- Referendum collection and detail responses expose campaign polling, public
  party positions, timing, and resolved results without publishing cohort or
  campaign-spend internals.

## Improved

- Game state now reports the active world's real era calendar, preset, iteration,
  status, and turn timing instead of deriving its date from a fixed 2020 anchor.
- Country economy responses now include live GDP and population, per-capita GDP,
  fiscal balance, debt, credit rating, currency, and investor confidence.
- The machine-readable endpoint catalog now includes every implemented route,
  and a parity test prevents implemented and advertised routes from drifting.
