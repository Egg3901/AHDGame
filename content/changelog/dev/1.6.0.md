Singleplayer is not a cut-down mode. It is the same engine, the same seeds and the same client, packaged to run locally and driven by a launcher that will not claim it is ready until the game answers on the port. The rest of the release is the accountability pass that came with it: approval reaches executive party members, inflation adds up, and a corporation's screens agree with its ledger.

## What landed in this release

19 changes shipped under 1.6.0.

### Elections and campaigns

- **FPTP spoiler modelling recognises live UK and Japanese parties**
  The major-party set for spoiler modelling was a list of seed slugs, while live candidates carry a party's sequential id, so for the UK and Japan the set matched nothing and the whole spoiler pass (and its poll mirror) was skipped. Majors now resolve through the party abbreviation, with a two-largest-parties fallback for rosters the config does not name.
- **The electoral college follows the game clock**
  Every presidential caller now passes the live year into the apportionment, so DC gets its three electoral votes from 1961 and the Maine and Nebraska district gates can activate. Snapshots and fallbacks use the live unit list and its total instead of the modern 538.
- **The UK confidence gauge and NHS quality actually tick**
  Both per-turn ticks sat inside a one-time seed function that a live world never re-entered, and the gauge write targeted a collection that does not exist, so the Westminster panel showed defaults for the whole iteration. They now run every turn, the write upserts, and a bottomed-out gauge can dissolve Parliament behind its flag.

### Government and legislation

- **Bills only move their own country, and national growth reads the same everywhere**
  A bill that targeted an economic metric applied its delta to every region in the world. Fixed, and the budget page's national growth figure is now the live number rather than a once-a-year snapshot. Countries without a national metrics document now record measured regional growth instead of a seed constant.
- **Government approval now reaches executive party members**
  Low government approval now creates a bounded favorability cost for members of the party currently holding executive office, including NPP heads.

### War and diplomacy

- **Small allies stop paying a coalition tax**
  A unit's casualties were scaled by its share of its OWN country's force rather than of the whole side, so a nation with three divisions in a ten-nation coalition was charged as though those three were the entire army. The same army bled 37% harder for being split across four flags. Casualty share is now measured against the side.
- **Losing a coalition war reaches every government**
  A country could lose a war as part of a coalition and see its approval recover as soon as the front closed. Recent defeat now remains visible for three years, applies to every losing roster country including treaty allies, fades to zero, and caps when several wars overlap. Winners and stalemates are unaffected.

### Economy and corporations

- **Corporate numbers stop disagreeing with each other**
  Policy credit can no longer push a sector's profit above its revenue, the public API reports the same profit as the corp page, index fund units count toward wealth, the margin shown on the financials and CEO tabs is the net margin over every cost, and index funds cap any single name at a fifth of the fund.
- **GDP growth and output gap remain one bounded transition**
  Integrated GDP growth and the output gap now share one bounded transition, so registry limits cannot leave the persisted gap and headline rate out of sync.
- **Inflation breakdowns now reconcile to the settled rate**
  Inflation reports now account for the final stabilization step when a per-turn limit, floor, ceiling, or rounding changes the calculated rate.
- **The demand caps now record what they cut**
  Two 1.5x demand caps set recorded demand to at most 1.5x supply before anything reads it, so no commodity could ever report worse than a third short. The truncated units and the latent shortage multiple are now written to the price documents, their history and the vital signs. Nothing about prices changes.

### Interface and platform

- **Singleplayer runs the real game on your own machine**
  The singleplayer build is the multiplayer game, unchanged, pointed at a database in your home folder with one local account. A launcher fetches MongoDB on first run, a start screen picks the era, and turns advance from an End turn button instead of the clock. Art is mirrored locally as it is seen, so a world you have looked at keeps rendering offline.
- **Singleplayer and Worldsim beta for AHDClient 2.0.1**
  Adds seamless local character setup, permanent Head of State play, playerless simulation, and anonymous aggregate reporting for the desktop client.
- **Anti-abuse scans run every third turn**
  The three anti-abuse scans looked at the same six-turn window on every turn, and were close to a fifth of everything a turn reads. They now run every third turn. Nothing leaves the window unexamined; turns get faster.
- **Singleplayer launcher can be driven by a host app**
  The singleplayer launcher gained the three flags a desktop shell needs: pick the data directory, skip opening a browser, and shut everything down when the host process disappears.
- **The singleplayer launcher says ready only when the game answers**
  The launcher announced readiness as soon as the server's port opened, which on a fresh database is before first-run setup has finished. It now waits for the game itself to respond before saying so.
- **Show the public-expectations drag on first approval load**
  Countries without a stored approval snapshot now show the same public expectations modifier that is already included in their fallback rating.
- **Stop local world child processes through the supervisor**
  Adds a desktop control message that closes the local game server and MongoDB before the launcher exits, including when startup is cancelled.

### Other changes

- **Add 2023 world entity manifest coverage**
  Register the 2023 reset preset with the world entity manifest so country enablement and manifest-backed world consumers resolve consistently.
