## What changed

- House impeachment needs a seat-weighted majority of the full chamber and
  Senate conviction needs a seat-weighted two-thirds of the full chamber.
  Abstentions and seats that never vote now count against passage, so a
  handful of player votes can no longer carry a 435-seat chamber. The
  impeachment panel shows the exact seat bar and how close the vote is.
- NPP blocs no longer vote on impeachment by party label alone. The target's
  party defends them, opposition major-party blocs back removal unless they sit
  ideologically close to the target, and minor-party or unaligned blocs abstain
  unless the ideological distance is clear. Abstaining weighs against passage,
  so unaligned blocs are a conservative voice, not a hidden one.
