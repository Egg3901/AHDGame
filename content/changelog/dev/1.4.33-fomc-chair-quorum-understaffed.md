  the President refills the board. Full boards are unchanged.

## Changed

- A motion now carries on a **strict majority of the seated members** instead
  of the full board. Vacant seats are outside the quorum: they cast no ballot
  and neither add to nor subtract from the tally. On a board reduced to the
  chair alone, the chair's motion passes on their own vote, so a staffing
  shortfall no longer freezes rate policy entirely. Seated members who do not
  ballot still abstain and still count against the motion.
- The committee page and wiki describe the seated-member quorum and note that
  a lone chair sets the rate alone (the understaffed-board banner is no longer
  a deadlock warning).
- The committee API now returns `seatedMembers` alongside `majorityNeeded`.

## Unchanged

- Board staffing rules are untouched: the engine never seats a machine
  candidate (#1195), and the President nominates with Senate confirmation.
- On a fully staffed board the threshold is exactly what it always was, and
  no-shows still count against a motion.

## Corrected

- The 1.4.32 notes ("motions cannot pass while the board is understaffed")
  described the old full-board rule; that rule is superseded by this change.
