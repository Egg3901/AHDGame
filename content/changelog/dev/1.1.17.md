## Fixed

- A bond default could sell **every sector a corporation owned**. Restructuring
  is meant to sell the minimum needed and leave the business running, and the
  notice you received said exactly that, but nothing stopped it taking the last
  sector. A corporation with one sector could lose it outright, keeping only its
  shell and its cash. Restructuring now refuses when covering the debt would
  take every sector, and the choice returns to the CEO instead.
- Auto-refinance and auto-restructure notices never reached the inbox. Both were
  written without a recipient, so the per-character filter and the unread badge
  skipped them and a CEO could lose sectors with no notice at all. Both now
  carry the recipient and the corporation.
