## Changed

- The state economy page now leads with one thin strip of state-wide headline
  stats instead of four separate cards.
- The sector picker sits directly above the selected sector's detail.
- The full sector breakdown grid and national economic model are collapsed by
  default at the bottom of the page.
- Selecting a sector from the full grid keeps the primary picker in sync.
