Cut from development.

## The waterfall now reconciles

A corporation with $72.6K of gross revenue showed a waterfall reading
maintenance $14.9K, marketing $0, R&D $42 and net interest $7.3K, then a net
income of $32.4K. Those numbers do not meet: the visible rows leave $50.3K, not
$32.4K.

Two causes, and both were structural rather than arithmetic.

`buildAllocation` never read `laborCosts`. For the corporation above that was
$17.9K per turn, the single largest cost line it carried, and it appeared
nowhere on the bar. `regulatoryBurden` was missing on the same terms. Both are
charged into `income` by the backend, so the total accounted for money the rows
did not.

The bottom line was also passed in rather than computed. `netIncome` came
straight from `f.income` and had no arithmetic relationship to the segments
drawn above it, so a missing cost row changed nothing about the total and left
no trace.

Net interest was clamped with `Math.max(0, ...)`, which meant a corporation
whose coupons exceeded its debt service had that inflow dropped from the bar
while `income` still carried it. The signed value is now kept and inflows are
returned separately.

The total is now `revenue` minus the sum of every signed row. Any part of
`income` the rows do not explain appears as an explicit `Other` row instead of
being absorbed. A visible discrepancy can be reported; a hidden one cannot.

Covered by four tests, including the reconciliation identity itself and the
case where a remainder must surface.

## Wages and regulatory compliance have rows

Both were charged every turn and shown nowhere. `Wages` sits under cost of
revenue next to sector maintenance, and `Regulatory Compliance` under operating
expenses.

## The masthead dividend matches what is charged

The dividend tile read `corporation.dividendRate`, the rate the CEO sets. The
income statement charges `effectiveDividendRate`, which is the higher of that
rate, the legal-structure minimum and any parent floor. A US LLC carries a 20%
minimum distribution, so the tile showed 0% on the same screen where the
statement charged 20%.

The tile now shows the effective rate, and when a structure forces it above the
CEO's setting it says so and names the rate that was set.

## The masthead income tile is labelled correctly

It reports the figure after the dividend payout, which is retained earnings, not
income. It is now labelled `Retained`.

## The UK PLC description said it was Japanese

`uk_plc` was described as "Japan's standard joint-stock company", copied from the
`jp_gk` entry below it. Every UK player read that on the legal structure screen.
