Cut from development.

Revenue realization, margins, scarcity and the NPC brain all moved to the
reachable market this week; the cost side had one wire left. Under plants a
sector's input bill is a real physical purchase — units times unit price —
and that unit price still read the planet-wide ratio, which for fertilizer
was 2.38x and RISING (the Eastern bloc's shortage keeps the world aggregate
short) while the Western market it actually buys in had already cleared to
near base.

The result was exactly backwards: as NPC retooling flooded the West with
cheap fertilizer, Western farm revenue rose with the local market while
Western farm costs rose with the world — margins drifted DOWN through the
recovery. The input bill now prices each commodity at the sector country's
reachable ratio, falling back to the world ratio only where no reachable
book exists. Modern integrated worlds carry no books and are unchanged.
