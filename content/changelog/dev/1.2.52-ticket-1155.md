## Fixed

- The Market Position pie on a sector could read 100% after the unowned slice
  was removed, which looked like the market was full and further farms would
  be wasted, including farms already paid for and still building. 100% means
  you are the only producer currently selling there. The pie now labels that
  as "of producers," and a note under it says so in words, names capacity
  still in the queue, and says whether buyers still have room for more output.
