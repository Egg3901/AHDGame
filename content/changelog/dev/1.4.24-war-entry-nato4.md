## Fixed

- Added `scripts/migrations/heal-war-entry-nato4-0830.ts`, a one-off heal that finds every
  signed, NPP-sponsored `join_conflict` bill for The War for Germany whose country is on
  neither roster, refuses unless that set is exactly the four reviewed countries, and then
  runs the same `prepareAutonomousWarEntry` and `joinSide` path the bill would have run.
  Each country deploys its standard NATO commitment (20% of ready reserve power,
  weakest-first) and receives a country-history line for the entry. The join is stamped at
  the current turn and front position so the late arrivals are scored from where they
  actually arrived. Dry run by default; `--yes` applies; the conflict document and every
  moved unit id are snapshotted to a backup collection first. Applied to production on
  turn 499.
