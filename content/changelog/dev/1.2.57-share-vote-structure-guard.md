## What changed

- Stock splits, public share issuances, and CEO self-issuances are blocked while
  a shareholder vote is open.
- A vote is cancelled defensively if its voting-power denominator changes
  before resolution, preventing ballots cast under an obsolete share structure
  from deciding a later structure.
