## Fixed

- Docket case resolution (`decideCaseOutcome`) now treats a headcount tie
  among seated justices the same as an empty bench: no clear majority means
  no ruling, so the case affirms and no policy effect is enacted. Previously
  a tie only failed to match the authored historical direction and was
  scored as a "diverged" ruling, which could enact a synthetic bill and
  alter game history off a vote the Court never actually carried.
