## The valve was blind to strategy-produced shortages

1.1.29 added a generic (non-extraction) re-strategize pass so NPCs would retool
toward any critically short commodity, not just mining. It shared the extraction
passes' `prices` fetch — which is filtered to `EXTRACTABLE_RESOURCES` (oil,
coal, iron, gas, timber, rare earth). Fertilizers, chemicals and every other
strategy-produced good were therefore absent from the price-ratio map, so the
generic decider saw a neutral 1.0 signal for all of them and could never
conclude that fertilizer was short.

Confirmed on production, turn 168: the auto-strategy pass fired
(`lastExtractionAutoStrategyTurn` advanced) and switched exactly zero chemical
plants to fertilizer, while fertilizer sat at 2.3x base and US agriculture
margins stayed at −9% (farms throttled to 85% by the fertilizer shortage).

The generic pass now fetches all commodity price ratios in a separate query,
independent of the extractables-only extraction fetch. The extraction passes
are unchanged. NPP chemical plants will now switch to fertilizer where it is
short, US fertilizer supply climbs over the transition window, and the
fertilizer throttle on farms lifts — the relief 1.1.29 was supposed to deliver.
