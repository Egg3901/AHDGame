## The margin vise had no lever

Rising prices are only good news for a sector whose margin is positive —
income is revenue times margin, so a negative-margin farm watching food
prices recover was losing MORE money every turn, not less. The margin itself
only heals when the recipe fits the price regime, and nothing in the NPC
brain ever changed a recipe: glut mothballing (2c) handles unfilled sectors,
but a squeezed sector sells everything it makes and still bleeds.

## Section 2e: input-squeeze strategy shift

Each turn, on the same cohort stagger as mothballing, an NPC corp with a
sector at sustained negative margin re-scores every strategy available to
that sector type: output rates weighted by how far each commodity trades
above base in the corp country's reachable market, minus input rates weighted
the same way. If a candidate beats the current recipe by a real threshold,
the sector switches — transition ramp, 24-turn cooldown, and capacity
renormalization identical to the player path. One shift per corp per turn,
SOEs exempt, extraction keeps its deposit-aware pass.

Measured against live prices: industrial-chemical plants in the fertilizer
squeeze score the switch at +0.42 revenue share, so fertilizer supply grows
where it is scarce — which is what actually brings farm input costs, and
farm losses, back down.

The NPC price brain (production policy, mothball/restart, founding valve)
also now reads reachable-market prices before national and global, matching
the pricing engine.
