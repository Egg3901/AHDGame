## What changed

- The sector money panel listed every policy modifier in dollars, but a type-switch penalty of -10pp could show as +$535K in green, and a tech-tree bonus of +3.3pp as -$174K in red.
- The split divided the booked policy credit by the sum of the named percentage points. When those two had opposite signs (unlisted terms and the soft cap are not in the named list), every dollar amount flipped.
- The drilldown now uses the engine's applied policy points so the unnamed slice lands on "Other factors". A bonus stays a credit and a penalty stays a charge, and the rows still add up to the policy line.
