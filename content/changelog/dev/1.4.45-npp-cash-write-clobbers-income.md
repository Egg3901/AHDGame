Cut from development.

## Fixed

- A caretaker run company lost its whole operating income on any turn it placed
  a capacity build or founded a sector. The turn paid the income into the
  treasury, then the caretaker's own write of the balance landed afterwards
  carrying the figure from before the payment, and the income was gone. The size
  of the build made no difference: a build costing a few hundred erased millions,
  because placing any build at all triggered the write.
- Caretaker spending is now recorded as a change to the balance rather than as a
  replacement for it, so it settles alongside the income instead of replacing it.
  Bond coupons and supply settlements were never affected, because those are
  applied later in the turn by the same kind of change.
- Companies funding a new market with borrowed money still receive those
  proceeds exactly once. The loan and the entry cost travel together as a single
  net change.

## Changed

- Caretaker run companies will now build up a treasury over time instead of
  sitting near zero. Expect knock on movement in share prices, credit ratings,
  dividends and expansion rates, since a company's cash feeds all four.

One visible symptom of this: a company could show healthy earnings every turn
while its cash on hand never moved, and players holding its shares could not sell
them back, because selling into the float is paid out of the issuer's treasury
and there was never anything in it.
