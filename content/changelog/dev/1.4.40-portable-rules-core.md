## What changed

- Added a blocking architecture-audit check over the rules zone (`rules.ts` and any `rules/` directory under `src/`). Inside it, database access, `new Date()` / `Date.now()`, `Math.random()`, `process.env`, `fetch`, Sentry, `@/app` imports, and `async` all fail the audit.
- Documented the split in AGENTS.md and CONTRIBUTING.md: rules take plain data, an injected rng, and a turn or in-game date; the turn phase or route around them owns loading, writing, and notifying.
- The three existing rules modules (`pensions`, `indexFunds/petitions`, `world/transitions`) already pass. Existing systems are not retrofitted in bulk; the rule bites on formulas a change adds or reworks.
