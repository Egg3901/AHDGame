Cut from development. Raised on ticket #1129.

## Fixed

- Three cabinet buildings were charging upkeep and doing nothing. The State
  Publishing House was the worst of them: both of its effects went nowhere, so
  it was inert from the day it was built. The House of Culture lost its national
  pride effect, and the Internal Troops Garrison lost its border security
  effect. All three now land. A check was added so a new building whose effect
  has nowhere to go fails before it can ship.

## Changed

- The four playable countries gave every region the same political values plus a
  short list of hand written exceptions. That list covered 24 of 63 areas, so
  most of the board was identical everywhere: in the United States, 47 of 63
  areas held the same number in all fifty one states. Public safety was one of
  them, which is why the Attorney General's regional breakdown showed the same
  crime rate in every state.
- Regions now carry their own values, derived from the regional data the game
  already held and which every non playable country was already using. In the
  United States the number of identical areas falls from 47 to 9, and in the
  United Kingdom from 48 to 8. Hand written entries still win where they exist,
  so deliberate regional history is untouched.
- National levels are unchanged. Regional values are spread around each
  country's existing average rather than replacing it, so no country has been
  made stronger or weaker overall.
- The Soviet Union and East Germany gain less regional variety than the United
  States and the United Kingdom, because their underlying regional data records
  one national figure for several areas rather than a value per region. Their
  public safety numbers in particular are still the same everywhere.
