Cut from development.

## Fixed

- Profitable AI-run corporations at the ordinary five-sector limit can enter a
  market when one of the candidate sector's outputs crosses the established
  critical-shortage price threshold. Balanced and glutted markets retain the
  five-sector limit.
- Cash-constrained entrants may finance the founding cost with a corporate bond
  under the existing credit rating, interest, leverage, maturity, and issuance
  cooldown rules. The proceeds are matched by recorded debt and are never a
  free cash grant.

## Guarded

- Shortage exceptions stop at a higher hard ceiling and are spread across
  deterministic corporation cohorts. An eligible corporation can found only
  one starter facility in its slot, and capacity still observes construction
  time and available market headroom.
- Tests cover deep-shortage entry at the old cap, rejection of balanced and
  glutted opportunities, the entry cadence, and cash conservation against the
  recorded bond liability.
