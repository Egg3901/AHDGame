## Changed

- Rally and Rally Tour support now counts during the **primary** phase, not only
  the general election. Support raises a candidate's vote share on a 0.6x to
  1.4x mood curve (neutral at 50); previously a whole primary season of rallying
  moved zero votes because only the general read the support figure. The primary
  projection reflects it too, so a rally shows up in your standings right away.
- Canvassing's 2x "campaign season" bonus now fires for presidential races. It
  used to look for a state-level election in the state you canvassed, but the
  presidential contest is national, so the bonus never triggered where it
  mattered.
- The canvassing panel now knows your country's demographics, how many canvasses
  you can afford, and refreshes your funds and actions after you spend. It was
  previously mounted without that information, so affordability and the wallet
  readout were broken.
- The presidential candidate table shows **NPI** (National Political Influence)
  instead of a separate influence percentage. NPI is what the election engine
  actually uses for reach and persuasion, so the table now matches the number
  that drives results.
- Campaign strength in the presidential table is shown compactly (for example
  26.1k instead of 26100).
