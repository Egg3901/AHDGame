## The covert programme

The DDR's defence minister now has a second, unwritten portfolio. The covert
nuclear programme is a five-stage grind: ore skimmed from the Wismut
shipments, centrifuges in a closed mine, a design bureau with an unofficial
charter, cold implosion tests, and finally a device waiting on a political
decision.

Nothing about it appears on any public surface. Progress accrues only in
turns the programme is funded, off the books, out of the defence
appropriation. Three funding levels trade pace against exposure: a patient
trickle takes decades of game time; a crash programme moves four times as
fast and runs hot.

## Suspicion and the crackdown

Soviet counterintelligence is always listening. Suspicion rises while the
money flows and cools while the programme sleeps, and every funded turn risks
discovery. A crackdown costs the programme a full stage plus everything in
progress, halts the funding, and makes the papers: the world learns the
Soviets raided undeclared East German facilities, and cold war tension rises.
The programme survives. The crawl can begin again.

## Breakout

A completed device does nothing until the government chooses to announce it.
The breakout test is public, deliberate, and irreversible: East Germany joins
the nuclear powers with the largest tension spike in the system, and from
that day climbs the same tree as everyone else.
