Cut from development.

## Fixed

- The final region of a dissolving country now reapportions its share of the
  national tax bases, spending baselines and grants to the survivor instead of
  stranding it on the dissolved shell.
- A stored marketization level now survives the compiled era schedule, so a
  regime carried onto a country the schedule never listed keeps being
  simulated after a restart.
- Dissolved countries are out of every per-country turn loop: parliamentary
  government processing, bill lifecycles, election spawners, fiscal years,
  sovereign issuance and the command-economy phase all skip them, and the
  single-country access path now reads them as unregistered.

## Changed

- A country merge now carries the whole national layer: treasury and debt
  (signed, FX-converted, debt mirror recomputed), the defence account,
  unmatured sovereign bonds (units scaled value-preservingly, re-denominated
  to the survivor's currency), the national enacted-law book with its money
  fields converted, the military (units, command groups, formations, arsenal,
  manpower), national-pool politicians, tariff records and the legislative
  corpus. Pending bills lapse with the state that proposed them.
- Non-bloc organisation memberships transfer to the survivor; which alliance
  the unified state joins remains the settlement's decision.
- Party treasuries and carried region party-organisation treasuries convert at
  the merge FX scale, exactly once.
- The winner's economic regime carries: a planned absorbed economy makes the
  unified state planned, with the Gosbank directive preserved and planning
  panels operated through the survivor's economy and finance portfolios.
- When the absorbed country was open to players, the unified state opens to
  players at the same moment.
