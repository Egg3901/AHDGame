## Fixed

- A country whose legislature ratified entry into a bloc war (the NATO and
  Warsaw Pact call-to-arms path) deployed its army and then never attacked:
  every decision slot went to organization votes, tariffs, and embargoes,
  because the choice to conduct the war scored below all of them and only the
  single top-ranked choice acts. War conduct and war exhaustion now rank above
  routine diplomacy for a country already fighting, so an ally with ready
  forces at the front files its own offensives at the existing cooldown pace,
  and a government whose approval or army has collapsed seeks peace instead of
  filing another tariff. The readiness, approval, and pending-offensive gates
  are unchanged, and a maximal alliance vote can still take the slot.
