Cut from development.

### Added

- **Vote suppression.** Spend $70,000 from the war chest and 5 actions to take
  2.5% off a rival's vote in one state for 8 turns. It applies to the projection
  and to the wave from the same rule, so the board never shows a lead the night
  is about to remove. However many rivals converge on one candidate, suppression
  can never take more than 15% of a state's vote off them.
- **Demographic turnout suppression.** Spend $50,000 and 4 actions to take 1.5
  points off one demographic group's turnout in one state. It is indirect on
  purpose: the group's turnout falls for everyone there, including you, so it
  only pays when aimed at a group a rival depends on. It fades on the same slow
  decay every turnout modifier does rather than expiring on a timer.
- Both sit behind the opponent row in State operations, alongside the local
  attack. Turnout suppression asks which group after it asks which state, from
  the same list the canvassing desk offers.
- Rapid Response blunts ads and vote suppression. It does not cover turnout
  suppression, which acts on the electorate rather than on the candidate, and
  the panel now says so rather than implying blanket cover.

### Fixed

- **The primary board now reads turnout modifiers.** Party GOTV and player
  canvassing have always moved the actual result, because the turn engine reads
  the turnout modifiers they write. The projection never did, so their effect
  was invisible on the board and the numbers you were looking at were the ones
  from before your canvassing landed. Both now read the same channel the same
  way. Expect projected primary numbers to shift in states where turnout has
  been worked.
