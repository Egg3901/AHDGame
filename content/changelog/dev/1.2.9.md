Cut from development. Raised on ticket #1107.

## Fixed

- The national budget page only carried written copy for a handful of
  countries, and everything else silently fell back to the United States
  labels. Playing East Germany meant a page headed "Federal Budget", subtitled
  "United States Federal Finances", listing "Payroll Tax" and a "Debt Ceiling"
  over DDR marks. The republic now has its own set: Staatshaushaltsplan, the
  state enterprise levy, the product-related levy, social insurance
  contributions, and a borrowing limit, with descriptions that match how the
  planned economy actually raises and spends the money.
- The regional officials API matched region ids exactly as they arrived in the
  URL, while the region page itself uppercased first. A lower-case id returned
  nothing and the parliament section rendered empty even though the deputies
  were there. Both paths now normalise the same way.
- Both officials lookups now filter by country as well as by region. Region
  codes are not unique across the world, so a shared code could pull another
  country's officeholders into the list.

## Not changed

- National debt does accrue in East Germany. The treasury phase runs over every
  country's budget with no exclusions, and the DDR's principal, deficit, and
  credit rating all move with it. Two deputies of the same party sitting for
  Berlin is also correct: proportional allocation gives one row per deputy, so
  a party that wins two seats fills two rows.
