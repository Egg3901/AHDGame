## Changed

- State Organization now states that per-state investment counts in the
  presidential primary **and** in the general election, with both caps shown:
  up to +25% in-state vote weight in the primary and up to +15% in the general.
- The screens now explain the timing: levels do not reset when the primary
  ends, you can keep building during the general, and levels only drop to 25%
  of investment after the presidential general resolves. Organization is per
  player and per state.
- The state detail panel shows a projected general bonus next to the projected
  primary bonus, and the map tooltip lists both.

## Renamed

- Campaign operations: "Digital Ads" is now "Television" and "Digital Ops" is
  now "Direct Mail". The Broadcast branch now reads as radio, newsreel and
  press. Names only, no change to cost or effect.

No mechanics changed. These are labels and explanations of behaviour the engine
already had.
