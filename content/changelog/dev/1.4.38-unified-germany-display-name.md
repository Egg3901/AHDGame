## Fixed

A country renamed by an event is now called by its new name everywhere, not just
on the pages that could look it up.

Reunification already recorded that the unified state is called Germany, and the
country pages read it — they are rendered on the server, where the record is
reachable. Everything else is drawn in the browser, and a page in the browser
cannot reach that record, so all of it fell back to the name the country was
built with: the navbar, the nation switcher, the Select a Nation cards, the world
map, the country map header, the shortage board and the world banking hub. The
result was one country with two names, and which one a player got depended on
where they happened to be standing.

The new name now travels out to the page along with the rest of the world's
setup, so every one of those surfaces reads it.

Era names still work underneath. The Federal Republic goes on showing as West
Germany in a year when there were two Germanies; an event-driven rename answers a
different question — what the country is called NOW — and only that can overrule
the compiled name.
