## Fixed

Every party's organisation record in Germany has been put back under its own
party, and the merge that scrambled them will not do it again.

A regional party record is filed under a name built from its region and its
party. Reunification changes both: the absorbed state's parties are renumbered so
they do not clash with the survivor's, and East Berlin was folded into Berlin. The
merge updated what each record SAID, but the name it was filed under cannot be
changed once written, so every western German record ended up filed under one
party while describing another.

Nothing warned, because the two screens that show Org look records up
differently. The region's party list goes by what the record says, and was right
all along. The party's own page goes by the filing name, and so displayed the
neighbouring party's organisation — and Build Org, which also goes by the filing
name, moved that other party's number. In North Rhine-Westphalia the SED's record
was filed under the SPD's name and the two had been reading across each other
ever since the merge.

The merge now re-files these records instead of only relabelling them, in two
passes — the renumbering is a shuffle, where one party takes the name another is
about to give up, so everything moves aside before anything lands. Where a name
is already taken by a record that is staying put, the merge leaves both alone
rather than overwrite one: a key that still disagrees can be found and fixed,
whereas a party's organisation written over is simply gone.
