Cut from development.

## Fixed

- Sending or transferring party and caucus treasury funds, subscribing to or
  redeeming from an investment fund, and relocating a corporation across a
  currency border could all fail with a blank server error when the database
  handed the request a stale session. Those paths now retry on a fresh session
  automatically, the same way sector splits already did.
- Appointing a cabinet member at the same moment as another appointment
  reported a server error to whoever lost the race. It now explains the seat or
  appointee was just claimed, and the acting-appointment charge is refunded as
  before.
- Two corporations founded in the same instant could collide on the same
  generated ticker symbol and fail. The loser now draws a new ticker and
  retries.
- Presidential races could fail to re-initialize their vote tally on worlds
  whose tally records predate the current id scheme.
