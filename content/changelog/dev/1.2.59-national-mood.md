## Added

A retrospective economic channel in presidential general elections. Each
accumulation turn, national unemployment, poverty, inflation, and the real
income trend are priced against fixed anchors into a single signed share
shift, applied to the incumbent party's candidate and offset across the rest
of the field in proportion to their shares. A strong economy helps the
incumbent party; a weak one costs it. The shift is capped at eight points in
either direction.

Term fatigue scales the penalty side only. A party seeking its second term
takes the reading as it is; the penalty grows for a third term and again for
a fourth or later. The bonus side never scales, so long tenures stay possible
when times are good.

The National Mood panel on the presidential race page shows the current
reading, each component's contribution, and the fatigue multiplier when it
applies, before and after election day. The elections wiki documents the
channel, its cap, and the fact that moving the underlying metrics through
legislation is what moves the reading.
