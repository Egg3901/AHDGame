## Added

- `GET /api/public/v1/country/[code]/economy/history` combines per-turn
  interest-rate, inflation, and GDP-growth series with annual fiscal and
  sovereign-debt snapshots.
- `GET /api/public/v1/trade/flows` returns bounded world trade history with
  optional country, commodity, and turn-range filters.
- `GET /api/public/v1/openapi.json` publishes an OpenAPI 3.1 contract for every
  route in the tested public v1 catalog.

## Privacy and stability

- History routes use the existing API-key guard, durable rate limits, and
  bounded result sizes.
- Trade history omits raw bilateral matrices and reachable market books.
- Fiscal history omits actor ids, tax bases, policy drivers, and enacted-law
  implementation details.
- `/meta` and OpenAPI now share one route catalog, so discovery surfaces cannot
  drift independently.
