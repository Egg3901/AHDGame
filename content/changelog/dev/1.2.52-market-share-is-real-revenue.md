Cut from development.

## Fixed

- Market share divided your revenue by an "unowned pool" — a figure that grew
  with the economy every turn, was never drawn down when a company built
  capacity, and represented demand nobody earns. So a company could hold most of
  a market where every unit it made was selling and still read a low share
  against a large "unowned" slice it could never claim. Market share is now your
  revenue over the total revenue of every real producer in that market
  (state + sector), so the slices add up to the real players and a sole producer
  honestly reads 100%. The Market Position pie drops the "unowned" slice
  entirely.

## Changed

- The same real-revenue definition now drives every place market share is shown
  or used — the sector and company pages, the state economy view, the Discord
  market-share command, and the dominance/antitrust tolls — so no two surfaces
  disagree any more.
- Because shares are no longer diluted by a phantom pool, dominance shows up
  where it actually exists: a company that holds most of a market now reads as
  dominant and is treated as such.
- Expansion is building plants where buyers have room, shown on each sector, so
  the game no longer suggests "claiming" an unowned pool that cannot be claimed.
