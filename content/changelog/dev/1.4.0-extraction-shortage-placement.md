Cut from development.

## Fixed

- States with idle seeded deposit capacity can now receive a focused NPC mining
  corporation when the resource is in genuine global shortage and no existing
  extraction sector occupies the state. The placement uses the established NPP
  corporation founding path, including its NPP CEO, sector creation, capacity
  accounting, and unowned-market drawdown.
- The standard extraction retool pass is now explicitly limited to NPC-owned
  corporations. Player-owned extraction sectors remain protected even when an
  NPP caretaker is operating the corporation.

## Guarded

- Retooling now runs every turn with up to seven direct shortage retools plus
  the existing three expected-revenue retools. The total cap of ten is fast
  enough to address chronic shortages without changing a 1959 mining fleet all
  at once. New corporation placement is counted separately and capped at three
  per turn.
- Placement requires a global supply-to-demand ratio below 0.6, positive state
  deposit capacity, and state supply below 98 percent of that capacity.
- Tests cover idle iron placement, player-sector protection, both throughput
  caps, focused founding capacity, and saturated-state rejection.
