Cut from development. Raised on ticket #1106.

## Fixed

- Picking "Taxes" when proposing a state bill, or when queueing one through a
  party legislator from the governor's office, showed the category but left the
  legislation type list empty. There was nothing to choose, so the bill could
  never be written. The state tax rate laws had been authored into the law
  book, but the law book is only copied into a world when that world is
  created. A world already running never received them, so the only tax laws it
  held were the federal ones, and those are national scope. Live worlds are now
  topped up with any law added since they were seeded, and the state tax rates
  appear where they should.
- The same top-up covers every future law addition, so a new law no longer
  needs a world reset to become proposable.

## Not changed

- Existing laws are left exactly as they are. The top-up only adds what is
  missing, so nothing already enacted or edited is overwritten.
