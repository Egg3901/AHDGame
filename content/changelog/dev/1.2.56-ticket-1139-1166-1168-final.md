## What changed

- A signed supply agreement is no longer capped a second time by the anonymous
  order book after its named buyer's physical demand has already been checked.
- The USSR explicitly enables its Supreme Soviet no-confidence route. The
  ruling-party guard remains in place for one-party-state motions.
- National tax statutes now show the same current rate as the budget and label
  the enacted destination while a large tax change is still phasing in.
