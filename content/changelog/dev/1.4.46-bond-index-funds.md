  Twelve new index funds hold bonds instead of shares: a government bond
  fund for each of the eight fund countries, and global investment grade
  sovereign, emerging markets sovereign, investment grade corporate, and
  high yield corporate funds. They buy from the bond market pool at its ask,
  earn coupons into their NAV, and sell to the pool at its bid to pay
  redemptions. Any fund short of cash for a queued redemption now sells bonds
  after shares.

Cut from development. PR #1338. Phase 4 of the bond market pool; earlier phases were #1330, #1333, and #1334.

## Added

- `IndexFundKind` gains `bond`. `BOND_FUND_DEFINITIONS` in `fundDefinitions.ts`
  declares each fund's `bondUniverse`: issuer type, inclusive rating band
  (`minRating` is the worst grade allowed, `maxRating` the best), and whether
  it holds home paper only. Sovereign ratings come from the budget's
  `creditRating`; corporate ratings from `creditRatingSnapshot`, read as BBB
  when unrated.
- Allocation rule for bond funds (`fundAllocation.ts`): no equities, the
  whole backing in the reserve bucket, bond target equal to backing less the
  five percent cash buffer.
- `loadBondFundCandidates` in `fundBondReserve.ts`: the universe filter, home
  or tradable-currency scope, breadth ranking, equal-notional slices across
  up to 48 issues. Bond funds always use the liquidity-target deployment.
- `sellFundBondHoldingsForCash` (`src/lib/bonds/sellFundBondUnits.ts`): sells
  a fund's bond holdings to the pool at its bid, largest position first, as far
  as the pool can pay; units return to the pool, cash lands on the fund in
  anchor terms, transaction kind `bond_sale`. Wired into queued redemptions
  after the equity sale for every fund.
- Migration `2026-09-03-bond-fund-seed` inserts the twelve funds on the same
  footing as the equity funds (initial NAV, reserve position, seed cash).
- Fund tables label bond funds as `US Bonds` or `Global Bonds`.

## Changed

- The fund cron skips constituent selection, target rebalancing, and
  cross-fund rebalancing for bond funds. NAV, backing, bond deployment,
  redemptions, and snapshots run as for every fund.
- NPP and pension scheme investing are unchanged: they still buy broad
  equity funds only.
