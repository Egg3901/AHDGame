Cut from development.

## Changed

- The Market Position ring groups every NPP-run corporation into one arc. Your
  own company, and any other player-owned rivals, keep their own colours in
  front of it. In a sector where the autonomous corps hold most of the market,
  a 5.7% share was previously one wedge among eighteen and impossible to pick
  out; it now sits against a plain background.

- The list under the ring puts player-owned corporations at the top. The NPP
  field collapses into a single row carrying its combined share and how many
  corps are behind it. Open it to see the roster; it stays closed by default,
  because seventeen near-equal rows are what buried the rivals that matter.

- A corporation being minded by an NPP caretaker stays with the player
  corporations. The autonomy brain drives it, but its owner is still a player,
  and filing it under the NPP field would tell that owner their company had
  disappeared into the background.

## Fixed

- Rival colours no longer shift when the field changes. Colours were handed out
  by position in the list, so a competitor leaving the sector repainted every
  company after it and "the blue one" stopped meaning anything. A corporation
  now keeps its colour.

- Two rivals can no longer be drawn in the same colour. The old ring cycled a
  list of nine hues, so the tenth company reused the first one's colour, and
  past that the repeats stacked up.

- The rival colours are legible to colourblind readers. Two of the old hues were
  effectively identical to a red-green colourblind reader, and another pair was
  hard to separate with full colour vision. The replacements were measured
  against the card background in both light and dark themes.
