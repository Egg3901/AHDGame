Cut from development.

## Fixed

- Supply-agreement shortfall damages were invisible. A corporation supplying
  under an agreement whose contracted volume exceeds what its plants can make
  is charged damages on the shortfall every single turn, which can cancel its
  entire per-turn income while the income statement still reads healthy. There
  was no notification, and nothing on the corporation page said it was
  happening, so the only symptom was cash that never grew.
- The paying owner now receives a notice each turn damages are assessed,
  naming the commodity, the contracted and produced volumes, and the amount
  charged. Damages that were owed but suppressed by the solvency floor are
  reported too, so a corporation that cannot pay still learns what it owes.
  Company-run and state-run corporations are skipped. The notice is on a
  cooldown per contract, so a contract that stays oversized reminds you
  periodically rather than filing the same line every turn.
