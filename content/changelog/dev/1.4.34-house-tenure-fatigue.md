Cut from development.

## Fixed

- Long service used to subtract a flat charge from a representative's political
  influence and favorability, one that grew with every term and stopped only at
  a total wipe. Because it was a fixed charge against standings that are
  themselves modest, it fell hardest on ordinary members and barely touched the
  most prominent ones: a representative of average influence had none of it left
  after five terms, and under half their favorability. Time in office now takes
  a small share of both, so every officeholder keeps the same proportion of
  their standing however long they have served, and no length of service can
  reduce anyone to nothing.

- In the 1962 United States House the old charge had reached the point of
  erasure. Both returning nominees in Florida had contested every prior cycle,
  which left them with no influence at all and under half their favorability,
  and a first-time nominee for a party holding under two percent of the state's
  registered voters was carrying 88 percent of the vote and every one of the
  eight seats. Thirty-five of the forty-nine open United States races were
  running under the same charge, and delegations across the country were being
  handed to brand-new parties on the strength of it. Those races now turn on
  organization, registration and the candidates themselves, as they were meant
  to.

## Changed

- Time in office is now a modest tilt against entrenchment rather than a term
  limit in disguise. A representative at the maximum still carries 85 percent of
  their influence and favorability into the race, and a first-term member is
  untouched, exactly as before. Long-serving incumbents will hold their seats
  more often than they have been; they are no longer removed by the vote engine
  rather than by voters.

- The player wiki never described any of this. Both the election mechanics page
  and the formulas reference now explain what holding a seat costs an
  officeholder at the ballot box, and how it differs from the party-tenure
  fatigue priced in the National Mood channel.
