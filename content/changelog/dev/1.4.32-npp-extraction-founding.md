## Fixed

- AI-run mining companies never founded a first mine on an unexploited deposit.
  The founding scorer ranked every candidate site on unmet local demand, and an
  extraction site's local demand is zero by construction, so every mine was
  filtered out of candidacy before it could be considered. A rich coal deposit
  in a state that does not itself burn coal stayed unmined for the life of the
  world, even while other countries were short of coal.
- A mine's output is a traded commodity, so founding value is now deposit
  richness against how short that commodity is, not local consumption. The
  state deposit haircut still caps what a new mine actually produces, and the
  existing reinvestment leg deepens it over later turns if the output sells.
  Demand-side sectors are unchanged.
