## Fixed

- Curing a defaulted corporate bond with cash paid out characters, imperial
  characters and companies, then marked the whole bond settled. Units held by
  an index fund or an AI-run party were never paid, but they still vanished
  with the closed bond.
- The issuer was charged for those units either way, because the cure price is
  the bond's whole face value rather than a per-holder total. So the money was
  not merely uncollected, it was destroyed: it left the issuer and arrived
  nowhere.
- Both holder types are now repaid their share of the principal before the
  bond is cured, into the same accounts that receive their ordinary coupon and
  maturity payments. The amount the issuer pays is unchanged.
