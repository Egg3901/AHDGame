Cut from development.

## Fixed

- The attack cost and estimated capture shown on each rival's card in the state
  market board now match what the attack itself charges. Under the plants market
  the card was still quoting the old revenue-based price while the attack was
  priced on the target's capacity at the build-price floor, so a card could read
  "Cost: $889K" and the attack then fail with "Insufficient capital. Attack costs
  $2,588,197." The board now sizes the attack against the defender's capacity and
  applies the same build-price floor the attack route does, so the number you see
  is the number you pay.
