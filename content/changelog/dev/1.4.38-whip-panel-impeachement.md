Cut from development.

## Added

- A motion to vacate the chair can be whipped. It sits on the Leadership tab
  beside no-confidence motions, for player members and for blocs alike, and the
  two buttons say what they do: vacate the chair, or keep the Speaker. Caucus
  chairs get the same directive over their own members.

- Impeachment votes can be whipped, and the whip follows the case. While the
  lower chamber is voting to impeach, the directive falls on your members there.
  Once the case advances to trial it moves to the upper chamber, and the two
  stages keep separate whip counts, so spending your attempts on the impeachment
  does not leave you silent at the trial. A governor's trial is whipped from the
  state party instead, by the state legislators who actually sit in judgement.

- Blocs now take a side on a motion to vacate once its voting window closes. A
  bloc of the Speaker's own party defends the chair, an opposing major party
  moves to vacate, and everything else is decided by how far apart the two sit.
  A bloc with nothing to go on keeps the Speaker.

## Fixed

- A motion to vacate could not carry. It needs an absolute majority of every
  seat in the chamber, but only players ever cast a ballot on one, so the bar
  sat permanently out of reach no matter how the vote went. Blocs voting at the
  close of the window is what makes the motion a real instrument again.

- Whipping a member on one of these votes and then having them vote for
  themselves left the "Whipped by Party" badge on their ballot. Casting a vote
  now clears it, and the badge offers the same revert other votes do.

- A whip issued during an earlier motion to vacate counted against the attempt
  limit of the next one, because every motion is filed under the same record.
  Attempts are now counted per motion. The same fault was fixed for caucus
  whips on Speaker and chamber leadership races, where it had never been.

- The Whip Room could list a leadership vote on the strength of members who sit
  in another country's legislature. Party numbers are reused between countries
  and chambers share their names, so a party could be offered a target it had
  nobody eligible to vote on. Every whip surface now scopes its members to the
  country it belongs to.
