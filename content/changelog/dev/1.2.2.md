## Fixed

- Refinancing a defaulted bond was priced off the credit rating the default had
  just set, so the replacement bond carried the worst coupon in the table no
  matter how healthy the company was underneath. Since a refinance rolls your
  existing holders over and hands you no cash, that tripled your interest bill
  and left you no better able to pay it. The rating now reads your real position.
- A refinance quote counted the money it does not give you. The replacement
  bond's face value was being added to your cash before the rating was worked
  out, so a company with nothing in the bank scored as though it had just been
  handed its own principal.
- A refinance quote left out the debt being rolled when working out whether you
  can cover your interest. If the defaulted bond was your only bond, you scored
  a perfect result for having no interest to pay, on the very bond you had just
  failed to pay.
- The region demographics tab printed a bare "%" next to a blank turnout cell,
  so a dimension with no turnout estimate read as an empty percentage instead of
  no data. In chart and table view the summary strip listed only the GOTV swing,
  which is zero until a party campaigns, so a full electorate looked like it had
  zero turnout. The strip now carries the turnout rate and shows the swing only
  where one exists. (ticket #1121)
