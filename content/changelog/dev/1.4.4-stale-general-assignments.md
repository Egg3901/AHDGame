Cut from development.

## Fixed

- A commissioned general who relocated to another country kept everything they
  held in the one they left. Relocation already resigns your office, your cabinet
  seat, your central bank chair, your party posts and your company, but it had no
  military step at all, so the saved command went on naming a character who now
  lived abroad. The consequences all followed from that one dangling id: the
  defence office could not draw a row for someone its roster no longer held, so
  the commander count stood above an empty list with no way to remove anyone;
  every later edit to any command in the country was refused because of that
  name; and authority to post generals and name Theater Commanders still read
  straight off it, so a player who had emigrated went on commanding the army they
  had left. Moving country now takes you off every command roster, vacates any
  command you led, drops your conflict postings, and hands your divisions back to
  that country's General Staff.

- Dismissal already did all of that correctly and relocation did none of it.
  Both now run the same cascade, so the two cannot drift apart again.

- A migration hands back what the generals who already left were still holding:
  seven Russian warships and one British brigade, none of which could be sent to
  a front by anybody, because the officer they answered to had emigrated.

## Changed

- Relocating tells you when the move cost you a command, alongside the office,
  company and party it already reports.
