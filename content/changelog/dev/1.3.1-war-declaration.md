Cut from development.

## Fixed

- A declaration of war against a fellow member of your own alliance is now refused.
  Every other bar on declaring war was already enforced, the target, the war goal,
  the cooldown and the truce, but nothing compared the two countries' membership, so
  one NATO state could take another NATO state to war.
- The refusal names the treaty rather than a bare error, so it is clear which
  alliance is holding and that withdrawing from it lifts the bar.

## Changed

- The declaration panel greys out every ally in the target picker and labels it with
  the alliance, alongside the truce labels it already showed. The bar is stated up
  front instead of being discovered after a declaration is filed.
- Membership is read from the live treaty roll for the running era, not a fixed
  table, so a country that acceded last turn is an ally this turn and one that
  withdrew becomes a legitimate target again.
- A ratified declaration is re-checked at the moment it takes effect. A declaration
  sits before the chambers for turns, and if the two countries became allies while it
  waited, the war no longer opens.
- Non-alignment is not treated as an alliance. Two countries that no bloc seats can
  still go to war, which keeps the modern eras playable: they carry NATO with no
  eastern counterpart, so most of the board reads as non-aligned there.
