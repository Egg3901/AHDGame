## State enterprises explain excess capacity

The command-economy director panel now warns when an enterprise is using less than half of its capacity. If an investment request is still active, it explains that more investment adds capacity and upkeep before buyers exist, tells the director to set the request to zero, and links directly to the enterprise where low-demand regional plants can be mothballed or abandoned. It also explains that persistent low utilization contributes to pressure for market reform.

The planned-economy guide now names those real controls instead of telling players to use a partial capacity-closing action that does not exist.

## National Corporation revenue is earned revenue

The National Corporation overview no longer counts a plant's full capacity nameplate as gross revenue. In the plants economy it now sums realized sales, so unsold production and mothballed plants cannot inflate the headline or the R&D funding guidance. Legacy worlds retain their existing calculation.
