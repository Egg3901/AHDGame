## What changed

- Autonomous governments can cast opinion-based organization votes and table
  trade agreements, aid, sanctions, statements, and alliance war-entry calls.
- Hostile or improving relations can produce targeted tariff bills and bounded
  ministerial embargoes, with pair cooldowns and real trade dependence limiting
  escalation.
- War support uses material aid. War entry requires alliance approval, domestic
  ratification, ready forces, fiscal room, and public approval.
- Admins can keep the planner off, inspect shadow decisions, or activate real
  actions from the feature-gates panel.
