Cut from development.

## Added

- The **Support Campaign** dialog on a presidential race now takes an amount:
  **x1**, **x5**, or **Max**. Max is the largest number of contributions the
  player's campaign funds and action points can cover. Steps that are out of
  reach are shown disabled with their cost rather than hidden, so the row does
  not reshuffle as rivals push the price up.
- A batch is priced as exactly the run of single contributions it replaces, on
  both the money and the action point. The funds curve was already additive
  across a purchase, so it needed nothing; the action cost is charged per click
  rather than on the merged total, because rounding the merged total would have
  handed low national influence players a discount for pressing x5 that a high
  influence player could never get. Batching saves clicks and nothing else.
- Max is resolved on the server, not taken from the number the page previewed.
  A campaign's strength moves whenever anyone contributes to it, and its price
  moves with it, so a rival's contribution between opening the dialog and
  confirming now produces a slightly smaller batch instead of an outright
  failure.
- The **Actions** page has a card prompting players to endorse a candidate for
  president. It appears only for players with a live presidential race in their
  own country and no active endorsement in it, lists the leading candidates with
  their party, and records the endorsement without leaving the page. During a
  primary it lists only the player's own party, matching the rule the
  endorsement endpoint enforces.

## Changed

- The electoral map is now the first thing on a presidential race page, directly
  under the year and race title. It previously sat below the national mood
  gauge, the factor ledger and the battleground shell, all three of which
  decompose the result the map shows.

## Technical

- `POST /api/campaigns/[id]/campaign-strength` accepts an optional
  `{ clicks: 1..100 | "max" }` body. An absent or empty body still means one
  contribution, so existing callers are unchanged. Batches are a single debit
  and a single audit row, tagged with the click count.
- New `GET /api/elections/endorsement-prompt` returns the endorsement nudge for
  the calling character, or nothing. It filters out every case the endorsement
  endpoint would reject, so the card never shows a button that fails.
