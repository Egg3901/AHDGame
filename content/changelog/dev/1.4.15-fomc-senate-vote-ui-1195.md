## Added

- A "Senate confirmations" panel on the FOMC committee page. It lists every
  pending Federal Reserve nomination the President has sent up — the nominee, the
  seat (chair or governor), whether they are a player or an NPP, the running
  tally, and when voting closes. A sitting Senator sees For / Against / Abstain
  controls and casts a real ballot; everyone else sees the pending nomination and
  its tally. Confirmation is now decided by the Senate's actual votes, not only by
  automatic party-line votes.
