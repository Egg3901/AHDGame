## Added

- A new Factor Ledger card on the presidential general view. Pick a candidate
  and see their projected votes split into named factors: state lean and
  standing, policy fit, name recognition, turnout, persuasion swing, spoiler
  effect, national mood, and campaign. A final line accounts for rounding.
- Each factor is a signed bar: positive pushes the candidate up, negative
  drags them down. The bars always add back to the votes the engine stored,
  so the breakdown is honest, not an estimate.
- For a candidate you own, the card also shows where their support comes from
  by voter bucket, plus the breakdown in the closest states. For everyone
  else you see the national picture only.

## Guarded

- The ledger is read-only and descriptive. It is built from the exact numbers
  the vote engine already computed for the turn, never a second calculation,
  so it cannot change who wins. Adding it leaves every existing race result
  identical.
- The breakdown is checked to reconstruct the stored votes exactly before it
  is shown, and races that ran before the ledger existed simply show no card.
