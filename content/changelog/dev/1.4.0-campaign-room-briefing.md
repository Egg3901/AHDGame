Cut from development.

## Added

- A Campaign Room briefing at the top of your own campaign page. Five cards at
  a glance:
  - Path to victory. In the primary it shows your delegates against the number
    you need to clinch, plus the field of delegate leaders. In the general it
    shows your electoral votes against the majority line and your closest
    states.
  - Cash runway. Your treasury, your net per turn, and how many turns you can
    keep spending before the money runs out.
  - Coalition weakness. The voter groups where your support runs thinnest,
    read straight from the same breakdown the results page already shows.
  - Operations saturation. How built out each of your campaign operations is
    against its ceiling.
  - Action tradeoffs. Each next upgrade you could buy, what it costs in funds
    and actions, and what it does.

## Unchanged

- The briefing only composes figures the race already computed. It never
  recalculates votes, delegates, or electoral votes, so no result moves because
  of it.
- Only the nominee and campaign managers see the briefing. A running mate,
  a party viewer, and the public do not: the coalition read is exactly the
  intel an opponent should not have, so it stays behind the same fog of war as
  your exact campaign levels.
