## Fixed

- The Influence tab priced every nation with a dollar sign even when the
  organisation fund is denominated in another currency, so the Warsaw Pact
  quoted a cost per point in dollars directly above a commit form that pays in
  Soviet rubles. The per-point cost, the turn ceiling cost, the cheapest-to-flip
  ranking, and recent play amounts are now tied to the fund's actual currency,
  so a Warsaw Pact dossier reads in rubles alongside the fund balance and the
  commit box, which already did.
