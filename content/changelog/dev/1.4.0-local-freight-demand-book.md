Cut from development.

## Fixed

- Freight demand now includes price-tolerant loads that could not move because
  the origin state's freight capacity was full. The consumed load remains a
  separate measurement, so network reporting still shows what actually moved.
- Freight demand is booked into the same state market where local logistics
  sectors sell. A state no longer reports output stuck for lack of freight
  while withholding that demand from the logistics sectors able to add local
  capacity.

## Guarded

- Regression coverage follows a real NY haul from sourcing through state
  demand booking and local logistics clearing.
- A second case verifies that 40 TEU of booked NY demand clears 40 percent of
  100 TEU of local freight output.
