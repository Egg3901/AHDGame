## What changed

- Added a two-axis Governance Style balance to the national political-metrics overview.
- Left and right reflect policy direction, while institutional strength runs independently from Failed State to Healthy Democracy.
- Institutions now gain distinct flavor as they fade, strain, recover, or become self-reinforcing.
- Lopsided lower-chamber control and long executive tenure weaken democratic health without changing a nation's left-right position.
- Governance Style is shown for player-run liberal democracies and remains separate from one-party-state mechanics.
