## Fixed

- The influence commit form accepts the K/M/B/T shorthand every figure around
  it is printed in, so a player who reads "spend at least $4.0M" can type 4.0M
  rather than 4,000,000. The field was a plain-units number box, so "49m" could
  not even be typed, and players who typed 1 or 49 meaning millions saw "buys
  0.00" and a button that would not press (ticket #1236). A line under the field
  now says the shorthand works.
- The shared money parser behind the field also strips comma grouping, so
  49,000,000 lands as forty-nine million rather than being refused, and it
  normalizes full-width digits and letters so IME input parses. Every other
  money input that already used the parser gains the shorthand at the same
  time; no amount that parsed before changes what it means.
