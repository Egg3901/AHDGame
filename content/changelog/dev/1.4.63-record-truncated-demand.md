  Two 1.5x demand caps set recorded demand to at most 1.5x supply before
  anything reads it, so no commodity could ever report worse than a third

  prices changes.

Cut from development. PR #1473.

## Added

- `computeRawSupplyDemand` returns `demandTruncated` and
  `computeHouseholdConsumption` returns `truncated`: per commodity, the units
  the `PLANTS_LEDGER_DEMAND_SUPPLY_CAP` and `PLANTS_HOUSEHOLD_SUPPLY_CAP`
  passes removed this turn.
- `commodityPrices` and `commodityPriceHistory` carry
  `demandTruncatedUnits` and `latentShortageMultiple` (demand plus truncated,
  over supply) whenever a cap engaged. Coal, timber, fertilizers and rare
  earth, which sit at exactly 0.667 supply over demand and scarcity 1.917
  because of the cap, will show their real gap from the next turn.
- Vital signs `production` gains `demandTruncatedCommodities` and
  `maxLatentShortageMultiple`.

## Not changed

- The scarcity integrator, NPP shortage scores and prices still read the
  capped demand. Feeding them the latent figure is a balance decision that
  needs a sim run first; this change makes the decision measurable.
