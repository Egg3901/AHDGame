Cut from development.

## Fixed

- A country absorbed into another no longer acts on the world stage. Its
  alignment stops drifting, it stops asking to join blocs, and it is no longer
  pulled out of blocs it still sits in. Reunification left West Germany dissolved
  but still leaning Western, and it applied to NATO two turns later.
- Nations that are not modelled as full countries, such as Canada, the Benelux
  and Albania, are unaffected and keep applying to blocs as before. Being absent
  from the country registry is not the same as having ceased to exist.
- East Germany showed a West German flag, which rendered as the letters "DE" on
  the platforms that spell out a flag they cannot draw. It now carries its own
  code.
- Bloc member lists were the one country surface still reading the name a
  country was created with. They now use the same answer every other screen
  does, which fixes two labels at once: a state renamed by a reunification is
  listed under its current name, and in a Cold War world the Soviet Union is
  listed as the Soviet Union rather than as Russia.

## Changed

- A member list shows the flag a runtime event gave a country, where one has
  been set, instead of its original flag. Members the game does not model as
  full countries, such as Canada and Jordan, are named and flagged exactly as
  before.
