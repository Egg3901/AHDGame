## Defence contracts stop stranding small suppliers (ticket #1083)

A player with naval lines accepted a Ministry of Defence order and it delivered
nothing for eighteen turns. Not for lack of money — the appropriation held
billions — and not a re-tooled plant: the plant was certified naval and the
order was for naval.

Delivery output is `floor(revenue-derived lots)`, and their plants produced
about 0.17 lots per turn. `floor(0.17)` is zero, so every turn the plant's
fractional output was computed and then discarded, and the order never moved.
The mechanic worked only for large suppliers; any plant under one lot per turn
was frozen out of procurement entirely, permanently, with only a generic
"producing no output" note that reads as false to a CEO who built the line.

Deliveries now carry the sub-lot remainder from turn to turn (`deliveryCarry`,
always under one), so a plant producing a third of a lot per turn ships one lot
every three turns instead of never. Long-run throughput is unchanged — this
banks output that was being thrown away, it does not manufacture new materiel,
and whole lots the appropriation cannot pay for are still dropped rather than
stockpiled. The supplier and order-book panels now show the true fractional
rate, so a slow line reads as slow rather than broken.

## Reachable market scope (ticket #1077)

Design doc: ops-knowledge `reachable-market-scope-design`.

### The report

A US player saw the Commodities tab list Food Products at D/S 2.88 with the
copy "scarcer, which sells at a premium. Supply these", then found every market
in Build a new sector reporting "ROOM FOR 0 farms / 0 units/day open" and the
expand dialog saying the market was oversupplied.

### Root cause

Three surfaces read three different market scopes, none of them the scope the
engine clears on.

- The shortage panel read `nationalDemand / nationalSupply` (country, production
  only): 863,550.81 / 299,765.18 = 2.8808.
- Both build gates read `max(0, globalDemand - globalSupply)`, min over output
  legs. World food: 14,360,715.65 demand against 21,071,946.52 supply, so the
  gap was 0 and agriculture's single `{food: 0.5}` leg zeroed headroom
  everywhere on Earth.
- The engine clears each sector against its home country's book from
  `market/tradePartition`: `max(0, D - imports) + exports`.

The world aggregate sums three populations that cannot substitute for each
other: reachable supply, supply behind an embargo, and supply from countries
outside `COUNTRY_ORDER` that trade with nobody. Measured on prod at turn 97:

| commodity   | global D/S | old gate  | US-reachable D/S | untraded supply | blocked from US |
| ----------- | ---------- | --------- | ---------------- | --------------- | --------------- |
| oil         | 0.82       | 0 room    | **1.82**         | 0               | 164,002         |
| steel       | 1.25       | 822,839   | **2.27**         | **1,412,598**   | 696,494         |
| electronics | 1.70       | 2,268,406 | 1.63             | 169,266         | 1,160,097       |
| plastics    | 1.68       | 308,298   | 1.18             | 57,130          | 62,240          |

Ukrainian steel alone is 41% of world supply from a country the clearing engine
never sees. The correction runs both ways: plastics room drops from 308,298 to
51,687 once unreachable demand is also removed. This is a correctness fix, not
a buff.

### Changes

- `market/tradePartition.reachableDemandUnits` is now the single definition of
  sellable demand, shared by the engine's clearing books and every read surface,
  so a quote and a settlement cannot drift.
- New `trade/reachableBook`: per-country, per-commodity books in units, carrying
  `supply`, `demand`, `imports`, `exports`, plus `blockedSupply` and
  `untradedSupply` for disclosure only. Those two are never added back into a
  ratio; they exist so the UI can name what it excluded instead of showing a
  bare 0 that reads as a dead market.
- `commodityPriceTurn` builds the books from the SAME pre-convergence balances
  the clearing ran on, immediately before `applyTradeConvergence` mutates them
  in place, and persists them on the per-turn `tradeFlowSnapshots` doc. Reading
  post-convergence would understate every importer's deficit by the k = 0.5
  convergence factor (verified: imports / stored deficit is exactly 2.0000 for
  all eleven net importers).
- New `market/sectorDemandGap.sectorDemandGapUnits` extracts the min-over-legs
  computation that `expand-suggestions/route.ts` and `queries/sectorDetail.ts`
  each carried their own copy of.
- Both gates now resolve the gap per candidate market's country. `sectorDetail`
  uses the sector's home country.
- A world with no persisted book (has not run a turn since 1.1.2) falls back to
  the previous global behaviour rather than reporting no room anywhere.
- Lazy `{turn: -1}` index on `tradeFlowSnapshots` for the latest-book read.

### Deliberately not in this change

- Commodities page map rework (commodity dropdown, choropleth, flow arcs). It
  belongs on top of a correct scope layer.
- The shortage heat map's country lens still quotes the stored national
  balance, which is a price-formation intermediate rather than physical unmet
  demand. Next PR on this branch.
- Adding UKR/BLR/BAL to `COUNTRY_ORDER`. Owner decision: it would put 6.1M
  units/day of Ukrainian grain and 1.4M of steel onto a live world's market.
  Scoping the read surfaces removes them from every player's signal without
  touching trade balance.
- Advertising at 43.70x oversupplied, healthcare_services 5.75x and natural_gas
  5.10x with 143.8 turns of stock cover look like seeding faults and still
  suppress the gate for any sector whose mix touches them.

## Player banking and funds gated to the basics for now

Two player-facing capabilities are held back behind config flags until they are
ready. Both apply only when a charter or fund is created or switched, so anything
already active keeps operating unchanged, and the flags can be turned on later
without a deploy.

- **Bank charters:** players may only charter, or switch to, a retail bank.
  Investment and universal charters are withheld behind
  `playerAdvancedBankChartersEnabled`. The gate lives in the one function both
  new charters and type switches consult, so a withheld type is refused
  everywhere with a single check.
- **Index funds:** a corporation cannot sponsor its own index fund. The default
  system funds still trade normally; player sponsorship is withheld behind
  `playerFundSponsorshipEnabled`.
