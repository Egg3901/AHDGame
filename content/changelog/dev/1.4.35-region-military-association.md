Cut from development.

## Fixed

- The builder's logistics suggestion counted assigned regions against the region cap
  instead of looking at where those regions were. Since the cap is three and the builder
  stops you at three, the warning appeared on every filled Homeland Defense or Regional
  Command and on no partial one. A Command holding South Asia, East Asia and Southeast
  Asia was told to go find a Logistics command; the same Command holding any two of the
  three was told nothing.

## Changed

- The suggestion now reads the macro theatres a Command covers. Regions inside one
  theatre share a supply tail, so a Command that keeps to a single theatre draws no
  suggestion however many of that theatre's regions it holds. A Command reaching across
  two theatres draws it at once, at two regions rather than three. Logistics commands are
  still never told to go find a Logistics command.
- The suggestion now also appears on a Command you have already created, under its
  assigned regions. It was only ever worked out for a Command being drafted, so the create
  dialog was the one place it could be read, even though regions are added and removed from
  the Command's own panel afterwards, which is where a Command picks up a second theatre in
  the first place.
- The Command Structure wiki page matched the old rule and has been rewritten to the new
  one. It also claimed Logistics commands were exempt from the region cap and that the
  builder warned past four regions; the builder does neither, so both claims are gone.
