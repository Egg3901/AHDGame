## v5: governing goals that survive a recompute

The governing agenda was recomputed wholesale every cycle, so a government could
pursue a domain for a week, get nowhere, and have the next recompute replace it
with something else. No verdict, no memory, no consequence.

At v5 a government keeps at most five goal records on its formation document.
Each is opened from an agenda item, graded at every review against the same
domain health the accountability nudge already reads, and closed achieved,
revised or failed. Inside its hold window a goal is held whatever a fresh scan
ranks higher, which is the anti-oscillation rule. Verdicts re-weight the next
agenda, and standing commitments are pinned onto the agenda the executors read,
still capped by the breadth clamp v1 set.

The record set is bounded at five, always. There is no history array and nothing
that grows with playtime. v5 also adds no new power: the records are intent, and
that intent is executed by the same ministerial, sponsorship and fiscal
pipelines v4 already used, with their existing validation, costs, cooldowns and
player-impact budgets untouched. Ministers get the same treatment one level
down, holding a working posture past the settings cooldown unless the brief is
failing, an emergency arrives, or the posture has stopped helping.

Everything is gated at v5. A v4 world takes the path it always did and writes
nothing new. Nothing defaults to v5: fresh worlds still seed v4, and a setup
request that omits the level still gets v4.

## Difficulty: two halves, both named honestly

Difficulty had one consumer, the NPP fund phase, which grants action points and
scales donor funds. That is a resource bonus. It is now labelled as one, and a
second table covers the other half: how competently autonomous politicians
decide. Seven fields, each consumed by a named mechanism — goal slots, goal hold
window, replan responsiveness, candidate breadth, a decline threshold, a
spending reserve, and opposition discipline.

`normal` is defined as the shipped v4 behaviour for every lever that has a
pre-v5 equivalent, so a v4/v5 comparison isolates v5 and an easy/hard comparison
isolates difficulty. Difficulty never unlocks or removes an activity, and never
relaxes a law, cost, cooldown, visibility rule or authority check.

Difficulty now reads from the persisted local-world config rather than the
`SINGLEPLAYER` environment flag. Hosted worlds never persist that field, so
multiplayer resolves to `normal` and is unchanged. The old key meant the
headless harness silently ignored a local world's own setting.

## Also

The autonomy-level scanner that blocks strict-equality gating covered only
v0 to v4, so a new tier would have reintroduced the bug it exists to catch. The
harness accepts `--autonomy=v5` and a `--difficulty` flag, and records both on
the run document.
