Cut from development. Raised on tickets #1072, #1143, #1145, #1153, #1156,
#1159, #1161, #1162, #1163, #1166 and #1167.

## Fixed

- Sectors paid for everything they produced but earned only on what they sold,
  and unsold output was not stock they could keep, it simply evaporated. A
  plant making 60,000 units and selling 10,000 was charged full inputs, wages
  and overheads on all 60,000 against a sixth of the revenue, which is where
  the wild negative margins came from. Nothing turned production down toward
  what buyers would take, so the loss compounded every turn and mothballing the
  whole sector was the only lever anyone had. Plants now aim at what they sold
  last turn plus a small margin to keep testing for more demand, so a glut
  costs what it should and a recovering market is followed back up. A sector
  running at capacity and clearing its output is unaffected.
- The unclaimed slice of a market grew on its own. Founding a sector drew down
  the market it entered, but adding capacity to an existing one did not, and a
  separate yearly process pushed the unclaimed figure back up whenever a
  commodity was struggling. So a market could read as most of the way unowned
  long after the world had built it out, while the build button, which checks
  real unmet demand, refused to build into it. Building now consumes market
  share the way founding does, cancelling an order returns the part that never
  got built, and the yearly relief skips markets that already have more going
  spare than anyone has built.
- A sector screen showed three different measurements as though they were one.
  Market Position now separates "Unclaimed share" from "Room to build", where
  room to build is the same figure the build dialog gates on, so the panel and
  the button stop disagreeing. The build dialog also tells you which limit you
  hit: a market whose share is entirely spoken for is a different situation
  from one where buyers want nothing more, and both used to read as
  "oversupplied".
- The commodity heat map judged shortage and surplus at whichever scope you had
  selected and never said so, so a state shipping its whole output to buyers
  elsewhere was labelled oversupplied while every unit of it was selling. The
  legend and the tooltip now name the frame they are measuring inside.
- Corporate rankings added up revenue from different countries without
  converting it, so a single sector in a weak-currency country could make a
  small company outrank a genuinely much larger one. Every sector is now valued
  in its own country's money first.
- Merger review warned about industries the company being bought does not
  operate in, because it flagged any industry either side was in rather than
  the ones they share. A takeover of a chemicals company no longer raises an
  extraction warning.
- Organisation members that cannot vote were contributing nothing at all.
  Tribute, the levy meant to cover them, only exists for NATO and the Warsaw
  Pact, so everywhere else those members sat on the roll for free. They now pay
  ordinary dues.
- Being refused permission to campaign for a party said only that you were not
  authorised. It now names who may act, which roles count, and that a party
  leader can appoint you. The two party organisation spending refusals got the
  same treatment.

## Changed

- Chamber Leadership now covers the Soviet Chairman of the Presidium, which is
  the ceremonial head of state rather than anything like a Speaker or a
  Majority Leader, and carries no actions or party weight at all.
