Cut from development.

## Changed

- State pages, poll results, the map breakdown, and the wiki now present the
  electorate as demographic buckets: racial groups, age bands, education,
  wealth, each with its share, turnout, and positions. Archetype rosters are
  gone from every player-facing surface. Elections were already decided by
  these buckets; the screens now show the same thing the engine computes.
- The historical era arcs that moved archetypes (the Southern realignment and
  the school prayer reaction) now move the underlying buckets directly, with
  arithmetic verified identical to what the old projection produced.
- Bill authoring and admin demographic tools target buckets instead of
  archetypes, so no new archetype-shaped data can be created.

## Fixed

- Ukraine's archetype-keyed effects (approvals, favorability, lean shifts)
  were silently discarded because the country was missing from the
  archetype-to-bucket mapping. They now land on the buckets they describe,
  and a test guards that every country has a mapping.

## Not changed

- Election results. The vote engines have run on the demographic buckets for
  some time; this pass removes the leftover archetype layer around them.
