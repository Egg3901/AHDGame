Cut from development.

## Fixed

- Starting a Congress leadership election from the admin controls opened a race that was already over. The window carries two end markers, a turn number and a timestamp, and the reader trusts the turn number; the start button only wrote the timestamp, so the marker left behind by the previous race survived and the new election read as closed the instant it opened. The page then resolved it, crowned the sitting holder unopposed, and reposted their victory notice to the feed. Both chambers were affected. Opening a race is now one shared piece of code that always writes both markers, and resetting a race clears them.

## Changed

- The President Pro Tempore is now a majority party office. Only the Senate's single largest party may declare, vote, or hold it, the same rule the Majority Leader and Majority Whip already followed. It was previously open to every seated senator regardless of party.
- Changing party has always cost you a congressional leadership office, but the chair then simply sat empty until an admin started an election by hand. It now goes straight to a fresh 24 turn election, the way the Speaker's chair already refilled itself. Applies to President Pro Tempore and to the Majority Leader and Majority Whip in both chambers, and the vacancy is announced to the country feed naming who left. The departing holder is not placed on the ballot of the election they triggered.
- Losing the majority is still handled the old way: if your party is displaced as the chamber's largest, the seat reopens at the next chamber changing vote rather than the moment the count shifts, and the incumbent keeps the chair until that race resolves.
