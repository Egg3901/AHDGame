## Accounts

- Patreon reconciliation uses linked provider account IDs to match membership updates. Unlinked supporters are reported for review without removing their existing grants.

## Labour and unions

- Banned unions can keep organizing underground through quiet cell work or louder mass drives. The drives use action points, leave the frozen treasury untouched, and expose a vague dark / suspected / exposed risk state.
- Repealing a ban converts underground strength into legal strength at half value, while a fresh ban clears the previous underground trail.
