Maintainer record for the 0.4.2 onboarding-legibility wave. Source of truth for
the findings: an internal review of new-player help-channel friction (1,057 messages). Each item below is a recurring question the community has had
to answer by hand — often 5+ times, months apart. Ranked by leverage.

## Planned (P0 — the two most-confusing loops)

- [ ] **Per-turn income waterfall on the corp overview.** Gross → −maintenance →
      −growth → −budgets → Net. Kills the two highest-volume complaints at once:
      the "my share price dropped after I made money" paradox and the live July
      "profit ≠ margin even at 100% sold" thread. Verify no
      genuine P&L leakage hides behind the UX before closing.
- [ ] **Growth-impact breakdown on the share-price / market-cap widget.** Show
      the book-value delta ("−X from growth spend, +Y from earnings") so raising
      growth to 15% visibly explains the market-cap dip.
- [ ] **One "Fund company" affordance.** Collapse the four half-primitives
      (bonds / issue shares / CEO purchase / private injection) into a single
      capital-injection action routed through CEO-purchase, with a one-line note
      on the 15% fee (which goes to the company anyway). This is the single
      most-repeated question in the channel.

## Planned (P1 — surface hidden state on its own screen)

- [ ] **Market Strength on the corp header + tooltip:** current pool, accrual /
      turn, the bank-and-spend-1-split rule, split-cost doubling, and that MS is
      also the attack/defense weight. (It was silently removed from the infobar
      once; keep it pinned.)
- [ ] **R&D budget screen:** accrued R&D, current per-6-turn boost chance, next
      threshold (0/50/100/200 → 0/25/50/100%).
- [ ] **Logistics & Operations budget:** sector cap (base 16 → 30 at max),
      LS needed for the next slot, current penalty %. Kill the per-turn-vs-total
      confusion.
- [ ] **Party org cap trajectory,** not just the current number ("rising to 65%,
      ~+0.3%/turn") so a slow-momentum cap stops reading as a stuck bug. Add a
      national-PS spend affordance/explainer for NPP-only states.
- [ ] **Self-funding confirm dialog** that states the Infamy cost before commit;
      show Infamy decay (5%/turn) on the stat tooltip.
- [ ] **Attribute the tariff/friction cost line** in the sector breakdown to the
      specific input commodity causing it (fixes "why am I paying tariffs when I
      only sell domestically").

## Live-bug sweep (surfaced in-channel; verify + ticket)

- [ ] **Supply-agreement card vanishes on refresh / no recipient-side accept
      view** — headline 0.4.1 feature; being fixed as the 0.4.1 announce gate.
- [ ] Private-corp partial-stake sales don't complete (player went public as a
      workaround, 15 Jul).
- [ ] Corp doesn't receive funds after buying its own public float (31 May).
- [ ] Currency display reverts to A$ when set to USD.

## Meta (tooling / process, not player-facing)

- [ ] Role-impersonation/QA tooling so chair/PM/cabinet screens can be inspected
      without holding the role ("literally can't see the screens as the chair so
      guessing").
- [ ] Move the marquee formulas (share price, MS, R&D, hostile takeover) into
      in-context tooltips so veterans stop copy-pasting them into chat.
