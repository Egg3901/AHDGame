Fixes ticket #1083.

## What was broken

Corporate bond issuance had a flat minimum (₳100,000) but a corp-scaled maximum:
you may not carry more than 2x your equity in debt, so the largest bond a small
corporation could issue was `2 x equity - existing debt`. When that ceiling fell
below ₳100,000 — a corp with roughly ₳48,500 of equity has a ₳97,000 ceiling —
the valid range was empty. Every amount at or above ₳100,000 failed the maximum,
and every amount below ₳100,000 failed the minimum, so the corporation could not
issue bonds at all. Neither threshold was era- or inflation-scaled, so this hit
small corporations hardest in early-era worlds where nominal values are low.

## The fix

The minimum now clamps to the corporation's own ceiling: the effective minimum
is `min(₳100,000, the corp's issuance headroom)`. A corp capped at ₳97,000 can
now issue up to ₳97,000, and larger corporations keep the ₳100,000 floor. Below
a small dust floor (₳10,000 of headroom) bonds are shown as unavailable with a
"too small to issue bonds yet" message rather than an impossible range.

## Changes

- `effectiveBondIssuanceWindow` (`src/lib/constants/bonds.ts`) is the single
  source of truth for the issuance window: `maxAllowed = min(per-issuance cap,
2x-equity headroom)`, `effectiveMin = min(MIN_BOND_ISSUANCE, maxAllowed)`, and
  an availability flag below `BOND_ISSUANCE_MIN_HEADROOM`.
- `POST /api/corporations/[id]/bonds` enforces the clamped minimum and the dust
  floor; `GET` returns `maxAllowedIssuance`, `minIssuance`, and `bondsAvailable`.
- The Bonds tab uses the server's effective minimum instead of a hardcoded
  ₳100,000, and disables issuance with a clear message when unavailable.
