## Changed

- The "unions are banned" banner on the union pages now explains how to lift the
  ban: propose an Industry bill, include a union-law provision, and choose
  "Repeal the union ban." It links directly to Propose Legislation for the
  country. The repeal power already existed but was buried inside the industry
  bill category with no signposting, so players reported it as missing.
