## Fixed

- The sector margin drill-down on the corporation page now shows the physical
  cost build (inputs, wages, upkeep and other operating costs, as shares of
  realized revenue) whenever the margin is cost-derived. The lines reconcile
  exactly to the shown margin. The old additive modifier list remains for
  worlds and sectors where the margin really is base plus modifiers.
- The sector page's input rows and physical P&L inputs line now price at what
  the engine actually bills (base price through the dampened market
  realization function) instead of the raw regional price, so the displayed
  bill matches the booked one.
