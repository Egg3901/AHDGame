Cut from development.

## Fixed

- A presidential primary now locks to the wave cadence it opened on. If a race
  is re-pointed to a ruleset with a different calendar after its primary has
  begun, the in-progress primary keeps its original spacing instead of trying
  to adopt the new one, which would have released every remaining wave at once.
  Only a primary that has not started yet takes the ruleset calendar.
- Momentum stays coupled to the effective calendar, so a compact-in-progress
  primary never begins accruing momentum even if its race is re-pointed.

## Guarded

- Tests cover a primary that opened compact staying compact, one that opened
  spread staying spread, a not-yet-started primary taking the ruleset calendar,
  and the wave-release count staying at one per turn for a re-pointed
  in-progress primary.
