Cut from development.

## Fixed

- An NPP assigned through the Slate tab no longer disappears. The turn that
  files slate assignments onto the ballot used to tombstone any row it could not
  file, and the Slate tab hides tombstones, so the row simply stopped being
  there. Chairs saw the assignment marked "Likely to Accept", came back later,
  and found nothing at all, while NPPs who refused stayed visible. Rows the turn
  cannot file now stay on the slate, marked "Not Filed" with the reason.
- The reasons a row can carry are: the party cannot contest that region, the
  slot is already filled, the party is not eligible to field a candidate in that
  race, the NPP is no longer available, and the NPP was slated into another race.
- A regional party can no longer be assigned to a race it is barred from
  standing in. Plaid Cymru stands in Wales, the SNP in Scotland, and the
  Northern Irish parties in Northern Ireland. Assigning outside that used to be
  accepted and then silently undone a turn later; it is now refused at the point
  of assignment, with a message.
- The party activity feed no longer reports these as "removed from slate", which
  read as though the chair had done it.

## Changed

- A chair's slate assignment now outranks a candidate the game filled the slot
  with on its own. When the party's challenger slot in a race is held by an NPP
  that entered through automatic party fill, that candidacy is withdrawn and the
  chair's pick files in its place. A slot held by a sitting officeholder
  defending their seat, or by another NPP the chair slated themselves, still
  stands, and the newer assignment is kept on the slate with its reason.
