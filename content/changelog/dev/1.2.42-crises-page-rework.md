Cut from development.

## Changed

- The Sovereign Debt Watch panel now lives on its own "Debt Watch" tab, next to
  Global, National and Regional, instead of an always-open block above the
  crisis list. The list is the first thing you see again.
- The Global tab is now a single feed: live conflicts appear as their own cards
  alongside global crises, each linking through to the conflicts hub. A world
  with conflicts turned off simply shows crises, as before.

## Fixed

- Sixteen crisis hero images were wrong or broken. Nine showed the wrong
  subject, including a surf van standing in for a national power blackout, green
  hills for a water shortage, and a personal tax form for a sovereign default.
  Seven more pointed at images that no longer existed and rendered as broken
  tiles. All sixteen now use images that match the crisis.

## Not changed

- No player asset, seat, office or balance is affected. This is a change to how
  the crises page reads and which images it shows.
