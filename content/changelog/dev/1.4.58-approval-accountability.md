## What changed

- Applied a visible -5 public-expectations modifier to national approval.
- Applied a linear favorability drain below 50 approval to every player and NPP
  member of the current executive party, capped at 0.25 per turn per member.
- Resolved the executive party from the sitting president or head of
  government, rather than from legislative majority records or archived
  governments.
