Cut from development. Third of the banking rebuild pull requests tracked in
issue #1328 (#1354).

### Savings accounts

- Your savings in each currency are now an account of record, with the holder
  (central bank or a bank), the balance and the interest earned in one place.
  The figures you see are unchanged; what changed is what stands behind them.
- Pointing savings at a bank moves the backing cash into the bank's vault and
  the bank carries your balance as a real liability. Withdrawals are paid from
  that vault, so a bank that cannot pay refuses rather than pretending.
- Bank-paid interest stays in the vault as your backing instead of leaving it,
  so a bank's books can no longer show it paying interest twice.
- The rollout is staged per currency. Until a currency is switched over, the
  accounts are kept in the shadows and compared with the old fields every turn.

### Bank lifecycle

- Every charter now has an explicit stage: operating, under watch, impaired,
  failed, in resolution, resolved or revoked. The console shows it and says
  what the stage refuses (an impaired bank may not take on new exposure, pay
  capital up to its parent or change charter type) before you try.
- Failure resolution and revocation freeze the bank's accounts, return the
  household book and the player book through one waterfall, and release your
  account back to the central bank, open, only once the cash has landed there.
  Two attempts racing each other settle once.

### Recovery

- A settlement that crashed between two writes is finished from its own record
  at the start of the next banking turn, with the same guards. What cannot be
  finished is reported on the admin health surface, never forced.
- The health surface carries the activation gate the staged rollout consults:
  nothing unfinished from an earlier turn, no estate stuck in resolution, no
  savings drift. The worldsim balance report includes it.

### Fixed

- A payout to the parent could be rounded up a few cents past the vault and
  then refused at the write; it is now floored.
- A household run could ask the vault for more than it held and be refused
  entirely; the outflow is now capped at the cash on hand and the rest stays
  on the book for the solvency pass to judge.
