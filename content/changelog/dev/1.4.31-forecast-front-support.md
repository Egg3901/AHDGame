## Fixed

- The war room battle forecast ignored the naval and air layer entirely: it projected
  both sides at zero support, so air superiority, close air support and interdiction
  moved the resolved battle but never the displayed odds or supply. The forecast route
  now computes the same per-side front support the turn resolver uses, for both the
  "You attack" and "They attack" rows, so the projection can no longer disagree with
  the outcome it predicts.
