Cut from development.

### Added

- **State operations**, a section under Strategic operations on the campaign
  manager. Everything a presidential primary candidate does to a state is in
  it: where you are camped and the ticks you have banked, the one-off home-state
  surge, how many states you hold Campaign Presence in and what the next level
  costs there, and whether canvassing is open and where.
- **Local attacks.** The field of rivals is listed by delegates. Open a rival's
  row and you can spend $40,000 from the war chest and 4 actions to run negative
  ads against them in a state you choose. Their favourability there falls 0.4 a
  turn for 8 turns.
- Attacks are attributed both ways. Anything running against you is listed by
  name, state and turns remaining, and the campaign wire carries the headline
  when an attack opens, so a drop in your numbers can be traced to whoever
  bought it.
- The Rapid Response branch of Media already blunted incoming opposition
  research. It blunts a local attack by the same amount, and the panel says how
  much it is absorbing. The rate is stamped on the attack when it is bought, so
  a rival retuning their tree later cannot change the terms of a hit you have
  already paid for.

### Changed

- Campaign Presence is priced per state off that state's own level in the
  chooser, rather than quoting one flat figure for a ladder that escalates.
- Prices in this section come from the server, in the campaign's own currency.
  Nothing here is a number typed into the page.
