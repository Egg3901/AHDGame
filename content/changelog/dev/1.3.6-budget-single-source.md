## Fixed

- Seventeen countries recorded a fixed 2.5% annual growth figure on their budget
  because they had no national statistics record to draw one from. Several of
  those economies were contracting between 3% and 9% at the time. The figure is
  now measured from the country's own regions, weighted by the size of each
  regional economy, which is how the national total actually moves.
- Autonomous central banks in those countries were choosing open market
  operations against that 2.5% figure, so they tightened into contractions
  instead of easing. They now read the real rate.
- The region page and the sovereign debt gauges briefly showed an era reference
  rate for the same seventeen countries rather than their measured growth.
  Austria read 5.5% against a real 0.7%.
