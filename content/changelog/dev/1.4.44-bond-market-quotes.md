Cut from development. PR #PR_NUMBER. Phase 2 of the bond market pool; phase 1 was #1330.

## Added

- A per-turn pool step at the top of the bond turn (`marketPoolTurn.ts`).
  Each pool's target is re-sized to five percent of its currency's latest
  broad money. Below target, two percent of the shortfall flows in each turn
  as institutional savings (`inflowIn`). Above twice the target, cash is
  swept back down to one and a half times it (`sweepOut`). The pool's
  appetite for each sovereign issuer in its currency is refreshed from the
  sovereign-default demand model and stored on the pool.
- Pure quote model (`marketPoolQuotes.ts`). Bid is the mid less a half
  spread less a skew; ask is the mid plus the half spread less the same skew.
  Half spread is one percent on sovereign paper and two on corporate. The
  skew is the pool's cash shortfall against target (up to three percent) plus,
  for sovereigns, one minus the demand ratio (minus one to plus five
  percent), capped at eight percent in total. Defaulted bonds have no dealer
  market and quote at recovery value.
- Bond detail API returns `bidPricePerUnit`, `askPricePerUnit`, and
  `marketDepthUnits` (whole units the pool can buy at its bid right now).
- Bond page: bid / ask in the stats strip, market depth in the details, and
  the trade modal prices a sale at the bid and a purchase at the ask, caps a
  sale at the depth, and says so when the order is larger.

## Changed

- Player, corporate, and index fund purchases from the float settle at the
  ask; issuer buybacks of live bonds too. Sales settle at the bid, and the
  depth refusal is computed at the bid. NPP flows still trade at the mid.
- `Bond.marketPrice` is unchanged and remains the mid used by every
  valuation surface.
