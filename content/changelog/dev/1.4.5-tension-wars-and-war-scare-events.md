## Added

- Active wars on the Conflicts board now feed the world tension pressure
  floor. A war with nuclear-armed countries on opposing sides guarantees at
  least CRISIS for as long as it burns, even at low intensity. Other wars are
  weighted by intensity. The tension header on the Conflicts page shows the
  new WARS pressure card alongside the existing four.

- Declaring a war spikes the index immediately: 20 tension for a war with
  nuclear powers on opposing sides, 10 for any other interstate war, 5 for the
  rest. The standing floor keeps it up afterward; the spike is what makes the
  day war breaks out feel like it.

- Standing pressure is now a real floor. Simultaneous diplomatic settlements
  can cool tension down to it, but cannot stack past it while an active war,
  crisis, or escalation ladder still holds the world at risk. A legacy reading
  below the current floor is corrected on the next tension turn.

- Four high-tension society events, offered to every country's executive
  while the tension reading is high enough, on short recurring windows so a
  long war scare keeps generating consequences:
  - **Panic Buying** (tension 60+): ration, appeal for calm, or open the
    strategic stockpiles as households strip the shelves.
  - **Run on the Banks** (tension 65+): guarantee deposits, declare a bank
    holiday, or stand by the banks and hope the run peters out.
  - **Civil Defense Fever** (tension 50+): fund a national shelter program,
    order drills and leaflets, or dismiss the panic.
  - **War Scare Protests** (tension 60+): address the nation, let the
    marches run, or clear the squares.

- Event definitions can now carry `minTension`/`maxTension` bounds, enforced
  by the world-events scheduler the same way era year bounds are. Admin
  manual triggers bypass them, same as era bounds.
