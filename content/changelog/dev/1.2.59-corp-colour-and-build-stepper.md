## Added

- `src/lib/corporations/brandColor.ts`: one home for the corporate palette and
  the shade ramp. `randomBrandColor` for creation, `resolveCorpColor` for the
  display fallback, `brandShades` for single-corp breakdown charts.
- New corps get `brandColor` at creation in all four paths that insert one:
  player founding, spin-off, imperial starter corp, and NPP spawn. NPP spawn
  kept its own private copy of the palette and now shares the one in the lib.
- Plus and minus ten buttons on the build dialog stepper, and `PageUp` /
  `PageDown` for the same step from the keyboard.

## Changed

- `MarketOverviewPanel` tints ownership and voting pies with `brandShades` off
  the corp's own colour. Public float stays outside the ramp in neutral grey,
  because those shares belong to no named holder and grey says so at any hue.
- The pie names the top ten holders and totals the rest into one slice. Prod
  cap tables reach 251 shareholders; a quarter of corps are over twenty. The
  legend rendered one chip per holder, uncapped, inside a 260px box.
- The ramp alternates between its two ends rather than walking light to dark,
  so neighbouring slices, which is where a reader compares them, sit most of the
  band apart. Hue and saturation drift alongside lightness. An achromatic brand
  keeps a grey ramp instead of being pushed up to the saturation floor and
  painted red.
- Pie and legend React keys are positional. Holder names are not unique.
- The build count input is `type="text"` with `inputMode="numeric"`. It was
  `type="number"` clamped to a minimum of 1 on every keystroke, so the field
  could never be empty and a typed number lost its leading digit. Wheel and
  spinner nudging of a large order goes away with it.
- The stepper walks a fixed 1 and 10 instead of a step that grew with the count.
  `stepMultiplier` reads the modifier keys off the click or keydown event.

## Fixed

- An empty count field reads as zero, so the cost preview and the build button
  go quiet rather than quoting the last committed number. Blur restores 1.
