Cut from development. PR #1467.

## Fixed

- `updateGovernmentSeats` returned after the shared seat update whenever the
  UK formation document already existed, and the confidence-gauge and NHS
  ticks lived in the seed branch it skipped. They now run in
  `tickUkGovernmentGauges` on every turn, seed turn included.
- `writeGauge` used a plain update against the legacy `ukGovernment`
  singleton, which worlds seeded after the parliamentary extraction do not
  have, so ticks and one-off events (minister fired, Budget pass or defeat)
  were silent no-ops. It now upserts.
- When the gauge bottoms out and `UK_CONFIDENCE_GAUGE_DISSOLUTION=1`, the
  turn dissolves Parliament through the shared snap-election path (limits
  bypassed, this is the House withdrawing confidence) and resets the gauge for
  the incoming government. Off by default, as before.
