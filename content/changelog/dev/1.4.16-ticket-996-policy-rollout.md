## What changed

- Active rollout now advances through organization votes, proposals, bilateral
  trade actions, non-belligerent war support, and guarded war operations.
- Worlds without an explicit rollout choice start active at the votes stage.
  Explicit off and shadow choices remain authoritative.
- Ratified war entry rechecks that the rollout is still at the war stage before
  deploying forces, so an admin downgrade stops pending escalation.
- The planner records no-action outcomes explicitly instead of leaving them as
  claimed decisions.
- The admin dashboard shows action mix, country activity, target concentration,
  vetoes, rejection reasons, embargo duration, trade movement, and war entries.
