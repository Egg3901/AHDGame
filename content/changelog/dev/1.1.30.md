## Any sector is buildable

Under the plants market, founding a sector was hard-gated to a corporation's
primary or secondary type — the server returned a 400 ("You can only build
sectors in your primary or secondary industry") and the picker offered only
those two types.

That gate is gone. A corporation can now found a sector of any type. The soft
economics are unchanged: primary and secondary matches keep their margin bonus,
and off-type sectors keep the standard off-type margin penalty. Building
off-type is a cost trade-off the player chooses, not a wall.

The build picker keeps the primary and secondary types at the top of the list
with their "Primary" / "Secondary" badges; every other type is listed below,
labelled with the margin penalty it carries. The plants-mode sector-type select
now lists all types instead of just two, and the server no longer forces the
requested type back to the primary.

Also restores the typecheck: the NPP generic re-strategize pass left
`extractionAutoStrategy.ts` with an untyped `genericTypes` array and two
undeclared result fields, which had `development` red on `tsc --noEmit`.
