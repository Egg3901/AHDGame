## Changed

- `yearOfTurn` / `toConflictView` take `preIterationTurns` so conflict copy
  matches the status bar on founding-offset worlds.
- `staticHostGeometry` for NVN/SVN returns both feature codes. `COUNTRY_ANCHOR`
  gains Hanoi and Saigon. Portrait hosts fall back north-south instead of
  west-east.
- `scripts/geo/build-vietnam-geo.mjs` reads `countries-50m.json`.
