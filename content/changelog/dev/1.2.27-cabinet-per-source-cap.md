## Changed

- Cabinet effects on a political metric are capped per channel instead of all
  together. There are six channels: ministerial orders, tier settings, the
  military, estates, energy and infrastructure. Each holds up to 8 points on a
  metric. Before this, all six shared a single 8 point ceiling, so on a mature
  cabinet the marginal estate, order or plant contributed exactly zero. On the
  live US board that ceiling was binding on one in five regional entries, and on
  civic life and market competition it was binding in all 51 states.
- Political metrics close 2% of the gap to their target each turn instead of
  0.5%. Half the remaining gap now closes in about 34 turns rather than 138. The
  channel is still deliberately slow, because a law does not remake a state
  overnight, but a metric that never visibly moves reads as broken.

## Fixed

- The at-ceiling warning on the Active modifiers panel now only appears when
  every channel is full, which is the only case where more building truly buys
  nothing. It names the per-channel ceiling rather than the old shared one.

## Notes

- Nothing needs rebuilding. Existing cabinet effects keep their current total on
  the turn this ships and are split across their channels from there, so no
  metric jumps or drops when it lands.
