Cut from development.

## Fixed

- A crisis running against your country now appears on your Actions page whether
  or not you are the one who answers it. Until now the page dropped any crisis
  whose decision belonged to a role you do not hold, so a recession was visible
  only to the head of government while it cut growth, raised unemployment and
  pushed confidence and approval down for every player in the country. On the
  live world that hid all but four of the thirty two crises then running.

- A crisis also stays on the page after its decision has been taken. Answering
  the prompt used to remove the card, which meant the leader who responded to a
  recession lost sight of it for the twenty or so turns it kept running. The
  card now remains for as long as the crisis is doing something.

- The decision itself is still shown only to whoever may take it. What everyone
  else sees is the name, the description and what it is doing to them, and the
  card can still be dismissed from the Actions page. Full detail stays on the
  Global Crises page.

## Changed

- A decision you can actually take now sits at the top of the Actions page,
  above the crises you can only watch. During the war scare an American player
  has six crises running at once, and a real prompt should not be the seventh
  thing you scroll past. The rest are ordered newest first.

- Crisis effects on the Actions page now read the way they do on the Global
  Crises page: rounded to two decimals rather than printing the whole floating
  point tail, and a card carrying more than three of them says how many it did
  not have room for. A recession has six.

- The severity a crisis is badged with on the Actions page now matches the one
  on the Global Crises page. The two were worked out differently, and they
  disagreed about sixteen of the thirty two crises running on the live world:
  the Actions page ignored one off shocks entirely, so it called both recessions
  more severe than they are and every civil defense scare less. The Global
  Crises reading was the correct one and is now the only one.
