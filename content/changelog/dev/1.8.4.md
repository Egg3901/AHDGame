A supply-agreement premium is a cash transfer on top of operating income. It
never appeared on Financials, and the settlement card only rendered for the
supplier, so a buyer CEO could watch $2k/turn income and a flat cash balance
with no named debit.

## What landed in this release

- Financials names last-turn supply-agreement net cash and any unpaid solvency remainder, separate from net income.
- Buyer-side agreement cards show the same settlement cash suppliers already see.
- Settlement persists the buyer's local cash delta, not only the supplier's.
- The NPP matcher will not insert a contract whose buyer is not an NPP corporation.

Fixes #2019.
