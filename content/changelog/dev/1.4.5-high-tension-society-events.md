## Added

- Four recurring high-tension society events are offered to every country's
  executive while the tension reading is high enough:
  - **Panic Buying** at tension 60 or higher.
  - **Run on the Banks** at tension 65 or higher.
  - **Civil Defense Fever** at tension 50 or higher.
  - **War Scare Protests** at tension 60 or higher.

- Event definitions can now carry `minTension` and `maxTension` bounds. The
  world-events scheduler enforces them like era bounds, while deliberate admin
  triggers continue to bypass scheduling gates.

- Vacant-executive fallback choices remain treasury neutral, and each option's
  approval, treasury, and sector-demand effects have adjacent contract tests.

## Wartime government response

- High-tension events now share a country-level interval. They remain common,
  but arrive as a staggered sequence instead of four simultaneous crises.
- Rationing, bank holidays, civil-defense mobilization, national addresses,
  and protest crackdowns buy limited breathing room. Stacking measures can add
  at most four turns to the crisis interval, so no government can make an
  active war scare disappear.
- Strong measures suppress retail, entertainment, and finance while lifting
  manufacturing, construction, and defense demand.
- Emergency powers reduce the institutional metrics behind Democratic Health.
  The strongest crackdown delivers the most relief and the deepest persistent
  damage to civil liberties and democratic institutions.
- A long war that stays below hot-war intensity slowly loses part of its
  initial shock after twelve turns. The reduction stops if the war heats up,
  and active nuclear conflict always retains a substantial pressure floor.
