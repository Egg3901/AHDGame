## Changed

- The Lean map modes (Combined, Economic, Social) now colour each state on a
  continuous scale fitted to the largest lean currently on the map, instead of
  rounding to the nearest whole point first. State leans are averages over many
  demographic groups, so the national spread is narrow; the old rounding put
  almost every state in the same centre bucket and painted the whole country
  one colour. Relative differences between states are now visible at any
  spread.
- The lean map has a legend. It shows the colour ramp for the selected axis,
  the numeric range it covers on the position scale, and which end is which.
- Clicking a state on the lean map opens a breakdown panel instead of leaving
  the page: the state's combined, economic, and social lean, and the
  demographic groups behind them with each group's share and positions. A
  button in the panel still takes you to the full state page, and clicks in
  every other map mode navigate as before.
- On the United Kingdom map the Social lean now uses the teal to amber social
  palette, matching every other country, instead of borrowing the red and blue
  economic colours. Economic and Combined keep the red left, blue right
  convention.

## Not changed

- The lean values themselves. This is a display change; state leans, labels,
  and everything the election engine reads are exactly as before.
