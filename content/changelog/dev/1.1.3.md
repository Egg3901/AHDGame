Follow-up to 1.1.2. Design doc: ops-knowledge `reachable-market-scope-design`.

## What 1.1.2 got wrong

1.1.2 moved the build gates off the global aggregate and onto the per-country
reachable book. Correct scope, wrong quantity. OVO reported "still the same" on
ticket #1077 with the identical "room for 0 farms" screenshot.

`clearCommodity` trades only the NET residual: it nets domestic supply against
domestic demand first and sets `imports = D - S`. So the clearing book,
`max(0, D - imports) + exports`, collapses to `supply` for every net importer.
Prod, turn 113, food, to every decimal place:

| country | supply        | clearing demand | imports   |
| ------- | ------------- | --------------- | --------- |
| US      | 323,218.24676 | 323,218.24676   | 1,115,631 |
| UK      | 161,664.3504  | 161,664.3504    | 245,443   |
| JP      | 370,004.72352 | 370,004.72352   | 803,508   |
| IT      | 372,077.962   | 372,077.962     | 1,594,918 |
| CN      | 769,021.85    | 769,021.85      | 2,960,763 |

`demand - supply` off that basis is identically zero for any commodity a country
imports. That is algebra, not a market condition. It also means the earlier
reading that the shortage panel was "the liar" and the gate "directionally
right" was backwards: the panel's shortage signal was pointing at a real
opportunity all along.

## The correct basis

Domestic output clears before imports, so new domestic capacity displaces
imports one-for-one and the deficit shrinks to match. Room is the deficit:

    max(0, domesticDemand + exports - supply)

Effect on live turn-113 data:

| market                    | old room | new room  |
| ------------------------- | -------- | --------- |
| US food                   | 0        | 1,115,631 |
| UK food                   | 0        | 245,443   |
| JP food                   | 0        | 803,508   |
| IT food                   | 0        | 1,594,918 |
| CN food                   | 0        | 2,960,763 |
| US electronics            | 116,372  | 116,502   |
| US coal (self-sufficient) | 0        | 0         |
| PL food (real glut)       | 0        | 0         |

Surgical by construction: it only moves where imports exist. Genuine gluts and
self-sufficient markets are untouched.

## Changes

- `ReachableBookEntry.domesticDemand` persisted explicitly, because the clearing
  `demand` destroys the information a build decision needs. Books written at
  turns 106-113 lack it and are healed by inverting the clearing formula, so the
  first turn after deploy is not a cliff.
- `reachableDemandGap` switched to the deficit basis. `reachableDemandUnits` in
  `market/tradePartition` is UNCHANGED — it is the engine's order-book quantity
  and is correct for what today's producers fill. Only the build signal moved.
- New `reachableSellableDemand`, shared by the gap and the Reachable lens so the
  panel, the map and the gate rank on one number.
- The shortage heat map's Reachable lens ranks on sellable demand rather than
  the pinned clearing book, which previously rendered every importing market as
  exactly "balanced".

## Note for reviewers

The test `closes the book when imports actually arrive` previously asserted a
zero gap when imports arrive. That assertion encoded the defect, and is now
inverted to assert the displaceable volume.
