Cut from development.

## Changed

- The NPP Management tab on the national and state party pages now names the
  race a candidate is standing in, so a row reads "Running: Senate (Class 2)"
  rather than just "Running". Races outside the politician's home state lead
  with the state the seat is in, while nationwide races such as the presidency
  are named on their own.
- Clicking or tapping an NPP's name in the management list opens that
  politician's profile. The gear button at the end of the row still opens the
  management drawer.

## Fixed

- Races in several chambers printed an internal code wherever the game named
  them, so a French senate race read "senat" and a Greek one read "vouli".
  Those chambers now read as names instead. The Austrian Nationalrat, Finnish
  Eduskunta, Italian and Turkish Senato, Spanish Senado and Brazilian Chamber
  are all covered, and so is any chamber added later.
