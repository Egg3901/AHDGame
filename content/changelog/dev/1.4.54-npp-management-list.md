## Changed

- The NPP Management tab on the national and state party pages now names the
  race a candidate is standing in, so a row reads "Running: Senate (Class 2)"
  rather than just "Running". Races outside the politician's home state lead
  with the state the seat is in, and each country's own chamber names are used
  instead of a raw internal type.
- Clicking or tapping an NPP's name in the management list opens that
  politician's profile. The gear button at the end of the row still opens the
  management drawer.
