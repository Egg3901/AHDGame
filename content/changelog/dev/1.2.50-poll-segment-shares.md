Cut from development.

## Fixed

- The poll segment explorer measured a segment's shares as a fraction of the
  whole electorate rather than of that segment, so picking a group that is a
  tenth of the state made your share inside it look roughly a tenth of its real
  value. The race-wide figure was not scaled the same way, so the "vs" number
  between them compared two different things. Segment shares are now a share of
  that segment, which is what a vote share means and what makes you, opponents
  and undecided add up.

## Changed

- The comparison now names both numbers, your share of the segment and your
  race-wide share, instead of a bare signed percentage against an unexplained
  "topline".
- Each bar spells out its split in text under it, so the numbers can be read
  without hovering.
- A poll taken during the primary says plainly that no rival candidates are
  modelled yet, so a near total share is not mistaken for a projection.
