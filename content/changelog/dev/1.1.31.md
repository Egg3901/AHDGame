## The founding valve was deaf to single-source shortages

The NPC founding selector picks an unowned market in three tiers: the corp's
primary type, then secondary, then — only if neither has a pool — any type by
shortage score. Freight is produced by exactly one sector type (logistics)
while it is a recipe input to six (manufacturing, extraction, chemicals,
agriculture, automobiles, retail — the whole physical economy). So freight had
no builder pool unless an NPC happened to be logistics-typed: it could run at
2.0x base with 19% US coverage and the tier cascade would still route every
agriculture or manufacturing corp back into its own type. The shortage
screamed into a valve that structurally could not hear it.

A tier-0 essential-shortage override now runs before the type cascade: if any
available market's producer is critically short — its shortest single output
at or above 1.6x base — a cash-healthy NPC founds it regardless of the corp's
own type. Gating on the SHORTEST output, not the blended score, matters:
logistics co-produces consulting services, which would otherwise average the
freight signal away. The override is self-disarming (as capacity lands the
ratio falls back under the bar), rides the same cadence / minimum-cash /
five-sector gates as every founding, and never re-founds a type the corp
already runs. Fertilizer (also >1.6x behind the closed curtain) rides the same
path.

## US logistics seed weight corrected

The 1953 US logistics weight was 4 — below retail and real estate — on a
"pre-Interstate" note. But even pre-Interstate the US ran the world's largest
freight system, and mechanically a 4-weight logistics sector cannot move a
26-weight manufacturing base plus autos and agriculture. Raised to 8. Affects
future world generations; the live world is served by the founding override
above as NPCs build into the existing unowned logistics pool.

## Not addressed here (flagged)

Freight runs ~60% coverage WORLDWIDE, not just the US — logistics' freight
output rate (0.45) against six consumers at 0.07-0.10 each is a global balance
question that wants a worldsim pass before any rate change, not a late-night
constant edit. The founding override relieves the acute national shortages; the
global rate is a separate, larger lever left for daylight.
