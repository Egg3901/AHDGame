Cut from development.

## Fixed

- The Total row under a corporation's Sectors table summed raw nameplate
  revenue, while each sector row rendered realized revenue. Any corporation
  whose realized revenue differs from nameplate saw a total that did not match
  the column above it. Both totals, capital tier and plants tier, now use the
  same realized-preferring basis the rows use.
- Sorting the Sectors table by Revenue ordered on nameplate revenue, so the
  ordering could contradict the numbers on screen. It now sorts on the figure
  the column displays.

## Changed

- Savings APY is half the real rate, that is the prime rate minus local
  inflation, floored at 0.5 percentage points. The banking hub showed the number
  with no explanation, so a 4.42% prime rate paying less than 2.21% read as a
  bug. Both the hero panel and the central bank cards now say what the figure is
  built from.
- The Savings and Interest wiki page said `APY = primeRate / 2`, which has not
  been true since savings moved to the real rate. Rewritten with the real
  formula, a worked example, the rate floor, and why the real rate is what stops
  a carry trade. Three broken code fences and a typo on the same page are fixed.
