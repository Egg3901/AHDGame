## Fixed

Market Control on the state economy page read 100% for every sector on every
board. The figure was derived from the unowned share of a market, and the
unowned share is always zero under the current market system, so the number
could never be anything but 100. On a sector with no companies and no revenue
it still claimed total control, which read as "this market is closed to you".
Sectors now show the largest company's share of the market, or the word Empty
where nobody operates, and the state header reports how many of its sectors are
active.

The Untapped Market panel had stopped appearing entirely. It was shown only
when a sector had unowned revenue, a figure the current market system always
reports as zero, so the one panel that tells you how much room a build has was
never drawn.

New markets reported room for zero plants across most of the board for any
country that exports. The room calculation added a country's exports back into
its own demand, and the trade engine sets exports to exactly the surplus a
country has left over, so the two cancelled out to zero every time no matter
how much the world wanted the goods. A country that exports now also sees the
demand abroad that nobody is currently supplying, weighted by who it can
actually trade with and how much it produces. Embargoed and closed trade routes
still count for nothing.

Where a market genuinely has no unmet demand, the market list now says you can
still build there. That was always true, and the confirmation step always said
so, but the list gave no hint of it.
