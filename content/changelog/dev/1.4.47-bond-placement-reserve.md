summary: >-
  The bond market pool holds a quarter of maturing sovereign face as a
  working balance for the next auction. Per-turn placement of earlier unsold
  units was spending that reserve, so a country could arrive at its quarterly
  auction to an empty pool. Placement now spends only cash above half the
  pool's target.

## Changed

- `unsoldPlacementBudget`: the per-turn placement budget is a tenth of the
  pool's cash above half its target, instead of a tenth of all cash. Found in
  the live-clone sim: Austria's t612 auction filled 0.02 percent because the
  ATS pool had been drained to 362M by placements, against a 9.5B target.
