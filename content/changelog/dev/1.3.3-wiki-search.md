Cut from development.

## Changed

- The wiki search box searches the whole wiki. It previously looked only at
  written articles, so a search for a party, a seat, a cabinet office, a past
  election, a category index or a learning path came back empty even though every
  one of those pages exists and is linked from the wiki front page.
- Results are now sorted under headings that name what they are: Pages, Parties,
  Seats, Offices, Cabinet, Leadership, Elections, Categories and Learning paths.
  The best match still comes first, and the arrow keys still walk the list from
  top to bottom in the order it is drawn.
- Search results carry their own address, so a party result opens the party page
  rather than guessing at an article of the same name.
- The wiki search still searches only the wiki. It does not reach into the main
  site, and it does not return character profiles, corporations or regions.

## Fixed

- Search results are now ranked. A page whose title is exactly what you typed
  comes first, ahead of a page that merely mentions the words somewhere in its
  body. Before this, results were cut off before being sorted at all, so an exact
  title match could be dropped from the list entirely while looser matches were
  kept.
- Private pages stay hidden from everyone but admins, as before.
