## Changed

- The metric board lumped six separate cabinet channels into one line. A player
  saw infrastructure being pulled down by "Cabinet, orders and estates" and
  reasonably asked which cabinet action does that, when the answer was none of
  them: the whole figure came from energy estates. The line now breaks out the
  channels that actually contribute, largest first, and marks any channel that
  is sitting at its own ceiling. Channels contributing nothing are left out
  rather than listed as zeroes.
