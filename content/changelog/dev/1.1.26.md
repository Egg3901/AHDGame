## Tech assets carry more weight

A corporation's unlocked tech-tree nodes are valued on its balance sheet at
`node.cost x ANCHOR x 0.5^(decades since unlock)`. The per-rdScore anchor was
10,000, which left large research investments booking at a fraction of what the
firm spent to reach them.

The anchor moves to 45,000 (4.5x). The decade decay is unchanged: current-decade
nodes carry full weight, each prior decade halves. The higher value flows through
the two existing consumers unchanged — the balance-sheet asset/book-value figure,
and the tangible-book floor in the share-price formula — so R&D-heavy corporations
now report assets, book value, and a valuation floor closer to their real research
outlay.
