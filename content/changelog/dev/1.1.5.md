Fixes tickets #1074 and #1088.

## What was broken

A player handed day-to-day operation of their corporation to an NPP caretaker,
which the feature says you can reclaim at any time, and then could not find the
button to reclaim it. Installing the caretaker made the CEO Office tab disappear
entirely, and the "Resume Control" button lives inside that tab.

The CEO tab is shown when the viewer is the corp's CEO. That check compared the
viewer against the _resolved_ CEO, which during caretaker rule is the NPP, not
the owner. Installing a caretaker sets `ceoType:"npp"` and points `ceoId` at the
NPP, so `isCeo` went false and the whole tab (and the reclaim button with it)
vanished. The owner was left with no in-game way to take the corporation back,
even though they still held `userId`, every share, and full server-side
authority to reclaim.

## The fix

The corp detail now surfaces the caretaker's underlying owner, and the page
treats that owner as CEO for tab visibility. So the CEO Office tab stays
reachable for the appointing owner throughout caretaker rule, and the "Resume
Control" button is where it always was.

While fixing this, we also added a reappointment cooldown: after you reclaim a
corporation you must wait 3 days (72 turns) before handing it to a caretaker
again. This stops a corporation from flipping between owner and caretaker
turn-to-turn to game whichever operator is momentarily better. The card explains
the wait and disables the hand-off button until the cooldown ends; the server
rejects an early hand-off with a 400.

## Changes

- `corporationDetail` returns `caretakerUnderlyingCharacterId` and
  `caretakerReappointCooldownTurnsRemaining`. The corporation page's `isCeo`
  gate now also matches the caretaker's underlying owner, so the CEO Office tab
  no longer disappears when a caretaker is installed.
- `dismissCaretakerCeo` stamps `caretakerCooldownUntilTurn` (turn +
  `CARETAKER_REAPPOINT_COOLDOWN_TURNS`, 72). `validateCaretakerAppointment`
  rejects a hand-off while that cooldown is active with a new `reclaim-cooldown`
  error. `CaretakerCeoCard` disables the hand-off button and shows the hours
  remaining.

## Banking from the bank's own page (ticket #1088)

- **Deposit and borrow in place.** A finance corporation that runs a bank now
  shows deposit and borrow controls on its own corporation page, for any viewer
  who is not the CEO. Depositing routes that currency's savings to this bank so
  it earns the bank's deposit rate; borrowing requests a personal loan in the
  bank's currency. Both previously lived only on the separate `/banking` hub.
- **Opt-in loan approval.** A bank CEO can switch the bank to require approval.
  With it on, new loan requests land as **pending** and the CEO approves or
  declines each from the loan book; the borrower is notified either way. With it
  off, loans auto-grant as before. Pending loans move no money and do not count
  against the loan book until approved, and are re-checked against headroom and
  the blacklist at the moment of approval.
- **Your funds are listed.** The sponsoring corporation's page now lists the
  index funds it sponsors, with a link to each. A chartered fund was previously
  invisible to its owner once the charter session ended.
- **Bank / fund separation.** A corporation may run a bank or sponsor an index
  fund, but not both. Enforced when chartering the second one; corporations that
  already hold both are unaffected.
