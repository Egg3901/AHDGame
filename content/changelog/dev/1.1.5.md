Fixes ticket #1088.

## Banking from the bank's own page

- **Deposit and borrow in place.** A finance corporation that runs a bank now
  shows deposit and borrow controls on its own page, for any viewer who is not
  the CEO. Depositing routes that currency's savings to this bank so it earns
  the bank's deposit rate; borrowing requests a personal loan in the bank's
  currency. Both previously lived only on the separate `/banking` hub.
- **Opt-in loan approval.** A bank CEO can switch the bank to require approval.
  With it on, new loan requests land as **pending** and the CEO approves or
  declines each one from the loan book; the borrower is notified either way.
  With it off, loans auto-grant as before. Pending loans move no money and do
  not count against the loan book until approved. Re-checked against headroom
  and the blacklist at the moment of approval.

## Index funds

- **Your funds are listed.** The sponsoring corporation's page now lists the
  index funds it sponsors, with a link to each. A chartered fund was previously
  invisible to its owner once the charter session ended.
- **Bank / fund separation.** A corporation may run a bank or sponsor an index
  fund, but not both. Enforced when chartering the second one; corporations that
  already hold both are unaffected.
