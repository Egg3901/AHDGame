Cut from development.

## Fixed

- Reunification no longer destroys the side that won it. The machinery it
  borrowed was written for a region leaving a country that carried on without
  it, where clearing the region's politics is right because the country it
  joins rebuilds them. Applied to a whole state being absorbed it deleted
  every seat, every regional party organisation and every officeholder, and
  turned every East German player into an independent with no seat. The
  Volkskammer's members now take their places in the Bundestag, land
  assemblies become Landtage, and Land First Secretaries become
  Minister-Presidents.

- East Germany's parties survive reunification. They cross into unified
  Germany as real parties, keeping their treasuries, their registration
  ledgers and their members, rather than being dissolved. Party membership is
  recorded per country, so without this every East German communist would
  have quietly been read as a West German social democrat.

- A chamber no longer arrives larger than the chamber it is joining. Seats
  carried across are rescaled onto the delegation waiting for them, so the
  five eastern Laender take the places Germany already holds for them instead
  of adding a five-hundred-seat assembly to a Bundestag that has room for
  forty-eight.

- East and West Berlin become one city. East Berlin is folded into Berlin and
  retired, and the two delegations are seated together in Berlin's chamber
  rather than each keeping a count sized for a chamber it is not in.

- A country converted to one-party rule now actually gets a ruling party. The
  conversion looked up each legislator's party by its name, but a seat records
  its party by number, so the lookup never matched: a presidential country
  converted this way ended up a one-party state with no party in charge and
  nobody barred. This also affected the regime-change settlement term.

- A unified Germany can legislate. It inherits the East's law catalogue
  alongside its own, instead of holding a book of enacted laws it had no way
  to amend.

## Changed

- Reunification does not call an election. Every other route into a change of
  system goes to the polls afterwards; this one deliberately does not, because
  the chamber that has just been carried across is the point of the exercise.

- The unified state is one-party, with the parties of the East governing and
  the western parties barred, following the constitutional settlement the
  winning side imposed.
