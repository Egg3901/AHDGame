## Fixed

- A FOMC rate meeting is no longer resolved on the same turn it opens. The
  automatic (NPP / vacant) seats still record their ballots when a meeting opens,
  but the motion no longer carries in that same turn phase before the chair and
  the seated members have a chance to vote. On a board where the automatic seats
  alone reached a majority, the meeting used to open and pass in one turn, so the
  chair never saw it. Meetings now stay open for the full voting window and
  resolve at the deadline, with any seat that never votes abstaining.
