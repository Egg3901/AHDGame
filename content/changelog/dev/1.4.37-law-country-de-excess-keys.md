Cut from development.

## Fixed

- The build stopped compiling after two reunification fixes landed with opposite
  answers to the same question. One widened the law catalogue's country roster to
  admit a reunified Germany; the other took it back out and gave Germany its own
  cost-anchor axis instead, because a roster member is obliged to supply a full
  law catalogue, a budget key map, a seed tax schedule and a calibration
  baseline, and Germany has none of those. A third change, written before the
  second landed, then filled those four tables with empty German rows that the
  roster no longer asks for. Those rows are removed. Nothing read them: the
  rescoped laws a reunified Germany inherits stay under their original country
  prefix and are priced through the anchor, not through a catalogue lookup.

## Changed

- The country roster and the country type are now one declaration rather than
  two that were merely asserted to match. Previously the list of countries was
  annotated as holding members of the type, which a shorter list satisfies
  perfectly well, so a country could be added to the type while the list and the
  tables keyed on it stayed behind, and only an unrelated table would fail. The
  type is now derived from the list, so adding a country is one edit and every
  table it keys demands its row in the same pass.
