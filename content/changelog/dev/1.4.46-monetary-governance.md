## Fixed

- Committee meetings no longer skip or get stuck. Sessions open on their
  regular cadence, stay open for the full vote window even when the automatic
  votes already decide the outcome, and force a resolution at the deadline with
  no-shows counting as abstentions. A board too empty to carry a motion opens
  no meetings until seats are filled, and the chair holds the rate directly in
  the meantime.
- Rate controls no longer leak across jurisdictions. Committee actions belong
  to the committee bank only, a shared-currency bank has exactly one
  authoritative record no matter which member country's page you open it from,
  and a command from outside the membership is refused.
- Valid rate changes no longer fail because the stored rate sits off the
  quarter-point grid. Every writer snaps onto the grid before validating, so
  the next on-grid action always goes through.

## Changed

- The committee tab now renders its vote buttons from the server: a disabled
  button names its reason, and the panel shows the next deadline, whether that
  is the vote closing or the next session opening.
- The prime rate card on committee banks takes its disabled state from the
  same server contract, with the reason shown next to the control.
