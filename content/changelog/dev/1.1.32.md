## The iron curtain stopped the goods, then the glut, but not the memory

The reachable-book work shipped in stages: clearing fills sellers from their
home country's book, and the state price blend's wide leg prices that same
book. What still read the worldwide aggregate was everything with MEMORY or
MARGIN attached:

- **Scarcity drift** integrated the planet's supply and demand. Food at turn
  167 carried a world multiplier of 0.71 — driven by a command-farm surplus
  behind the embargo wall — while the market bloc itself ran 33% short. Every
  Western farm's price base was scaled DOWN by grain it could not import.
- **Sector margin modifiers** blended their 50% wide leg from the same
  worldwide balances, so input scarcity and output gluts were assessed against
  markets the sector does not trade in. Throughput coupling throttled inputs
  the same wrong way.
- **Clearing's price-realization leg** multiplied every seller's revenue by
  the worldwide price-over-base ratio, so a seller in a locally short market
  realized glut-level revenue on shortage-level volume.

## What changed

Each country now advances its own scarcity multiplier on its reachable book
(seeded from the world multiplier on the first partitioned turn, so prices
drift apart instead of stepping). The national, wide, regional and
administered price legs all read the country multiplier. The per-country
reachable price is persisted and feeds both the state blend and, lagged one
turn, clearing's realization leg per seller group. Margin modifiers and
throughput read the sector country's reachable balances.

Modern integrated worlds build no books and are untouched. The world-aggregate
multiplier and global price remain for charts and as the fallback everywhere a
country has no book.
