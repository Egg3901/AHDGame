Cut from development. Raised on ticket #1129.

## Fixed

- The grid collapse crisis was reading the wrong number entirely. Its trigger is
  written as "grid uptime has fallen below ninety percent", but it was being
  handed the 0-100 political board score for the utilities family instead of the
  uptime percentage. A perfectly ordinary utilities score of seventy read as a
  national blackout. Almost every country in the world qualified, permanently.
  The trigger now converts the board score back into real uptime for the current
  era before comparing, so ninety means ninety percent uptime, which is what a
  grid collapse actually looks like.
- Every automatic crisis of this kind now has to see the situation genuinely
  improve before it can fire again, not merely stop being at its worst. Each one
  fires at its threshold and only re-arms once the number has recovered past that
  threshold by a clear margin. Without that gap a crisis that damages the very
  thing it watches re-fires forever on damage it caused itself.
- A crisis effect that repeats every turn is now limited across the whole crisis
  rather than on each turn separately. A one-off shock and a slow grind can each
  bend a political family by the same total amount, instead of the grind landing
  the full one-off allowance twenty-four times over and wiping the family out.

## Changed

- Long-running crises are meaningfully less punishing on the political board.
  The per-turn erosion of confidence, approval and public safety that a
  multi-turn crisis applies was landing at full one-off strength on every single
  turn. It is now spread across the crisis, so the total damage matches what the
  crisis was written to do.

## Not changed

- No player asset, seat, office or balance is affected. This changes when world
  crises start and stop, and how hard their per-turn effects land.
