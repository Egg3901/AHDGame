Cut from development.

## Fixed

- The Economy page showed a different national deficit from the Budget page. It
  read a stored surplus field that drifts between fiscal years, while the Budget
  page recomputed the figure from revenue and spending. Both now derive it the
  same way, so the two pages agree.
- National metric averages on the Metrics page were weighted against the whole
  country's population even when a region carried no value for that metric,
  which pulled the average down. The Economy page never had that problem. Both
  now share one weighting rule.
- The public API reported no inflation rate at all for countries that have a
  national budget but no central bank. It read the central bank's chart history
  rather than the budget figure the rest of the game uses.
- The stock market wealth list valued a holding at zero when a company had no
  share price recorded, instead of using the same default price every other
  screen applies.

## Changed

- The hourly turn now logs a warning when a budget's stored surplus or debt
  figure drifts from the values it is derived from, so the cause can be traced.
