## What changed

- A private subsidiary is seated with its own CEO, usually an NPP, so you are
  not the sitting CEO of the subsidiary even when your corporation owns it. The
  corporation page already treated the parent CEO as an insider for the main
  numbers, but the bonds request that the overview always loads did not. It
  returned a rating-only credit object, and the overview crashed while
  formatting a coupon rate that was not there, which took the whole page down
  with "Couldn't load corporation."
- The bonds route now uses the same parent-CEO insider rule as the rest of the
  page (including reserved corporate sells, so a listed block does not drop you
  through the floor). The overview also renders a dash when the coupon rate is
  missing, so a redacted payload cannot crash the page.
