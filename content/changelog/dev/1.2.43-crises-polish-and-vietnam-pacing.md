Cut from development.

## Fixed

- Crisis effect values no longer show a long decimal tail. A number that worked
  out to 0.44999999999999996 now reads 0.45.
- Regional crises name the region they hit. A card that read "Regions: FR_ARA"
  now reads "Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes", and each region is a link through to that
  region's page.
- The Vietnam war escalated far too fast. The ladder climbed on committed support
  alone, with nothing tying a rung to its date, so a world could reach the naval
  incident and the edge of a bombing campaign within a year or two of the first
  advisors. Each rung now also has an earliest year it can be reached, anchored to
  the real arc: materiel in 1959, the naval incident in 1964, the air campaign in
  1965, ground troops in 1966. Commitment still decides whether the war climbs;
  the calendar now decides how soon.

## Changed

- Regional crisis groups show the country flag next to the country name.

## Not changed

- No player asset, seat, office or balance is affected. Support already pledged to
  Vietnam still counts; it simply cannot push the war past a rung before that
  rung's year.
