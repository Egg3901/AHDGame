Cut from development.

## Fixed

- The Budget page reported a national GDP that only refreshed once a fiscal
  year, so it could sit several percent below the figure on the Economy page.
  It now shows the live total, summed from every region each turn.
- Debt-to-GDP and deficit-to-GDP sat on different denominators in the same
  panel, so the two percentages could not be reconciled against each other.
  Both now divide by the same figure, on the Budget page, the Economy page,
  the country directory and the sovereign debt gauges.
- The region page showed a different national growth rate from the Economy and
  Central Bank pages, because it averaged its regions by population rather than
  by the size of their economies. All three now report the same rate.
- Stock market totals added different currencies together. A country's Economy
  page could report a total market capitalisation many times larger than the
  stock market page it links to. Every total is now converted first.
- Company revenue on the stock list was the amount a company could bill at full
  capacity, not what it actually earned, so it disagreed with the same
  company's own page. Both now show earned revenue, and the stock list charges
  research and development spending it had been leaving out.
- The public API reported company revenue and profit on a third basis again,
  and added revenue from foreign operations without converting it.
- Holdings in Bulgarian, Czechoslovak, Hungarian, Polish, Romanian and
  Yugoslav companies were valued as though their currencies traded one to one
  with the anchor on portfolios, wealth lists and profile pages, while the
  stock market used the real rate.

## Changed

- Company financials shown to Discord now match the site for every figure,
  including per-sector rows.
