Cut from development.

## Fixed

- A sector split now reports its outcome in a toast. The result message used to
  live inside the split panel itself, and a split needs the target to hold at
  least two plants. Taking one off a two-plant rival dropped it to one, the
  target stopped being splittable, and the whole panel closed on refresh and
  took the message with it. The cleaner the win, the more likely you saw
  nothing.
- The toast says which way it went. A split that misses its roll still spends
  the committed cash and marketing strength, so a failed attempt now reads as a
  failure rather than as silence.
- Seizing a single plant no longer reads "1 whole plants".
