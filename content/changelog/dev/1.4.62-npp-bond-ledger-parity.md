## Fixed

- NPP sovereign-bond purchases now emit a `bond_purchase` transaction with a
  first-class `npp` subject instead of leaving the cash movement absent from
  financial audit history.
- NPP purchases use the same live ask quote as the player route, including the
  dealer spread, so equal units and currency produce equal native settlement
  costs.
- This also corrects the previous NPP pricing omission: NPP bond purchases had
  been charged at the market mid and therefore undercharged the native cost by
  the ask spread.
- The shadow ledger can map NPP purchase rows to distinct NPP investment
  accounts; NPP accounts remain outside stock checking until their anchor-backed
  balance is included in the authoritative snapshot.
- Added a real-Mongo sandbox fixture script covering player/NPP cash, bond-float,
  market-pool and balanced-ledger conservation, including a non-unit FX rate and
  failed-purchase refund.
