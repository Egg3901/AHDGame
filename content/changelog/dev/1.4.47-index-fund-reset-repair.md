## What changed

- Fixed world resets so fund definitions and their live accounting state are
  rebuilt together.
- Removed holdings that referenced corporations from an earlier world, which
  appeared as repeated “Unknown” constituents on fund pages.
- Reconciled each fund's outstanding unit supply with its surviving holder and
  reserve positions.

The repair writes off stale values attached to corporations that no longer
exist. It does not create replacement cash or synthetic shares.
