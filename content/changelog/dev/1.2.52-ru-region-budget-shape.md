Cut from development.

## Fixed

- A Soviet republic's Economy → Budget tab showed British local-government
  revenue lines. The region budget route picked its revenue breakdown by asking
  which optional field a `regionalBudgets` document carried, and used the UK
  shape when nothing matched. RU documents carry only `unionGrant`, so they
  matched nothing: the Russian Far East displayed Council Tax, Business Rates
  and a Westminster grant of zero each, and a total revenue of zero, while the
  balance beside it correctly showed a ₽807.6M surplus derived from the ₽1.54B
  union grant the page never read. Revenue read three UK-specific fields and the
  balance read the document, so the two could not agree.
- The Central Plan Received panel showed ₽0.0M for the same reason — it read the
  Westminster grant field rather than the union grant.

## Changed

- The per-country revenue view moved out of the route into
  `lib/budget/regionalRevenueShape.ts`, with a case for RU alongside the existing
  UK, JP, DE and CN ones, and tests for each. Route handlers stay thin.
- Every case now reports the document's own `totalBudget` instead of re-summing
  country-specific fields. A country without a case here can no longer render a
  revenue total that disagrees with its own balance — it degrades to a correct
  total rather than to zero.
