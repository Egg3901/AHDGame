Cut from development.

## Added

- A standing order on the conflict page: join allied offensives at this
  front automatically. With it set, your forces at that front fight in any
  offensive an ally declares there, without a separate order from you. Off
  is the old behaviour, and off is the default.

- The order is national policy rather than one commander's preference, so it
  survives a change of Theater Commander the way the deployment and the
  posture do. Nothing silently stops supporting an ally because a general
  was replaced.

## Changed

- Auto-joining requires troops already posted to that front, exactly as
  automatic defence does. A standing order does not move an army, and it
  never pulls a nation into attacking on a side it is not on.

- An ally that joins this way appears in the war log under its own flag with
  its own casualties, the same as one that declared. Withdrawing your forces
  from the front remains the way to stop supporting an offensive, which is
  the same escape hatch automatic defence has always had.
