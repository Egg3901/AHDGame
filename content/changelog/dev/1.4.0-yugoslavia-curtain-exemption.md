## Changed

- Yugoslavia is exempt from the iron-curtain trade wall. Historically the
  Tito-Stalin split (1948) put Yugoslavia outside the bloc before the era
  worlds begin: embargoed by its former partners, trading with the West. In
  game it was walled in with the bloc and cut off from its real trading
  partners.
- The exemption applies to trade only. Yugoslavia's domestic economy stays
  planned: administered prices, state enterprises, and the shortage machinery
  all continue to apply on their existing marketization schedule.
- Because the curtain is absolute in both directions, the exemption also
  cuts Yugoslavia off from the bloc's internal market, which matches the
  bloc embargo years the era opens in.

## Guarded

- Curtain membership now resolves through one shared function at both engine
  call sites (the world ledger and the corporate clearing books), so the two
  can never disagree about who is behind the wall.
- Tests pin the bloc curtained in 1953, Yugoslavia exempt but still planned,
  the historical marketization lift dates, and the flag-off no-op.
