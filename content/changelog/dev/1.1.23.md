Cut from development.

## Sector page money is engine money

1.1.22 moved the corp page's P&L, income statement and balance sheet onto the
margin the engine actually applied. The sector detail page had the identical
defect in its own copy of the computation: `computeSectorMarginSection` rebuilt
the margin from the modifier stack and priced the whole money block off it.

Observed on production: a California agriculture sector the engine paid
−6.19% — a real squeeze, inputs bought at shortage prices (fertilizers 2.26x
base, plastics 1.60x, freight 1.61x) while food sells into a global glut at
0.58x base — rendered on its own page at +48% margin with +238K/day of
operating profit. The corp page, post-1.1.22, showed the same sector losing
money. Two screens, one tap apart, opposite signs.

The money block (maintenance, profit, break-even, per-unit figures) now derives
from the stored `effectiveProfitMargin`, same rule and same fallback as the
corp page. The modifier stack remains the advisory breakdown.

## Known residual, deliberately flagged

The "Things you bought" line is an independent estimate (demand rows at market
prices) that understates the engine's actual input bill for shortage-priced
inputs — on the sector above, by ~50x. The bottom line no longer inherits the
error (the remainder lands in "Other running costs", whose tooltip already
defines it as the unexplained rest), but the attribution between those two
lines is wrong when input prices run hot, and fixing it means reconciling the
demand-row units with `computeInputsCost`. Follow-up, not guessed at here.
