Cut from development.

## Fixed

- An extraction sector offered the full commodity list to the market regardless
  of what its state actually holds in reserve. The offers for absent resources
  could never clear, so they sat unsold every turn.
- Those unsold phantom loads were counted in the sector's sell-through, which is
  what the sector page reads to decide the market is oversupplied. A mine in a
  state with one resource looked like a failing mine.
- Extraction now offers only the resources the owning state holds, so
  sell-through and the oversupply reading describe real output.
