title: "1.2.4: Mines stop being judged on ore their state does not have"
summary: >-
Extraction sectors were offering every raw material to the market, including
the ones their state holds no reserves of. Those phantom loads never sold, and
the unsold share was what the sector page used to call the market oversupplied.
Mines are now measured only on what they can actually dig.
