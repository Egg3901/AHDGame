Cut from development.

## The curtain is real now

1.1.27 stopped unreachable supply from setting open-market prices, and
disclosed the remaining gap: the curtain existed in clearing scope, not trade
policy. Poland was exporting 2.66M units of food into Western imports with
zero embargoes in force.

`buildTradeAffinity` now takes the planned-economy set (the engine's own
`MARKETIZATION_SCHEDULE` via `isPlannedEconomy` — RU, UKR, BLR, BAL, CN, DD,
PL, HU, CS, BG, RO, each until its historical marketization date) and returns
zero affinity between a curtained and a non-curtained country, both
directions. Trade WITHIN the bloc (Comecon) and within the open world is
untouched, as are FTA, tariff, org-bloc and embargo mechanics. Both affinity
consumers — the price/clearing turn and the corporate clearing books — pass
the set.

Deliberately closed at the TRADE layer, not as embargo documents: a
comprehensive embargo would also suspend cross-curtain players' foreign
sectors, and East Germany is playable. Corporate presence is untouched.

## The bloc works in autarky

With the curtain closed, the bloc's surpluses (13.45M units of food against
2.94M of internal demand) would crater its own market price legs and starve
every state farm of the revenue the plan guarantees. Administered pricing
previously governed only the national leg of the blend; in a planned economy
it now governs the final state price outright, scaled by `plannedShare` for
dual-track economies. The plan sets prices, the state eats the imbalance, and
the overhang machinery accounts it — which is both how those economies
actually worked and what keeps them numerically stable with nobody to sell to.

## Expected movement

- Western food and other bloc-dumped commodities reprice on Western
  fundamentals alone (US food book: 507K supply against 1.49M domestic
  demand, imports now limited to open-world partners). Prices rise further
  than 1.1.27 alone.
- Bloc internal prices hold at administered levels regardless of surplus.
- Cross-curtain import flows (US was importing 984K units of food) end;
  Western importers source from the open world or go short. Real scarcity is
  now a Western gameplay problem with a Western gameplay answer: build farms.
- The curtain lifts per country on the historical marketization dates
  (CN 1978, PL/HU/CS/RO 1989, DD/BG 1990, USSR republics 1991).
