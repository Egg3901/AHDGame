Cut from development. PR #PRNUM.

## Fixed

- `assemblePhysicalPnl` caps policy credit at the bills it offsets. The
  other-opex clamp only engaged when other opex itself was a credit, so a
  sector whose policy stack outran its costs booked a negative total cost and
  a profit above revenue. A `netMarginPct` line (profit over every cost,
  uncapped) now sits beside the operating `derivedMarginPct`.
- The public API corporation financials read the persisted plants P&L, the
  same reader the corp page and the Discord bot use, instead of inverting a
  capped operating margin that excluded upkeep and compliance.
- Character wealth counts index fund units at quoted NAV. The wealth list and
  its persisted snapshots never did, so subscribing to a fund made a player
  look poorer while the Legacy leaderboard and portfolio history disagreed.
- The financials tab, the CEO production tab and the sectors-tab average show
  the net margin over the full cost bill when the engine has one, with the
  effective margin on sold units kept in the tooltip. The sector row already
  led with it.

## Added

- `INDEX_FUND_MAX_SINGLE_NAME_WEIGHT` (0.20). Fund target weights cap any one
  constituent and hand the excess to the rest pro rata; funds with fewer than
  five names stay market-cap weighted.
