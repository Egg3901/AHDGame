  When governor terms lapse the FOMC board falls below working strength and no
  rate motion can ever carry, so the committee deadlocks every decision.
  Meetings now pause on such a board and the chair sets the rate directly using
  the ordinary central-bank controls, exactly as a single-governor bank does,
  until confirmations restore a board that can vote again.

## Changed

- A committee board that cannot carry a motion (fewer seats filled than a
  strict majority of the full board) now stands down: new meetings pause until
  confirmations restore a working board. An already-open meeting still resolves
  at its deadline.
- While the board is below working strength the chair sets the rate directly.
  A player chair gets the normal Prime Rate adjuster on the Overview page; an
  autonomous (NPP) chair uses the same Taylor-rule auto-setter as any
  single-governor bank. The chair's change obeys the usual cooldown and size
  limits and still spends one of the term's rate moves.
- The committee page banner and the Prime Rate card explain that the chair is
  setting the rate directly, and the vacancy notice to the President says the
  same instead of promising motions can pass.

## Unchanged

- A functional board still owns the rate: the chair cannot bypass a meeting,
  the full-board majority still governs motions, and staffing rules are
  untouched (the engine never seats a machine candidate, #1195).
