## What changed

- The sector page used to rebuild your profit out of your effective margin: revenue, times one minus the percentage. Your margin is not an input to that sum. It is the RESULT of it, and it stops at 100 because a margin above 100 tells a player nothing.
- On a sector where policy support outweighs the whole operating bill, the real operating cost is below zero and the real profit is above revenue. Reading it back off a percentage that stopped at 100 gave an operating cost of exactly zero and a profit exactly equal to revenue. Nine media sectors were sitting at or on that ceiling.
- Inverting the percentage also quietly dropped upkeep on idle capacity and the regulator's compliance charge, on every sector under the plants economy, because those two are charged outside the margin and inside the profit.
- The turn now writes down the cost lines it charged, and every surface reads them: the sector page, the corporation page, and the Discord financial cards. Nothing is rebuilt from a percentage any more.
- New line on the money chain: **Policy, tax and support**. Subsidies, tariffs, tech bonuses, state conditions, regional conditions and any live crisis all reach your profit through this one credit and nothing else. It has its own drilldown listing each modifier in money, and the parts sum to the line exactly. The corporation page's margin drilldown names the same line, so the two agree.

## Why

A player who wins a subsidy should be able to find it in their own accounts. It was there, folded into "other running costs" with no label, while the headline profit above it came from a percentage that had already been rounded off at the top. Two different problems with the same fix: report the money the engine moved, and say where it came from.

The engine's arithmetic is unchanged. This is what the page reads and how it is presented.
