Cut from development.

## Fixed

- A country that absorbs another no longer loses its new regions' mines.
  Extraction is the one industry placed region by region, on the strength of what
  is actually buried there, and that check was still asking about those regions
  under the flag they used to fly. The answer came back empty, so no mine was
  ever built. Every other industry moved across untouched, which is why the loss
  went unseen: sixteen kinds of plant arrived in the west and the seventeenth
  silently did not.

- The mines themselves are restored. A reunified Germany was sitting on coal,
  iron, oil, natural gas, timber and rare earths across ten regions with no way
  to reach any of it, and in a planned economy no private founder can step in and
  fix that. The state mining enterprise now operates in those regions, and the
  deposits that had gone untouched since the merge are being worked again.

- The same lookup was one re-seed away from erasing those deposits outright. A
  region absorbed into another country would have been written back with nothing
  buried in it at all, turning a recoverable gap into a permanent one.

- State enterprises now say which industry they are in. A carved-out state
  enterprise inherited the treasury's own description of itself and reported as a
  financial firm, whatever it actually operated, which is also what it would have
  expanded into when told to build. Thirty enterprises across three countries
  were affected.

- Unowned capacity is now filed under the country the region is in, rather than
  the country of whoever last built there, with no fallback to naming a country
  outright when it could not be worked out. Two markets on Ukrainian soil had
  been sitting under the United States, out of reach of Ukraine and counted as
  American.

## Changed

- The sector list names a country by the name it goes by now. A reunified Germany
  was still listed as East Germany, while the state it replaced offered a
  "Germany" filter that could never match anything. A country that no longer
  holds any territory has been dropped from the filter entirely.
