## What changed

- NPP war-entry laws recheck the active rollout, player-control rail, approval,
  debt, readiness, and deployable reserves before adding the country to a side.
- A bounded share of ready reserves deploys when entry takes effect, so an
  autonomous belligerent cannot appear on the roster with no force at the front.
- Later policy decisions can queue normal battle declarations or open a white
  peace offer. Existing battle, supply, settlement, and truce systems resolve
  the consequences.
