Cut from development. PR #1461.

## Fixed

- A legislation type whose effect target is a macro metric (economic or
  population) wrote its delta with an empty filter, so one country's bill
  nudged every region on earth. The write is now scoped to the enacting
  country, and a bill with no country is dropped rather than applied to
  everyone, matching the political branch beside it.
- `economicFactors.gdpGrowth` on the national budget was written only at
  fiscal year end, so the budget page and the public nations API showed a
  snapshot from the top of the last wave while every other surface read the
  live national number. It is now mirrored every turn alongside wage and
  trade growth.
- Countries with no national metrics document (France, Italy, Spain, Sweden,
  Turkey, Greece, Austria, Finland and the bloc) had their GDP growth history
  written as the era trend constant every turn, which forex and legitimacy
  then read. The snapshot now records the GDP-weighted mean of the country's
  regions, falling back to the era trend only when nothing is weightable.
  The per-turn tax base growth for those countries uses the same rule.
