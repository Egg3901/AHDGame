## What changed

- New parties are no longer headless at birth. The founder who proposed the charter is seated as the party's first chair the moment it ratifies, so they can set the party color and logo right away instead of waiting for a leadership election. Vice chair and treasurer still start vacant and are filled by the normal leadership elections; the chair seat opens for re-election on the next cycle like any other.
- Founders can replace an inactive co-founder before the charter is signed. If a listed founder cannot come online, any founder can swap their unsigned slot for another willing player from the charter page, reusing the same eligibility rules as the existing replacement flow. A founder who has already signed cannot be swapped out.
- Election graphs for single-party races now give each candidate a distinct color. In contests where every candidate shares one party (for example the USSR nationalities-deputy races), the trend lines were all the same red and impossible to tell apart. Each candidate now gets a distinct shade ranked by vote share. Ordinary multi-party races look exactly as before.

## Why

Three separate player suggestions pointed at the same rough edges: a brand-new party looked broken until an election it had to wait for resolved, a charter could stall on a founder who went quiet, and same-party election charts carried no readable information. Each fix removes a wait or a dead end without changing how the underlying systems work.
