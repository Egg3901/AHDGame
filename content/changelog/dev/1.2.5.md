  forward at any time, and the stand-in gives way as soon as one is on the table.
tags: [central-bank, politics]

## Changed

- A technocrat chair is a caretaker, not an office-holder. It only has the seat
  because nobody was available when the seat last came up. Nominations are now
  open the whole time one is sitting, instead of shut until the last game year
  of its term.
- Naming a candidate now triggers the selection straight away rather than
  queueing behind the caretaker's remaining term. A country that gains an
  eligible candidate no longer waits four game years to use them.
- A seated player chair is unaffected: their term still runs, and nominations
  still open a game year before it ends.
