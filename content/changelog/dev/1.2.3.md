## Fixed

- When the committee's chair seat reached the end of its term it appointed a
  technocrat and wrote a fresh four-year term in the same step. The appointment
  process runs later in the same turn and only acts on a term that has ended, so
  it never saw the opening. The seat is now handed over as a vacancy: a
  caretaker keeps the committee working while the nominations go to a draw and
  the winner is offered the job.
- Accepting the chair did not seat you on the committee. The caretaker kept the
  chair's seat, tabled the motions, and evicted you without notice when its own
  term ran out. Accepting now puts you in the seat.
- Accepting the chair left the bank in technocrat mode, so pages and bonuses
  kept resolving the technocrat over the player who had just taken office.
- A technocrat taking the chair left the outgoing player on the bank record.
  Banks could name a player and a technocrat as chair at the same time.
- Resigning, being dismissed, or standing down on taking executive office
  cleared the bank record but left you sitting on the committee, still voting
  the rate.
- The bank page and the committee could name two different chairs, because the
  fallback technocrat was a fresh appointment rather than the one already
  sitting in the chair's seat.
- Being granted independence by law rebuilt the committee from scratch and
  seated a technocrat over a sitting player chair.
