Cut from development.

## Added

- A cap meter on every institution card the personal tier can reach (the street
  and the Bundestag). It reads the open floor's net movement this turn against
  the per-turn ceiling, in the same east and west colours as the rest of the
  board, so the limit named on the open floor panel is now visible where you
  actually act.

## Fixed

- A maxed category is now unmistakable: the meter switches to a warning border,
  a MAXED badge appears, and a line states plainly that further personal plays
  cost their action point but move nothing there until the next tick.
- The open floor panel no longer reports a net of zero while plays are still
  pending. Mid-turn it now projects what the tick will apply after the cap,
  which is the number a player deciding whether to sign needs.
- Categories the personal tier cannot reach carry no meter at all, rather than
  implying a limit that never applies.
