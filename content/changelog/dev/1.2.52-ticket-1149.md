## What changed

- The ministerial award list treated a plant's host country as the supplier
  country. Delivery already paid on corporation HQ and currency, so a UK
  corporation's Greek or French works could fill a UK order but never appeared
  as something the minister could award. Those plants are now offered, labelled
  with the host country when they sit abroad. A foreign-owned plant inside the
  buying country is still omitted.
- The CEO order book names the contracted plant. The 1-4 production-line
  control allocates lines on that plant; it does not add plants, and the
  caption now says so.
