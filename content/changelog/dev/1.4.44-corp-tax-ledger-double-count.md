Cut from development.

## Fixed

- A corporation's per turn entry in the transaction log recorded its income
  after corporate tax, and then recorded the full tax bill again as its own
  line. The tax was counted twice, so the log understated what the company kept
  by the whole tax amount.
- Companies whose tax came to more than their post tax income showed a negative
  turn in the log despite trading profitably. At the time of the fix that was
  154 of 305 company run businesses and 24 of 47 player run ones.
- A company whose tax wiped out its income entirely got no credit line at all,
  only the tax charge, because the credit was skipped whenever post tax income
  was not positive. The credit is now written whenever there was income to tax.

This is a reporting fix. These lines carry no balance and move no money, so no
company gained or lost anything from the fault, and no treasury changes as a
result of the fix. What was wrong was the record, along with the cash forensics
views and the money supply reconciliation built on top of it.
