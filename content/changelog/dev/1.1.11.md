## What changed

On the corporation Operations tab, the sectors list gave each sector's capacity
in output units but not the number of actual plants behind it, so there was no
way to see at a glance whether a sector was one large plant or a dozen small
ones. That count now appears as a line under the capacity number on every row,
using the right word for the industry (plants, stores, mines, and so on), and it
shows in the mobile card view too.

The number is derived from capacity the same way the per-sector Plant panel
already derives it, so the list and the detail page always agree. No data or
economy change: this is a readout that was missing.
