Cut from development.

## Added

- A freight-settlement ramp. When a start turn and window are set, the active
  effect fades in linearly: a fraction from 0 at the start turn to full at the
  end of the window scales both the delivered-supply cap that limits a state's
  sales and the shipping money billed between buyers and freight carriers. Both
  billing sides scale by the same fraction, so the charge and the haul-revenue
  credit stay matched (a transfer, not a sink).
- The in-flight ramp fraction is recorded on the sourcing network doc while it
  is between 0 and 1, so the phase-in reads on the markets view and the sector
  Freight tag rather than appearing as one abrupt balance step.

## Unchanged

- With no ramp window configured the effect is instant at full strength, exactly
  as before. The ramp only softens the approach; it never gates activation on.
