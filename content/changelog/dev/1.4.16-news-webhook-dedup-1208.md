Cut from development.

## Fixed

- World News no longer posts the same headline twice. When a turn ran long enough
  to look stalled, a second run could take it over while the original was still
  finishing, and event announcements (Supreme Court rulings, cabinet office
  changes) went out twice. News posts now carry a short-lived de-duplication
  claim, so identical announcements within the same window are posted once even
  if the work behind them runs twice. A failure of the de-dup path never
  suppresses a real story.
