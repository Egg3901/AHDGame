Cut from development.

## Fixed

- Marketing costs now settle as payments from the corporation buying
  advertising to the advertising sellers whose offers filled, valued by
  delivered campaign volume at the clearing price.
- Unfilled marketing orders leave the unspent amount in the buyer's treasury.
- A corporation can place only the advertising demand its liquid capital can
  fund for the next turn. Unfunded budget settings no longer create demand.

## Changed

- Marketing strength now grows on what a corporation actually spends on
  delivered advertising, not on the budget it set. With advertising filling
  around a fifth of what is asked for, strength accrues more slowly than it
  did.

## Guarded

- Cash-conservation coverage includes multiple advertising sellers,
  cross-currency settlement, and a buyer with operating revenue and taxes.
- Regression coverage verifies that buyers are not charged for unfilled
  advertising demand.
