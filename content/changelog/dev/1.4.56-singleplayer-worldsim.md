## Changed

- Singleplayer setup offers normal or permanent Head of State play, separate
  NPP difficulty and autonomy controls, and applicable gameplay feature flags.
  Local players enter character creation directly and do not see sign-out controls.
- Head of State characters occupy the country's executive office and remain
  seated through presidential elections and prime minister appointments.
- Worldsim beta creates a playerless world and advances the existing turn engine
  in bounded requests. A spectator view exposes world statistics and nations.
- Desktop account linking reuses the existing account session and supporter
  entitlement checks.
- Optional desktop statistics accept only allowlisted world setup and numeric
  aggregates, without account identifiers, character names or raw saves. A
  registered TTL migration expires reports after 30 days.

## Compatibility

- Hosted multiplayer retains its existing NPP action and funding defaults.
- Local setup fields are optional additions to game state and character records.
  Existing saves use normal mode when no setup configuration exists.
