## Added

- Every page of a country involved in an armed conflict now opens with a red
  strip under the navigation, naming the war and linking straight to its record.
  Until now a nation could be at war and nothing on its executive, legislature,
  economy or party pages would mention it. The strip is absent in peacetime.
- A country whose territory is the theatre gets the strip too, worded for what is
  actually true: the fighting is on its soil even when its own army is not in the
  field. This is how West Germany reads during a war for Germany, which is fought
  over both German states while only one of them is a belligerent.
- A country in more than one war is told how many, and the link goes to the
  conflicts board rather than featuring one of them arbitrarily.
