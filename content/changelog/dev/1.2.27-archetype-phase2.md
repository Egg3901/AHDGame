Cut from development.

## Changed

- Legislation demographic effects target census buckets (for example
  "white voters" or "no college") instead of archetype labels. The seventeen
  effects that actually worked are converted to move exactly what they always
  moved; sixty-three entries that named groups which never existed in the game
  are removed rather than switched on.
- Policy approval tables are keyed on buckets. Character and politician
  approval data migrates through the same mapping the engine already used, so
  standing approvals carry over.
- Referendum campaign cohorts are re-authored as per-region bucket tables with
  their own historical judgment per cohort. Targeting is now sharper but each
  target is a smaller slice of the electorate, so a targeted broadcast moves
  less in aggregate than before; the balance contract test carries the new
  baseline.
- A new gate test proves every seeded region in every country resolves a
  granular demographic substrate, which is the safety condition for removing
  the old archetype data entirely in a later pass.

## Not changed

- Election results and vote mechanics. Votes have run on the demographic
  buckets for some time.
- Bucket population shares. Legislation cannot shift a demographic group's
  size; the entries that claimed to were among the ones that never worked.
