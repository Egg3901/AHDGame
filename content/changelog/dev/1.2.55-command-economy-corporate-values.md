## What changed

- Six command-economy currencies had no player-traded forex row by design. Some economic calculations treated that missing row as a 1:1 rate with the anchor currency, inflating corporate values by 13.5 to 27 times.
- The corporation turn, national metrics, country market snapshots, and admin economy totals now use each era's authored conversion rate when no live market rate exists. A live rate always wins where one exists.
- Cross-border sectors now enter market clearing in their host country's currency instead of their owner's home currency.
- The sector expansion and sector attack commands now enforce the existing command-economy market gate. Hiding those countries from suggestions was not enough because a direct request could still reach the write path.

Seeded state enterprises are unchanged. The private-sector gate only blocks player market entry while the country remains a command economy.
