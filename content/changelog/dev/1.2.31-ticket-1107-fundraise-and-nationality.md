Cut from development.

## Fixed

- The Fundraise card promised more money than it paid. Campaign funds convert
  from anchor to local currency at a frozen base rate that never moves with the
  market, but the card ran the same figure through the live exchange rate. In a
  1953 East Germany the live mark rate is 4.2 against a frozen campaign rate of
  2.22, so the card read M1.8M for a payout of roughly M1.0M. Every surface that
  quotes the yield now shows the exact face value that lands in the treasury.
- The Fundraise card also left out the Fundraising stat, which scales the payout
  by up to plus or minus 20%. A strong fundraiser was quoted low and a weak one
  quoted high. The card, the Donor Network panel, and the profile finances strip
  now all call the one helper the action itself uses, so the quote and the credit
  cannot drift apart again.
- Starting Nationality on the profile showed the country of a character you may
  no longer have. It read an account-level field that is written once, from the
  first character a player ever creates, so anyone whose next character started
  somewhere else saw the old flag. Starting nationality is now recorded on the
  character when it is created. Existing characters show the country they are
  currently in, and characters that emigrate from here on keep the country they
  actually started in.
