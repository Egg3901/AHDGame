## What changed (PR #1311)

- `assemblePhysicalPnl` now passes `otherOpex` through `clampOtherOpexCredit`. A negative residual (a credit) is floored at minus the sum of the named bills (inputs, labour, financial legs, upkeep, compliance, growth) net of a positive policy credit, so total cost is never negative and profit never exceeds revenue. A positive residual, and any credit that leaves total cost at or above zero, is untouched, so the calibration identity still holds wherever it held honestly. (The first cut of this in PR #1311 ignored the policy credit; 80 retail-shaped sectors were still 1 to 6% above revenue through it.)
- `PhysicalPnl` and the persisted `plantsPnl` carry `otherOpexCreditCapped` and `otherOpexUncapped` so a capped sector is visible in the data.
- Root cause on prod (turn 571): the residual is solved once per unit and re-multiplied by `producedUnits` every turn with nothing tying it to the bills. One NPC rare-earth sector carried anchor -1201 per unit across 161K units, booked -1.74T JPY/day of negative cost as profit, priced its corporation at 14x the next-largest on the exchange, and pulled 57% of the Global Top 50 index fund into it. Eleven live sectors booked profit above revenue through the same channel.

## Verification

- New tests: charge and honest-credit passthrough, clamp at exactly the bills, negative bill lines ignored, zero-bill credit clamps to zero, the corp 643 Kanto shape yields profit at most revenue.
