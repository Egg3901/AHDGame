  Defence appropriations and upkeep overdrafts now settle atomically, preventing
  concurrent turn passes from charging a treasury twice.

The per-turn defence account now commits its appropriation change and any
upkeep overdraft against the same federal budget document. Concurrent turn
passes cannot charge the treasury twice for one defence settlement.
