Raised on ticket #1072.

## Changed

- East German automobiles seed smaller. The previous change grew the car works
  tenfold to help soak the republic's steel, but automobiles make vehicles, and
  the DDR has nobody to sell vehicles to: every Warsaw Pact neighbour builds its
  own and the CoCom embargo walls off the West. The extra cars just piled up
  unsold and a state-run car maker bled on them every turn. The car works now
  seeds at a token size instead.
- Construction still carries the steel. Cutting the car plant does not re-open
  the steel glut, because the car works only ever ate a small slice of East
  German steel. The building programme is the real steel sink, and it keeps its
  full size.
- East Germany now has the appetite to match its building programme. The
  programme was seeded at plan scale but had almost no in-game buyer, so it sat
  near-idle at the price floor. New worlds lift East German construction demand
  to about three-quarters of what the republic can build, so the crews it seeds
  are crews it actually hires.

## Why it mattered

A command economy cannot export a surplus it is embargoed out of selling, and it
bans the private founding that would create new domestic buyers. Left alone, the
republic was stuck making cars nobody bought and standing up building crews with
no work to give them.

## Not changed

- This is a domestic, East-Germany-only adjustment. No other country's markets
  move, and the world price of construction or vehicles is untouched.
- Nothing anybody owns was cut. Existing sectors, including every plant a player
  runs, keep their capacity.
