## Changed

- **What a formation draws from the front's supply pool now depends on what it is and
  where it stands.** Supply demand was every formation's treasury upkeep divided by
  twelve, whatever it was and wherever it stood. A fighter wing costs 250 a turn against
  an infantry division's 70, so the model billed a wing as three and a half divisions of
  trucked tonnage at a land front, and the Soviet air arm and rocket forces alone were
  905 of the 1,871 demand that read CUT OFF on the German front at turn 503. Air, naval,
  rocket and space formations now draw 25 percent of their upkeep from the land-front
  pool; ground and marine formations still pay in full.
- **Formations the engagement plan holds in depth draw 60 percent.** Depth engages at a
  tenth and bleeds at 0.15, and was the one place the model still charged full
  ammunition and fuel. Keyed on the plan, not the player's role label, for the same
  reason the tail bonus is.
- **A Logistics command is sized by the force it feeds.** It delivered a flat 20 supply
  at full effectiveness, which was calibrated against an eleven-formation front and was
  worth two points of supply to a fifty-nine formation coalition. It now delivers 25
  percent of its own contingent's front demand, times command effectiveness, so
  commanders and capacity still matter and one command per flag covers its own
  formations without stacking.
- **The side fighting on its own soil hauls 25 percent more.** The side holding the
  conflict's host country, which in an interstate war is the nation that was declared
  on, gets interior lines, depots and rail as a throughput multiplier applied before
  enemy interdiction. Measured at 1.10 (indistinguishable from none), 1.25 and 1.50
  (which let a defender stack a front without limit); 1.25 keeps the cost of stacking
  real.
- Measured on the live German front (turn 503): the Warsaw Pact moves from 22 CUT OFF
  to 63 STRAINED as deployed, or 75 with a Soviet Logistics command; NATO from 53
  SHORTAGE to SUPPLIED. Odds barely move; attrition for the starved side falls from
  about 2.15x to 1.4x. Full workings in `scripts/sim/reports/front-supply-2026-08-30.md`.
- Wiki: the war-room and occupation pages now say what feeds supply and what does not.
