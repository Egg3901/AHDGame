Cut from development.

## Added

- Region page, Politics > Elections: every race renders as a results card with a
  phase badge, a headline keyed to the viewer's own party during a primary, the
  count (share, votes, seats where the chamber apportions them), turnout against
  the region's estimated electorate, and a link into the full count. A wire strip
  below the sections carries the whole ballot's leads and the region's turnout.
- Election detail page, non-presidential races: a verdict hero, a final seat
  allocation block, the count with per-candidate shares, the clock, and the trend.
  Region names and chamber labels come from the region and country configuration,
  so a Belarusian Supreme Soviet race reads "Gomel Supreme Soviet Race" instead of
  its internal keys.
- Down-ballot primaries accrue real ballots. Each turn of the closing stretch of
  the primary (as long as the race's general window) releases a slice of each
  party's registered-voter pool, split by that turn's standings, and the nominee
  is whoever the cumulative count says. Primary standings on the cards and the
  detail page show those cumulative shares.

## Fixed

- General elections only count registered voters. The unregistered share of a
  region's registration pool could never cast a ballot, but the vote pool spanned
  the whole electorate. Vote shares, seats and winners are unchanged; ballot
  counts and turnout percentages are now honest.
- The presidential count uses the voting-eligible population in each state, the
  same basis as every other race, instead of total population.
- A race can no longer certify more ballots than it has voters. The final turn of
  a general election was releasing the last slice of the pool twice, and the
  office-strength multiplier sat outside the electorate cap; a DD governor race
  certified 102% turnout. The count now runs through the resolution turn with
  the pool conserved across every turn, and a cumulative ceiling holds the total
  under the registered electorate.
- A turn that stalls in a later phase and is re-run (a cleared processing lock)
  no longer banks the same election slice again. On the live world one turn ran
  three times and every open general counted it three times.
- Union-republic Supreme Soviets (Ukraine, Byelorussia, the Baltics) apportion
  their whole delegation by vote share. They resolved as single-winner races,
  seating one deputy in a chamber of 85 to 129.
- Multi-seat chambers outside the US, UK, Japan and China (Bundestag, Landtag,
  Dail, soviets, eastern-bloc assemblies) project seats on the detail page. The
  projection kept its own list of chamber types and had drifted from the engine.
- A primary whose parties already sat within the advance cap (UK, Japan and
  Germany multi-advance chambers, one-party states) records its primary result
  instead of going through the general without one.
- Region Officials tab: the Soviet of Nationalities shows each deputy's seat
  count instead of "Class", which only applies to a rotating-class senate.
