Cut from development.

## Fixed

- A region's road condition, which sets a margin penalty on every logistics-
  dependent sector including manufacturing, moved only on government
  infrastructure spending. The size of the local logistics sector was not an
  input at all, so a player could build freight until the region reported it
  over-supplied and still watch manufacturing lose money on "poor logistics" —
  two screens that never reconciled. A region's own logistics-sector output now
  feeds its road/transport reading: a strong freight sector lifts it, a thin one
  drags it. This mirrors how installed generation already feeds grid
  reliability. Regions with no logistics data keep their previous value exactly.
