Cut from development.

## Added

- Game Health gained a **Phase Budget** warning source. Each turn phase is
  killed if it runs past four minutes, and that failure takes the whole turn
  with it. Until now nothing reported a phase approaching that limit, because a
  phase that is slow but still finishes produces no warning, no skip and no
  error. The check reads the timings each turn already records and reports the
  single worst phase per turn once it passes half its budget, escalating to an
  error past three quarters.

  Only the worst phase in a turn is reported, so a broadly slow turn produces
  one useful line rather than thirty. Measured against the last three weeks of
  recorded turns, this stays silent on healthy turns: the busiest recent turns
  sit between a tenth and a quarter of the budget.

## Changed

- The four minute phase ceiling is now defined in one place and shared by the
  turn engine that enforces it and the health check that measures against it,
  so the two cannot drift apart.
