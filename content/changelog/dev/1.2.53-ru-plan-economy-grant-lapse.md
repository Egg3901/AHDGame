Cut from development.

## Fixed

- RU's regional grant was never booked as national spending. The union
  distributes its whole regional budget as a population share of the central
  grants pool, and — like CN, DE and the UK — has no `isGrant` law to record it,
  but RU was missing from the config-derived transfer table. So ₽44.00B a year
  reached the republics while the national budget recorded ₽0 of it: money that
  existed on one side of the ledger only.

## Changed

- **Plan allocations now lapse.** A planned economy is charged for what its
  republics actually draw down, capped at their allocation, rather than for the
  whole allocation. Live RU allocates ₽44.00B and its republics spend ₽24.34B —
  six of fourteen spend nothing at all — and the ₽19.66B remainder banks into a
  regional surplus that is never accumulated anywhere. Booking it would charge
  the union for programmes that do not exist. A market economy's transfer is
  genuinely disbursed whether the region spends it or not, so it still books in
  full; the gate is `isPlannedEconomy`, so this cannot touch the UK, DE or JP.

## Balance impact

Measured against the live 1957 world:

|                 | allocated | booked  | lapsed  | national surplus                                  |
| --------------- | --------- | ------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| RU (planned)    | ₽44.00B   | ₽24.34B | ₽19.66B | +₽22.12B → −₽2.22B                                |
| CN (planned)    | ₽1.94B    | ₽1.94B  | ₽0.00B  | unchanged — republics overspend, so the cap binds |
| DE, UK (market) | —         | in full | —       | unchanged                                         |

RU's resulting deficit is **0.19% of GDP** against a treasury at 1.65%
debt-to-GDP; at that rate its debt would take roughly 44 in-game years to reach
10% of GDP, so this is nowhere near the sovereign-risk machinery. CN is
untouched because its provinces already spend far beyond their transfer.
