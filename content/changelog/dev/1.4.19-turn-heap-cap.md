Hotfix to main.

## Fixed

- Turn processing no longer runs out of heap partway through. The start command
  capped V8 old-space at 1024 MB, which V8 reports as a 1275 MB heap limit, so the
  heapWatchdog's 75% threshold put its kill line near 956 MB. Idle sits around
  400 MB and a turn adds roughly 600 MB, so turns crossed the line: turn 460
  tripped at 993 MB. The cap is now 3072 MB, which puts the kill line near 2.4 GB
  against a container with about 5 GB, and leaves the watchdog's RSS ceiling in
  place as the real backstop.

- One trip used to cost two turn slots, not one. The watchdog exits the process
  mid-turn, which leaves `isProcessing` set on game state, and `TURN_LOCK_STALE_MS`
  holds that lock for twenty minutes before anything may reclaim it. With turns on
  an hourly schedule, that reliably swallowed the following turn as well. Raising
  the cap stops the trips; the lock interaction itself is unchanged and still
  worth revisiting.

## Notes

Turn duration has been climbing with world size, from roughly 75s to 260s over the
last forty turns, tracking growth in elections, NPPs and corporations. This change
buys headroom, it does not remove the trend. The heaviest phases in the tripping
turn were `corporationTurn`, `nppActionProcessing` and `indexFunds`.
