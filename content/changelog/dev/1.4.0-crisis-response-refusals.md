Cut from development.

## Fixed

- Responding to an international crisis reported "Internal server error" for
  every kind of refusal. Choosing an option your nation lacks the capacity for,
  answering after your government had already responded, answering a crisis that
  had closed, or picking an option that was no longer on the table all produced
  the same blank failure, so there was no way to tell a rule from an outage. Each
  of those now returns its own reason, and the crisis card shows it.
- The crisis card on the Actions page offered every option as clickable, even
  ones your nation could not meet the requirements for, so the only way to find
  out was to pick one and be refused. Those options are now greyed out with the
  requirements listed underneath, matching how the full crisis page has always
  shown them.

## Notes

- Genuine faults are unchanged: they still report as a server error and are
  still captured for the team. Only refusals the player can act on were being
  misreported as failures.
