Cut from development.

## Fixed

- A commanding general opening "view this command's structure and units" was sent
  into the defence seat's office, which the cabinet records gate closes to
  everyone but the seat holder, their head of government and their head of state.
  The answer was "Office records restricted" on their own command. The command's
  structure, regions and units are now published on the commanding general's own
  page, read-only, and the link into the office is offered only to a viewer who
  may actually open it.

- The commanding general's page counted a general's force from the units inside
  the command alone. A unit travels with the general it is assigned to, whichever
  command holds it, so a general could be posted to a war with divisions the page
  never showed. Both are now counted.

- "Who is posted here" and "Your profile" on a conflict's command panel both led
  to a page that does not exist and answered 404. The conflict record now carries
  a roster of your nation's generals standing at that front, with their rank,
  their divisions and who holds the theater, and the profile link goes to your
  profile. The roster follows the same rule as the rest of the force detail: a
  seat in a live war sees it, a citizen of a belligerent nation does not.

- A command that listed a commander who had emigrated or been dismissed could not
  be edited again. The panel could not draw a row for someone the country's roster
  no longer held, so the count stood above an empty list with nothing to remove,
  while every save was refused over that same name. Such a commander is now
  dropped when the office loads, the office says why, and a commanding general who
  has left the country no longer keeps command of its army. A migration clears the
  two commands already in that state, Russia's and one of the United Kingdom's, so
  neither waits on its defence secretary opening the office.
