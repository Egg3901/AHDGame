Cut from development.

## Fixed

- Authored 1953 extraction deposits for Ukraine, Poland, Czechoslovakia,
  Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria, Byelorussia, the Baltics, and Yugoslavia.
  These countries seeded every state with an empty deposit record, which
  caps extraction at zero; only a shortage-relief loophole let their mining
  enterprises produce anything. Ukraine's entry splits the original combined
  budget (Donbass coal, Krivoy Rog iron, Dashava gas) across its states
  without changing the country total.
- China's extraction enterprise retools from coal to iron mining in its four
  iron-rich regions (Northeast, North, Central, Southwest), matching the
  deposits it actually sits on. The change uses the normal strategy
  transition: blended output over the transition window, a cooldown, and the
  standard capital rescale, so nothing teleports.

## Guarded

- The live-world migration fills deposits only where the stored record is
  still empty, so prospecting gains and admin edits are never overwritten.
- Tests pin the authored coverage, the preserved Ukraine country budget, the
  headroom scaling, the empty-only backfill rule, and the retool transition
  fields.
