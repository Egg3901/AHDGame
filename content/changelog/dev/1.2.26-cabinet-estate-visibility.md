## Fixed

- The Active modifiers panel now lists "Cabinet, orders and estates" as its own
  line, and the target it prints includes that term. The served target used to
  be laws plus structural conditions only, so it disagreed with the number the
  engine actually drifts toward and a newly built estate changed nothing on
  screen.

## Added

- The panel warns when the cabinet channel is at its ceiling for a metric in
  most of the country. At the ceiling, another order or estate aimed at that
  metric contributes exactly zero, which is worth knowing before you spend on
  one.
- The panel states the pace: roughly how many turns it takes for the value to
  close half the remaining gap to its target. Metric movement is deliberately
  slow, and it reads as broken when nothing names the speed.
