Fixes ticket #1072.

## What was broken

A player set a high modernization (R&D) budget on a state enterprise and its
modernization chance stayed at 0% no matter how high the budget went. The
enterprise earned revenue in the millions per turn, but its score never moved.

R&D spend was gated on `liquidCapital`: a turn only advanced modernization if the
corp held enough cash to pay for it. But an SOE's operating profit is remitted to
the treasury each turn and is never banked as corp cash, so `liquidCapital` sits
at ~0 in steady state. The gate could therefore never pass for a normally
operating enterprise. The only corps that modernized were ones still draining a
seeded starting balance, and they would have silently stopped once it ran out.

## The fix

Modernization is funded from the enterprise's own revenue, which is what the
mechanic always intended: spend is capped at a fraction of the corp's per-turn
revenue, so it is affordable from operations by construction. The cost is charged
as an operating expense in the same estimate that drives both the treasury
remittance and the budget page's State Enterprises line, so the treasury funds
R&D by remitting less. It never touches `liquidCapital`.

## Changes

- `buildRdModernizationOps` drops the `liquidCapital` affordability gate and the
  liquid debit. A funded SOE advances its score regardless of cash on hand;
  spend is still capped at revenue and still decays to zero when unfunded.
- `estimateNationalizedOperatingIncome` subtracts the R&D charge as an operating
  cost, so remittance and the budget line both reflect it with one source of
  truth. The charge is capped identically to the turn math.
