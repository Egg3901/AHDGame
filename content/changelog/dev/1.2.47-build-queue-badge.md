## Fixed

- The pill on a sector row reading something like "+6.9k 38T" is capacity you
  have paid for and is still being built, with the turns until the next order
  lands. It sat next to the freight badge with no label of its own, so it read
  as part of the freight figure, and the only explanation was a tooltip that
  never appears on a touch screen. The pill now says "building" on its face and
  carries the same sentence as its accessible name, so it reads the same with a
  mouse, on a phone, and in a screen reader.
