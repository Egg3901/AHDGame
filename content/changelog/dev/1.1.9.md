## What changed

Two player-facing capabilities are held back behind config flags until they are
ready:

- **Bank charters:** players may only charter, or switch to, a **retail** bank.
  Investment and universal charters are withheld behind
  `playerAdvancedBankChartersEnabled`. The gate lives in the one function both
  new charters and type switches consult, so a withheld type is refused
  everywhere with a single check.
- **Index funds:** a corporation cannot **sponsor its own index fund**. The
  default (system) funds still trade normally; player sponsorship is withheld
  behind `playerFundSponsorshipEnabled`.

Both gates apply only when a charter or fund is created or switched, so anything
already active keeps operating unchanged. The flags can be turned on later
without a deploy.
