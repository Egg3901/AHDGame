summary: >-
  The 1953 era demand corrections for advertising, healthcare services and
  natural gas were sized from an old simulation that measured deep shortages.
  The live world has far more supply, so those corrections overshot into large
  gluts: advertising traded at 40 percent of its base price, healthcare at 47
  percent, and gas at 59 percent, which kept media, entertainment and
  healthcare companies loss-making no matter how well they were run. Demand
  for these three goods is now sized from live market data, so their prices
  can recover and the companies that sell them can earn a margin again.

## Fixed

- Recalibrated 1953 era demand for advertising, natural gas and healthcare
  services against live market flow instead of a pre-launch simulation. The
  old corrections cut demand 3 to 28 times too far, leaving these goods in
  permanent glut at floor prices and their producers structurally unprofitable.
