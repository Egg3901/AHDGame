Cut from development.

## Fixed

- Party registration in a state moves again once the state's Independent and Unregistered
  pool is empty. Since the pool cap shipped, the only mover left was the tiny passive decay,
  so a party with 38% organization could sit at 1% registration indefinitely while a party
  with 13% organization held 77%. The passive climb toward organization now draws from the
  non-party pool first and then from parties whose registration exceeds their organization,
  in proportion to that surplus. Nothing is minted: the state pool still totals 100%, no
  bucket goes negative, and a party is never drawn below its own organization.

## Changed

- The passive registration climb is a little faster: 0.075 points per turn toward
  organization, up from 0.06 (1.25x). A party 35 points below its organization now closes
  the gap in about 20 game days instead of 25, and two organized challengers pull a 77%
  incumbent under 60% in roughly 150 turns, the low end of the design's stronghold-fall band.
  A state where no party is below its organization does not move, so unchallenged seeded
  strongholds stay as durable as before. The governor's party contributes at a reduced weight,
  the same home-field relief its decay already has.
