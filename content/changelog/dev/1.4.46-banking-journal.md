Cut from development. Second of the banking rebuild pull requests tracked in
issue #1328 (PR pending).

## Fixed

- A loan a bank writes now leaves the bank's vault. Origination used to credit
  the borrower and touch nothing on the bank but a counter, so lending created
  money and every repayment credited the bank with cash it had never paid out.
  The bank's cash reserves and lendable headroom always assumed the bank funds
  the loan; now it does. The same applies when a CEO approves a pending loan.
- A pending loan at an investment bank can be accepted; approval measured
  headroom against a deposit base that kind of bank does not have.
- A bank that has already been resolved no longer receives interbank
  recoveries. Its owner was paid out and its depositors made whole when it
  closed, so a later recovery goes to the deposit insurer that stood behind it.
  A bank that has failed but not yet been resolved takes the recovery into its
  estate, where its own resolution distributes it.
- A three-way resolution could fail its last debit by a fraction of a cent.
  Pro-rata shares are now split to the cent, with any remainder going to the
  largest claim.

## Changed

- Every money-moving bank command is decided by one pure rules boundary
  (snapshot in, allowed or refused with a reason the player can read, and one
  balanced transition out) and landed by one settlement journal that claims the
  key, moves the legs, and applies the follow-up writes with an idempotency
  stamp. Retries replay; crashes leave a record the repair queue can finish.
- The repair queue now says exactly which half of a crashed move landed, and
  which follow-up writes did not.
- Loan servicing, interbank interest and the household deposit flow advance a
  record at most once per turn however many times a turn is re-run.
- Lifetime counters on the insurance fund and the treasury's record of a
  backstop are written with the money they describe, so a retry can neither
  omit nor double them.
- The in-memory test store now understands array-index paths and pushes with
  slices, and a crash-injection harness can kill a flow at any write.
