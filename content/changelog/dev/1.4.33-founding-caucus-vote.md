Cut from development.

## Fixed

- Founding elections waive the 24-hour new-character wait and the party-tenure
  requirement so brand-new players can seat their first leadership at the start
  of an iteration. Party chair and state party chair races did this; caucus
  chair races did not, so the same player was accepted in one race and turned
  away from the other on the same day.
- A caucus race takes its whole schedule from its party's chair election, so
  during the founding phase it was already running in the accelerated window.
  It just was not marked as a founding race, and the vote check ran before the
  election was even loaded, so it had no way to know. Caucus races now inherit
  that status and waive the same two gates.
