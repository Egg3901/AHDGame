Cut from development.

Reported as ticket #1116: a Centre-left character drifted to Moderate over a
handful of turns with no deliberate ideology change by the player.

The ideology label is a pure bucket read of the stored `policies.economic`
and `policies.social` values, so the label was not lying. The stored position
really was moving. Every non-abstain vote calls `applyBillVotePolicyShift`,
which walks the voter 0.25 toward the provision's position on each axis. Bill
validation stamped an omitted axis as `0` rather than leaving it absent, and
`computePolicyShift` only skipped `undefined`. A bill about taxes therefore
carried an implicit "social position: dead centre", and voting on it pulled
the voter's social axis 0.25 toward zero. Repeat across a session's worth of
routine votes and a real ideological position erodes into Moderate.

Two changes, both needed. Validation now stamps `undefined` for an unset axis
and omits the key entirely, so new bills stop recording a phantom centre
stance. `computePolicyShift` now treats `0` as no stance alongside `undefined`,
which also stops the drift on the bills already sitting in the database with
the old stamped zeros. No backfill is required: the shift function is the only
consumer that read those zeros as a target.

Non-zero positions still shift exactly as before, including a genuine centre
target expressed as a real position on an axis a bill does address.
