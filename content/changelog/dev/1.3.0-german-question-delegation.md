## Changed

- Every delegation on the German Question board now lists the two offices that
  can act for it and who holds each. A seat is exercisable by its country's
  head of government and its foreign minister, sharing one budget, but the
  board never said so: a player looking at "United States · NSC · SECONDARY ·
  1.0× · quiet" had no way to learn that the seat runs through the President
  and the Secretary of State, let alone whether anyone was in either chair.
- Offices are named in each country's own terms rather than a generic label.
  Washington's seat is the President and the Secretary of State, London's the
  Prime Minister and the Foreign Secretary, Moscow's the Premier and East
  Berlin's the General Secretary, both alongside a Minister of Foreign
  Affairs.
- Both offices are always listed, including empty ones. A delegation with two
  vacant chairs is not missing information: it is the fact that nobody can
  currently act for that capital, which is worth as much to a rival as a name
  would be. An office held by a non-player party reads as vacant too, matching
  what the board will actually accept as a play.
