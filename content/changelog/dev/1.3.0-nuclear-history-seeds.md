## Historical arsenals at world start

World resets now seed the nuclear age as it actually stood at the seed year.
The United States, the Soviet Union, and the United Kingdom each open with the
programme nodes their real counterparts had adopted by that year, and a
game-scale stockpile to match: a 1945 world starts with a single American
fission device and nothing else, a 1959 world starts with three tested powers
and hundreds of decisions already made for you.

Live worlds pick this up too, without disturbing anyone's progress: a country
whose defence seat has already opened a programme keeps exactly what it built.

## The wiki learns about the bomb

Two new pages in the Conflicts & Military category:

- **The Nuclear Programme**: entry gates, the device ladder and its public
  tests, delivery legs, production rates and warhead costs, and a word of
  warning about covert programmes.
- **Cold War Tension**: the world's shared gauge, its five bands, what moves
  it, and what it does to readiness, procurement, and diplomacy.

The Conflicts overview now points at the cold war layer and its tension
header, so the new surfaces are one click from the front page of the war
docs.
