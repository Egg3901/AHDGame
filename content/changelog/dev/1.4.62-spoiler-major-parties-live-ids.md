Cut from development. PR #1469.

## Fixed

- `getMajorPartiesForRegion` returns seed slugs and abbreviations for the UK
  and Japan (the US already carried both encodings). A new
  `partitionMajorParties` helper matches a candidate's `party` or `partyAbbr`
  against the set, and, in a live race with three or more parties where the
  config names none of them (a 1953 Japan has no LDP), treats the two
  heaviest parties as major. Fixtures without abbreviations keep the legacy
  behaviour: no config match, no spoiler.
- Both vote engines and the poll estimate use the helper, so spoiler leakage
  and polling agree again for the UK and Japan.
