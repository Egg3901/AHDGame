  A turn was pulling hundreds of thousands of documents out of the database and
  throwing most of them away. The ledger was loaded 61,398 rows at a time to
  compute a sum the database could have done itself, every corporation carried a
  block of policy positions no turn phase reads, and each turn wrote a megabyte
  of market diagnostics nothing ever read back. Decoding those documents, not
  running the simulation, was the bulk of the work. The reads are narrower now
  and the numbers they produce are unchanged.

Cut from development.

## Changed

- Ledger turnover is grouped in the database instead of in the application.
  The economic vital signs phase was loading every ledger entry in a twelve-turn
  window — 61,398 documents, 23.5 MB — to add up primary-leg amounts per
  account. The same aggregation returns 1,389 rows and 88 KB. Balances are
  unchanged: both paths were run over identical data and agreed to fifteen
  decimal places, the last-bit difference that any change in floating-point
  addition order produces.

- Index-fund dividend pass-through and equity market pool credits are applied in
  bulk rather than one accrual at a time. Both were touching the same handful of
  documents repeatedly — the equity pools collection holds one row per currency
  and was being read once per accrual.

- Corporations no longer carry their plant profit-and-loss block into the four
  turn reads that never open it, and the per-turn market snapshot keeps a sample
  of empty market cells rather than all of them. The snapshot fell from 893 KB
  to 144 KB per turn; every total it reports is still exact, and it now records
  how many rows the sample omitted so nothing can mistake it for the full list.

## Added

- A profiler that attributes every database command to the turn phase that
  issued it, counting documents returned as well as calls made. Turn cost is
  dominated by decoding documents, so the two rank phases differently: a single
  query returning sixty thousand rows is one call and a great deal of work.
  Enabled with `AHD_TURN_ROUNDTRIP_PROFILE=1`.

## Fixed

- Database commands issued from an async continuation inside a turn phase are
  attributed to that phase instead of to nothing. A quarter of a turn's reads
  were previously unattributable, which hid two phases entirely.

- Paginated query batches are logged against their real collection. Every
  `getMore` had been recorded as "unknown", which affected the existing
  diagnostic breadcrumbs as well as the new profiler.
