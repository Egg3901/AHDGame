title: "1.1.8: Input bills capped at world prices"
summary: >-
  Hotfix for the corporate margin collapse introduced in 1.1.7. Plant input
  bills switched to each country's reachable market prices, but in markets
  where reachable prices sit far above the world level this raised costs 30
  to 60 percent in a single turn while revenue stayed flat, pushing most
  corporations into losses. Inputs are now billed at the lower of the world
  price and the local reachable price, so buyers in cheap markets keep their
  discount and no one pays a premium the market never lets them earn back.
tags: [corporations, economy]

## Fixed

- Plant input costs are billed at the lower of the world price ratio and the
  sector country's reachable price ratio. 1.1.7 billed the reachable ratio
  directly, which in high-priced markets charged corporations input premiums
  far above what their own sales could ever recover, and most corporations
  worldwide went loss-making within a turn.
