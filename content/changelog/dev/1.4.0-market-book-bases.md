Cut from development.

## Changed

- Commodity flow and sourcing records now carry explicit measurement bases.
  New basis-specific fields preserve the existing values while making their
  scope clear to new readers.
- The commodity page and corporation commodity view now label pooled world
  estimates as pooled figures. They no longer describe frictionless world
  availability as route-level transactions.

## Added

- A reconciliation report helper compares calibrated ledger demand, buyer
  intent, pooled availability, and country-scoped clearing without forcing the
  books to agree. Each difference includes a named explanation.

## Guarded

- Focused tests pin the known turn-365-shaped demand, energy shortage, and
  clearing differences, and verify that basis markers survive persistence
  round-trips.
- This is a measurement-only change. Commodity prices, sourcing decisions,
  trade clearing, stock movement, and every other economic outcome retain their
  existing arithmetic.
