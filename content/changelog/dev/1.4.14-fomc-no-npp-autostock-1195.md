## Changed

- When a seat on the Federal Reserve committee reaches the end of its term, it
  now falls **vacant** instead of being auto-filled with an appointed technocrat.
  A vacant seat abstains, so an understaffed committee simply cannot move the rate
  until the President nominates a successor and the Senate confirms them. The Fed
  is meant to be staffed by appointment, not by the engine.

- The Federal Reserve chair is no longer auto-appointed to a machine candidate
  when no nominee is queued. The chair seat stays vacant and waits for a
  presidential nomination, ending the loop where the same AI chair kept
  re-appointing itself. Central banks outside the United States keep their own
  governor model unchanged.
