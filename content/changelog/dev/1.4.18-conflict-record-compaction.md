Cut from development.

## Changed

- The state of the front no longer repeats the totals printed directly above it.
  The dead and the engagement count are stat tiles at the top of the page; the
  sentence under the verdict now states how far the line has moved and, when
  nothing has been contested yet, that nothing has. A front where no shot has
  been fired still says so.

- Momentum states the span it measured. It reports ground moved over the window it
  actually drew rather than reprinting the war's totals, which is what made it
  look like it was contradicting the verdict: one figure is measured from the
  opening of the war and the other over the last few dozen turns, and neither said
  which.

- An ally dragged in by a charter is explained once, in the belligerents roll,
  which now reads "Warsaw Pact → DD" instead of leaving a prose line above the
  masthead to repeat what the column already said.

- A war fought over ground with no map drops the map. Some conflict hosts have no
  drawable territory, and the page was reserving a full column for a box
  containing the sentence "no mapped territory" while every panel worth reading
  queued down the narrow rail beside it. Without a map the panels spread across
  the width instead.

- The command panel comes first. Declaring an offensive sat fourth in the rail
  behind the belligerents, the momentum track and the force readout, which put the
  one control on the page below the fold. Your role, your orders and your generals
  now lead, and the reference panels follow.

- Force composition withheld from a reader without a seat is one row rather than
  six cells all reading the same thing, so the casualty figures, the numbers that
  are public, are no longer pushed down behind them.

- The commitment banner only appears for a reader who holds a country. A logged
  out visitor cannot post units anywhere and was being given the widest strip on
  the page to be told what would happen if they did. The rule that allies posted
  to a front defend it automatically now lives in that banner instead of being
  restated at the foot of the page.
