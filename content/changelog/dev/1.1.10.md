## What changed

Money on the corporation pages was shown per financial day (24 turns), which
made it hard to line a figure up with what actually happens on a single turn.
The default view is now **per turn**, labelled `/turn`. The money toggle still
offers Day and Year, so nothing is lost, and the default is what most players
were mentally converting to anyway.

This covers the whole corporation surface: the Financials tab and its summary
band, the Overview and CEO budget and production views, the sectors list, the
bonds panel, the expand-market screen, and the deep per-sector financials. On
the per-sector Financials panel each row shows the per-turn amount with the
daily figure kept in its tooltip, so you can still see both.

## What did not change

This is a labelling change. The stored figures, wages, margins, growth costs,
taxes, and every underlying calculation are exactly as before; the display just
divides the daily rate by the 24 turns in a financial day. Growth rates are
still shown per game year, and physical capacity is still shown per day for now.
