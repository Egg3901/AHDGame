## Fixed

- Net margin on the corporation Financials page now uses the income the
  engine actually booked last turn instead of the projected income
  statement, matching the net income headline. A company with a realized
  loss no longer shows a healthy positive margin two tiles away.
- The Dividends and Retained Earnings lines in the income statement now use
  the same realized basis, so retained earnings can no longer show a profit
  while the headline shows a loss.
- The income statement bottom line is labeled "Net Income (last turn)" when
  it reflects a booked turn, with the itemized projection lines above marked
  as such in the tooltip.
