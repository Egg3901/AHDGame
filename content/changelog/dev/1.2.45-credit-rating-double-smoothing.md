## Fixed

- A corporation could show two credit ratings at once: AA at 76 out of 100 on
  the bonds panel while its header, health card and peer comparison all showed
  BBB at 47. The score carries deliberate inertia, blending three quarters of
  the new reading with one quarter of the previous one so a single bad turn
  cannot wreck a rating. The turn does that blend and saves the result. The
  bonds panel was then feeding that saved result back in as though it were the
  previous reading and blending it a second time, reporting a score the turn
  never recorded. Only the turn blends now, and every screen reads the same
  saved number.
