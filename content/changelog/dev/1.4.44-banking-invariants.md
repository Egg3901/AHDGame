Cut from development. First of the banking rebuild pull requests tracked in
issue #1328 (PR #1332).

## Fixed

- A loan made by an investment bank now advances every turn. The banking turn
  only walked retail and universal charters, so a corporation loan an
  investment bank was allowed to write was never charged interest or
  principal, never fell into arrears, and never paid the bank. Loan servicing
  now runs for every active charter with a loan book; deposits, household
  funding and insurance stay with deposit-taking charters.
- A pending loan at an investment bank can now be accepted. The approval path
  measured headroom against a deposit base the bank does not have, so it
  refused every loan it had itself parked as pending.

## Changed

- What a charter may do is decided from one table. Deposit taking, household
  and named lending, interbank activity, the prop desk, the discount window,
  the margin line, rate setting and the branch network each come from the same
  capability decision, with one player-facing sentence per refusal.
- Feature switches are read once per request or turn and carried as a frozen
  policy, so a route and the turn cannot disagree about what is enabled.
- The balance-sheet, reserve, insurance, lending, deposit-share, rate and
  capacity formulas live in one rules module each, with a numeric lock test
  taken from the previous implementation. Values are unchanged.
- Every banking action now writes a uniform audit event: charter issue, revoke
  and switch, savings holder change, deposit and withdrawal, loan origination,
  approval, rejection, disbursement, payment, delinquency and default, bank
  failure and resolution, committee meeting transitions, votes and policy rate
  changes. Events carry the correlation id, the command, the turn, the actor
  class, the currency, before and after status and the settlement key.
- New per-turn banking counters (rejected, stale, replayed, partial and
  rejected settlements, stage timings) and an admin health report that
  reconciles player-held savings against what each bank claims, per currency,
  and reports the oldest unfinished settlement. The turn summary now reports
  unfinished settlements directly.
