## Fixed

- A Senate-confirmed nominee who already held another cabinet seat could not be
  seated: the one-seat-per-character rule blocked the appointment, the
  nomination stayed open, and the confirmation silently never happened. The
  confirmation now vacates the nominee's previous seat first, so the move
  completes and the old position opens up for a fresh appointment.
