## The wide price leg reads the reachable book

State prices blend three legs: 50% wide, 25% national, 25% regional. The wide
leg was the planet-wide supply/demand aggregate — one number for every country
on Earth, iron curtain included.

The clearing engine stopped using that aggregate in 1.1.2 (#1077): sellers are
filled from per-country REACHABLE books built on the live trade-policy graph.
But pricing never followed. Partitioned clearing with unpartitioned pricing
meant an embargo or an autarkic bloc stopped the goods and not the glut:
supply that could never arrive still set the price when it got there.

Observed on production, turn 150: Ukraine's command farms carried 6.32M units
of food supply while trading with nobody — every one of those units was
`untradedSupply`, absent from every clearing book, unreachable by any buyer —
and the single global ledger still counted them, holding food at 0.58x base
across a Western sphere that was 2.7M units short of feeding itself. 92 of
149 of the world's farms ran negative margins under a price signal made of
grain that did not exist for them.

The wide leg is now the price of each country's own reachable book — the same
`buildReachableBooks` output the engine already computes, persists, and fills
sellers from, so price and fill finally quote one market. Countries outside
the trading set keep the world aggregate as a fallback. Pegs, nudges, the
national leg (including planned-economy administered pricing) and the
regional leg are untouched, as is the stored `globalPrice` (world telemetry).

Expected movement: Western food prices rise toward base as the phantom supply
leaves their ledger (US book: 507K reachable supply against a 1.49M domestic
demand, deficit served by real imports); bloc-internal prices sink under
their own surplus. Farm margins in food-short countries recover on revenue,
compounding the 1.1.24 cost pass-through.

## Disclosure, not a change

Bloc members with open trade routes genuinely export west — Poland moved
2.66M units of food into Western imports this turn, with zero food embargoes
in force. The curtain exists in clearing scope, not in trade policy. Whether
it should be policy-closed until trade mechanics arrive is a design decision,
not a bug, and nothing here changes it.
