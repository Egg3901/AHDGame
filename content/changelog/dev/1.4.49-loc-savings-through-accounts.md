Cut from development. (PR pending)

### Fixed

- The line-of-credit turn pays from your wallet first and takes any shortfall
  from savings. It wrote the savings figure directly. That was harmless while
  a savings balance was only a number, but once a currency's accounts hold the
  real cash it would have lowered the displayed figure while the account kept
  the balance and the money behind it never moved. The overflow is now
  withdrawn through the account itself, so the cash leaves where it was held
  and the payment is funded by it. Currencies still on the old model are
  unchanged.
