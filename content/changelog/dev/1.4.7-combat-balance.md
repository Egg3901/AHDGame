Cut from development.

## Fixed

- Escorts no longer fight inland battles as though they were at sea. Frigates,
  destroyers, amphibious groups and attack submarines screen a carrier and hold
  sea lanes. They have very little to contribute to a division fighting inland
  of them, and they now contribute accordingly. An attack submarine was
  previously slightly better than neutral on open farmland.

- A carrier still reaches. Its air wing is the one thing a fleet has that
  genuinely strikes inland, so a carrier keeps its full effect on a coastal
  front and a reduced one further in. That distinction is the point of the
  change: it is not a blanket penalty on ships.

- Fronts now know whether they touch the sea. It is worked out from the host
  country rather than set by hand, so a war in Germany keeps its Baltic
  coastline while one in Czechoslovakia does not. No existing conflict needed
  editing, and a war can override the answer where the fighting is genuinely
  inland of a coastal nation.

## Changed

- The two inland terrain families, open plain and open desert, previously
  carried no naval modifier at all. Between them they cover every landlocked
  front in the game, which is why this went unnoticed: mountains and jungle
  already handled it correctly.
