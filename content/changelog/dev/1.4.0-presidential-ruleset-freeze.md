Cut from development. Part of the presidential rework: the "rules freeze"
release gate. The current version's values are identical to live behavior, so
this ships as pure infrastructure with zero gameplay change.

## Added

- Presidential elections are stamped with a ruleset version at spawn and the
  vote engine resolves every versioned knob through that frozen ruleset.
  Unstamped races (including the open 1960 cycle) resolve to the rules they
  opened under; an unrecognized future stamp falls back to the newest known
  ruleset instead of failing a live race.
- First versioned knob: the campaign-strength vote-multiplier cap. Its value
  is unchanged today; converting campaign strength into bounded operational
  effects can now ship for the next cycle without confiscating anything from
  campaigns already underway.

## Guarded

- Tests pin the legacy default, stamp retention, the unknown-version
  fallback, the v1/v2 identity (zero behavior change today), and the
  multiplier honoring a ruleset-supplied cap.
