1.12 is the foundation for the fresh 1991 world. It closes the open issue backlog and fixes systems at the root: money flows through funded accounts, every era seeds its own values, turns make fewer database trips, and every error carries a code you can trace. Fresh worlds start with every gameplay system on except NPP v5.

Reset prevention is built in. Required reset steps fail closed, reseeds clear old runtime state, and CI contracts block unclassified collections, game state fields, missing index policies and database calls inside turn loops. The 1991 economy and political opening are calibrated for 1991 only, and regression tests keep every other era unchanged.

## What landed in this release

11 groups of changes shipped.

### Elections and campaigns

- **Politics and government**
  Governments pursue the mandate they won, laws flow through department budgets, parties build durable regional organization, and polls and elections follow each country's own rules and era.

### Government and legislation

- **Banking and money**
  Banks earn what they are actually paid, underwrite offerings, finance construction and can fund government debt. Government payments now come out of real Treasury cash.

### War and diplomacy

- **Conflict and diplomacy**
  Player-founded Blocs can vote on collective war declarations, with consent from every player nation and separate legislative approval where required.

### Economy and corporations

- **Economy**
  Tariffs, exchange rates, central banks and local services behave more realistically, trade and production conserve available supply, and computer-run firms respond to shortages and market payouts.

### Interface and platform

- **Turn speed**
  Turns spend less time on the database, so large worlds finish faster.
- **Interface**
  Prices, dates and several pages read more clearly, and the phone app drops Google tags.
- **Singleplayer builds use less memory**
  Packaging the singleplayer download now fits on small build machines instead of running them out of memory.

### Other changes

- **Media and products**
  Media and manufacturing companies can build named products and brands, advertise with each other, and operate under ownership and press rules.
- **The 1991 world**
  A fresh 1991 world opens with accurate economies, regions and countries, and the Yugoslav crisis now costs real people and capital.
- **Data and tooling**
  Fresh worlds start with every feature on, resets are validated first, and simulation reporting and release checks are broader.
- **Errors and reliability**
  Every failure now shows an error code and reference you can quote, players can report and block other players, and several recurring failures are fixed.

## Detailed release notes

### Banking and money

#### What changed

- Bank insurance premiums follow measured claims and insured deposits, between 0.4% and 2% before the bank's own risk weight.
- A bank's earnings count only what it is really paid: funded Treasury bill coupons and the gain or loss on bills it bought, with returned principal excluded.
- Investment banks can underwrite IPOs and corporate bond offerings for 1.5%, charged only on what buyers actually take.
- Investment and universal banks can subscribe to unplaced government bond offers with real vault cash.
- Player banks choose bonds, index units and forex for new investments.
- Corporations and NPPs can finance plant construction through bank loans, and the collateral stays protected through sales, takeovers, nationalization and other restructuring.
- Construction loans settle once and resume safely after an interruption.
- A failing bank gives the head of government, finance minister or central bank chair three turns to recapitalize, guarantee deposits or resolve it before it fails.
- Government coupon payments, bond maturities, nationalization payouts, royalties, dues, aid, indemnities and event costs now need real Treasury cash and retry safely.
- Governments pay the coupons their bonds were issued with, and new bonds are priced by the issuer's credit rating.
- Eligible public bond holdings roll into new debt at maturity instead of draining the Treasury.
- Corporate credit estimates count bond principal due in the next half game year.

### Media and products

#### What changed

- Media corporations fund named titles through paid research and advertising to improve quality and audience.
- Manufacturers develop products at 5% of their plant capital, put marketing into product advertising to build brand, and sell product output through market clearing.
- Products keep progressing after development is paid, then grow, mature, decline and retire.
- CEOs can agree to spend part of their marketing budget with a media supplier, and the supplier's reach in the buyer's states builds more brand.
- Advertising agreements settle for their full term and notice period instead of one turn short.
- Heavy US ad concentration unlocks ownership laws, and an enacted ownership limit caps each owner's delivered share in a state.
- Audience limits and state press controls bound how far outlets can reach.
- Political ads in a state with no outlet fund a weaker local fallback, and the rest is refunded.
- Entertainment keeps its own market and full output, separate from the news media penalty.
- Vehicle plants keep their automobile recipes under manufacturing.

### Politics and government

#### What changed

- Spending laws flow through persistent department accounts, so funding, capacity, regional responsibility and Cabinet priorities decide how much a program delivers.
- Computer-run governments shape their agenda around their party's platform and pledges, scaled by the size of its win, and review it every game year.
- Presidential voters judge inflation against their country's target for the era.
- Granular polls include voter approval, and the poll screen leads with your appeal, party colors and a trend chart.
- From 1994, Japan can pass a bill moving the lower house to 300 constituency seats and 200 regional list seats.
- Veto-override cards show the vote that actually passed, and court nomination cards publish once.
- Nigerian chamber leadership results keep the same winner if resolution is retried.
- The 1991 world opens with its own political board, law levels and regional variation, and national approval uses one calculation everywhere.
- Build Org adds a durable contribution to the party's regional organization share alongside a permanent unaffiliated stake. Organization can decay after a party becomes inactive in a region, and national actions charge their full Political Strength cost.
- Elections explicitly configured for ranked PR-STV count saved preferences, transfer surplus and eliminated votes, and retain each count. Aggregate elections keep their existing method.
- Commons and governorship vacancies that occur far enough before a general election open a by-election. Election and party pages label and link those races consistently.
- Party mergers enforce regional and national NPP capacity, remove excess incoming NPPs, keep surviving-party candidates attached to the surviving party, and clear obsolete regional organization rows.
- Planned economies can legislate a move to reform socialism, a market economy or back to central planning.

### Conflict and diplomacy

#### What changed

- A player-founded Bloc can vote to declare war on a player nation. Every player nation in the Bloc must consent, NPP-governed members join automatically, and other eligible player nations still require their own legislative declaration.

### The 1991 world

#### What changed

- The world opens with the European Community and no euro area, period country names and no duplicate Soviet republics.
- France, Italy, Spain, Sweden, Turkey, Greece, Austria and Finland start with real 1991 GDP, debt and exchange rates.
- All 24 Soviet regions are counted at the January opening, and obsolete regional statistics are gone.
- UK and Japanese regional adjacency maps use symmetric, geographically correct neighbors for distance and regional checks.
- Military escalation in the Yugoslav crisis removes civilians from the affected regions, and the accepting country pays for the people it receives.
- War damage destroys part of the fought-over regions' capital, and the state pays to rebuild it over two game years.
- UK 1991 regions keep their census composition, and age, education and income attitudes follow published 1991 survey data.
- 2027 worlds get Bulgarian regional demographics, regional populations that match national totals and a modern Turkey profile.
- A fresh reset restores the banking, product and media safeguards and starts bond markets with funded pools.

### Data and tooling

#### What changed

- A first start or reset turns every gameplay feature on, except autonomous politicians, which start at v4 instead of v5. Running worlds are unchanged.
- Resets check the full configuration and database target before tearing anything down, and restore required banking, product and media indexes and bond pools.
- Admin gets separate version controls for metrics, legislation and Cabinet.
- Simulation runs record per-turn macro, fiscal, trade and securities history, with a bounded admin export.
- Bank calibration and release checks cover banking, underwriting, sector and media behavior.
- Large code modules were split with no change to gameplay, and the README and contributing guide match the current process.

### Economy

#### What changed

- Tariffs cut how much can be imported even from a single supplier, and tariff inflation follows the goods households really import.
- Large currency trades pay a size and liquidity fee, while traded volume has a smaller effect on exchange rates so one trader cannot move a currency sharply.
- An exchange-rate move causes a one-off inflation push instead of permanent extra inflation.
- Central bank credibility is judged against each country's own neutral rate and inflation target.
- Monetary committee votes resolve as soon as the outcome is decided, so no-shows no longer delay a rate change.
- Hospitals, construction, leases and entertainment events sell only inside their own state.
- Countries cannot export more of a good than remains after domestic demand.
- Mixed plants follow the product buyers value most, while energy and raw-material demand can clear without overstating unclaimed capacity.
- Weather disasters respond to emissions and funded climate programs. Regional resilience limits additional losses, and retry-safe treasury costs are charged once.
- Computer-run firms open or expand healthcare and entertainment producers when those services stay far above base price.
- Computer-run mining firms forecast revenue the way the market actually pays out.
- Sector sentiment events move stock prices again.
- The 1991 world opens with affordable construction, profitable default production methods, 3 to 4.5% prime rates and modest deficits, while other eras keep their existing prices.
- Seeded competitors have country-specific names, and mining firms leave room for new entrants.
- Foreign builds are charged exactly what the preview quoted, currency spread included.
- Poland, Hungary, Romania and Bulgaria trade currencies in 2019 and 2027 worlds.

### Errors and reliability

#### What changed

- Error pages, failed actions and API errors show a short error code and a reference id for bug reports.
- Players can report another player from their profile and block them from direct interaction. Moderation and account history survive a world reset.
- Browser error reports are no longer dropped when only the server reporting address is set.
- Supporter benefits are rechecked against Patreon every six hours.
- Action costs come from one shared source, so the price shown is the price charged.
- A fresh world starts clean: old ledgers, ballots, political effects and caches are cleared, while accounts and moderation history are kept.

### Turn speed

#### What changed

- Index fund rebalances settle as one recoverable batch, and the fund phase reads its data once per pass.
- Computer-run corporations skip strategy work when nothing is up for review.
- Union bargaining on computer-run turns does fewer database reads.
- A new benchmark and index check catch turn slowdowns before release.

### Interface

#### What changed

- Share prices read the same everywhere and dates use the game calendar.
- The crisis, Supreme Court, trade and White House pages are clearer.
- The iPhone and Android app no longer loads Google Analytics or Google Ads tags.
- Headings, buttons, tabs and table headers read in sentence case, and small uppercase labels are now regular text.
- Action cards use period photos for every era from 1953 to 2027, with a neutral set where no era art exists.
