## What changed

- Added an opinion-driven foreign-policy planner for every autonomous country with a foreign ministry, including France and the Eastern bloc.
- Governments can weigh organization votes, agreements, aid, sanctions, tariffs, embargoes, public statements, war support, and war entry.
- The first rollout is shadow-only: one idempotent decision is audited per country and strategic cycle without changing world state.
