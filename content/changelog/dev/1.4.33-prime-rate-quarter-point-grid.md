## Fixed

- A player taking over a central bank from an autonomous chair could no longer
  change the rate. Every attempt came back with "Rate must be in 0.25%
  increments", and neither the plus nor the minus button helped.
- The cause was the autonomous chair. It sets the rate from a continuous policy
  rule, so it would leave the bank on a value like 4.65% rather than a whole
  quarter point. The rate card offers the current rate plus or minus 0.25, so
  from an off-grid rate every option it could offer was also off-grid, and the
  bank refused all of them. The autonomous chair now rounds to the quarter-point
  grid before it writes.
- The rate card also steps from the nearest quarter point rather than from the
  raw stored rate, so a bank that inherited an off-grid rate is adjustable
  straight away instead of waiting for the engine to move it.
- Existing off-grid banks have been rounded onto the grid.
