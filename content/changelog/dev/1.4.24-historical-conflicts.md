Cut from development.

## Added

- A Historical Conflicts section at the bottom of the conflicts hub. Every war
  that has ended is listed, newest first, with its dates, both sides, the
  outcome, its cumulative casualties, and a link to its record. The card says
  whether the record is open or which turn the fog lifts.

## Changed

- A resolved war no longer opens its full record the moment it ends. For 480
  turns after resolution it reads exactly as it did while it ran: territory,
  casualties and verdicts for everyone, and your own side's rosters if you hold
  a belligerent seat. Once the window lapses the record opens to everyone. Wars
  resolved before this change were already open and stay open.
- The record page and the war log now say which turn a resolved war's record
  opens, rather than promising that it opens when the war resolves.
- Conflict date ranges read "1960 to 1962" rather than using a dash.
