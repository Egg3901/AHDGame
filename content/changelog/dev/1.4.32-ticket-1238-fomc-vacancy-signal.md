  When governor terms lapse the board falls vacant by design and waits for a
  presidential nomination, but nothing told the President that, so the Fed
  committee could sit dead for months with every motion failing 1-0-6. The
  executive now gets an in-app notice the moment seats fall vacant (plus a
  throttled reminder while they stay empty), the wire carries the story, and
  the committee page shows an understaffed-board banner explaining why motions
  cannot pass.

## Added

- Seats that fall vacant (ticket #1238) now notify every player holding the
  nominating executive office (the US President) in-app, and post a system
  news item, the moment the seats lapse. While seats stay vacant and no
  nomination is before the Senate, the notice repeats at most once per game
  year (48 turns).
- A rejected FOMC nomination now also notifies the president who proposed it.
  Before, only the nominee heard the outcome, so a silent Senate rejection
  could leave the board dead again with nobody prompted to try again.

## Changed

- The FOMC committee page shows a prominent banner when seats are vacant: how
  many seats, how many votes a motion needs, and that the President nominates
  governors for Senate confirmation. The committee API now returns
  majorityNeeded so the page reads the threshold instead of re-deriving it.

## Unchanged

- Board staffing rules are untouched: the engine still never seats a machine
  candidate (#1195), vacant seats still abstain, and the full-board majority
  still governs motions.
