## Added

- `GET /api/public/v1/country/[code]/metrics` returns national metric aggregates,
  trends, regional extremes, GDP, population, and public approval readings. Use
  `category` to request one metric family.
- `GET /api/public/v1/organizations` lists international organizations with
  membership, leadership, and activity counts.
- `GET /api/public/v1/organizations/[id]` adds the charter, proposals,
  resolutions, country-level votes, leadership elections, and withdrawals.
- `GET /api/public/v1/trade/tariffs` lists active tariff layers with filters for
  the imposing country, target country, scope, and result limit.
- `GET /api/public/v1/trade/embargoes` combines active ministerial, legislative,
  and organization restrictions. `includePending=true` also returns embargo
  bills still in progress.
- `GET /api/public/v1/sovereigns` exposes the same demand, credit, crisis, and
  debt-sustainability signals used by the in-game sovereign watch.

## Privacy and stability

- Organization votes identify the voting country, not the character record
  behind the vote.
- Trade restrictions expose public legislative provenance without the internal
  actor IDs used to create them.
- National metrics omit policy tick rates and private modifier ledgers.
- The machine-readable v1 catalog includes all six routes and remains checked
  against the implemented route tree.

## Documentation
