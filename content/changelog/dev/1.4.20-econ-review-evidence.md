## Fixed

- A skipped stock-versus-flow reconciliation reported zero divergent accounts
  and a green status, which is indistinguishable from a check that ran and
  passed. The count is now null and the status is amber, so a waived check
  cannot be read as a passing one. Turn 457 in production recorded zero
  divergences between neighbouring turns holding 1,033 and 1,060, because the
  turn before it died without writing an opening snapshot.

## Added

- The economic vital signs record window coverage: how many turns the window
  should hold, how many produced a snapshot, and which turns are missing. Four
  turns are absent from the current production window, all from the turn
  failures on 2026-08-28, and nothing in the series said so.

- Rolling twelve-turn medians ship alongside the point readings for order-book
  depth, two-sided listings, traded listings, and unheld sovereign issues. A
  single turn's securities reading is spiky enough that a frozen one-turn
  baseline can manufacture a regression later: the depth baseline taken at
  turn 445 sat inside a transient spike, six times the level either side of it.

- The equity book now reports organic depth and organic two-sided listings
  separately from the liquidity facility's own quotes. The facility posts both
  sides itself, so it meets the plain two-sided measure by construction. In
  production 320 of the 350 open orders are facility quotes, covering all 160
  two-sided listings, and there are no sell orders from anyone else at all. The
  headline share reads 25.97%; the organic share is zero.

## Changed

- The naval and air mission route parses its request body through the shared
  validator instead of reading raw JSON, which is what the architecture audit
  requires. The route was blocking the audit for every branch.
