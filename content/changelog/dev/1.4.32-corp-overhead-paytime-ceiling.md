Cut from development.

## Fixed

- Logistics and R&D spending is now capped each turn at the same 150% of gross
  revenue the budget screen enforces when you save. Marketing and CEO salary
  already had that ceiling at payment time; these two legs did not, so a budget
  set while revenue was healthy carried on charging in full after the sectors
  behind it were gone. A corporation with no gross revenue pays nothing on
  either leg.
- Logistics strength and R&D score now grow on what a corporation actually paid
  rather than what its budget asked for. Previously a budget too large to be
  charged still bought the full strength gain, so unaffordable spending produced
  real competitive advantage at no cost.

Corporations operating inside the 150% limit are charged exactly as before.
