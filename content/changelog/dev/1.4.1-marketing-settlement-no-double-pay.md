Cut from development.

## Fixed

- A corporation that sells filled advertising is paid for it once. The
  marketing settlement is netted against the advertising the seller already
  books as sector revenue, instead of being paid on top of it.
- The buyer still pays its marketing budget as a real cost, and still only for
  advertising actually delivered. What changes is the seller side: the delivered
  value is removed from the seller's cash leg so the settlement receipt is its
  only advertising cash, exactly mirroring how the buyer's cost is deferred to
  the same transfer.

## Guarded

- Coverage pins that a funded seller's settlement receipt is netted out to zero
  net cash, across two currencies, so a filled sale is not double-counted.
