## What changed

- Added national bill processing for France, Italy, Spain, Sweden, Turkey, Austria, Finland, Greece, Brazil, and Nigeria.
- Made government formation respect era-specific political systems and chambers, including Fourth Republic France and 1953 Spain.
- Added cabinet portfolios for previously cabinet-less econ-only countries and connected their finance, diplomacy, trade, and defence authority.
- Existing bills stranded by the missing lifecycle receive a fresh vote window after deployment.
