## Fixed

- Founding a rival union no longer fails with a database error. Every industry
  can now hold a seeded union plus any number of player-founded challengers,
  which is what makes raiding a shop possible at all.
- The registration fee to charter a union is now 500,000 in campaign funds. It
  was being deflated as though it were factory-floor money, which priced a
  union charter at a few hundred: less than a rounding error against a normal
  war chest.
- Union treasuries, dues and service costs now display as money, with a
  currency symbol and proper grouping. Small figures keep their cents, so two
  different dues rates no longer look identical.
- The membership figure no longer gets cut off on a phone. It is the headline
  number for a union, so the rank, president and funds columns step aside on
  narrow screens instead of pushing it out of view.

## Changed

- Union tables show the president's portrait next to their name, and the name
  links through to their character or party profile.
