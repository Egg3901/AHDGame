Cut from development.

## Fixed

- Down-ballot primaries are described as they are now decided: the party's
  registered voters cast ballots over the primary's closing window, split each
  turn by the primary-score standing, and the cumulative count picks the
  nominee. Ten pages still said the highest score at the deadline advanced,
  including the FAQ, the glossary and the beginner walkthroughs.
- Presidential thresholds are era-derived. The Electoral College, the House
  contingent ballot and the Senate's vice-presidential vote were all quoted at
  the modern 270 / 26 / 51, which a 1950s world never uses; the general
  elections page also claimed a 269-269 tie was settled by a coin flip.
- A peace settlement has four terms, not three. The peace page listed four,
  called them three, and contradicted itself on whether a zero indemnity was a
  white peace. Demilitarisation's default and ceiling are stated. The
  occupation page no longer says reaching a pole ends the war on the spot: it
  opens the terms window, and the conflicts overview lists that status.
- Every sovereign country has a defence seat. The conflicts overview listed
  nine of them as read-only.
- The FOMC is the Federal Reserve's alone, its seats fall vacant rather than
  being filled by the engine, and the chair is nominated and confirmed. The
  FOMC page, the central banks page, the offices reference and both glossaries
  described a committee on every market bank and a chair drawn from a lobbying
  pool.
- Government approval is documented with the war block, national address,
  organisation statements and the shared positive cap. Six pages said approval
  was the head of government's own favorability, which the approval page
  itself denies.
- Acting secretaries, campaign managers appointed by the candidate (not an
  admin), the 24-turn terms window, the standing order to join allied
  offensives, treaty allies pulled in by mutual defence, the bloc war-entry
  vote, frontage, naval reach, the state arms industry, the reunification
  settlement, the iron curtain and Yugoslavia's exemption, the wars term in the
  tension floor, high-tension society events, the UK Budget, the vote-position
  shift and its preview, and relocation's military cascade all have wiki
  coverage now.
- The turn-order reference lists the phase families it was missing: cold-war
  tension, banking, pensions, prospecting, the Supreme Court, naval and air
  operations, battles under ministerial orders, settlement crises, alignment,
  impeachment, the census, era crossing, the command economy, FOMC meetings and
  nominations, and referendums. Its NPP entry chances were a dice roll that no
  longer exists.
- Numbers that had drifted: the trait tree has 115 nodes (three places said
  117); doctrine gains a point a year (the war walkthrough said the starting
  twelve was all a nation would ever have); Logistics commands are exempt from
  the three-region cap; the spoiler transfer is 4 percent, not 10; a full
  demographic poll is 6 actions, not 5; the Bundestag is 487 seats in the 1953
  preset; the Bank of Japan's default rate is 1.0 percent, not 0.25; a Japanese
  governor serves four years, not six; a strategy switch has a 24-turn
  cooldown; the whip compliance roll uses the engine's actual weights.
- Claims with nothing behind them are gone: defying a whip does not add infamy
  (six pages said it did), there is no committee stage for a bill to be stuck
  in, party org does not enter a primary score, party leadership is a member
  vote rather than an influence score, and a Governor cannot also sit as a
  Senator.
- The country roster is current on the character-creation, multi-country and
  parties pages, which listed six countries and in one case a Canada that does
  not exist. Beta 2 is no longer described as the current iteration.
- Marketing strength grows on delivered advertising, corporate bond issuance is
  capped by realisable assets and maturity is named as a default trigger, and
  active freight settlement bills buyers and credits carriers; the corporate,
  bonds and logistics pages said otherwise.

## Changed

- Combat inputs that were added to the battle page (readiness recovery, frontage,
  naval reach) are described directionally, in keeping with the category's rule
  against publishing resolution math.
- Pre-existing em dashes in the private banking and unions pages were replaced.
