## What changed

- East Germany's borrowing limit now has a 40% of GDP floor. Credit ratings,
  interest costs, and debt penalties still discipline sustained deficits.
- The national policy screen now shows the same enacted tax rates as the budget
  screen instead of backfilling a second, older tax law at the nearest ladder
  value.
- The Volkskammer and executive pages now show the same Chairman of the Council
  of State. Regional politics no longer presents the unelected 25-member
  Staatsrat as a vacant two-seat senate.
- Union services now show current approval, its target, and the expected
  next-turn change. The dues preview says explicitly that its approval number is
  only the change caused by editing dues.
- Autonomous parties cannot propose the same legislation type again for 96
  turns, so a failed top-scoring bill no longer repeats at every opportunity.
