Cut from development.

## Fixed

- Content scheduled against a calendar year now resolves against the calendar
  year the world actually shows. A world whose founding phase burns turns before
  the calendar starts runs its raw turn counter ahead of its own date, and
  anything scheduled off that counter arrived a full game year early: the Supreme
  Court decided its docket, and its scripted seats turned over, before the
  country reached those years. Surprise cases were stamped with the early year
  too, which also fed the anachronism filter the wrong year.
- A turn recovered after a crash wrote that same too-early year onto the world
  clock, where every reader saw it until the next turn repaired it. It now writes
  the same date the rest of the clock agrees on.
- World News no longer carries events from a world that is not the live one.
  Webhook destinations are stored with the world, so a copy of the live world
  taken for testing inherited the players' channels and posted its own events
  there, announcing offices and rulings that had not happened in the live game.
  Saving a webhook destination now records which deployment it belongs to, and
  any other deployment holding a copy stays silent.

## Changed

- The helper that turns a turn number into a calendar year lives in one place
  now, shared by the conflicts board and by era scheduling.
