Cut from development.

## Changed

- Plaid Cymru, the SNP, the DUP, Sinn Fein and the UUP can now stand for
  election in any region of the country. Filing a candidacy, a chair slating
  someone, and the votes themselves are all open to them everywhere.
- Those parties now hold an organisation row in every region rather than only
  their home nation. Outside the places they already stood they begin on the
  minimum organisation floor with no registered support, so they start as a
  fringe presence and have to build from there.
- Organisation these parties had already built outside their home nation now
  counts. It was being ignored before, and none of it was reset or reduced.
- A world reset no longer deletes these parties' organisation outside their
  home nation.

## Fixed

- A chair could no longer be told a candidate was filed and then watch the
  entry disappear, because the geography rule that removed it is gone.
- A slate row rejected for the wrong region now reads "Not Eligible in This
  Region" instead of blaming the party, which is no longer the reason it can
  happen. The remaining cause is a candidate who does not live in the region.
- A region voting to leave no longer dissolves the party it voted for. The
  check for whether a party is rooted only in that region now looks at real
  organisation instead of counting the token presence every party now has
  everywhere.
