## Added

- **Union political contributions.** The head sets a percentage of remaining
  budget (dues income minus the service bill that actually ran). It pays out
  each turn, the same timescale as union dues and services, and cannot exceed
  half of free cash flow.

- **Paid by influence.** If you hold 40% of the union's banked strength, you
  receive 40% of that turn's contributions, credited to campaign funds. Each
  payout is logged as a Union Contribution on the financial ledger, and the
  same amount shows up as income on your profile and in the campaign-cash
  breakdown.

- **Approval cost.** Members dislike dues spent on politics. The penalty scales
  with the slider and is 5 points at the 50% cap.
