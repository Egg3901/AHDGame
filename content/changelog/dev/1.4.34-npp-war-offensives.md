Cut from development.

## Added

- Admin, World, Conflicts now carries an NPP Offensives panel with two
  independent switches. Initiate Offensives lets an NPP belligerent queue a
  battle declaration of its own. Join Offensives lets one follow an ally's
  offensive at a front where it already has troops posted, which is the standing
  order a player government sets for itself and an NPP government has nobody to
  set. Both default to off.
- The panel warns before either is turned on: NPP countries have no Generals or
  military technology behind them yet, so their attacks go in without the
  command bonuses and research a player run army brings, and more of those
  offensives will fail.

## Changed

- An NPP belligerent no longer declares offensives unless Initiate Offensives is
  on. When it is off the choice is never put in front of the government at all,
  so the country spends its foreign policy slot on diplomacy rather than on a
  refusal.
- Neither switch reaches a player government. Players declare through the
  cabinet battle room and set their own auto join orders, exactly as before.
- Defence is untouched by both. Any country with troops at a front still defends
  automatically, because holding ground under attack was never a decision.
