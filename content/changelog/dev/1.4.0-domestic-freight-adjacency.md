Cut from development.

## Fixed
- Party charters in those countries can now place founding members in regions
  bordering the chair's home region, not just the home region itself.
- Parties in those countries can now declare priority regions spanning several
  neighboring regions. The connectivity check previously rejected every
  multi-region cluster there.

- Domestic freight in France, Italy, Spain, Sweden, Turkey, Greece, Austria,
  Finland, Ireland, Brazil, Nigeria, Hungary, Poland, Romania, Yugoslavia,
  Bulgaria, Ukraine, Byelorussia, Czechoslovakia, the Baltic republics,
  Scotland, and Wales now uses real hops between neighboring regions.
- Domestic hauls in those countries no longer fall back to the six-hop ocean
  import price simply because their regional route maps were empty.
- Island regions use practical ferry connections to the mainland, keeping
  domestic freight routable without treating islands as adjacent by land.

## Guarded

- Tests require every country's route graph to be symmetric, free of self
  links, limited to seeded region ids, and connected except for the two
  previously documented isolated regions.
- Serialized hashes pin the seven pre-existing country maps so this data
  addition cannot silently alter their routes.
