## Changed

- The Persuasion Drivers card now states the ceiling for the pair you have
  selected: the most of the opponent's vote that can be persuaded across to your
  candidate this cycle, the most of your own vote that can be persuaded away,
  and what your driver rows add up to inside that slice.
- The Registration Influence card now shows a movable figure next to each
  party's lean. That is the share of the party's own vote that campaigning can
  still shift in one cycle. The rest is held in place by the lean itself.

No election math changed. These are readouts of numbers the engine was already
using.
