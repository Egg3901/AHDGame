## Fixed

- Power stations no longer damage their own region's grid reliability. The
  energy step compared a region's mix-implied reliability target against its
  current standing on two different scales, and the target scale topped out
  below where most regions already sat, so every fleet in the game scored as a
  downgrade. Even a perfectly firm, fully diverse mix came out negative.
- Grid reliability feeds the utilities family on the metric board, so this was
  visible as a standing negative cabinet line on utilities that no order,
  estate, or tier setting could account for. Countries with a strong fleet now
  see that line move the other way.
- A mediocre fleet still lowers reliability, and a region whose grid already
  matches what its fleet supports stays flat. Only the direction was broken, not
  the strength of the effect.
