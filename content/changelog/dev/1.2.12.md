Cut from development. Raised on ticket #1072.

## Changed

- New worlds seed East German automobiles and construction at real scale.
  Before this, the republic read as one of the most oversupplied economies in
  the world: on a live 1953 world it produced about 63,100 units of steel and
  consumed about 10,800, while Czechoslovakia at a smaller population consumed
  about 80,400. West Germany, Poland and Czechoslovakia all had downstream
  industry sized to their own output. The DDR did not.
- The lift is additive, not a reshuffle. Sector seed sizes come from a
  normalised share of the national economy, so raising one sector's weight
  takes the space out of every other sector. East Germany's problem was
  absolute size, not mix, so its vehicle works and its building programme are
  now multiplied on top of their share instead of being carved out of steel,
  chemicals or lignite. No seeded sector gets smaller.
- Capacity and revenue move together, so the new plants are real production
  capacity from turn one rather than a paper nameplate.

## Why it mattered

East Germany could not trade its way out. The CoCom embargo walls DDR steel
out of Western markets, and a command economy bans private founding, so no
player could build the missing consumers either. The republic was left
permanently oversupplied with nothing it could do about it.

## Not changed

- Nothing anybody owns was cut. This is a seed-time change: existing sectors,
  including every plant a player runs, keep their capacity.
