Cut from development. Raised on ticket #1169.

## Fixed

- The Delivery Limited pill was measured against a whole state's production
  while the Fill chip beside it was measured against the sector's own output.
  The gap between them was wide enough to put "Fill 100%" and "Delivery
  limited 96%" on one row, which reads as a contradiction because it is one.
  The warning is now capped at the share the sector genuinely did not sell, so
  it can only ever explain a shortfall the row is already showing. A sector
  that sold everything it made no longer carries the warning at all.
- Grid sectors, which is to say electricity and natural gas, were told to lower
  the sector's price so distant buyers could afford it. No such lever reaches
  the grid: deliveries are sourced against the state market price, and a
  sector's pricing posture only ever affects who it outsells in its own market.
  The advice now says so and points at the one thing that does work, which is
  siting capacity nearer the buyers.
- When the route type behind a delivery limit was not yet known, all four
  places that explain the warning told players to add freight capacity. That is
  wrong for anything moving on the grid, which cannot use freight at all. The
  unknown case now only promises what holds for every route, and names the grid
  exception outright.
- A private corporation seen from outside had its delivery-limited share hidden
  but its route type left visible, which published why a plant was stranded
  while withholding only the size of the problem. Both are hidden together now.

## Changed

- The four surfaces that explain a delivery limit (the sector row pill, its
  expanded panel, and the stranded-plant and money panels on the sector page)
  now read their wording from one shared source instead of each keeping its own
  copy. The four had already drifted apart, and the drift is what let the wrong
  freight advice reach players on sectors that could never use freight.

## Not changed

- A stranded sector still books its revenue in full. Whether badly sited
  capacity ought to lose money for it is a separate decision about game balance
  and is deliberately left where it was.
