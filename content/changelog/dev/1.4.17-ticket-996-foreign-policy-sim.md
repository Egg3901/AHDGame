## What changed

- The world simulator accepts explicit foreign-policy mode and capability-stage
  controls and records both with each run for controlled comparisons.
- Full-autonomy setup applies the chosen controls consistently to fresh and
  cloned sandbox worlds.
- Production-shaped runs can preserve player-country access while exercising
  autonomous policy in econ-only countries.
- Every remaining engine random draw uses the run seed, and quiet mode suppresses
  high-volume turn logging during long audits.
