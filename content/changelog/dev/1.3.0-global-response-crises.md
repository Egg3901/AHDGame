## Global response crises

Major world events now ask every relevant head of government to respond. A
belligerent, superpower backer, neighboring state, alliance member, and neutral
government each receive a different menu. Choices are recorded publicly on the
crisis page and combined into one global outcome when the response window
closes.

Commitments are real. Military and humanitarian responses can spend a share of
national GDP from the treasury. National political and economic effects apply
immediately, while the combined result changes the persistent conflict's
intensity, commitment pressure, readiness, and path through later phases.

## Vietnam rebuilt

Vietnam is no longer a private meter for Washington and Moscow. China,
alliance governments, neighboring powers, and non-aligned countries can arm a
side, organize relief, declare neutrality, or build an international conference.

The historical escalation floors remain, but the years between them are active.
Every phase opens with a world response and generates another consultation
after 24 turns if the conflict remains there. This fills the long 1959 to 1964
gap without forcing the Gulf incident or full war ahead of the calendar.

Enabling the 1.3 system closes the old Vietnam choice chain. The existing combat
front, Cold War dials, procurement pressure, and conflicts-hub ladder continue
from the new persistent state.

## New international chains

- Berlin Crisis, from the 1958 ultimatum through a possible checkpoint
  confrontation.
- Congo Crisis, from independence breakdown into a possible international proxy
  intervention.
- Suez and Decolonization Crisis, covering the canal settlement and the wider
  sovereignty shock that follows it.
- Global Oil Disruption, from shipping closures to coordinated producer
  embargoes.
- Nuclear Alert Crisis, from ambiguous strategic warnings to a possible missile
  standoff.

Every chain uses the same role, response, tally, outcome, and compatibility
module. New global crises can be authored as data without adding another bespoke
decision engine.

## Administration and rollout

The expansion is controlled by the Living conflicts and global responses gate.
It remains off until the complete 1.3 release is promoted. Turning it on is a
one-way gameplay cutover from the legacy Vietnam prompts to the new system.
