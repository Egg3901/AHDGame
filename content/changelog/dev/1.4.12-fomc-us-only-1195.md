## Fixed

- The FOMC-style rate committee is once again the US Federal Reserve's
  institution alone. Every central bank that was not directly government-run had
  been seeded a seven-seat committee of appointed technocrats, including the Bank
  of England, the Bundesbank/ECB, the Bank of Japan and others. A committee takes
  rate-setting out of the government's and the legislature's hands, so on those
  banks you could no longer change the rate or table a bill about it. Central
  banks outside the United States now keep their own governor model, and their
  governments and parliaments can set and legislate the rate as before.
