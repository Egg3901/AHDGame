## What changed

- Index funds can quote every eligible corporation covered by their mandate,
  including bid-only quotes used to acquire initial inventory.
- Market sells now use the best sufficient index-fund bid before falling back to
  an issuer buyback.
- Funds reserve cash for queued redemptions and automatically recover from a
  backing-ratio pause when they become solvent again.
- Profitable NPP corporations can keep paying dividends while they invest in
  growth, without distributing their operating reserve.
