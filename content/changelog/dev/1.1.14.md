## Changed

- The state economy page now leads with one thin strip of state-wide headline
  stats (total market, top sector, corporations, market control) instead of
  four separate cards.
- Picking a sector is a single dropdown that sits directly above the sector
  detail, so the market you are inspecting is the focus of the page.
- The full sector breakdown grid and the national economic model card are now
  collapsible sections at the bottom of the page. Both start collapsed. The
  grid still switches the selected sector when you open it, in step with the
  dropdown.
