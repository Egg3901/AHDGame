## Campaign operations become skill trees

The four campaign investment levers (Fundraising, Opposition Research, Ground
Game, Media Spending) are no longer straight-line upgrades. Each lever is now a
starter unlock plus three branches you level independently, so how you build a
campaign is a real choice rather than a single track everyone climbs the same
way.

### Tap a lever, see its tree

Strategic Operations shows one button per lever. Tapping a button opens that
lever's tree: the starter node at the top and its three branches below, sized to
read cleanly on a phone. Nothing is spread across the page at once.

- Fundraising splits into Grassroots (steady income each turn), Bundlers (a
  one-time cash infusion when you buy the tier), and Digital Ops (a multiplier
  on all campaign income).
- Ground Game splits into Field Offices (swing-area performance), Get-Out-The-
  Vote (a smaller boost in every area, not just swing), and Volunteer Corps
  (cuts the lever's ongoing upkeep).
- Media Spending splits into Broadcast (the strongest favorability driver),
  Digital Ads (cheaper favorability, lower upkeep), and Rapid Response (blunts
  opponents' opposition research aimed at you).
- Opposition Research splits into Dossier (a recurring drain on your target),
  Scandal Leak (a one-time favorability hit the moment you buy it), and Counter-
  Intel (sharpens your recurring research). You can change your target from the
  lever without paying for another level.

### Every branch does something real

Get-Out-The-Vote now actually moves votes in every area, not only swing states.
Volunteer Corps really does discount upkeep. The favorability shield from Rapid
Response reduces incoming opposition research on the turn it lands. Existing
campaigns keep everything they already bought: purchased levels convert into the
new starter and branches automatically, so no investment is lost.
