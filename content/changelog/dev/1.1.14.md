## What changed

The direct-action card for boosting an NPP's favorability showed a flat
"+3 favorability - +2 relationship" line, but the applied gain has always been
scaled by the acting character's charisma stat (the same gentle +/-20% scaling
used elsewhere, such as the "Run Advertisements" action). A character with
above-neutral charisma could apply +4 favorability while the card still said
+3, which read as a bug. Reported in ticket #1104.

The card text now reads "+3 favorability - +2 relationship (more with high
charisma)". No mechanic change: the charisma scaling behaves exactly as
before, the card just states it accurately.
