## Fixed

- Being offered the central bank chair showed a card reading "Appointment
  pending" and "Awaiting acceptance", with a countdown and no way to respond.
  The accept and decline endpoints already existed; the card simply never
  called them, so the only thing a nominee could do was watch the offer expire
  while the previous chair stayed in post. The nominee now gets Accept and
  Decline buttons on that card, and only the nominee sees them. A refusal from
  the server is shown instead of the click doing nothing.
