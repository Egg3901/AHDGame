Cut from development.

## Changed

- Vitest now runs on the worker-threads pool rather than forked child processes.
  Measured over the full 33,441-test suite this takes 215.01s to 173.96s, with
  module import down 26 percent, transform down 45 percent and setup down 38
  percent while test execution itself barely moves. It also stops each worker
  claiming its own 6 GB heap allowance on a CI runner that only has about 7 GB.
- The unit tests moved out of the `verify` job into their own `test` job, split
  across four shards that run in parallel. Measured at CI-like worker counts, a
  single job takes 770s and the slowest of four shards takes 201s, a 3.83x
  improvement. The shards balanced to within 8 percent of each other with no
  tuning.

- Removed two long-disabled test suites covering NPP auto-voting in House and
  Senate leadership elections. Those elections became player-only and
  `processSpeakerVoting` is a deliberate no-op, so the suites asserted behaviour
  the game no longer has; enabling them fails. The active suite alongside them
  already pins the current contract, that no votes are cast even when a speaker
  election is open, so nothing is left uncovered.

- Removed the unreachable half of the NPP leadership-voting module. Speaker vote
  processing, leadership whip resolution, the ideological alignment helper and
  its two bonus constants had no caller anywhere in the game, along with an empty
  speaker-recalculation shim and its re-export. Vote casting itself stays exactly
  as it was: it is reached whenever a player whips their caucus in a leadership
  race, and keeps its tests.

## Fixed

- Nothing behavioural, and no coverage lost. Fourteen tests came out: four that
  were skipped and never ran, and ten covering the helpers this change deletes.
  Nothing that still has code behind it lost a test, and the suite runs clean.
