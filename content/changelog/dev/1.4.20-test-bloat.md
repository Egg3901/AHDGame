Cut from development.

## Changed

- Vitest now runs on the worker-threads pool rather than forked child processes.
  Measured over the full 33,441-test suite this takes 215.01s to 173.96s, with
  module import down 26 percent, transform down 45 percent and setup down 38
  percent while test execution itself barely moves. It also stops each worker
  claiming its own 6 GB heap allowance on a CI runner that only has about 7 GB.
- The unit tests moved out of the `verify` job into their own `test` job, split
  across four shards that run in parallel. Measured at CI-like worker counts, a
  single job takes 770s and the slowest of four shards takes 201s, a 3.83x
  improvement. The shards balanced to within 8 percent of each other with no
  tuning.

## Fixed

- Nothing behavioural. No test was deleted, rewritten or skipped, and the suite
  still reports the same 33,441 cases.
