## Fixed

- Freight-limited sectors no longer show contradictory numbers. A sector that
  sold its whole output was told on the same page that a large share of it "went
  unsold with buyers still wanting it" and "never reached a buyer." That share is
  what a thin freight network cannot ship OUT of the state, not a measure of what
  went unsold, and the plant is paid for it. The row tag, the Selling tile and
  the Unsold tile now say so plainly: the freight figure is an export constraint,
  and the unsold attribution counts only units that actually went unsold
  (`min(freight-stranded, unsold)`).

## Not changed

- No economics move. Whether freight-stranded output should stop selling (so the
  signal gains economic teeth) is a separate, owner-gated decision with a
  worldsim and restitution plan, tracked in ops-knowledge.
