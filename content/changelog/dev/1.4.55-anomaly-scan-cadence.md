## Changed

- The financial suspect scan, the audit anomaly scan and alt detection run
  on every third turn instead of every turn. Each already examines a rolling
  window of at least six turns, so every transaction is still inspected; it
  is just inspected once or twice rather than six times. Moderators can
  restore every-turn scanning during an incident without a deploy.
