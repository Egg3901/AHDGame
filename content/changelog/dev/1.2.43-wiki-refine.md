Cut from development.

## Added

- First-occurrence jargon tooltips on wiki pages (keyboard and touch), driven
  by a typed glossary. Terms inside headings, links, and code are left alone.
- An "Updated" date on each seeded page, plus a "Report page issue" dialog that
  stores a copy in Mongo and optionally relays to `WIKI_REPORT_ENDPOINT`.
- A "Design doc" chip on core economy, elections, turns, military, parties, and
  legislatures pages, pointing at the public docs host.
- A Playwright harness (`npm run wiki:screenshots`) that writes 1280px guide
  images under `public/wiki-images/guides/` from a local or staging login.

## Changed

- Getting-started guides (and Tips for Beginners) are task-first: what to
  click, in what order, what happens next, and a "Your first week" checklist.
  Screenshot slots render nothing when the PNG is missing.
