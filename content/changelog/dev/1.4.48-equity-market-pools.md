Cut from development. Applies the bond market pool model to public equities.

## Added

- One finite equity market pool per currency, initially sized to five percent
  of broad money and counted in M2. Pool cash moves gradually toward its target
  and excess cash is swept once it grows past twice the target.
- Executable equity quotes with a two percent half-spread and a bounded cash
  skew. Corporation pages now show the bid, ask, and immediate bid depth.
- Primary-market underwriting for IPOs and later share issues. The pool funds a
  bounded first tranche; approved shares it cannot fund remain unissued and
  place gradually without consuming the secondary-market reserve.

## Changed

- Player, corporation, NPP, index-fund, at-market, and resting-order trades all
  settle against the same currency pool. Buys add cash and sells are limited to
  real cash, with partial fills where the pool can fund only part of an order.
- Dividends attributable to public float are paid into the market pool instead
  of disappearing. Existing NPP dividend policy now feeds the same rail.
- Index-fund rebalancing and redemption sales execute at the pool bid and obey
  its depth. Explicit take-private buyouts remain funded by the corporation at
  fair market value.
- Legacy corporation buyback escrow remains available only as a compatibility
  fallback in worlds or currencies that do not yet have a market pool.
