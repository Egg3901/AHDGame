## Fixed

- A bloc influence play priced below a hundredth of a point is now refused
  before any money moves, with a message that names the smallest spend that
  shifts the nation. A point of alignment costs a share of the target's economy,
  which runs to hundreds of millions on a large nation, so an amount typed in
  plain units bought nothing. The play used to be taken anyway, resolve to zero
  applied points, and refund on the next turn, which read as a dead button
  (ticket #1213).
- The commit form disables the button and shows the required minimum while the
  typed amount buys nothing, rather than letting the player spend a submission
  and a turn to learn it.
- The dossier's cost line now states the correct price. It read "one percent of
  its economy" after the per-point cost was cut to a tenth of a percent, so the
  stated figure was ten times the real one.
