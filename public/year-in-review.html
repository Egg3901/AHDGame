The Fragmentation Story

Alpha's most distinctive political feature was its extreme party fragmentation. By iteration's end, the US had 19 political parties — but no party topped 11 members. The Freedom Party led in raw numbers, yet the Democrats dominated executive power.

The Regionalist Party collapsed entirely, ending at -17 members. Meanwhile, single-member parties like the Monarchists, Pacific Independence Party, Maine Separatist Party, and RATEL all survived, proving niche ideologies could persist in the simulation.

Three coalitions formed: the Anti-Neoliberal America League, The Left Coalition, and the Liberty Coalition.

United Kingdom

British Politics

88 player characters across 12 regions. A hung parliament with no Prime Minister. Active legislation. And a Labour plurality that couldn't translate into government.

Constitutional Crisis: Alpha ended without a sitting Prime Minister. No single party or coalition could assemble enough Commons support to form a government. Westminster operated in a state of managed instability for the entire duration.

Business Moguls

Ron Swanson

Senator (NH), Fed Chair, CEO of JPMorgan Chase. Peak portfolio: $1.07B. The most powerful person in Alpha — simultaneously controlling monetary policy, holding elected office, and running a major bank.

Roseanne Park

President, CEO of Blackwell Inc. Peak portfolio: $6.68B (turn 769) — the single largest portfolio value ever recorded. Crashed to $1.46B the following turn.

Loco King

Senator (MO, Monarchists), CEO of King Corporation — the highest share price in the game at $271.81. Built from 1M shares to $183M in liquid capital, all while maintaining 60+ favorability in the Senate.
