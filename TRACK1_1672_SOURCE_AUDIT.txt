TRACK1 PRIMITIVE CAVEATS (crash-recovery / TTL / key-history)
--------------------------------------------------------------
1. TTL: Track1 be95c93078 is STRICTLY BETTER than source. Source expires ALL
   receipts at createdAt+30d (in_progress included: recovery evidence deleted
   mid-window). Track1 expires only terminal receipts at updatedAt+30d and
   retains in_progress. NEVER re-import the source index file.
2. appliedMoneyFlowKeys capped at 100 ($slice, "bounds growth, not
   correctness"): after 100 later flows on the same hot doc (fund/corp pool,
   character campaign funds), an old key is evicted and a retried old attempt
   double-applies. Time variant: terminal receipt TTL-expires after 30d while
   legs may still be in-window, or vice versa. Known residual; hot-account
   consumers should document it, not silently accept it.
3. One-leg-per-document rule (documented in primitive): two legs on one doc
   under one key collide via the $ne guard and the second balance change is
   SILENTLY skipped. Every extraction must preserve the merged-leg shape.
4. Terminal keys fail closed (MoneyFlowTerminalError; new key per retry) and
   fingerprint mismatches throw MoneyFlowKeyConflictError. Routes must mint
   fresh keys per attempt and validate/forward client Idempotency-Key; port
   the source route replay/conflict/terminal tests with each slice.
5. UNCOMPENSATED marker: a revert-less step failing after an applied prefix
   settles failed+UNCOMPENSATED:<step>:<reason> (fail closed for ops). Source
   deliberately leaves several writes as post-commit best effort (financial-tx
   rows, full-retirement flags, zero-unit holder cleanup, bond_issuance /
   bond_maturity ledger rows). Extraction must preserve that classification,
   never silently drop those writes, and ops needs a ledger/runbook entry.
6. No generic orphan sweep exists in the primitive. Orphan/key-only recovery
   is wired per consumer (forex fills re-driven by turn driver; share fills
   via shareFillAudit + turn-driver orphan scan). Route-path slices rely on
   client retry; their in_progress receipts linger by design (TTL ignores
   them) so collection growth needs monitoring.
