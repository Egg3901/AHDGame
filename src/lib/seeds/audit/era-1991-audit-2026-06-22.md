# Era Audit — 1991 (`1991-default`)

**Date:** 2026-06-22
**Auditor:** Claude (seed-readiness audit, `src/lib/seeds/audit/seedReadiness.test.ts`)
**Scope:** US 1991 reset era. Every number below is measured from the live seed
code — none is estimated.

## Verdict

**1991 is structurally complete and internally valid, but has one unresolved
calibration decision that materially affects play balance:** the era is anchored
to **two different elections at once**. Initial party **Org** is seeded from the
**1988** result (Bush landslide); the demographic-derived **lean** and the
calibration **target** are anchored to **1992** (Clinton). The two disagree in
direction in ~8 swing states, and this single inconsistency is the cause of the
weak reconstruction score (70% sign agreement, ρ 0.685).

Nothing here is a data-presence gap — all ten seed artifacts exist and contain
real 1991-era values. The finding is a calibration/anchor decision, not a missing
file.

## 2. Baseline reconstruction — SOFT FAIL (the headline issue)

Derived lean projected back to a margin (`margin ≈ −lean × 10`) vs the real 1988
result:

| Metric                           | Measured     | Bar     | Result                 |
| -------------------------------- | ------------ | ------- | ---------------------- |
| Sign agreement (decisive states) | **70%**      | ≥ 85%   | ✗ soft fail            |
| Mean abs margin error            | **11.51 pt** | ≤ 14 pt | ✓ pass                 |
| Rank correlation (Spearman ρ)    | **0.685**    | ≥ 0.70  | ✗ soft fail (marginal) |
| n                                | 51           | —       | —                      |

These warn in soft mode and fail under `CALIBRATION_HARD=1`.

### 2a. The 15 sign misses are a single coherent pattern

Decisive states where the derived sign ≠ the 1988 winner:

`NH, NV, NJ, DE, ME, VT, CT, OH, CA, MD, CO, MI, NM, IL, PA`

**Every one is a state Bush won in the 1988 landslide that the demographic seed
derives as left** (verified: `All Bush-won-1988 states derived LEFT: true`). There
are zero misses in the other direction. These are precisely the states that voted
**Clinton in 1992** — CA, CT, DE, IL, MD, ME, NJ, NM, NH, NV, OH, PA, VT, CO, MI
all flipped to Clinton. The seed is not wrong about these states; it is being
scored against the wrong election.

### 2b. Margin error is partly a projection-ceiling artifact

The worst margin errors are landslide states, not swing states:

| State | real 1988 | derived disp | proj margin | abs err  |
| ----- | --------- | ------------ | ----------- | -------- |
| DC    | +68.4     | −2.21        | +22.1       | **46.3** |
| NH    | −26.2     | −0.55        | +5.5        | 31.7     |
| UT    | −34.2     | +0.84        | −8.4        | 25.8     |
| NV    | −21.0     | −0.45        | +4.5        | 25.5     |

DC's 46-pt error is structural: derived display leans cluster in roughly
±2.2, so `lean × 10` tops out near ±22 and cannot reproduce a +68 (or −34)
blowout regardless of calibration. Landslide cells therefore inflate the mean
error. The swing-state errors (the politically meaningful ones) are smaller —
MO 1.8, WI 0.3, MN 1.4. **Interpretation:** 11.5 pt overstates the real
miscalibration; the directional (sign) problem in §2a is the substantive issue.

## 3. Root cause — 1991 is anchored to two elections at once

| Subsystem               | Anchored to               | Source                                                      |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Initial party **Org**   | **1988** (Bush v Dukakis) | `marginsForPreset('1991-default')` → `ELECTION_1988_MARGIN` |
| Reconstruction baseline | **1988**                  | `electionBaselines.ts` (kept in sync with Org)              |
| Demographic **lean**    | ~**1992**                 | `deriveRegionLeans` from `stateCensusData1991`              |
| Calibration **target**  | **1992**                  | `targets.ts`: `election: "US 1992 presidential, by state"`  |

The demographic seed and the calibration target already agree on **1992**. Only
the Org seed (and the reconstruction baseline I aligned to it) use **1988**.

### Evidence the seed reconstructs 1992 well

Against the 1992-anchored target anchors in `targets.ts`, the same derived leans
satisfy **50 of 51** sign constraints:

- `expectRight` (UT, ID, WY, NE, KS, AK, OK, SC, IN): **all 9 positive** ✓
- `expectLeft` (DC, MA, RI, NY, MD, HI, MN, IL, WV, AR): **9 of 10 left** ✓ — only
  **AR** derives right (+0.22), and AR is the **documented-accepted** case (the
  README notes AR's moderate-right seed with a Democratic org shift is intended).
- Ordering (MA < TX, NY < WY): both hold ✓.

So the seed is a strong 1992 reconstruction and a weak 1988 one — consistent with
1988 being an outlier Republican landslide.

## 4. Org ↔ lean directional conflict — the play-balance consequence

Because Org is seeded from 1988 but lean from ~1992, they point in **opposite
directions** in the swing states Bush won in '88. Measured org (Dem party id `1`
vs Rep `2`):

| State | Dem org | Rep org | Org favors | Demographic lean | Conflict |
| ----- | ------- | ------- | ---------- | ---------------- | -------- |
| CA    | 25.0    | 27.5    | REP        | −0.93 (left)     | ✗        |
| NJ    | 25.0    | 34.5    | REP        | −0.99 (left)     | ✗        |
| IL    | 25.0    | 26.5    | REP        | −0.59 (left)     | ✗        |
| CT    | 25.0    | 28.6    | REP        | −0.98 (left)     | ✗        |
| MD    | 25.0    | 26.5    | REP        | −1.05 (left)     | ✗        |
| PA    | 25.0    | 26.6    | REP        | −0.31 (left)     | ✗        |
| MI    | 25.0    | 30.5    | REP        | −0.38 (left)     | ✗        |
| VT    | 25.0    | 27.5    | REP        | −1.27 (left)     | ✗        |
| WV    | 28.3    | 25.0    | DEM        | −0.16 (left)     | ✓        |
| MA    | 30.5    | 25.0    | DEM        | −1.41 (left)     | ✓        |

This directly violates the design principle that _seed org + seed lean should
jointly point to the same election baseline_. A player starting in 1991 California
sees a demographically blue state where Republicans hold the stronger initial
ground organization — an incoherent starting position.

**Why the audit's own org-coupling test still passes:** `[3]` scores org against
the same 1988 table it is built from, so it is internally consistent and cannot
detect the org-vs-lean disagreement. This is a blind spot in the current audit
(see §7).

## 7. Secondary finding — audit coverage regressions to close

The rewrite is broader but dropped two checks the old suite had; worth restoring
for full 1991 confidence:

1. **Base-policy value invariants.** The new audit confirms `basePolicies1991`
   exists but no longer asserts the era rates (US corp 34%, JP 3%, IE 40%). They
   are currently correct (§1) but unguarded against regression. Recommend a
   declarative `POLICY_INVARIANTS` table analogous to `ANACHRONISMS`.
2. **Org-vs-lean cross-check.** Add an audit assertion that initial Org direction
   agrees with the demographic lean direction in decisive states. This would have
   caught §4 automatically; today it is invisible to `[3]`.

---

## 8. Recommendations (prioritized)

1. **Resolve the anchor (blocking decision).** Pick one election for 1991:
   - **Recommended — anchor on 1992.** Re-seed Org from a 1992 by-state margin
     table and repoint `electionBaselines.ts` 1991 → 1992. This aligns Org, lean,
     target, and reconstruction; resolves all 15 sign misses and the §4 conflicts;
     and matches the demographic seed (which already reconstructs 1992 at 50/51
     anchors). Requires adding `1992ElectionResults.ts`.
   - **Or — keep 1988 deliberately** (model a game that starts in the wake of the
     '88 landslide) and then _also_ anchor the demographic lean and target to
     1988, and document that reconstruction is expected low for a landslide era
     (raise/relax the 1991 sign bar accordingly).

   The current half-and-half state (Org 1988, lean/target 1992) is the only
   genuinely wrong configuration.

2. **AR** stays as-is — accepted moderate-right seed; the org-leadership system is
   designed to shift it. No action.
3. Restore the two audit checks in §7.
4. Treat reconstruction **rank correlation** (0.685) as the truer signal than mean
   margin error (which §2b shows is inflated by landslide projection ceilings).
